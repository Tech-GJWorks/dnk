How to Read This Book हा ग्रंथ कसा वाचावा

Astrologer-training notice / ज्योतिषी-प्रशिक्षण सूचना

This book is written for the reader's own training as a Jyotish practitioner. The native whose chart is analysed — Dharmesh Naresh Kandare, born 25 November 1995 at 16:02 IST in Ichalkaranji — is a real person. Every placement, every dasha date, every yoga and dosha verdict in this book is genuine. The reader (Gitesh Jawale) uses this chart as a complete worked-out case study to learn how a Vedic chart is actually read: rule by rule, planet by planet, house by house, dasha by dasha. Nothing is hidden — every classical possibility, every cancellation, every "hard reading" is shown. Treat the book as a teaching specimen, not a delivered client report.

हा ग्रंथ वाचकाच्या स्वतःच्या ज्योतिष-अभ्यासासाठी लिहिला आहे. ज्या कुंडलीचे विश्लेषण इथे केले आहे — धर्मेश नरेश कांडारे, जन्म २५ नोव्हेंबर १९९५, सायं ४:०२ IST, इचलकरंजी — ती एक प्रत्यक्ष व्यक्ती आहे. प्रत्येक ग्रहस्थान, प्रत्येक दशा-तारीख, प्रत्येक योग-दोष या ग्रंथात अस्सल आहे. वाचक (गितेश जवळे) ही कुंडली एक संपूर्ण सोडवलेले उदाहरण म्हणून वापरतो — कुंडली प्रत्यक्षात कशी वाचली जाते: नियम-नियम, ग्रह-ग्रह, भाव-भाव, दशा-दशा. इथे काहीही लपवलेले नाही — प्रत्येक शास्त्रीय शक्यता, प्रत्येक रद्द-योग, प्रत्येक "कठोर वाचन" मांडले आहे.

This is not the reader's first kundli book — that role belongs to the parent volume, Gitesh's Kundli (Tech-GJWorks/gitesh-kundli), which carries the full encyclopedic teaching of every rashi, nakshatra, house, graha, yoga, dosha, dasha, and transit. This DNK book sits on top of that teaching — it does not repeat the generic Layer-1 material. Where a section needs a one-line rule-nod, it gives the rule and links the chapter in the parent book that unpacks it. The bulk of every page here is application — what does this rule mean for this specific chart, born at this specific moment, in this specific city, with this specific dasha now running?

1.1 Why a second book on the same shelf? एकाच कपाटात दुसरा ग्रंथ का?

A serious astrology student must read more than one fully worked chart before they can read a stranger's chart competently. The parent book covers Gitesh's own chart — Cancer lagna, debilitated Sun in the 4th house, Jupiter in the 6th, Saturn-Ketu yuti in the 9th. That is one signature. Dharmesh's chart is a very different signature: Aries lagna, lord Mars in own-nakshatra Mula in the 9th house with Venus + Moon + Neptune; Sun-Mercury-Jupiter-Pluto stellium in the 8th house Scorpio; Saturn alone in own-sign Aquarius 11th; Rahu-Ketu axis on 1H–7H. Studying both side-by-side teaches the reader how the same rules play out across utterly different temperaments. The parent book shows what a watery, sensitive, family-anchored Cancer lagna looks like; this book shows what a fiery, head-forward, identity-driven Aries lagna with three planets in the 9th house of dharma looks like.

The second purpose is harder to admit: an astrologer who reads only their own chart is dangerously vain. The reader sees the same pattern from too many angles, develops favourite verdicts, ignores the placements that don't flatter them. Forcing oneself to read a chart that is not one's own — to find the strengths and weaknesses of another human, in writing, without softening — is a discipline. The reader will encounter placements here that flatter Dharmesh (Saturn own-sign 11th, Vipareeta-Harsha-adjacent Jupiter in 8th) and placements that do not (Sun-Mercury combust in dusthana 8th, Kalathra-yoga from Mars-Venus yuti in 9th, Mars MD currently activating the surgical-litigation signature). Both kinds of reading must appear on the same page, without flinching.

1.2 What this book promises — and what it refuses ग्रंथ काय देतो आणि काय नाकारतो

This book promises a complete, classical, multi-school reading of Dharmesh's chart. It draws on Parashari technique as the primary frame, with Jaimini cross-checks for karaka-rotation, Krishnamurti-Paddhati cross-checks for cusp-nakshatra fine-tuning, and the Tajaka-school's varshaphal only where annual-chart questions arise. Where two schools disagree, the disagreement is shown — never silently resolved. The reader is supposed to learn how a rigorous astrologer holds two contradictory rules in mind, evaluates which fits the chart's overall signature, and then writes the most probable verdict alongside the alternative.

The book refuses three things outright. First, it refuses single-date predictions. Vedic technique gives windows — months at PD resolution, weeks at Sookshma-Dasha resolution. Anyone reading this book and walking out saying "the chart predicts marriage on March 14, 2027" has misread it. The book will say: strongest marriage window is Mars–Jupiter AD (Jul 2026 – Jun 2027), with Mars–Venus AD (Dec 2029 – Feb 2031) as the backup window if the first does not crystallise. The reader learns the windowed-forecast language and never lapses into single-date predictions when consulting on real clients later.

Second, it refuses to flatten Dharmesh's free will. The chart shows probabilities, weighted by current dasha and transit, but every probability assumes the native continues to behave as the chart's signature suggests. If Dharmesh, having read this book or worked with another astrologer, changes his exercise routine, his anger-management, his alcohol intake, his partnership selection criteria — many of the "hard reading" probabilities shift. The book records the chart's natural trajectory; the native's choices either ride that trajectory or bend it.

Third, it refuses fear-based remedy selling. Chapter 32 lists every classical remedy this chart calls for — Hanuman Chalisa for the Mars MD, Friday Lakshmi puja for Venus marak, pitru-tarpan for the 8H Sun-Jupiter, daily meditation for Ketu on lagna. Each remedy is explained by its classical mechanism, not as a guaranteed cure. Gemstones in particular are flagged with caution: this chart, during Mars MD, is not recommended for sapphire/diamond/cat's-eye until Rahu MD opens in 2032. A reader who internalizes the "by what mechanism does this work?" question protects themselves and their future clients from predatory consultants.

1.3 How a page in this book is structured पानाची मांडणी

Every chapter from ch07 onwards follows the same skeleton, designed to teach the reader the standard analytical sequence used by working astrologers when they sit down to a new chart:

  1. Lede — one paragraph naming the placement and what it controls. The reader's first sweep of the chart is here.
  2. Generic rule (one or two paragraphs) — the classical Sanskrit-source teaching for this placement: what Parashara, Phaladeepika, Brihat Jataka, Saravali say. This is intentionally short; the parent book carries the full unpack with all 12 sign/house/yuti permutations. A link points the reader there if they need to read the long form.
  3. Applied to this chart (the bulk — 400+ EN words per section) — exactly how this placement is realised given the surrounding planets, the lordships, the nakshatra of the planet, the dasha that activates it, the transit that triggers it. Every conclusion is backed by a "Proof from chart" pointer to the chart-page reference.
  4. Marathi summary block — a 200–300 word Marathi pass that covers all the EN possibilities discussed, including the cancellations and alternatives. Designed for a reader fluent in Marathi who wants a vernacular synopsis.
  5. Learning Note block — the most teaching-dense unit. Rule (classical citation), Applied to this chart, Alternative (what changes if a single placement were different), Hidden risk (the failure mode a junior astrologer would miss here).
  6. Alt-readings block — two or three bullets showing how a Krishnamurti consultant, a Jaimini consultant, or a Lal-Kitab consultant would read the same placement differently. Teaches the reader to hold multiple lineages without confusion.
  7. Conclusion block — chart-proof, 5–7 probability paths with explicit percentages, the cancellation map (yogas that break the prediction), transit triggers, and the 1–5% "hard reading" — the worst-case the rules permit, never hidden.

The reader is encouraged to copy this skeleton the first dozen times they read a real client's chart in the future. After about fifty charts, the skeleton becomes second nature; after two hundred, the reader can compress the analysis into a thirty-minute conversation without losing any of the structure. The skeleton exists because a junior astrologer's first mistake is always the same: they jump to a verdict before they have walked the full sequence.

1.4 The recurring terms used in this book वारंवार येणारे शब्द

The full glossary lives in ch33. Below are only the terms a reader must hold in working memory while reading any chapter:

  • Lagna / लग्न — the eastern horizon rashi at the moment of birth. Here: Aries. The lagna's lord is the chart's lagnesh. Here: Mars.
  • Rashi / राशी — sign. The 12 signs of the zodiac. A planet's rashi shapes its style.
  • Nakshatra / नक्षत्र — the 27 lunar mansions. A planet's nakshatra shapes its nuance and donates a sub-lord. Here: lagna nakshatra is Ashvini Pada 3 (Sun-sub-lord, Mercury-pada-lord — pada points to Gemini sub-attributes).
  • Bhava / भाव, Ghar / घर — house. The twelve life-domains laid over the zodiac, anchored by the lagna.
  • Graha / ग्रह — planet. Nine in classical Jyotish (Sun through Saturn + Rahu + Ketu); modern practice often adds Uranus, Neptune, Pluto for psychological/generational layers — we name them but treat them as secondary.
  • Lord / स्वामी, Lagnesh / लग्नेश — the planet that owns a sign. The lord of the lagna is the lagnesh — the most personal planet in the chart. Here: Mars.
  • Karaka / कारक — natural significator. Sun = karaka of father and self-authority; Moon = mother and mind; Mars = courage, siblings, property; Mercury = communication, intellect, business; Jupiter = wisdom, children, husband; Venus = wife, comfort, art; Saturn = service, longevity, career; Rahu = innovation, foreign, illusion; Ketu = detachment, moksha, past-life karma.
  • Drishti / दृष्टी — aspect. The classical aspects: every planet aspects the 7th from itself; Mars also 4th and 8th; Jupiter also 5th and 9th; Saturn also 3rd and 10th.
  • Yuti / युती — conjunction. Two or more planets in the same sign.
  • Dasha / दशा — planetary period. Vimshottari dasha is the dominant system; 120-year cycle of nine MDs. Here: Mars MD running 19-Jan-2025 → 19-Jan-2032.
  • Antardasha / अंतर्दशा (AD), Pratyantar-dasha / प्रत्यंतर्दशा (PD), Sookshma-dasha — sub-periods at decreasing resolution.
  • Gochar / गोचर — transit. Where each planet currently is in the sky, mapped over the natal chart.
  • Yoga / योग — special planetary combination that produces a distinct effect (Sasa-Mahapurusha, Vipareeta-Raja, Chandra-Mangala, Gajakesari, etc.).
  • Dosha / दोष — afflictive combination (Kalathra, Pitru, Stree-rina, Guru-rina, Mangal, Kaal-Sarpa, etc.).
  • Maraka / मारक — death-inflicting karaka. Lords of the 2nd and 7th houses are the classical marakas. Here: Venus (owns 2nd Taurus and 7th Libra) is the double-marak — handle with care in all longevity discussions.
  • Combust / अस्त — a planet within roughly 12° of the Sun loses its independent voice. Here: Mercury is combust at 10°08' Scorpio, only 1°15' from Sun. This single fact alters every Mercury-related reading in the chart.

1.5 The reader's mindset while studying this chart वाचकाची मानसिकता

Three attitudes serve the reader best.

First, hold the whole chart in mind before reading any one placement. A junior mistake is to read "Sun in 8th = inheritance + father-health issue" and stop there, missing that Sun is also Bhagyesh of Aries (lord of 5th — children, intellect, devotion), so a 5L-8H placement also activates the children/devotion line and brings the father-line and the children-line into the same dusthana. The full reading is much richer than the soundbite. Train yourself to ask, every single time: what does this planet rule (lordship), where does it sit (house), with whom (yutis), looked at by whom (aspects), in whose nakshatra (sub-lord), at what dasha (current)? Six axes, every placement, no shortcuts.

Second, name the cancellation before naming the conclusion. Every "good" yoga in this chart has a cancellation rule that may apply, and every "bad" combination has a vipareeta or neech-bhanga or aspect-rescue that may neutralize it. Naming the cancellation rule out loud, even when it does not apply, trains the reader's nervous system to never deliver a verdict without first having canvassed the rescues. In Dharmesh's chart specifically — Sasa-Mahapurusha-partial has the kendra-cancellation question (Saturn is in 11H upachaya, not strict kendra), Vipareeta-Harsha through 6L Mercury in 8H is qualified by the combustion of Mercury, and Kalathra-yoga from Mars-Venus yuti has the Jupiter-aspect-from-8H rescue. Each of these is named in the chapters where it appears.

Third, the chart is a probability map, not a sentence handed down. When the book says a 60% probability path leads to marriage in the Mars–Jupiter AD window, it means that given the chart's natural trajectory and standard rates of agency, six in ten people with this exact signature would experience this in this window. A reader who is going to consult on real clients later must internalize this language. Saying "you will marry in Mars–Jupiter AD" sets up a brittle reading; saying "the strongest probability arc of the next eighteen months is the marriage signature peaking during Mars–Jupiter, with backup windows in Mars–Venus and early Rahu MD if the first does not crystallise" sets up a robust one. Robust readings survive the chart not delivering on schedule; brittle readings shame the consultant when the date passes.

Learning Note — The 6-axis read habit / सहा-अक्ष वाचनपद्धत

Rule: Every planet has six axes that must be checked before any verdict is written. Skipping even one produces shallow readings.

The six axes: (1) Lordship — which houses does this planet rule? (2) House — where does this planet sit? (3) Yutis — what other planets share its sign? (4) Aspects in — which planets aspect this planet? (5) Aspects out — which houses and planets does this planet aspect? (6) Sub-lord — in whose nakshatra and which pada does this planet sit?

Applied to Mars (this chart's lagnesh): (1) Mars lords 1H Aries and 8H Scorpio. (2) Mars sits in 9H Sagittarius. (3) Yutis with Moon, Venus, Neptune. (4) Aspects from Saturn 3rd-drishti? — Saturn in 11H Aquarius, 3rd from 11H is 1H Aries → Saturn does not aspect Mars's house; check 10th aspect of Saturn from 11H → 8H Scorpio. No Saturn aspect on Mars itself. (5) Mars's outgoing aspects: 4th from 9H = 12H Pisces; 7th from 9H = 3H Gemini; 8th from 9H = 4H Cancer. (6) Mars sits in Mula Pada 1 — sub-lord is Ketu, pada-attributes lean towards Sagittarius itself (Pada 1 ruled by Mars; same sign — Mars in its own pada). The six-axis read of Mars takes about 90 seconds aloud and produces a vastly richer verdict than "Mars in 9th = courage and dharma."

Hidden risk if axis is skipped: A reader who reads only axis 2 (Mars in 9H) misses that Mars is the 8L (Scorpio) sitting in 9H — an 8L-9H placement that imports research/occult/sudden-event flavour into the dharma-house. This 8L-flavouring is the subtle reason Dharmesh's spiritual instinct is research-led rather than devotion-led. Without the lordship axis, the reading would have read him as a temple-going devotee — incorrect.

Alt-readings — lineage differences

Krishnamurti-Paddhati (KP): KP collapses house-cusps to sub-lord level. A KP consultant reading this chart would not say "Mars is in 9H" — they would say "Mars sits in the Ketu-Mercury-Venus cuspal sub-stretch, signifying houses owned by those sub-lords." The verdict would feel more granular but less narratively rich. KP is excellent for timing-question precision and weaker for character-arc storytelling.

Jaimini: A Jaimini consultant would re-rank the karakas (Char-karaka system — based on highest-degree planet). In this chart, Saturn at 24°11' Aquarius is the Atma-Karaka. That single fact reframes the whole reading: the soul's primary lesson is not Aries-style self-assertion (Parashari lagna-frame) but Saturn-style endurance, service, structure (Jaimini AK-frame). Both readings are true; they answer different questions.

Lal-Kitab: A Lal-Kitab consultant would focus on the 1H-7H Ketu-Rahu axis and the 8H stellium with a fatalistic-karmic frame, prescribing very specific household-level remedies (silver in pocket, peacock-feather under pillow). Lal-Kitab is descriptively weaker but remedially specific — useful when a client needs a daily ritual to anchor a difficult period rather than a multi-axis analysis.

मराठी सारांश

हा ग्रंथ वाचकाच्या ज्योतिषी-प्रशिक्षणासाठी आहे. धर्मेश नरेश कांडारेची प्रत्यक्ष कुंडली एक संपूर्ण उदाहरण म्हणून घेतली आहे — मेष लग्न, मंगळ ९व्या भावी मूळ-नक्षत्रात स्व-नक्षत्रात, शुक्र-चंद्र-नेपच्यून सोबत; सूर्य-बुध(अस्त)-गुरू-प्लूटो ८व्या भावी वृश्चिकात; शनी स्व-राशी कुंभात ११व्या भावी; राहू-केतू अक्ष ७-१. प्रत्येक नियम, प्रत्येक रद्द-योग, प्रत्येक "कठोर वाचन" दाखवले आहे; काहीही लपवलेले नाही. वाचनाची सहा-अक्ष पद्धत — स्वामित्व, भाव, युती, येणारी दृष्टी, जाणारी दृष्टी, उप-स्वामी — प्रत्येक ग्रहावर वापरली जाते. वेगवेगळ्या परंपरा (पाराशरी, जैमिनी, KP, लाल-किताब) कशा वेगळ्या वाचतात हे ठळकपणे दाखवले जाते. हा ग्रंथ नेमक्या तारखा देत नाही — कालखंड देतो. कुंडली नियती नव्हे, संभाव्यतेचा नकाशा आहे. पुढच्या ३३ अध्यायांचा पाया हाच आहे.
