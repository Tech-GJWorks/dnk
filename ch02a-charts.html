Key Yogas at a glance

  • शश-महापुरुष योग (Sasa Mahapurusha — partial) — Saturn own-sign Aquarius. 11H is upachaya not strict kendra → partial Mahapurusha effect through own-sign dignity.
  • विपरीत-राज (Harsha-adjacent) — Mercury (6L) sits in 8H (dusthana). Combust-Mercury caveat softens the bhava-vipareeta but preserves Harsha-yoga via 6L position alone.
  • राहू-केतू अक्ष 1H–7H — past-life karmic axis across self ↔ partner.
  • चंद्र-मंगळ युती — Moon-Mars conjunction in 9H Sagittarius: emotion-courage fusion; finance-and-courage yoga active when activated.
  • केमद्रुम-भंग — Moon has Mars+Venus+Neptune adjacent in same sign → Kemadruma is broken (rescued).
  • गजकेसरी-भंग — Moon 9H and Jupiter 8H are NOT in mutual kendra → Gajakesari does NOT form.
  • नीच-भंग — no debilitated graha at exact debilitation degree; structurally absent.
  • स्त्री-रिण / पितृ-रिण / गुरु-रिण — partial doshas from 8H Sun-Jupiter-Mercury and Mars-Venus yuti; chapter 17 details.

Atma-Karaka (Jaimini)

Highest-degree planet (excluding nodes): Neptune at 29°38′ Sagittarius in modern reading, but traditional Jaimini ignores outer planets → Jupiter at 27°26′ Scorpio is the AK. Atma-Karaka in 8H Scorpio Jyeshtha P4 — the soul's primary lesson is research, regeneration, hidden-knowledge, dharma-through-transformation. The Karakamsa lagna becomes the D-9 placement of Jupiter (Pisces in D9) → spiritual sensitivity, devotional-philosophical pull.
