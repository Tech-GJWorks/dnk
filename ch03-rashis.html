Rashis in this Chart या कुंडलीतील राशी

A rashi (rāśi, राशी) is a 30° sector of the ecliptic — a sign. The twelve signs run Aries through Pisces in fixed order, and the lagna (rising sign at the birth-moment) determines which sign sits at the 1st-house cusp. Once that anchor is fixed, the other eleven signs lock into their houses. This chapter does not teach all twelve signs generically (the parent book does that). It walks only the five rashis this chart actively uses: Aries (lagna + Ketu), Scorpio (8H stellium), Sagittarius (9H stellium), Aquarius (Saturn own-sign 11H), and Libra (Rahu 7H). The other seven rashis hold empty houses; their interpretive load comes from their house-lord's placement, which is treated in later chapters.

3.1 Aries · मेष — the lagna rashi मेष — लग्न-राशी

Aries is the first sign of the zodiac. Its tattva (element) is fire, its modality is cardinal/movable (chara), its lord is Mars, and its symbol is the ram. Aries-lagna natives lead with their head — both literally (the body-part ruled is the head and skull) and metaphorically (their action precedes their reflection). The Sun is exalted in Aries (10° is the exaltation degree); Saturn is debilitated. Mars's own-sign placement here gives Aries-lagna natives a built-in capacity for self-assertion, sudden initiative, physical courage, and a difficulty with sustained patience.

Applied to this chart: Dharmesh's lagna is Aries at 08°28′59″ — meaning the Ascendant has just passed the Ashvini-pada-3 boundary into the deeper layer of the first nakshatra. The lagna degree being only 8°28′ in Aries places him in the early-Aries band, which is classically more impulsive, more head-forward, and less polished than late-Aries (which absorbs more of the Mars-mature ripening). Mars as lagnesh is in Sagittarius 9H in own-nakshatra Mula — the lagnesh has placed itself in the dharma-house in its own nakshatra. This is a strong positional dignity for Mars, even though Sagittarius itself is Jupiter's sign (Mars is a friend of Jupiter, so the placement is harmonious). The result: a head-forward Aries native whose energetic drive is naturally channelled into dharmic, philosophical, and long-arc pursuits — not into pure animal aggression.

Aries-lagna also makes the 7th-house cusp Libra — Venus-ruled, airy, partnership-oriented. The partner-archetype for an Aries-lagna native is opposite to themselves: refined, diplomatic, harmony-seeking. The chart's partner-axis (Libra ↔ Aries) is also the Rahu-Ketu axis. This single fact — that the lagna-partner axis coincides with the nodal axis — is the most important architectural feature of the chart and will be returned to in chapters 7, 11, and 28.

3.2 Scorpio · वृश्चिक — the 8H stellium rashi वृश्चिक — ८व्या भावी ग्रहसमूह

Scorpio is the 8th sign of the zodiac. Tattva: water. Modality: fixed (sthira). Lord: Mars (in classical Parashari assignment, with Pluto as a co-lord in modern Western-Vedic blending — used here for psychological layering only). Symbol: the scorpion (the older esoteric symbols are also the eagle and the phoenix — three levels of Scorpio's evolutionary stages). Scorpio is the sign of transformation, hidden depths, regenerative crisis, occult knowledge, joint resources, surgery, and what classical texts call marma (vulnerable energy-points in body and life). The Moon is debilitated in Scorpio (3° is the exact debilitation degree). Rahu is exalted in Scorpio in many traditions; Ketu is the corresponding debilitation-equivalent.

Applied to this chart: Scorpio occupies the entire 8th house of the chart. The 8th house is itself the natural Scorpio house, so we have a "double-Scorpio" intensification — Scorpio's nature pours into the 8H's significations (longevity, sudden change, joint finance, in-laws' wealth, occult research, surgery, inheritance) and simultaneously the 8H's natural intensity pours back into Scorpio's already-intense temperament. Four planets sit here: Sun 08°53′ Anuradha P2, Mercury 10°08′ Anuradha P3 (combust within 1°15′ of Sun), Jupiter 27°26′ Jyeshtha P4, and Pluto 06°43′ Anuradha P2. This is a stellium — a tight cluster of four bodies in one sign and one house — and stellia always concentrate the chart's psychic gravity onto that point.

The Scorpio-8H stellium is the chart's research/regeneration engine. Sun in Scorpio gives a deep, investigative father-figure and an introspective self-authority; Mercury combust here means the analytical mind operates underground (silent in conversation but constantly processing); Jupiter in Scorpio is the dharma-seeker (Jupiter's 9th-lordship places dharma into the regenerative-crisis arena, meaning Dharmesh meets dharma through experiences of transformation rather than through smooth philosophical exposition); Pluto in Anuradha amplifies the depth-psychology theme. Chapter 9 (Sun 8H), ch12 (Mercury 8H + Rahu), and ch13 (Jupiter 8H) treat each planet individually; this chapter only frames the rashi's role.

3.3 Sagittarius · धनु — the 9H stellium rashi धनु — ९व्या भावी ग्रहसमूह

Sagittarius is the 9th sign. Tattva: fire. Modality: dual/mutable (dwiswabhava). Lord: Jupiter. Symbol: the centaur archer — half-human, half-horse, drawing a bow toward the sky. Sagittarius rules dharma, higher learning, the guru-principle, long-distance travel, foreign cultures, philosophical synthesis, faith, generosity. Ketu is exalted in Sagittarius (some say in Scorpio — schools differ); the South Node finds its philosophical home here. Sagittarius is friendly to Mars (mutual friends through their fire-tattva alignment), so Mars in Sagittarius is a comfortable placement.

Applied to this chart: Sagittarius occupies the 9th house. Like Scorpio-8H, this is a double-dignity: the 9th house is itself naturally Sagittarius's house. The dharma-amplification is therefore extremely strong. Four bodies sit in Sagittarius 9H: Mars 02°20′ Mula P1 (lagnesh in dharma-house), Venus 03°34′ Mula P2, Moon 17°53′ Purvashadha P2 (the chart's most important moving body), and Neptune 29°38′ Uttarashadha P1 at the very end of the sign.

The 9H Sagittarius stellium is the chart's dharma/relationship/aspirational engine — the counterweight to the 8H Scorpio research/crisis engine. Where the 8H stellium pulls the native into hidden depths (investigation, occult, transformation), the 9H stellium pulls them toward visible aspiration (philosophy, partnership, long-arc faith, foreign culture, art). This dual-engine architecture is the most defining feature of Dharmesh's psyche: he is simultaneously drawn down (8H Scorpio) and up (9H Sagittarius) at all times. A junior astrologer reading only the 9H stellium will see a dharma-bound idealist; reading only the 8H stellium will see a research-driven introvert; the truth is both, and they alternate in cycles set by the Vimshottari dashas. The Mars MD (Jan 2025 – Jan 2032) is firing the 9H engine; the Jupiter MD (2050 – 2066) will fire the 8H engine. This is the chart's strategic rhythm.

3.4 Aquarius · कुंभ — Saturn's own-sign 11H कुंभ — शनी स्व-राशी ११वा भाव

Aquarius is the 11th sign. Tattva: air. Modality: fixed. Lord: Saturn (with Uranus as modern co-ruler). Symbol: the water-bearer (a misnomer — Aquarius is an air sign whose symbol is poured-knowledge, not poured-water). Aquarius rules large groups, social ideals, innovation, friend-networks, the elderly, structured non-conformity, and long-term institutional gains. It is the more humanitarian of Saturn's two signs (Capricorn being the more authoritarian).

Applied to this chart: Aquarius occupies the 11th house. Saturn sits in its own sign here at 24°11′ — a very late-sign degree, in Purvabhadrapada nakshatra pada 2. Own-sign placement of any planet is one of the strongest single dignities in classical Jyotish: Saturn gives stable, long-term, low-volatility benefits when seated in its own sign. The 11th house being the natural gains-house, friends-house, fulfilment-of-desires-house adds compound benefit. The Sasa-Mahapurusha-yoga rule requires Saturn in own/exalted sign in a kendra (1/4/7/10); 11H is upachaya, not kendra, so the Mahapurusha-yoga is only partial — Sasa-yoga's energetic signature is present (slow, steady, late-ripening rewards; respected elder-status; long-life karaka strong) but not the full Mahapurusha-stamp. This is the chart's strongest single anchor for late-life security and social standing. Chapter 14 (Saturn 11H + Ketu 1H) unpacks fully.

3.5 Libra · तूळ — the 7H Rahu rashi तूळ — ७वा भाव राहू

Libra is the 7th sign. Tattva: air. Modality: cardinal/movable. Lord: Venus. Symbol: the balance-scales — the only zodiacal sign whose symbol is an inanimate object, hinting at the abstract, choice-weighing nature of Libran psychology. Libra rules partnership, marriage, contracts, diplomacy, refinement, art, justice, and the formal "other" in any one-to-one relationship. Saturn is exalted in Libra; Sun is debilitated. Rahu finds its most-cooperative seat in Libra (Rahu's outward-aspirant nature aligns with Libra's people-orientation).

Applied to this chart: Libra occupies the 7th house. Rahu sits here at 00°34′ Chitra P3 — within one degree of the sign's beginning, which is the sandhi zone (junction degrees, classically considered weak in Parashari but strong in KP-school). Rahu in Libra in 7H is one of the most-debated placements in classical literature: some schools call it "Rahu's exaltation" (Rahu seats well in Venus's airy-partner-sign), others class it as a midpoint between exaltation and neutral. The reading depends on which school the consultant trains in. This book treats Rahu in Libra 7H as moderately strong — capable of producing both excellent unconventional partnership and intense relationship-illusion, depending on dashas and the strength of the 7L (Venus). The Rahu-Ketu axis (1H Aries ↔ 7H Libra) is the chart's karmic spine, treated in detail in chapter 7 (Aries lagna section on Ketu) and chapter 28 (marriage).

3.6 The seven empty rashis — read by lord-placement रिकाम्या सात राशी — स्वामी-स्थानावरून वाचन

Taurus (2H), Gemini (3H), Cancer (4H), Leo (5H), Virgo (6H), Capricorn (10H), and Pisces (12H) hold no natal planet. Their interpretive weight comes from the placement of each sign's lord:

  • Taurus 2H — Venus rules. Venus in 9H Mula → 2H significations (savings, family-line wealth, speech, food-habits) read through Venus's actual placement.
  • Gemini 3H — Mercury rules. Mercury combust in 8H → 3H significations (younger siblings, communication, courage, hands) muted by Mercury's combustion.
  • Cancer 4H — Moon rules. Moon in 9H Purvashadha → 4H significations (mother, home, vehicles, emotional anchor) elevated into dharma-frame.
  • Leo 5H — Sun rules. Sun in 8H Scorpio → 5H significations (children, intellect, devotion, speculation) pulled into 8H transformation/research/hidden-grief.
  • Virgo 6H — Mercury rules. Mercury combust in 8H → 6H significations (enemies, debt, disease, service) read with Mercury's combustion caveat — Vipareeta-Harsha rule applies (6L in 8H gives windfall-from-disorder yoga).
  • Capricorn 10H — Saturn rules. Saturn in own 11H 24° → 10H significations (career, status, action-house) strengthened by 10L Saturn's own-sign dignity and 10L→11H placement (career converts directly into gains).
  • Pisces 12H — Jupiter rules. Jupiter in 8H Scorpio → 12H significations (loss, foreign, bed-pleasure, moksha) read through Jupiter's research/transformation flavour — moksha-instinct is investigative, not devotional.
Learning Note — How rashi-tattva interacts with house-bhava / राशी-तत्व आणि भाव-नैसर्गिकता

Rule: When a sign's natural-house and its actual house in a chart match (e.g., Scorpio in 8H, Sagittarius in 9H, Aquarius in 11H), the sign's signature is doubled — the rashi's nature and the bhava's nature reinforce each other. When they mismatch (e.g., Cancer naturally a 4th-house sign but appearing in some other house), the reading must blend the two natures into a synthesis.

Applied to this chart: Three of the chart's five active rashis are in their natural houses: Scorpio in 8H, Sagittarius in 9H, Aquarius in 11H. This is the rare "alignment chart" pattern where dusthana-natural (8H), dharma-natural (9H), and gains-natural (11H) are all double-activated. The chart's overall thematic clarity is therefore unusually high — the native's life-task does not present itself with mixed signals at the rashi level. The two active rashis NOT in their natural houses are Aries (sign 1, naturally 1H — and it IS in 1H here, so this is actually a third natural-house match) and Libra (sign 7, naturally 7H — also matched). So all five active rashis are in their natural houses. This is structurally significant: the chart speaks with a unified architectural voice rather than mixed signals.

Hidden risk: The downside of double-alignment is that weaknesses are also doubled. Scorpio's combust-Mercury and 8H's affliction-nature combine to produce a deeper Mercury-vulnerability than a Mercury-combust in a non-Scorpio sign would. Similarly, Saturn's Aquarius-own-sign in 11H amplifies both strengths (Sasa-partial, gains-stability) and weaknesses (over-detachment, late marriage, slow-build that can frustrate during youth).

Alt-readings — rashi-frames in different schools

Western tropical Vedic: Would shift every rashi by ~24° (the Lahiri ayanamsa). The native's tropical Sun-sign becomes Sagittarius (instead of Vedic Scorpio); tropical lagna becomes Taurus (instead of Vedic Aries). Some hybrid practitioners read the chart twice — once tropical for psychology, once sidereal for timing.

Krishnamurti-Paddhati: KP does not weight rashi-nature heavily; it operates almost entirely at the sub-lord (sub-nakshatra) level. A KP consultant would barely mention "Scorpio 8H" and would instead talk about "the Anuradha-Saturn-Mercury cuspal sub-stretch in 8H." Both frames are valid; they answer different questions.

Jaimini: Uses rashi-aspects (sign-to-sign aspects independent of planet) more heavily. Cardinal signs (1, 4, 7, 10) aspect fixed signs (2, 5, 8, 11) and mutable signs (3, 6, 9, 12) under specific rules. A Jaimini reading would foreground Aries-Libra-Cancer-Capricorn rashi-aspect dynamics, which Parashari rarely centres.

मराठी सारांश

या कुंडलीत पाच राशी सक्रिय आहेत — मेष (लग्न + केतू), वृश्चिक (८व्या भावी सूर्य-बुध(अस्त)-गुरू-प्लूटो), धनू (९व्या भावी चंद्र-मंगळ-शुक्र-नेपच्यून), कुंभ (शनी स्व-राशीत ११व्या भावी), तूळ (राहू ७व्या भावी). उल्लेखनीय: या पाचही राशी त्यांच्या नैसर्गिक भावात आहेत — मेष १व्या, वृश्चिक ८व्या, धनू ९व्या, तूळ ७व्या, कुंभ ११व्या → कुंडलीची संरचनात्मक स्पष्टता प्रचंड. ८वा भाव-वृश्चिक संशोधन/परिवर्तन इंजिन; ९वा भाव-धनू धर्म/नातेसंबंध इंजिन — हे दोन इंजिन एकाच कुंडलीत एकाच वेळी चालतात आणि जातकाच्या मानसिकतेला "खाली खेच आणि वर खेच" अशी द्वि-दिशा देतात. मंगळ महादशा (२०२५-२०३२) ९वा-इंजिन चालू ठेवते; पुढची गुरु महादशा (२०५०-२०६६) ८वा-इंजिन ठळक करेल. राहू-केतू अक्ष १-७ ही कर्म-रेषा — विवाह आणि स्व यांच्यातील पूर्व-जन्म कर्म. शनी ११व्या भावी स्व-राशीत — दीर्घकालीन यश, उशीरा परिपक्व बक्षीस, सशा-महापुरुष-योगाची आंशिक छाप. रिक्त सात राशींचा अर्थ त्यांच्या स्वामींच्या ठिकाणावरून निघतो.
