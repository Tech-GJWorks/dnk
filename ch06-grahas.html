Grahas in this Chart या कुंडलीतील ग्रह

A graha (ग्रह, literally "the seizer") is a planet — but more precisely, a living planetary intelligence that "seizes" a portion of consciousness when its dasha or transit fires. Vedic Jyotish classically counts nine grahas: the seven luminaries-and-classical-planets (Sun, Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn) plus the two lunar nodes (Rahu, Ketu). Modern Vedic-Western synthesis often adds the three outer planets (Uranus, Neptune, Pluto) for psychological and generational layering — this book names them but treats them as secondary. This chapter walks each graha's role in this specific chart — what it rules, where it sits, with whom, looked at by whom, in what nakshatra, with what dignity, and which dasha activates it. The deep individual unpacks are in chapters 7–14.

6.1 Sun · सूर्य — atma-karaka of self, father, authority सूर्य

Position: 08°53′ Scorpio Anuradha Pada 2, in 8H. Sign-dignity: Friendly (Sun is a friend of Mars who rules Scorpio). Lordship: Sun rules Leo, which is 5H here — so Sun is 5L, lord of the children/devotion/intellect/past-life-merit house. Nakshatra-amsha: Anuradha Pada 2 = Capricorn-amsha (Saturn). Yutis: Mercury (combust within 1°15′), Jupiter (within 18°34′ — too wide for tight yuti but same-sign), Pluto (within 2°10′ — tight). Aspects on Sun: Saturn's 7th aspect from 11H Aquarius falls on 5H Leo — not on Sun directly; check Saturn's 3rd-aspect from 11H = 1H Aries (Ketu) — not on Sun; check 10th aspect = 8H Scorpio — yes, Saturn aspects 8H, therefore Sun. So Sun receives Saturn's 10th-aspect — a classical "Saturn dampens Sun's vitality" signature that often gives the father-figure a serious, eldering, or distant quality. Aspects from Sun: 7th-aspect on 2H Taurus (food, family-wealth). Karakas: Sun is karaka of self-authority, father, vitality, eye, heart, government, ego. Dasha: Sun MD ran 19-Jan-2009 → 19-Jan-2015 (age 13–19); next Sun PD-firing in Mars MD is Mars-Sun AD 13-Feb-2031 → 21-Jun-2031.

The chart's Sun is structurally muted — combust by its own placement-as-Sun (the technical-language "Sun is combust" is a contradiction; rather, Sun is the burning agent making Mercury combust). In dusthana 8H, the Sun's natural king-light is dimmed; the native's self-authority operates underground rather than centre-stage. Combined with Saturn's 10th-aspect, the early-life pattern of "muted Sun" is unmistakable: shy authority, deferred recognition, father-figure carrying a serious/health-marked dimension. Sun's strength returns in later periods when the chart's 9H stellium fires; Mars MD (current) and Rahu MD activate the Sun's 5L position via the 5H-related Dharma-line. Chapter 9 unpacks fully.

6.2 Moon · चंद्र — karaka of mind, mother, emotion चंद्र

Position: 17°53′ Sagittarius Purvashadha Pada 2, in 9H. Sign-dignity: Neutral (Moon in Sagittarius is neither exalted nor debilitated; the Jupiter-rulership is benefic-friendly to Moon). Lordship: Moon rules Cancer, which is 4H here — so Moon is 4L, lord of home/mother/emotional-anchor. Nakshatra-amsha: Purvashadha Pada 2 = Capricorn-amsha. Janma-Nakshatra status: This is THE most important nakshatra in the chart — the Moon's own. Yutis: Mars (15°33′ separation — moderate yuti), Venus (14°19′ — moderate yuti), Neptune (11°45′ — moderate yuti). All in 9H Sagittarius. Chandra-Mangala yoga: Moon-Mars yuti is the Chandra-Mangala yoga — an emotion-finance-courage fusion. Aspects on Moon: Saturn's aspects from 11H — 7th aspect on 5H Leo (no), 3rd aspect on 1H Aries (no), 10th aspect on 8H Scorpio (no Moon there) — Moon receives no Saturn aspect. Jupiter is in 8H (5th aspect of Jupiter from 8H falls on 12H Pisces; 9th aspect falls on 4H Cancer; so Jupiter does not aspect 9H Moon directly — same-sign Jupiter is in 8H, one sign behind). Karakas: Moon is karaka of mother, mind, emotions, fluids, monthly cycles, public mass, intuition.

The Moon's placement determines the chart's emotional architecture. In 9H Sagittarius Purvashadha pada 2, Dharmesh's mind defaults to a dharma-philosophical emotional ground — emotional stability comes through faith, long-arc meaning-making, and structured (Capricorn-amsha) discipline. The Moon yuti with Mars (Chandra-Mangala) makes financial-courage-emotion a single fused tone — emotions cannot be separated from financial-status and from courage-deeds. The Moon's lordship of 4H means home, mother, emotional anchor are all routed through 9H — the native's emotional home is the dharma-line itself. Moon MD ran 2015–2025; the next Moon PD-firing inside Mars MD is Mars-Moon AD 21-Jun-2031 → 19-Jan-2032 (closing months of Mars MD). Chapter 10 unpacks.

6.3 Mars · मंगळ — lagnesh, karaka of courage and property मंगळ — लग्नेश

Position: 02°20′ Sagittarius Mula Pada 1, in 9H. Sign-dignity: Friendly (Mars-Jupiter friendship). Lordship: Mars rules Aries (1H, lagna) and Scorpio (8H) — so Mars is lagnesh AND 8L, the chart's most consequential planet because its lordship spans self-identity and transformation-house. Nakshatra-amsha: Mula Pada 1 = Sagittarius-amsha (Jupiter own — double Sagittarius). Yutis: Moon, Venus, Neptune in 9H. Aspects on Mars: none from major grahas (Saturn doesn't aspect; Jupiter in 8H — 9th aspect would fall on 4H Cancer; 5th aspect on 12H Pisces; no direct Jupiter aspect on Mars). Aspects from Mars: 4th from 9H = 12H Pisces (foreign/loss/moksha); 7th from 9H = 3H Gemini (younger-siblings/courage/communication); 8th from 9H = 4H Cancer (mother/home). Karakas: Mars is karaka of courage, younger-brother, property/land, surgery, accidents, blood, muscles, dispute. Dasha: Mars MD currently running 19-Jan-2025 → 19-Jan-2032 — this is the chart's PRIMARY active dasha.

Mars is the chart's most active planet right now. As lagnesh placed in own-nakshatra Mula in 9H, Mars carries the entire identity-and-dharma stream during Mars MD. The 4th-aspect on 12H supports foreign-travel and moksha-yoga firings; the 7th-aspect on 3H supports younger-sibling courage and writing/communication thrust; the 8th-aspect on 4H is the watch-line (8th-aspect of Mars on the mother-house = mother-health and home-disruption potential during Mars-major-AD windows). Chapter 11 unpacks the Mars-Venus 9H yuti specifically.

6.4 Mercury · बुध — karaka of intellect, speech, business बुध (अस्त)

Position: 10°08′ Scorpio Anuradha Pada 3, in 8H. Sign-dignity: Neutral-to-mild. CRITICAL: Combust within 1°15′ of Sun — Mercury's voice is fully suppressed by Sun's brightness. Lordship: Mercury rules Gemini (3H) and Virgo (6H) — so Mercury is 3L AND 6L. The dual-lordship of an upachaya (3H) and a dusthana (6H) by the same planet is structurally interesting: 6L in 8H gives Vipareeta-Harsha-yoga (windfall through opponents losing), but the combustion qualifies it. Nakshatra-amsha: Anuradha Pada 3 = Aquarius-amsha (Saturn). Yutis: Sun (combust-yuti, 1°15′), Jupiter (17° separation), Pluto (3°25′). Aspects: Same Saturn 10th-aspect on 8H falls on Mercury. Karakas: Mercury karaka of intellect, speech, education, business, hands, nerves, younger-siblings (with Mars).

Combust Mercury is the single most consequential affliction in this chart. The mind operates underground; speech is muted; business-instincts are second-guessed. The Vipareeta-rescue is partial — Mercury's combust state cannot fully energize the 6L-in-8H windfall potential. But the rescue is not zero: in dashas where Mercury fires (Mercury MD opens 2085; Mars-Mercury AD inside Mars MD is Jul-2028 → Jul-2029), some 6H-dusthana significations release benefit. Chapter 12 unpacks fully, including Rahu's 7H placement and Mercury-Rahu cross-influence.

6.5 Jupiter · गुरु — karaka of wisdom, children, dharma गुरु

Position: 27°26′ Scorpio Jyeshtha Pada 4, in 8H. Sign-dignity: Friendly (Jupiter-Mars friendship; Scorpio is a sign Jupiter handles well, despite being a Mars-sign). Lordship: Jupiter rules Sagittarius (9H) and Pisces (12H) — so Jupiter is 9L AND 12L. Nakshatra-amsha: Jyeshtha Pada 4 = Pisces-amsha (Jupiter own — Karakamsa lagna Pisces). Yutis: Sun, Mercury(c), Pluto all in 8H Scorpio. Aspects from Jupiter: 5th from 8H = 12H Pisces (own sign — strengthens 12L's house); 7th from 8H = 2H Taurus (Venus-ruled — wealth/family-line); 9th from 8H = 4H Cancer (Moon-ruled — mother/home). Karakas: Jupiter karaka of wisdom, husband (in female chart), children (in male chart), expansion, dharma, teaching, banking. Atma-Karaka: Yes — Jupiter at 27°26′ is the highest-degree among the seven classical planets (Saturn 24°11′ is next), making Jupiter the Atma-Karaka under Jaimini.

Jupiter in 8H Scorpio is a complex placement. As 9L (dharma-lord) sitting in 8H (transformation), the dharma-line is pulled into research-and-regeneration; the native's faith ripens through crisis-experiences rather than through smooth instruction. As 12L (moksha-lord) in 8H, the moksha-path is investigative — Dharmesh's spiritual breakthrough will come through experiential transformation (8H), not through prescribed devotion. The Pisces-amsha at pada-4 gives Jupiter own-amsha rescue: Karakamsa lagna in Pisces flags this as a soul-signature of devotional sensitivity that ripens only after age 50 (Jupiter MD opens 2050). Chapter 13 unpacks.

6.6 Venus · शुक्र — karaka of wife, comfort, art; double-marak शुक्र — द्वि-मारक

Position: 03°34′ Sagittarius Mula Pada 2, in 9H. Sign-dignity: Neutral. Lordship: Venus rules Taurus (2H) and Libra (7H) — so Venus is 2L AND 7L. CRITICAL: 2L + 7L = double-marak (maraka-karaka). Venus periods are health-watch periods, no matter how otherwise auspicious. Nakshatra-amsha: Mula Pada 2 = Capricorn-amsha (Saturn — discipline overlay on Venus's natural pleasure). Yutis: Moon, Mars, Neptune in 9H. Aspects: No major-graha aspects on Venus. Aspects from Venus: 7th-aspect on 3H Gemini. Karakas: Venus is karaka of wife (in male chart), comfort, art, beauty, vehicles, jewellery, sweet-taste, romance. Dasha: Venus MD was 0 → 13 years (1995–2009); next Venus PD-firing inside Mars MD is Mars-Venus AD 13-Dec-2029 → 13-Feb-2031 (~14 months) — this is the critical marriage window combined with marak watch.

Venus carries double-load: positively, it is the marriage karaka in 9H dharma-house with Capricorn-amsha discipline (suggesting a structured, foreign-or-dharma-connected partner met in late-20s/30s); negatively, as double-marak, Venus periods are longevity-watch periods. The Mars-Venus AD (Dec 2029 – Feb 2031) is therefore the chart's most-pivotal 14-month window — strongest single marriage probability + parallel marak watch. Chapter 11 and chapter 28 elaborate.

6.7 Saturn · शनि — karaka of service, longevity, career-discipline शनी — स्व-राशी

Position: 24°11′ Aquarius Purvabhadra Pada 2, in 11H. Sign-dignity: Own sign — the strongest placement Saturn can have here (the only stronger placement would be exaltation in Libra). Lordship: Saturn rules Capricorn (10H) and Aquarius (11H) — so Saturn is 10L AND 11L, lord of the two upachaya-significator houses (career and gains). Self-placed (Saturn-in-Aquarius-which-is-Aquarius). Nakshatra-amsha: Purvabhadra Pada 2 = Pisces-amsha (Jupiter). Saturn in Jupiter-amsha softens Saturn's pure austerity with dharma-flavour. Yutis: None — Saturn is solitary in 11H. Aspects on Saturn: No major-graha aspects (Mars's 8th-aspect from 9H = 4H Cancer, not on Saturn). Aspects from Saturn: 3rd from 11H = 1H Aries (Ketu) — Saturn's 3rd-aspect on lagna; 7th from 11H = 5H Leo (empty); 10th from 11H = 8H Scorpio (4-planet stellium). Karakas: Saturn karaka of longevity, service, work-discipline, slow-progress, elders, lower-class people, iron, sorrow-then-mastery. Atma-Karaka in some readings: Saturn at 24°11′ is the second-highest; primary AK is Jupiter, but Saturn-as-AK reading is also valid in some Jaimini schools.

Saturn in own-sign 11H is the single most-valuable structural anchor in this chart. The Sasa-Mahapurusha-partial yoga, the 10L→11H wealth-conversion, the Saturn-3rd-aspect on Ketu-lagna (giving structural-discipline to identity), and the Saturn-10th-aspect on the 8H stellium (giving the research-engine its slow-build temperament) — all four effects come from this one Saturn placement. The Chitra-pada-3 Aquarius-amsha gives Rahu a craftsmanship-and-structure overlay — Dharmesh's partnership-line attracts unconventional, foreign-tinged, technologically-savvy, perhaps craftsmanship-oriented partners; but the Saturn-amsha imposes structure, meaning the relationship that endures will be the one built with structural maturity rather than impulsive romantic surge. The 9th-aspect of Ketu on the 9H stellium reinforces the dharma-line's research-and-detachment flavour. The Ketu-Rahu axis 1H-7H is the chart's karmic spine, decoded fully in chapter 14.

6.10 The outer planets — Uranus, Neptune, Pluto बाह्य ग्रह — युरेनस, नेपच्यून, प्लूटो

Modern Vedic-Western synthesis treats these three as generational layers rather than personal karakas. They move slowly (Uranus 7 years/sign, Neptune 14 years/sign, Pluto 12–32 years/sign) and place an entire birth-cohort under their stamp. Their personal influence on the chart depends on (a) which house they occupy, (b) whether they aspect or are aspected by a personal planet, (c) their nakshatra placement.

  • Uranus 03°51′ Capricorn Uttarashadha P3 in 10H — career-revolution potential; tech/innovation signature; alone in 10H, gives Dharmesh a generational pull toward unconventional career structures. No major-graha yuti or close aspect.
  • Neptune 29°38′ Sagittarius Uttarashadha P1 in 9H — at the very tail of Sagittarius, just before crossing into Capricorn. In 9H dharma-house, Neptune adds psychic/dreamy/devotional/idealistic dimensions to faith and partnership. Wide yuti with Moon-Mars-Venus group.
  • Pluto 06°43′ Scorpio Anuradha P2 in 8H — Pluto in own-sign equivalent (Pluto modern-rules Scorpio). In 8H with Sun-Mercury, gives the chart's depth-psychology engine its full Pluto-archetype: deep regeneration, hidden power, transformation-through-crisis.

The outer planets are referenced when relevant in chapters 9, 11, 13 — but treated as flavouring rather than primary indicators. A classical-only reading (Parashari-strict) would ignore them entirely; a modern psychological reading would centre them. This book takes the middle path: name them, weight them lightly.

Learning Note — Sequence to read a new planet / नवीन ग्रह वाचण्याचा क्रम

Rule: Every planet must be examined in the same fixed sequence: (1) sign + dignity, (2) house, (3) lordship of which houses, (4) nakshatra + pada-amsha, (5) yutis, (6) aspects in, (7) aspects out, (8) karaka-significations, (9) which dasha activates it next. Skipping any step produces a one-axis reading.

Applied to this chart (worked example on Saturn): (1) Aquarius — own sign, strongest dignity. (2) 11H — upachaya, gains-house. (3) Rules 10H and 11H — both upachayas; the only planet ruling two upachayas. (4) Purvabhadra P2 — Pisces-amsha (Jupiter — softening). (5) None — solitary. (6) None — unaspected by major grahas. (7) 3rd on 1H Aries (Ketu), 7th on 5H Leo (empty), 10th on 8H Scorpio (stellium). (8) Karaka of longevity, service, work, elders, slow-but-permanent gains. (9) Saturn MD opens 2066; before then, fires during Mars-Saturn AD Jun-2027 → Jul-2028, Rahu-Saturn AD 2037-2040. The 9-step sequence takes ~2 minutes aloud and produces a vastly richer reading than "Saturn in own sign is good."

Hidden risk: Skipping step 4 (nakshatra-amsha) is the single most common junior mistake. Without it, the reader misses that Saturn's Pisces-amsha softens the natural Saturnian austerity — a structurally important nuance.

Alt-readings — outer planets and Atma-Karaka

Classical Parashari (strict): Ignores Uranus, Neptune, Pluto entirely. Reads the chart as 9-graha. Atma-Karaka under Jaimini-Parashari hybrid = Jupiter (highest degree among 7 classical). Karakamsa lagna = Pisces.

Modern Vedic-Western synthesis: Includes outer planets. The Atma-Karaka may shift to Neptune at 29°38′ (highest of all 10 bodies excluding nodes), reframing the soul-signature toward dreamy/idealistic/dissolution-of-ego rather than wisdom/expansion. Both readings have merit; this book stays with Jupiter-AK for primary reading.

K. N. Rao tradition (modern Indian classical-strict revival): Reads only Vimshottari dasha + Parashari technique. Would not weight Pluto-in-8H even though it adds psychological precision; would lean heavily on Jupiter's Vipareeta-related signatures.
