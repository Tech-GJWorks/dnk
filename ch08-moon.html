Moon in Depth — Mind, Mother, Memory; All Classical Possibilities & This Native's Frame चंद्र — मन, माता, स्मृती; संपूर्ण शास्त्रीय शक्यता आणि या जातकाची चौकट

In Vedic astrology the Moon (चंद्र) is the second pillar of the chart after the lagna. The lagna shows the body and the entry-point of identity; the Moon shows the inner identity — mind, emotion, memory, mother, mood, the felt sense of being-here. Janma-rashi (the Moon's sign) is so important that traditional Indian society asks first "राशी काय?" before "lagna काय?". The dasha system (Vimshottari) is anchored on the Moon's nakshatra. Predictive transits (Sade-Sati, Chandrashtama, Janma-tara) all key off the Moon. This chapter unpacks Dharmesh's Moon: Sagittarius (धनू) rashi, Purvashadha (पूर्वाषाढा) Pada 2, 9th house, conjunct lagnesh Mars and 2L/7L Venus in own-nakshatra Mula territory — and tests every classical Chandra-yoga that can fire from this configuration. Five-block scaffold per section: condition · all classical possibilities · astrologer-to-client narrative · hard verdict · full Marathi mirror.

8.2 Moon Nakshatra — Purvashadha Pada 2 चंद्र-नक्षत्र — पूर्वाषाढा पाद २

Condition / state. The Moon sits in Purvashadha (पूर्वाषाढा) nakshatra, Pada 2. Purvashadha spans 13°20′ to 26°40′ of Sagittarius; Pada 2 covers 16°40′ to 20°00′ (Capricorn-amsha, Saturn-amsha). The nakshatra's lord is Venus (शुक्र); its deity is Apas (आपस्), the waters; its shakti is "invigorating strength" (बल-आधान शक्ति); its symbol is the elephant-tusk or the hand-fan. The native's Janma-Nakshatra (the Moon's nakshatra at birth) is the single most consequential nakshatra in the chart — Vimshottari dasha starts here, sankalpa-mantras key off it, and personality-resonance comes from it.

All classical possibilities (Layer 1).

  • Shakti "balaadhana": the power to infuse strength into others — the leader who energises a team, the teacher who lifts the student, the friend who restarts a depressed companion. This is the active phalashruti of Purvashadha.

तुझ्यावर नाही. २०१४-२०२२ साडे-साती फक्त गोचर-घटना, आता खरंच मागे. मन नैसर्गिक जड नाही. चार: लग्नावरून केतूची ९-वी दृष्टी चं-मं-शु तारकाहारावर — म्हणून वैराग्य, संशोधन, गूढ-छटा कायम मन, धैर्य, भागीदारी-जीवनात मिसळते. ऐहिक कारकीर्द चालवतानाही एक पाय शोधक-गोटात; 8.5 Chandra-Yogas — Classical Check & Live Yogas in This Chart चंद्र-योग — शास्त्रीय तपासणी व या कुंडलीतील जिवंत योग

Condition / state. A yoga in Vedic astrology is a specific planetary configuration (placement + dignity + aspect + house) that produces a named, predictable result. Chandra-yogas are the subset of yogas built around the Moon. Each classical text (BPHS, Phaladeepika, Saravali, Jaimini) lists Chandra-yogas with precise conditions. The competent astrologer enumerates each, tests against the chart, and reports which fire and which do not.

All classical possibilities (Layer 1) — major Chandra-yogas and their conditions:

  • Gaja-Kesari-Yoga: Moon and Jupiter in mutual kendras (1-4-7-10 from each other). Result: wisdom, fame, generosity, protection, eloquence. Strongest when both planets are bright and in good houses.
  • Chandra-Mangala-Yoga: Moon and Mars in same sign (yuti) or in mutual aspect. Result: wealth through courageous action, business acumen, courage-emotion fusion. Strongest when in 1H, 5H, 9H, 10H, 11H.
  • Adhi-Yoga (Lunar-Adhi): Benefics (Jupiter, Venus, Mercury) in the 6th, 7th, 8th from the Moon. Result: leadership, power, longevity. Three benefics required; partial credit for two.
  • Sunapha-Yoga: a planet (other than Sun) in the 2nd from Moon. Result: self-earned wealth, intelligence, possessions.
  • Anapha-Yoga: a planet (other than Sun) in the 12th from Moon. Result: graceful disposition, refined manners, comforts.
  • Durdhura-Yoga: planets in BOTH the 2nd AND 12th from Moon. Result: wealth, vehicles, abundant resources.
  • Kemadruma-Yoga (negative): NO planet in 2nd, 12th, or 1st-with-Moon (other than Sun/nodes in some schools); cancelled if Moon is in kendra from lagna OR has any benefic-aspect OR Jupiter in kendra OR Moon is exalted/own-sign. Result if active: loneliness, poverty, mental unrest. Cancellations are common.
  • Vish-Yoga: Moon-Saturn yuti. Result: melancholy, slow-mature mind, delayed rewards.
  • Punarphoo-Yoga: Moon-Saturn yuti or hard aspect — gives marriage-and-decision delays.
  • Amavasya-Yoga: Sun-Moon yuti (new-moon birth). Result: emotional-identity fusion, sometimes ego-mood instability.
  • Purna-Chandra (Full-Moon) advantage: Moon at maximum brightness (~14-tithi to amavasya-distance-180°). Result: emotional radiance, popularity.
  • Bhagya-Yoga (Lunar-Bhagya): Moon in 9H or 9L-Moon strong, with benefic support. Result: dharma-mind, fortune-flow, foreign-affinity.
  • Chandra-Adhi-Yoga (specific to 9H): Moon in 9H conjunct/aspected by benefics — produces gurukul-yoga, teacher-or-student-of-renowned-teacher.
