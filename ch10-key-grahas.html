Key Grahas कुंडलीतील प्रमुख ग्रह

A chart of nine grahas does not weigh them equally. Some sit in own-sign or own-nakshatra and dominate; others sit muted in dusthana or combust. A working astrologer's first analytical pass identifies the chart's top firing planets — the small handful whose dignities, dashas, and aspects steer every later reading. For Dharmesh, four grahas anchor everything that follows: Mars (lagnesh + current MD), Saturn (own-sign Sasa-partial), Jupiter (Atma-Karaka), and the Rahu-Ketu axis across 1H-7H. Mercury and Venus are touched briefly as the dignity-check tail.

10.1 Mars — lagnesh in own-nakshatra मंगळ — लग्नेश, मूळ-नक्षत्र, सध्या MD

Condition. Mars at 02°20′ Sagittarius Mula Pada 1, in 9H. 1L + 8L (rules Aries 1H and Scorpio 8H). Friendly sign (Mars-Jupiter), own-nakshatra (Mula's Pada 1 is Sagittarius-amsha — Mars's own-pada). Mars MD currently running 19-Jan-2025 → 19-Jan-2032.

Possibilities. A lagnesh in own-nakshatra in 9H produces a Bhagya-yoga — fortune-via-identity. The 1L-8L double-rulership for Aries lagna is a classical Yogakaraka-adjacent feature: 1L is always benefic to itself; 8L is dusthana-rulership normally feared, but when the same planet rules 1H, the 8L's destructive edge is rerouted toward research-and-regeneration of the self. Currently in Mars MD: this is the chart's seven-year identity-rewrite window. Mula nakshatra's deity Nirriti gives Mars the licence to dismantle dead structures so dharma can rebuild.

This chart specific. Mars sits with Moon (Chandra-Mangala yoga), Venus (Mars-Venus 9H yuti — chapter 11), and Neptune. Outgoing aspects: 4th on 12H (foreign/moksha), 7th on 3H (effort/courage), 8th on 4H (home-watch). The Mars-MD timeline already in motion: Ma-Ma past, Ma-Ra current (May 2026), Ma-Ju next (Jul 2026 – Jun 2027 — strongest marriage window), Ma-Sa (2027-28), Ma-Ve (Dec 2029 – Feb 2031 — critical marak window).

Conclusion. Mars is the chart's primary firing planet — lagnesh, own-nakshatra, currently driving the dasha. Every life-event 2025-2032 is filtered through Mars.

10.2 Saturn — own-sign Aquarius 11H शनी — कुंभ स्व-राशी ११व्या भावी

Condition. Saturn at 24°11′ Aquarius Purvabhadra Pada 2, in 11H. 10L + 11L (rules Capricorn 10H and Aquarius 11H — both upachayas). Own sign — strongest dignity Saturn can hold here (only exaltation in Libra would be stronger). Pisces-amsha (Jupiter-flavoured — softens austerity). Solitary in 11H — no yutis. Sade Sati Phase 3 active (current Saturn transit through Pisces post-Aquarius).

Possibilities. Saturn own-sign in 11H upachaya is one of the strongest structural anchors any chart can carry. Sasa-Mahapurusha-yoga is partial here (Sasa technically requires kendra — 1/4/7/10 — but own-sign in upachaya 11H is read as Sasa-adjacent by most modern lineages). Saturn as 10L in 11H is a classical Dhana-yoga: career-lord in gains-house. As 11L in own 11H, gains are self-built and durable. Saturn's slow-but-permanent gift is wealth-and-respect that crystallises in later life.

This chart specific. Saturn's 3rd-aspect lands on 1H Aries (Ketu) — structural-discipline imported into identity. Saturn's 7th-aspect on 5H Leo (empty). Saturn's 10th-aspect on 8H Scorpio — touches Sun, Mercury, Pluto, and arguably Jupiter. This 10th-aspect on the 8H stellium is the slow-build temperament behind the chart's research-engine. Saturn MD opens 19-Jan-2066 (age 70) — the chart's wealth-and-respect peak is structurally aligned with late life, not mid-life.

Conclusion. Saturn is the chart's second firing planet — structural anchor, late-life crystalliser, and slow-build engine behind the 8H research and the 1H identity.

10.3 Jupiter — Atma-Karaka in 8H Scorpio गुरु — आत्म-कारक, ८व्या भावी

Condition. Jupiter at 27°26′ Scorpio Jyeshtha Pada 4, in 8H. 9L + 12L (rules Sagittarius 9H and Pisces 12H). Friendly sign (Jupiter-Mars). Jyeshtha Pada 4 amsha: Pisces (Jupiter's own sign — own-amsha rescue). Atma-Karaka — highest-degree planet among the seven classical grahas (27°26′ tops Saturn's 24°11′). Yutis: Sun, Mercury(combust), Pluto. Karakamsa lagna: Pisces (Jupiter's D9 placement).

Possibilities. Atma-Karaka shows the soul's primary lesson under Jaimini. AK Jupiter in 8H makes the soul-task research, regeneration, hidden-knowledge, dharma-through-transformation. 9L in 8H is dharma-line through crisis; 12L in 8H is moksha-path through investigation. Pisces-amsha at pada 4 gives Jupiter own-amsha rescue — the planet's own sign is its inner-amsha pocket, providing structural protection even in dusthana. Jupiter's outgoing aspects: 5th on 12H Pisces (own sign — strengthens 12L's house), 7th on 2H Taurus (family-wealth), 9th on 4H Cancer (mother/home — graced).

This chart specific. Inside Mars MD, the Mars-Jupiter AD (06-Jul-2026 → 12-Jun-2027) is the next AD-firing — a Jupiter-graced 11-month window inside the Mars-identity rewrite. This is the chart's strongest marriage-window per classical timing (AK Jupiter activated, 9L-dharma blessed by lagnesh). Jupiter MD itself opens 19-Jan-2050 (age 54) → 19-Jan-2066 — the chart's spiritual peak.

Conclusion. Jupiter is the chart's third firing planet — Atma-Karaka, dharma-and-moksha lord, 9L-grace-via-crisis, and the planet whose AD opens the strongest mid-Mars-MD window.

10.4 Rahu-Ketu axis: 1H-7H राहू-केतू अक्ष: १-७

Condition. Rahu at 00°34′ Libra Chitra Pada 3 in 7H · Aquarius-amsha (Saturn-structured). Ketu at 00°34′ Aries Ashvini Pada 1 in 1H · Aries-amsha (double-Aries, Mars-flavoured). Mutually-aspecting nodal opposition.

Possibilities. The Rahu-Ketu axis is the chart's karmic spine. 1H-7H placement makes the self-versus-partner axis the karmic theme of this incarnation — every major partnership decision carries past-life weight. Ketu-on-lagna gives identity-detachment current; Rahu in 7H Libra gives partnership-as-aspiration. The combination is read by classical texts (Phaladeepika, Saravali) as: native attracts unconventional partners, faces karmic-test relationships, and resolves past-life relational karma through structured (Aquarius-amsha) and detached (Aries-amsha) navigation.

This chart specific. Rahu in 7H Libra Chitra Pada 3 (Aquarius-amsha) is structured-Rahu in marriage-house — partners attracted will tend toward foreign, technological, craftsmanship, unconventional, but the structural-maturity overlay means the marriage that endures is the one built with patience rather than impulsive surge. Ketu in 1H casts 9th-aspect onto the 9H Sagittarius stellium — flooding the dharma-house with detachment-current. Rahu MD opens 19-Jan-2032 → 19-Jan-2050 — the chart's foreign/status-leap decade.

Conclusion. The nodal axis is the chart's karmic spine. Ketu-1H questions identity; Rahu-7H structures partnership. Rahu MD from 2032 is the status-leap window.

10.5 Mercury and Venus — dignity quick-check बुध व शुक्र — गौण-स्थिती

Condition. Mercury at 10°08′ Scorpio Anuradha Pada 3, in 8H — combust (1°15′ from Sun). 3L + 6L (rules Gemini 3H, Virgo 6H). Aquarius-amsha (Saturn). Venus at 03°34′ Sagittarius Mula Pada 2, in 9H. 2L + 7L — double-marak. Neutral dignity. Capricorn-amsha (Saturn).

Possibilities. Mercury's combustion is the single most consequential affliction in this chart — mind underground, speech muted, business-instincts second-guessed. 6L-in-8H gives a partial Vipareeta-Harsha rescue (windfall through opponents' loss) but combustion qualifies the yield. Venus as double-marak means Venus periods are always longevity-watch, regardless of how romantic or auspicious the AD seems. Venus in 9H with Mars yuti is the Kalathra-yoga question (chapter 11) — partner-line stressed but Jupiter-aspect-from-8H rescue.

This chart specific. Mercury fires next during Ma-Me AD (21-Jul-2028 → 18-Jul-2029) — twelve months of submerged intellect surfacing through Mars-action. Venus fires next during Ma-Ve AD (14-Dec-2029 → 13-Feb-2031) — fourteen months of strongest marriage probability paired with marak watch.

Conclusion. Mercury is muted but rescued partially; Venus is double-marak but Kalathra-rescued. Both fire in the back half of Mars MD.

10.6 Top-three firing planets — synthesis शीर्ष-तीन सक्रिय ग्रह

Condition. Of the nine grahas, three carry the chart's primary weight by combined dignity, lordship, dasha, and aspect-reach: Mars (lagnesh + 8L, own-nakshatra, current MD), Saturn (10L+11L, own-sign 11H, Sasa-partial), Jupiter (9L+12L, AK, Pisces-amsha rescue, Karakamsa lagna). The Rahu-Ketu axis is the karmic spine but operates as a frame, not a firing-engine until 2032.

This chart specific. The three firing planets are sequenced exactly in dasha-time: Mars MD 2025-2032 → Rahu MD 2032-2050 → Jupiter MD 2050-2066 → Saturn MD 2066-2085. Three of the four next-MDs sit in or strongly aspect the 8H-9H spine; Saturn's MD anchors the 11H wealth-line. The structural reading: this is a dharma-research-transformation life-arc in which the soul (Jupiter AK) crystallises through research (8H stellium), the identity (Mars lagnesh) rewrites first, the karmic axis (Rahu MD) opens foreign and status thereafter, and the late-life consolidation (Saturn) follows.

Conclusion. Mars now → Rahu next → Jupiter spiritual peak → Saturn late-life crystallisation. The chart's firing sequence is structurally coherent: identity rewrite, karmic leap, dharma ripening, wealth-respect consolidation. The native is engineered for the long game.

Learning Note — Identifying the top-three firing planets / सक्रिय ग्रह कसे ओळखावे

Rule: A chart's top firing planets are identified by stacking five criteria: (1) lordship of kendra/trikona houses (1/4/5/7/9/10), (2) own-sign or exaltation dignity, (3) own-nakshatra placement, (4) currently-active or next-major dasha, (5) major aspect-reach to populated houses. A planet that scores high on three or more is a firing planet.

Applied here: Mars scores on (1) lagnesh, (3) own-nakshatra Mula, (4) current MD, (5) aspects 12H/3H/4H = five. Saturn scores on (1) 10L, (2) own-sign, (5) 3rd on 1H + 10th on 8H = four. Jupiter scores on (1) 9L, (3) own-amsha Pisces, (5) aspects 12H/2H/4H + AK status = four. These three are the firing engines; no other graha clears three. Mercury fails (combust), Venus fails (double-marak liability outweighs), Sun fails (8H dusthana), Moon fails (no kendra-lordship past-MD).

Hidden risk: A reader who treats all nine grahas equally produces a flat reading where Mars and Saturn's signals are diluted by giving equal weight to the muted Sun or combust Mercury. The top-three filter is the working astrologer's most important early-analysis tool.
