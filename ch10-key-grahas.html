Mars is lagnesh (1L of Aries) AND 8L (Scorpio is 8H from Aries — Mars rules both Aries and Scorpio for Aries-lagna, so 1L=8L Mars). Four powerful roles overlap in this single planet — making Mars the chart's operating-system planet for this seven-year window.
