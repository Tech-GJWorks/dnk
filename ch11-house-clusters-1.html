House Clusters 1: Houses 1-4 भाव-समूह १: १-४ भाव

The first four bhavas (भाव) form the personal-foundation quadrant of any chart: who I am (1H — तनू-भाव), what I own (2H — धन-भाव), what I push for (3H — पराक्रम-भाव), what holds me emotionally (4H — सुख-भाव). For an astrologer in training this cluster is the first laboratory — you learn here how to read an empty house (no occupants), a house signed by a shadow-graha only (Ketu alone), a house with the lord-displaced-far-away, and a house compressed by two opposing drishti-streams. Dharmesh's chart presents all four of these reading-challenges in one quadrant. Only Ketu sits in this quadrant; the four lords (Mars, Venus, Mercury, Moon) are scattered across 8H, 9H, and the Mula-Purvashadha stellium. The story is one of lord-strength compensating for occupant-emptiness — a classical pattern the astrologer must teach the client without slipping into either pessimism ("your foundation is empty") or fake reassurance ("everything is fine"). This chapter walks each of the four houses in the full 5-block teaching format: condition, all classical possibilities, astrologer-to-client narrative, hard verdict, and the Marathi mirror.

कोणत्याही कुंडलीचे पहिले चार भाव (तनू-धन-पराक्रम-सुख) हे व्यक्तिगत-पाया-चतुर्थांश असतात: मी कोण आहे (१), माझ्याजवळ काय आहे (२), मी कशासाठी झगडतो (३), मला भावनिक आधार कोण देते (४). ज्योतिषी-प्रशिक्षणार्थ्यासाठी हा भाव-समूह म्हणजे पहिली प्रयोगशाळा — इथे रिकामा भाव कसा वाचावा (ग्रह नसताना), केवळ छाया-ग्रहाने चिन्हित केलेला भाव कसा वाचावा (एकट्या केतूचा भाव), दूर-गेलेला स्वामी असलेला भाव कसा वाचावा, आणि विरोधी दृष्टी-धारांनी चेपलेला भाव कसा वाचावा हे शिकता येते. धर्मेशच्या कुंडलीत हे चारही वाचन-आव्हाने एकाच चतुर्थांशात येतात. ह्या चतुर्थांशात फक्त केतू बसलेला आहे; मंगळ-शुक्र-बुध-चंद्र हे चार स्वामी ८व्या-९व्या भावात आणि मूळ-पूर्वाषाढा-स्तेलियम मध्ये विखुरलेले आहेत. कथा एकच — स्वामीचे बल उपस्थित-रिक्ततेची भरपाई करते — हा शास्त्रीय नमुना ज्योतिषाने ग्राहकाला निराशेच्या ("तुमचा पाया रिकामा आहे") अथवा खोट्या-धैर्याच्या ("सर्व ठीक आहे") अतिशयोक्तीशिवाय शिकवायचा असतो. ह्या अध्यायात चारही भाव संपूर्ण ५-खंडी-शिक्षण-स्वरूपात — स्थिती, सर्व शास्त्रीय शक्यता, ज्योतिषी-ते-ग्राहक-संवाद, ठाम निष्कर्ष, आणि मराठी-प्रतिबिंब — असे चालले आहेत.

11.1 First House — Identity, Body, Self प्रथम भाव — लग्न, देह, स्व

Condition / state. Aries lagna at 08°28′ Ashvini Pada 3. Lagnesh (लग्नेश) Mars sits in 9H Sagittarius in its own nakshatra Mula Pada 1 at 02°20′ — dispositor-of-own-nakshatra Ketu in 1H, sign-dispositor Jupiter in 8H Scorpio (Mars-Jupiter parivartana, see ch14). Ketu (केतू) occupies the 1H itself at 00°34′ Aries Ashvini Pada 1. Rahu in 7H Libra Chitra Pada 3 aspects 1H by full 7th drishti. Saturn from 11H Aquarius own-sign casts its 3rd drishti onto 1H. No benefic occupant; one shadow-graha resident; two malefic aspects incoming.

All classical possibilities (Layer 1). The first house carries the body (देह), the head and face, complexion, sense-of-self, default temperament, the early-life self-image, and the soul-vehicle for this incarnation. Aries (मेष) as lagna is the cardinal-fire-movable sign of Mars — quick, headlong, courage-led, surgical in decision-making, impatient with slow-process, naturally a starter rather than a finisher. Ashvini-Pada-3 lagna places the cusp under the Ashvinikumara physician-twins (अश्विनीकुमार) with Gemini-amsha sub-lord — adding a healer-investigator-mercurial sub-layer to the Mars-fire base. Classical lagnesh-in-9H is one of the most-praised dispositor placements (Phaladeepika, Brihat-Jataka): identity welded to dharma, father-line, higher-learning, foreign-or-philosophical pull, long-distance travel, fortune. When the lagnesh sits in its own nakshatra, Parashara grants maximum sub-lord-dignity — the planet operates at full karaka-strength regardless of sign-friendship status. Ketu on lagna in classical readings produces (a) an existential detachment-current that questions "is this really me?" even while acting decisively, (b) head-related vulnerability (Ashvini governs the head; Ketu adds wound-prone signature near forehead), (c) investigative-spiritual pull from childhood, (d) past-life rishi/sadhaka temperament. Saturn's 3rd-drishti from own-sign imposes structural discipline and slow-ripening on identity (Phaladeepika: Saturn-aspect-on-lagna = late-blooming maturity, durable body, karmic-accountability stamp). Rahu's 7th-drishti from partnership-house pulls identity outward toward unconventional alliance and craving-for-recognition through-the-other. Mars-Jupiter parivartana exists at the dispositor level (Mars in Jupiter's sign Sagittarius, Jupiter in Mars's sign Scorpio) — this binds 1L+8L to 9L+12L at the deepest structural layer.

Astrologer-to-client narrative (Layer 2). "Dharmesh, your rising sign is Aries — the warrior's sign, ruled by Mars. Mars itself sits far from the rising-point, all the way over in your 9th house of dharma and meaning. This is not a weakness; it is the chart's first big signal. Your identity is not built on personal-circumstance — it is built on what you stand for. The warrior in you doesn't fight for ego; he fights for meaning. Your Mars sits in its own birth-star, Mula, which is the strongest position Mars can take in your chart short of full exaltation. So whenever you act on purpose, you act with full power; whenever you act without purpose, you feel scattered. The placement that needs honest discussion is Ketu sitting on your rising sign. Ketu is the south-lunar-node, the planet of detachment, doubt, past-life completion, and investigative spirituality. With Ketu on your lagna, a part of you will always question your own identity — even when you are succeeding, a corner of your mind will ask 'is this really me?' This is not depression; this is the chart's spiritual signature. In Ashvini Pada 1 — the nakshatra of the celestial physicians — your Ketu carries a healer-investigator quality rather than a nihilistic one. You investigate; you don't withdraw. Saturn from your 11th house looks back at this Ketu with its 3rd-aspect, and that is your stabilizer — it keeps the doubt structured rather than spiraling. Rahu from your 7th house pulls you toward unconventional partnerships and craving-for-status-through-others, which you will feel most during Rahu's main period starting 2032. Physically: lean, dry, occasional head-tension under stress, susceptible to small wounds near the forehead — classical Ashvini-Ketu body. Your identity-reset is happening now, in Mars major period, running till January 2032."

Hard verdict / forecast. 1H is structurally signed by Ketu with full Mars-9H-Mula support behind it. Identity = investigative-dharmic-warrior. Mars MD (Jan 2025 → Jan 2032) is the lagnesh's own period — identity-reset and self-assertion are the dominant theme of this seven-year window. Strongest sub-window: Mars-Jupiter AD (Jul 2026 – Jun 2027) when parivartana fires; backup self-firing: Mars-Sun AD (Jul 2030 – Nov 2030) and Mars-Moon PD (Jun 2031 – Jan 2032).

स्थिती. मेष लग्न ८°२८′ अश्विनी पाद ३. लग्नेश मंगळ ९व्या भावात धनू मूळ पाद १ — स्व-नक्षत्र, नक्षत्र-स्वामी केतू १व्यात, राशी-स्वामी गुरू ८व्यात (मंगळ-गुरू परिवर्तन). केतू १व्यात ०°३४′ मेष अश्विनी पाद १. ७व्यातील राहूची ७वी पूर्ण दृष्टी १व्यावर. ११व्यातील स्व-राशी शनीची ३री दृष्टी १व्यावर. कोणीही शुभ-ग्रह उपस्थित नाही; एक छाया-ग्रह; दोन क्रूर दृष्टी. शास्त्रीय शक्यता. पहिला भाव — देह, डोके-चेहरा, रंग-रूप, स्व-भान, सहज-स्वभाव, बालपणीची स्व-प्रतिमा, या जन्माचे आत्मा-वाहन. मेष = चर-अग्नि-राशी, मंगळाची — झटपट, धैर्यप्रवण, शस्त्रक्रिया-निर्णय, स्थिर-प्रक्रियेला अधीर, सुरुवात-करणारा (शेवटा-नेणारा नव्हे). अश्विनी पाद ३ = अश्विनीकुमार वैद्य-जुळे, मिथुन-अंश उप-स्वामी — मंगळ-अग्नि-पायावर वैद्य-शोधक-बुधीय थर. लग्नेश ९व्यात (फलदीपिका, बृहत् जातक) = सर्वोच्च-स्तुत्य स्थानांपैकी एक: ओळख ↔ धर्म, पिता, उच्च-शिक्षण, परदेशी-तत्त्व-ओढ, दीर्घ-यात्रा, भाग्य. लग्नेश स्व-नक्षत्रात = पराशर-नुसार उप-स्वामी-दिग्बल सर्वोच्च — राशी-मित्रता असो वा नसो, ग्रह कारक-शक्तीने पूर्ण कार्य करतो. लग्नावरील केतू = (अ) अस्तित्व-विरक्ती-धारा "हे खरंच मीच का?" प्रश्न कृती-शील-असतानाही उठतो, (ब) डोके-सबंधित नाजूकता (अश्विनी = डोके; केतू = कपाळाजवळ जखमा), (क) बालपणापासूनच आध्यात्मिक शोध, (ड) पूर्व-जन्मीचे ऋषी/साधक-स्वभाव. शनीची स्व-राशी ३री दृष्टी = ओळखीला संरचनात्मक-शिस्त व उशीरा-परिपक्वता; राहूची ७वी दृष्टी = ओळख-बाहेरील-संबंधांकडे, दुसर्‍याच्या-माध्यमातून-मान्यतेची भूक. मंगळ-गुरू परिवर्तन (मंगळ गुरूच्या राशीत, गुरू मंगळाच्या राशीत) = १L+८L आणि ९L+१२L यांचे सखोल बांधणी. ज्योतिषी-ग्राहक-संवाद. "धर्मेश, तुझे लग्न मेष — योद्ध्याची राशी, मंगळ-स्वामी. मंगळ स्वतः लग्नापासून दूर ९व्या धर्म-भावात बसला आहे. ही दुर्बलता नाही; ही कुंडलीची पहिली मोठी ओळख आहे. तुझी ओळख व्यक्तिगत-परिस्थितीवर बांधली जात नाही — ती तू कशासाठी उभा आहेस त्यावर बांधली जाते. तुझ्यातला योद्धा अहंकारासाठी लढत नाही; तो अर्थासाठी लढतो. तुझा मंगळ स्व-जन्म-नक्षत्रात मूळ-मध्ये — पूर्ण उच्चीशिवाय मंगळाची ही कुंडलीतली सर्वोत्तम स्थिती. म्हणून जेव्हा तू अर्थ-पूर्ण कृती करतोस, तू पूर्ण शक्तीने करतोस; अर्थहीन कृतीत तू विखुरलेला वाटतोस. प्रामाणिक चर्चा हवी ती केतू-लग्नाची. केतू = दक्षिणी-राहू, विरक्ती, संशय, पूर्व-जन्म-समापन, शोधक-आध्यात्म. लग्नावर केतू असल्यामुळे तुझा एक भाग सदैव स्वतःच्या ओळखीवर प्रश्न-चिन्ह ठेवतो — यश-वेळीही 'हे खरंच मीच का?' विचारतो. हे नैराश्य नाही; ही कुंडलीची आध्यात्मिक-मुद्रा. अश्विनी पाद १ — अश्विनीकुमार वैद्य-नक्षत्र — मध्ये तुझा केतू शून्यवादी नसून वैद्य-शोधक. तू शोधतोस; तू माघार घेत नाहीस. ११व्यातील शनी ३र्‍या दृष्टीने या केतूकडे पाहतो आणि तोच तुझा स्थिरीकरण — संशयाला संरचना देतो, गोंधळ करत नाही. ७व्यातील राहू तुला अपारंपरिक-भागीदारी व इतरांच्या-माध्यमातून-दर्जा-भूक यांकडे खेचतो — हे २०३२ पासूनच्या राहू-महादशेत सर्वाधिक जाणवेल. शारीरिक: सडपातळ, कोरडे, ताणाखाली कधी-कधी डोकेदुखी, कपाळाजवळ छोट्या जखमांची शक्यता — पाठ्यपुस्तकातला अश्विनी-केतू देह. तुझी ओळख-पुनर्बांधणी आत्ता मंगळ-महादशेत — जानेवारी २०३२ पर्यंत — चालू आहे." निष्कर्ष. १व्या भावावर केतूचा शिक्का; मागे मंगळ-९-मूळचे संपूर्ण समर्थन. ओळख = शोधक-धर्म-योद्धा. मंगळ महादशा (२०२५-२०३२) = ओळख-पुनर्बांधणीचा प्रमुख कालखंड. सर्वोत्तम उप-कालखंड: मंगळ-गुरू अंतर्दशा (जुलै २०२६ – जून २०२७) जेव्हा परिवर्तन प्रज्वलित होते.

11.2 Second House — Wealth, Family, Speech द्वितीय भाव — धन, कुटुंब, वाणी

Condition / state. 2H Taurus is empty. Second-lord (धनेश) Venus sits in 9H Sagittarius at 03°34′ Mula Pada 2 (Capricorn-amsha) with Mars, Moon, Neptune — a four-graha stellium. Venus is also 7L (saptamesh), making it the chart's double-marak (२-स्वामी + ७-स्वामी = दुहेरी-मारक). Jupiter from 8H Scorpio Jyeshtha Pada 4 casts its 7th drishti directly onto 2H Taurus. No other major-graha aspect on 2H itself. The 2H cusp at Taurus falls under Krittika Pada 2 (Krittika sub-lord Sun, in 8H — adds Sun-flavour to the wealth-cusp).

All classical possibilities (Layer 1). The second house governs accumulated wealth (संचित-धन — distinct from earned income, which belongs to 11H, and from career-revenue, which is 10H); the family-of-birth (कुटुंब — distinct from spouse-family which is 7H); the mouth, speech, voice, and the food one consumes; the right eye specifically; early-childhood resources and inherited values; jewellery, savings, property held. It is one of the two maraka (मारक — life-cutting) houses alongside 7H, but maraka-function activates only in dasha-windows of marakesh-planets and dies down in benefic windows. Venus as 2L is naturally benefic for wealth, family-beauty, voice-sweetness, and pleasure-resources. When 2L sits in 9H — a dharma-trine and an auspicious upachaya-friendly position — wealth flows through dharmic, educational, foreign, or philosophical channels rather than through commercial grind (Parashara: dhana-lord in trine = wealth via meaningful work). Venus also rules 7H (Libra rising on 7H cusp from Aries lagna), so the same planet carries both wealth-lordship and partnership-lordship — every wealth-event in the chart will be laced with partnership-events, and vice versa. Jupiter's 7th-aspect on 2H from 8H is a classical wealth-blessing (Brihat-Parashara: गुरू-दृष्टी on धनभाव = expanded wealth through wisdom, inheritance, dharmic income). When the dispositor (here, Mars in Sagittarius) is in own-nakshatra, the dispositor strengthens every planet sitting in its sign — so Venus inherits some of Mars's Mula-dignity through dispositorship. Capricorn-amsha (Mula Pada 2) overlays Saturnian discipline on Venusian natural-pleasure significations: wealth accumulates slowly and through structured channels rather than through windfall or art-flash. Krittika cusp-nakshatra brings Sun-flavour to the wealth-cusp itself, meaning the family-of-birth has father-line-prominence.

Astrologer-to-client narrative (Layer 2). "Dharmesh, your second house — money, family, voice — is empty of planets, but it is anything but weak. Three classical mechanisms feed it: first, your wealth-lord Venus is sitting in your 9th house of dharma, surrounded by Mars, Moon, and Neptune — a four-planet stellium. Wealth-lord in dharma-house means your money comes through meaningful work, not commercial grind. The same Venus also rules your 7th house — so your marriage and your wealth move on the same timeline; every major wealth-event will arrive with a relationship-event tied to it. Second, Jupiter from your 8th house throws its 7th-aspect directly on your 2nd house. Jupiter's gaze on the wealth-house is one of the most-praised blessings in classical Jyotish — it gives wealth-through-wisdom, inheritance, and dharma-aligned income. Third, your Venus sits in Mula Pada 2, which carries Capricorn-amsha — meaning Saturn-discipline overlays the Venusian softness. Your money will not arrive through one flash or one lottery; it accumulates slowly through structured channels — savings, professional services, intellectual property, dharma-work. There is one watch-line you should know: Venus is also your 7th-lord, which makes it a 'double-marak'. Maraka means the planet activates difficult life-events specifically during the planet's own dasha-window. For you, that watch-window is the Mars-Venus antardasha from December 2029 to February 2031 — that is a fourteen-month period to be careful about health-events for elders and about over-extension. Speech: your Venus sits with Mars in Sagittarius, so your voice carries a philosophical, direct, sometimes-cutting quality — Sagittarius-Mula gravity. Family of birth: father carries weight through your 8H Sun, mother is dharma-supportive through your 4L Moon in 9H. Wealth-peaks: Mars-Jupiter AD 2026-2027 (Jupiter aspects 2H), Mars-Venus AD 2029-2031 (with marak-watch), and full Rahu MD 2032+."

Hard verdict / forecast. 2H is occupant-empty but lord-strong via 9H trine plus Jupiter's 7th aspect plus dispositor-Mars's Mula-dignity. Learning Note — Reading an empty house / रिकामा भाव कसा वाचावा

Rule. An empty house is read in four layers, not one: (1) the house's natural significations stand by default, (2) the lord's placement, dignity, and condition tell the story of where those significations are routed, (3) any aspect-on-the-house from a major graha tells what flavour is poured in, (4) the cusp-nakshatra adds the fine-grained sub-amsha colouring. A junior astrologer often skips steps 3 and 4 and reads only the lord — producing a thin reading. A senior astrologer always walks all four layers before delivering verdict. Applied to this chart: 4H Cancer is empty. Step 1 — mother, home, peace stand as significations. Step 2 — 4L Moon in 9H Mula Pada 2 Capricorn-amsha routes the emotional life into the dharma-house with Mars-Venus-Neptune coloring and Chandra-Mangala-yoga firing. Step 3 — Mars 8th-drishti adds caveat-flavour (sudden events on mother/home) AND Jupiter 9th-drishti adds rescue-flavour (wisdom-shielding). Step 4 — Pushya Pada 1 on 4H cusp adds Saturn-flavour. Skipping step 3 would have produced "4L is fine, peace assured" — wrong. Reading "they cancel each other" is also wrong. The correct reading is "they take turns" — Mars firings during Mars-windows, Jupiter rescue during Jupiter-windows. This is the four-layer method.

Learning Note 2 — Lagnesh in 9H + Ketu on lagna combo / लग्नेश ९व्यात + लग्नावर केतू

Rule. When the lagnesh sits in the 9H and Ketu sits on the lagna simultaneously, classical literature (Phaladeepika 2.27, Brihat-Parashara 16.x) identifies a specific reading-pattern not reducible to either condition alone. The lagnesh-in-9H alone produces a dharma-welded identity (auspicious-but-dispersed). Ketu-on-lagna alone produces a detached-investigative identity (introspective-but-rootless). This is why such natives often go through 3-4 identity-redefinitions in early-to-mid-life before settling into their authentic dharma-form. The astrologer's job is to mark these windows on the timeline and counsel elder-health awareness during them — not to plant "maraka in general" anxiety. Same principle for Jupiter (8L of partner's death-house from 7H = pati-marak for the spouse-arc; Jupiter MD ends Jan 2033, so the Jupiter-marak-as-pati-marak is structurally near the end of the current MD).

नियम. २ व ७ मारकस्थान म्हणून वर्गीकृत, पण मारक कार्य आहे, निष्कर्ष नव्हे. मारक फक्त (अ) २ वा ७ स्वामी, (ब) २ वा ७ मध्ये बसलेला ग्रह, (क) २ वा ७ वर पूर्ण दृष्टी असलेला ग्रह, (ड) त्या स्वामींचा राशी-स्वामी — यांच्या दशा-खिडकीतच सक्रिय. इतर वेळी २ व ७ धन/कुटुंब/वाणी/विवाह/भागीदारी म्हणून चालतात, मारक-कार्याशिवाय. ज्येष्ठ ज्योतिषी "मारक सर्वसाधारणपणे" ची भीती न-विकता दशा-कालरेषेवर मारक-खिडक्या स्पष्ट चिन्हांकित करतो. धर्मेशवर लागू: शुक्र दुहेरी-मारक (२L+७L). पुढच्या २५ वर्षांत मारक-सक्रिय खिडक्या: (१) मंगळ-शुक्र अंतर्दशा डिसेंबर २०२९-फेब्रुवारी २०३१ (१४ महिने), (२) राहू महादशेत राहू-शुक्र अंतर्दशा २०३२+ (~३ वर्षे), (३) २ वृषभ वा ७ तूळमधून शुक्राचे कोणतेही मोठे गोचर. इतर वेळी शुक्र धन-व-विवाह-इंजिन म्हणून मारक-कार्याशिवाय चालतो. ज्योतिषाचे काम — या खिडक्या कालरेषेवर चिन्हांकित करणे व त्या काळात ज्येष्ठ-आरोग्याची जागरूकता सुचवणे — "सर्वसाधारण मारक" चिंता न-पेरणे.

Learning Note 4 — Dispositor-strength as rescue / राशी-स्वामी-बल संरक्षण

Rule. When a planet sits in a sign whose lord (the dispositor / राशी-स्वामी) is dignified and well-placed, the planet inherits a portion of the dispositor's strength regardless of its own sign-friendship status. This is the parampara-bala (परंपरा-बल — inheritance-strength) principle articulated in Brihat-Parashara and Jaimini Sutras. The strongest dispositor-rescue occurs when the dispositor is in (a) own-nakshatra, (b) own-sign, (c) exaltation, or (d) a kendra-or-trikona from its own position. Applied to Dharmesh: Venus, Moon, and Neptune all sit in Sagittarius — Sagittarius is ruled by Jupiter (sign-dispositor), and within Sagittarius they each sit in nakshatras whose lords (Ketu for Mula, Venus for Purvashadha, Sun for Uttarashadha) further nuance the inheritance. The chart's master dispositor-rescue: Mars in own-nakshatra Mula. Every planet in Mula Pada (Mars-Venus pair) inherits Mars's own-nakshatra dignity. This is why the 9H stellium reads as strengthened rather than dispersed-into-dusthana. The junior reading "Venus is far from 2H, weakening wealth" is wrong; the correct reading is "Venus is far from 2H but inherits Mars-Mula-dignity AND receives Jupiter's 7th-drishti on 2H, so wealth flows through three rescue-channels." Walk dispositor-strength explicitly when any 2/4/5/9/10/11 lord is sign-distant from its own house.

नियम. ग्रह ज्या राशीत बसला, त्या राशीचा स्वामी (राशी-स्वामी / दिस्पॉझिटर) दिग्बल-संपन्न व चांगल्या स्थानी असता, ग्रह त्या दिग्बलाचा अंश आपोआप मिळवतो — स्वतःच्या राशी-मैत्री-स्थितीशी काही न-संबंधित. हा परंपरा-बल सिद्धांत बृहत्-पराशर व जैमिनी-सूत्रांत. सर्वोच्च राशी-स्वामी-संरक्षण होते जेव्हा राशी-स्वामी (अ) स्व-नक्षत्र, (ब) स्व-राशी, (क) उच्च, वा (ड) त्याच्या स्वतःच्या स्थानापासून केंद्र-त्रिकोणात असतो. धर्मेशवर लागू: शुक्र, चंद्र, नेपच्यून सर्व धनूत — धनू-स्वामी गुरू. राशी-अंतर्गत प्रत्येक ग्रह वेगळ्या नक्षत्र-स्वामीच्या प्रभावाखाली (मूळ केतू, पूर्वाषाढा शुक्र, उत्तराषाढा सूर्य). कुंडलीचा मास्टर राशी-स्वामी-संरक्षण: मंगळ स्व-नक्षत्र मूळ-मध्ये. मूळ-पाद-२ मधील प्रत्येक ग्रह (मंगळ-शुक्र-जोडी) मंगळाच्या स्व-नक्षत्र-दिग्बलाचा वारस. म्हणून ९व्या स्तेलियम दुस्थानात-विखुरलेला नसून बलवान-केंद्रित वाचला जातो. कनिष्ठ वाचन — "शुक्र २ पासून दूर, धन दुर्बल" — चूक; योग्य वाचन — "शुक्र २ पासून दूर पण मंगळ-मूळ-दिग्बल वारसा + गुरूची ७वी २ वर — तीन संरक्षण-वाहिन्या." २/४/५/९/१०/११ स्वामी स्व-भावापासून दूर असता राशी-स्वामी-बल स्पष्ट चालावे.
