Aspects — Drishti in this Chart या कुंडलीतील दृष्टी

A planet's drishti (दृष्टी, gaze) is the long-distance way it touches a house or another planet without being physically present. The aspect carries the planet's significations, lordships, and dignity to the receiver — sometimes blessing, sometimes burdening, sometimes simply colouring. In this chapter the seven major drishti-streams of Dharmesh's chart are walked through one by one.

14.1 What drishti is दृष्टी म्हणजे काय

Condition / state. Vedic Jyotish defines drishti by house-counts from the planet's residence. Every planet aspects the 7th house from itself with full 100% strength. Mars additionally aspects the 4th and 8th; Jupiter additionally the 5th and 9th; Saturn additionally the 3rd and 10th. The lunar nodes (Rahu, Ketu) in most modern schools aspect the 5th, 7th, and 9th — although this is school-dependent.

Possibilities (Layer 1). Drishti carries three layers of effect onto its target: (a) the natural karaka-significations of the aspecting planet (Sun's drishti adds authority-flavour; Jupiter's adds wisdom; Saturn's adds discipline-and-delay; Mars's adds aggression-and-courage), (b) the lordship-significations the aspecting planet brings (a planet ruling 8H casting its drishti onto 5H carries the 8H-flavour into the 5H-target), (c) the dignity of the aspecting planet at the moment of aspect (a debilitated graha gives weak drishti; an own-sign or exalted graha gives strong drishti). Reading drishti without all three layers produces shallow verdicts.

This-chart specific (Layer 2). In Dharmesh's chart the most-consequential drishti-streams are: Saturn 3/7/10 from own-sign 11H, Mars 4/7/8 from own-nakshatra 9H, Jupiter 5/7/9 from 8H Atma-Karaka position, and the Rahu-Ketu axial drishtis. Three of these four planets are in own-sign or own-nakshatra — meaning the chart's aspect-streams are unusually dignified. This is why several "hard" placements (Mars 8th-drishti on 4H, Jupiter in 8H, Sun in 8H) operate with less damage than they would in a chart with weaker dispositors.

Conclusion. Three of four major aspect-streams in this chart are sourced from own-dignity placements — a rare structural blessing.

14.2 Mars's 4/7/8 drishti मंगळाची ४/७/८ दृष्टी

Condition / state. Mars sits in 9H Sagittarius at 02°20′ Mula Pada 1. Mars's drishtis fall on: 4th-from-9H = 12H Pisces, 7th-from-9H = 3H Gemini, 8th-from-9H = 4H Cancer. Mars is the lagnesh (1L) and 8L — so each drishti carries identity-energy and transformation-flavour simultaneously.

Possibilities (Layer 1). Mars's 4th-drishti carries the most-aggressive flavour (Mars's natural fighting-energy directed onto domestic/security significations of the target). 7th-drishti is the standard partnership-flavour. 8th-drishti is the watch-line aspect — Mars's 8th-aspect on any house signifies sudden-events, injury-watch, surgery-potential, or disruption of that house's smooth running. When Mars is dignified (here: own-nakshatra), the 8th-drishti operates with more focus than pure destruction — surgical precision rather than random injury. When Mars is a 6L or 8L of the chart, the 8th-drishti often manifests as a watch-window during Mars's major or sub-dasha.

This-chart specific (Layer 2). 4th-drishti on 12H Pisces — Mars activates foreign-travel, moksha-action, expense-on-causes, and bold dissolution-of-ego practice; also supports surgical/hospitalisation events in 12H during Mars MD. 7th-drishti on 3H Gemini — Mars feeds courage and action-thrust into the writing/communication/younger-sibling channel; with Mercury (3L) combust in 8H, Mars's drishti effectively compensates for the muted Mercury, giving Dharmesh action-led expression. 8th-drishti on 4H Cancer — the chart's single biggest 4H caveat. Mother-health watch and home-disruption potential elevate during Mars-Saturn AD (Jun 2027 – Jul 2028) and Mars-Moon PD (closing months of Mars MD). Jupiter's 9th-drishti from 8H on 4H stands as the rescue against Mars's 8th-drishti.

Conclusion. Mars's aspect-stream is dignified, action-feeding to 3H and 12H, watch-line on 4H. Mars MD activates all three drishtis at full strength through 2032.

14.3 Jupiter's 5/7/9 drishti गुरुची ५/७/९ दृष्टी

Condition / state. Jupiter sits in 8H Scorpio at 27°26′ Jyeshtha Pada 4 (Pisces-amsha). Jupiter's drishtis fall on: 5th-from-8H = 12H Pisces (own sign), 7th-from-8H = 2H Taurus, 9th-from-8H = 4H Cancer. Jupiter is the Atma-Karaka (आत्मा-कारक), 9L+12L, and carries Vipareeta-adjacent qualifications from 8H residence.

Possibilities (Layer 1). Jupiter's drishti is the most-classically-benefic of all planetary aspects. It carries wisdom, expansion, dharma, mentorship, child-blessing, wealth-blessing, and a general "rescue" flavour to any target. When Jupiter is in dusthana (6/8/12), its drishti is sometimes called "guru's gaze from the cave" — still benefic, but coloured by the dusthana experience. When Jupiter aspects its own sign, the aspect is doubled in strength — the lord looks home and the house is rescued from any prior affliction.

This-chart specific (Layer 2). 5th-drishti on 12H Pisces is the chart's most-powerful Jupiter drishti — Jupiter is the 12L aspecting its own sign. The Karakamsa-Pisces rescue mentioned in earlier chapters operates here at the macro-level: moksha-path, charity-inclination, foreign-settlement-with-dharma, devotional ripening — all are blessed. 7th-drishti on 2H Taurus is a classical wealth-blessing — Jupiter's gaze on the wealth-house expands family-wealth, expands food-and-resource channel, supports inheritance from family-side. 9th-drishti on 4H Cancer is a powerful mother/home blessing — wisdom and protection enter the emotional anchor; this drishti stands as the rescue against Mars's 8th-drishti on 4H (the two cancel cleanly). All three Jupiter-drishti targets are rescued or strengthened; the Atma-Karaka's gaze on the three houses lifts them substantially.

Conclusion. Jupiter's aspect-stream is the chart's primary benefic-rescue mechanism. 12H, 2H, 4H all benefit. The Mars-Jupiter AD (Jul 2026 – Jun 2027) is when this rescue-pattern fires most strongly.

14.4 Saturn's 3/7/10 drishti शनीची ३/७/१० दृष्टी

Condition / state. Saturn sits alone in 11H Aquarius at 24°11′ Purvabhadra Pada 2 — own sign. Saturn's drishtis fall on: 3rd-from-11H = 1H Aries (Ketu), 7th-from-11H = 5H Leo (empty), 10th-from-11H = 8H Scorpio (Sun + Mercury combust + Jupiter + Pluto stellium).

Possibilities (Layer 1). Saturn's drishti carries discipline, delay, slow-ripening, hardship-then-mastery, structure-imposition, and karmic-accountability to any target. The 10th-drishti is Saturn's strongest (full-100%) aspect; 3rd-drishti is also full-strength in classical Parashari. 7th-drishti is standard for any planet. When Saturn is in own-sign, its drishti is unusually productive rather than purely-punitive — the discipline it imposes converts into permanent gain rather than chronic suffering.

This-chart specific (Layer 2). 3rd-drishti on 1H Aries lands directly on Ketu — Saturn imposes structural discipline on the identity-detachment current. This is why Dharmesh's Ketu-on-lagna does not collapse into nihilism: Saturn from its own sign keeps the identity-detachment structured, methodical, investigative. 7th-drishti on 5H Leo — Saturn structures the creativity/children/devotion line, contributing to the "delayed and disciplined" reading of 5H. 10th-drishti on 8H Scorpio stellium is the chart's most-structurally-important Saturn aspect — Saturn slows the entire 8H research-engine down to a methodical ripen, prevents impulsive transformation-action, and gives the 8H stellium its mastery-with-age temperament. Sun (5L) under Saturn's 10th-drishti = father-line carrying serious dimension; Mercury combust + Saturn-10th = double-muted intellect; Jupiter (9L+12L) under Saturn-10th = dharma ripening only with time. Saturn MD (2066 onwards) is when this entire structuring fully delivers.

Conclusion. Saturn's aspect-stream is the chart's structural-backbone. All three targets (1H, 5H, 8H) carry significant Saturn-shaping. Productive rather than punitive due to own-sign dignity.

14.5 Rahu and Ketu's special drishti राहू-केतूची विशेष दृष्टी

Condition / state. Rahu in 7H Libra at 00°34′ Chitra Pada 3. Ketu in 1H Aries at 00°34′ Ashvini Pada 1. Most modern Vedic schools grant the lunar nodes 5/7/9 aspects (some classical schools restrict to 7th only). Both schools agree on the mutual 7th-drishti — Rahu and Ketu always aspect each other.

Possibilities (Layer 1). Rahu's drishti adds craving-for-the-unconventional, foreign / technological / scandalous / illusion-prone flavours. Ketu's drishti adds detachment, sudden-event, past-life-completion, and investigative-piercing flavours. The mutual 7th-drishti on the nodal axis itself amplifies the karmic intensity of whatever houses Rahu and Ketu occupy.

This-chart specific (Layer 2). Rahu's 5th-drishti from 7H lands on 11H — directly on Saturn. This is a structurally interesting aspect: Rahu's craving-channel feeds into the gains-and-network house through Saturn — Dharmesh's network and friend-circle carry tech/foreign/unconventional flavour, his gains flow partly through digital/cross-border / status-leap routes. Rahu's 7th-drishti on 1H Ketu is the standard nodal-mutual aspect. Rahu's 9th-drishti from 7H lands on 3H Gemini — adding unconventional/foreign flavour to the writing/communication/sibling channel. Ketu's 5th-drishti from 1H lands on 5H Leo — detachment-from-children/creativity (contributes to delayed-children reading). Ketu's 7th-drishti on 7H Rahu — mutual. Ketu's 9th-drishti on 9H Sagittarius stellium — the powerful detachment-from-dogma signature; Dharmesh investigates dharma rather than inheriting it. Combined, the nodal axis colours the chart's six houses (1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 11) — almost half the chart.

Conclusion. The nodal drishti is karmically saturating. Rahu MD (2032-2050) brings these six drishti-targets fully online; until then they operate as standing flavour.

14.6 Mutual aspects and Parivartana परस्पर-दृष्टी व परिवर्तन

Condition / state. A mutual aspect (परस्पर-दृष्टी) is when two planets aspect each other simultaneously — almost always creates a yoga-like fusion. A parivartana (परिवर्तन / sign-exchange) is when planet A sits in B's sign and B sits in A's sign — the strongest single mutual-bonding mechanism in Vedic technique.

Possibilities (Layer 1). Mutual aspects create thematic fusion: Mars-Jupiter mutual aspect creates dharma-courage fusion; Saturn-Sun mutual aspect creates authority-discipline tension; Venus-Mars mutual aspect creates passion-yoga. Parivartanas are even stronger — a Mars-Saturn parivartana would mean Mars in Aquarius and Saturn in Aries, fusing the two lord-functions completely.

This-chart specific (Layer 2). Check parivartanas first: Mars in Sagittarius (Jupiter's sign), Jupiter in Scorpio (Mars's sign) — YES: Mars-Jupiter parivartana exists. This is a major chart-feature. The 1L+8L (Mars) sits in 9L+12L's sign; the 9L+12L (Jupiter) sits in 1L+8L's sign. Identity-and-transformation are fused with dharma-and-moksha at the lord-level. This parivartana is the chart's single most-important yogic feature after Saturn-own-sign. Mutual aspects: Mars and Jupiter aspect each other? Mars in 9H aspects 12H/3H/4H — Jupiter is in 8H, not in any of these. Jupiter in 8H aspects 12H/2H/4H — Mars is in 9H, not in any of these. So Mars and Jupiter do not aspect each other classically — the parivartana is the only direct bond. Saturn aspects 1H Ketu — Ketu cannot aspect back to 11H (Ketu's 5/7/9 from 1H = 5H, 7H, 9H, not 11H). Rahu and Ketu mutually aspect (always, by axis). No major Mars-Saturn, Sun-Saturn, or Venus-Jupiter mutual aspects.

Conclusion. The Mars-Jupiter parivartana is the chart's hidden engine. It binds the dharma-line and the identity-line at the lord-level, structurally compensating for Jupiter's 8H residence and validating Mars's own-nakshatra 9H placement.

14.7 Conclusion — aspect-map of the chart दृष्टी-नकाशा

Condition / state. Combining all seven drishti-streams produces a complete aspect-map.

Possibilities (Layer 1). When the aspect-map is layered over the house-occupancy map, the chart reveals which houses are over-aspected (compressed), which are under-aspected (free), and which carry conflicting aspect-streams (need careful reading). Heavily-aspected houses fire more vigorously when their lord or dasha activates; under-aspected houses operate more independently.

This-chart specific (Layer 2). Over-aspected houses in this chart: 4H Cancer receives Mars's 8th-drishti (watch) + Jupiter's 9th-drishti (rescue) — a balanced compression; 8H Scorpio receives Saturn's 10th-drishti directly onto the stellium — structuring; 12H Pisces receives Jupiter's 5th-drishti (own-sign) + Mars's 4th-drishti — doubly-aspected, robust despite emptiness; 1H Aries Ketu receives Saturn's 3rd-drishti + Rahu's 7th-drishti — heavy karmic compression. Under-aspected houses: 2H Taurus receives only Jupiter 7th-drishti (mild, benefic); 6H Virgo receives no major aspect (operates independently — Vipareeta-Harsha-yoga is its own engine); 10H Capricorn receives no major aspect (operates through 10L→11H placement). Conflicting-stream houses: 5H Leo receives Saturn-7th (structure-delay) + Ketu-5th (detachment) — both restrictive; this confirms the "delayed children/creativity" reading.

Conclusion. The chart's aspect-map favours the 8H-9H-12H axis with dignified-drishti rescues, while leaving the personal-foundation quadrant (2H, 6H, 10H) to operate via lord-placement alone.

Learning Note — Reading conflicting aspect streams / विरोधी दृष्टी कशी वाचावी

Rule: When a house receives both a malefic and a benefic aspect, do not average them — read them as two parallel timelines. The malefic aspect manifests during the malefic's dasha or transit-trigger; the benefic aspect manifests during the benefic's dasha or transit-trigger. The house lives both lives in sequence.

Applied: 4H Cancer receives Mars's 8th-drishti (watch-line: mother-health, home-disruption) AND Jupiter's 9th-drishti (rescue-line: wisdom-protection, dharma-anchored emotional life). During Mars-major windows (Mars-Saturn AD 2027-2028, Mars-Moon PD 2031-2032), the 8th-drishti watch fires. During Jupiter MD (2050 onwards), the rescue-line dominates. Reading "they cancel" is wrong; reading "they take turns" is correct.
