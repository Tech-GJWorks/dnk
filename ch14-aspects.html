Aspects — Drishti in this Chart या कुंडलीतील दृष्टी

A planet's drishti (दृष्टी, "gaze") is the long-distance way it touches a house or another planet without being physically present. The aspect carries the planet's natural significations, its lordships, and its dignity to the receiver — sometimes blessing, sometimes burdening, sometimes simply colouring. In a chart where most major lords sit in the 8H-9H spine far from the personal-foundation houses, drishti is the chart's primary connective tissue: it is how the 8H-9H lords reach back into 1H-2H-3H-4H-12H to influence them. For an astrologer in training this chapter is the laboratory for learning four things: (a) how to map every major drishti-stream on a single chart, (b) how to distinguish dignified-drishti from compromised-drishti by the source-planet's strength, (c) how to read houses that receive two opposing drishtis without falling into "they cancel each other" simplification, and (d) how to identify the chart's mutual-aspects and parivartanas (sign-exchanges) — the hidden bonds that integrate distant houses. Dharmesh's chart presents each of these reading-challenges. Three of the four major aspect-sources (Mars, Jupiter, Saturn) sit in own-nakshatra or own-sign, meaning the chart's aspect-streams are unusually dignified; this is why several "hard" placements operate with less damage than they would in a chart with weaker dispositors. This chapter walks each of the seven major drishti-streams in the full five-block teaching scaffold.

ग्रहाची दृष्टी (gaze) म्हणजे शारीरिकरीत्या उपस्थित नसताना भाव वा दुसर्‍या ग्रहावर लांब-अंतर प्रभाव. दृष्टी ग्रहाचे नैसर्गिक कारकत्व, स्वामित्व व दिग्बल लक्ष्याला घेऊन जाते — कधी आशीर्वाद, कधी ओझे, कधी फक्त रंग. ज्या कुंडलीत बहुतेक मोठे स्वामी ८-९ कण्यात व्यक्तिगत-पाया-घरांपासून दूर बसले आहेत, तिथे दृष्टी कुंडलीचा प्राथमिक संयोजी-तंतू: ८-९ स्वामी १-२-३-४-१२ पर्यंत कसे पोहोचतात ते दृष्टीतून. प्रशिक्षणार्थीसाठी हा अध्याय चार-गोष्टींची प्रयोगशाळा: (अ) एका कुंडलीवर प्रत्येक मोठी दृष्टी-धारा कशी मॅप करावी, (ब) स्त्रोत-ग्रहाच्या बलावरून दिग्बल-दृष्टी व तडजोडी-दृष्टी कशी वेगळी ओळखावी, (क) दोन विरोधी दृष्टी मिळवणारे भाव "ते एकमेकांना रद्द करतात" अशा सरलीकरणात न पडता कसे वाचावे, (ड) कुंडलीच्या परस्पर-दृष्टी व परिवर्तन (राशी-अदलाबदली) — दूरच्या घरांना एकत्र करणारे लपलेले बंध — कसे ओळखावे. धर्मेशच्या कुंडलीत हे चारही वाचन-आव्हाने. चारही मुख्य दृष्टी-स्त्रोतांपैकी (मंगळ-गुरू-शनी) तीन स्व-नक्षत्रात वा स्व-राशीत बसले — कुंडलीच्या दृष्टी-धारा असामान्यपणे दिग्बलयुक्त; म्हणून अनेक "कठीण" स्थाने दुर्बल-राशी-स्वामी असलेल्या कुंडलीपेक्षा कमी हानीने चालतात. हा अध्याय सातही मोठ्या दृष्टी-धारा पूर्ण ५-खंडी-शिक्षण-स्वरूपात.

14.1 What drishti is and how it works दृष्टी म्हणजे काय व ती कशी चालते

Condition / state. Vedic Jyotish defines drishti by house-counts from the planet's residence. Every classical planet aspects the 7th house from itself with full 100% strength. Mars additionally aspects the 4th-and-8th from itself; Jupiter additionally the 5th-and-9th; Saturn additionally the 3rd-and-10th. The lunar nodes Rahu and Ketu — in most modern schools — aspect the 5th, 7th, and 9th (school-dependent; conservative-Parashari restricts the nodes to the 7th-aspect only; the BPHS commentary tradition grants the 5/7/9 special aspects). The outer planets (Uranus, Neptune, Pluto) in modern Vedic-Western synthesis are usually granted only the 7th-aspect or are read as occupants without aspect-rays. In this chart all four major aspect-sources are inner-graha and follow classical rules: Mars 4/7/8, Jupiter 5/7/9, Saturn 3/7/10, plus the nodes Rahu and Ketu with 5/7/9 each (acknowledged with the school-dependent caveat).

All classical possibilities (Layer 1). Drishti carries three layers of effect onto its target, and reading drishti without all three layers produces shallow verdicts: (a) Natural-karaka layer. The natural significations of the aspecting planet flow into the target. Sun's drishti adds authority-flavour and ego-presence; Moon's adds emotional-colouring and mind-flavour; Mars's adds aggression-courage-and-cutting-edge; Mercury's adds intellect-speech-and-communication; Jupiter's adds wisdom-expansion-dharma; Venus's adds aesthetics-pleasure-relationship; Saturn's adds discipline-delay-and-structure; Rahu's adds craving-foreign-unconventional; Ketu's adds detachment-investigation-and-piercing. (b) Lordship layer. The houses ruled by the aspecting planet are transmitted into the target by the drishti. A planet ruling 8H casting drishti onto 5H carries the 8H-flavour (transformation, sudden-event, hidden-knowledge) into the 5H-target (creativity, children, devotion). This is why drishti-reading requires the full lordship-map: an aspect that looks innocent in significations may carry heavy lordship-load. (c) Dignity layer. The aspecting planet's dignity at the moment of the aspect modifies the drishti's strength. A debilitated graha gives weak drishti — even Jupiter when debilitated transmits diluted wisdom; an own-sign or exalted graha gives strong drishti — even Saturn in own-sign transmits productive-structuring rather than punitive-restriction. A combust planet gives muted drishti even if its physical significations are otherwise strong. Reading all three layers per drishti produces a complete aspect-picture; skipping any layer produces the junior-astrologer's superficial reading.

Astrologer-to-client narrative (Layer 2). "Dharmesh, before walking each aspect-stream in your chart, I need you to understand what an aspect actually does. A planet doesn't only affect the house it sits in. From its position it also throws long-distance gazes onto specific other houses — these are called drishti, the planet's aspects. Mars throws gazes onto the 4th, 7th, and 8th house from itself; Jupiter onto the 5th, 7th, and 9th; Saturn onto the 3rd, 7th, and 10th; every planet on the 7th from itself. Now here is the part most junior consultants miss: an aspect carries three things, not one. First it carries the planet's natural personality (Mars carries fight-energy, Jupiter carries wisdom-energy, Saturn carries discipline-energy). Second it carries the houses that planet rules — so if Saturn rules your 10th house and Saturn throws an aspect on your 8th house, that aspect carries 10th-house career-flavour into 8th-house transformation-domain. Third it carries the planet's dignity at that moment — a strong planet throws strong aspects; a weak or combust planet throws weak aspects. In your chart, three of the four major aspect-sources are in own-nakshatra or own-sign: Mars in own-nakshatra Mula, Jupiter in own-amsha Pisces, Saturn in own-sign Aquarius. This is rare. Most charts have at least two or three of the major aspect-sources in weak positions; yours has three in strong positions. This is why several placements in your chart that look 'hard' on the surface — Mars's 8th-aspect on 4H, Jupiter in 8H, Sun in 8H — operate with less damage than they would in a chart with weaker dispositors. Your chart's aspect-streams are structurally dignified; that's the first headline."

Hard verdict / forecast. Drishti = three-layer transmission (natural karaka + lordship + dignity). In Dharmesh's chart three of four major aspect-sources are in own-nakshatra or own-sign (Mars-Mula, Jupiter-Pisces-amsha, Saturn-Aquarius) — a rare structural blessing. The aspect-streams are unusually dignified; this is why several "hard" placements (Mars 8th-drishti on 4H, Jupiter-in-8H, Sun-in-8H) operate with less damage than in a chart with weaker dispositors.

14.6 Mutual aspects and the Mars-Jupiter parivartana परस्पर-दृष्टी व मंगळ-गुरू परिवर्तन

Condition / state. A mutual aspect (परस्पर-दृष्टी) occurs when two planets aspect each other simultaneously — almost always creates a yoga-like fusion of the two planets' significations. A parivartana (परिवर्तन / sign-exchange) occurs when planet A sits in B's sign and B sits in A's sign — the strongest single mutual-bonding mechanism in Vedic technique, treated by some classical texts as more powerful than yuti itself. Checking this chart systematically: Mars in Sagittarius (Jupiter's sign), Jupiter in Scorpio (Mars's sign) — YES: Mars-Jupiter parivartana exists. This is a major chart-feature. No other parivartanas: Sun-Moon? Sun in Scorpio, Moon in Sagittarius — Moon not in Leo, no parivartana. Mercury-Venus? Mercury in Scorpio, Venus in Sagittarius — neither in the other's sign, no parivartana. Saturn-anything? Saturn in own sign Aquarius — no partner. Mars-Saturn? Mars in Sagittarius, Saturn in Aquarius — no parivartana. Sun-Jupiter? Sun in Scorpio, Jupiter in Scorpio (same sign, that's yuti not parivartana). Jupiter in 8H aspects 12H/2H/4H — Mars is in 9H, not in any of these. So Mars and Jupiter do not aspect each other classically — the parivartana is the only direct bond. Parivartanas are even stronger because the two lords' lordship-domains are fully fused — the lord of A sits in the house ruled by B, and vice versa, meaning every reading-rule for A's house must include B's planet, and every reading-rule for B's house must include A's planet. Classical texts say parivartana is "stronger than yuti, equal to mutual-7th-aspect, but more permanent because positional-based not aspect-based." Three types: Maha-parivartana (when one or both planets are kendra-lords + trikona-lords), Khala-parivartana (when one is a dusthana-lord), Dainya-parivartana (when both are dusthana-lords). The Mars-Jupiter parivartana here is the maha-variant: Mars is 1L (kendra-lord-by-lagna) and 8L (dusthana-lord); Jupiter is 9L (trikona-lord) and 12L (dusthana-lord). 14.7 The chart's complete aspect-map and conflicting-stream houses कुंडलीचा संपूर्ण दृष्टी-नकाशा व विरोधी-धारा-घरे

Condition / state. Combining all seven drishti-streams (Mars 4/7/8, Jupiter 5/7/9, Saturn 3/7/10, Rahu 5/7/9, Ketu 5/7/9, plus the Sun-Moon-Mercury-Venus universal 7th-aspects) and overlaying them on house-occupancy produces the chart's complete aspect-map. When the aspect-map is layered over the house-occupancy map, the chart reveals which houses are over-aspected (compressed with multiple drishtis), which are under-aspected (free of major drishti, operate independently via their lord), and which carry conflicting aspect-streams (both malefic and benefic drishti — need careful sequential reading). Heavily-aspected houses fire more vigorously when their lord or dasha activates; under-aspected houses operate more independently of dasha-firing; conflicting-stream houses live both lives in sequence (not in average).

All classical possibilities (Layer 1). Reading the aspect-map produces structural verdicts about which houses are the chart's busiest stages, which are quietest, and where the chart's tensions concentrate. Over-aspected houses (3+ major aspects) tend to be the most-eventful houses of the chart — their dasha-windows produce the most-significant life-events; the native experiences these houses as "loud" even when not actively triggered. Under-aspected houses (no major aspects from major planets) tend to operate as their lord dictates — quieter, more predictable, fewer surprise-firings. Conflicting-stream houses (both malefic and benefic aspects from major planets) carry the chart's most-subtle reading-challenges: the house lives both lives, the lives play out in sequence (malefic in malefic-dasha-windows, benefic in benefic-dasha-windows). Junior consultants average the streams ("they cancel each other"); senior consultants sequence them ("they take turns").

Astrologer-to-client narrative (Layer 2). "Dharmesh, let me give you the chart's complete aspect-map in one pass. The over-aspected houses in your chart are: 4H Cancer receives Mars's 8th-aspect (watch-line) and Jupiter's 9th-aspect (rescue); 8H Scorpio receives Saturn's 10th-aspect directly onto the 4-graha stellium (structuring the entire research-engine); 12H Pisces receives Jupiter's 5th-aspect (own-sign rescue) and Mars's 4th-aspect (courage-into-foreign); 1H Aries Ketu receives Saturn's 3rd-aspect (identity-structuring) and Rahu's 7th-aspect (nodal mutual); 11H Aquarius Saturn receives Rahu's 5th-aspect (networking the gains). The under-aspected houses are: 2H Taurus receives only Jupiter's 7th-aspect (mild and benefic); 6H Virgo receives no major aspect (operates entirely via combust 6L Mercury and the Vipareeta-Harsha-yoga); 10H Capricorn receives no major aspect (operates via 10L Saturn in 11H). The conflicting-stream houses are: 4H (Mars 8th + Jupiter 9th — watch vs rescue) and 5H Leo (Saturn 7th + Ketu 5th — both restrictive, both delaying creativity and progeny). The 4H is the chart's primary 'take turns' house — Mars's watch-line fires during Mars-major windows (Mars-Saturn AD 2027-2028, Mars-Moon PD 2031-2032), Jupiter's rescue dominates during Jupiter-major windows (Jupiter MD 2050+). The 5H is the chart's primary 'parallel restriction' house — both Saturn and Ketu impose delay-and-detachment, and they reinforce each other rather than alternating. This produces the 'delayed, structured, detached creativity-and-children' reading for your 5H. So the chart's aspect-map favours the 8H-9H-12H axis with dignified-drishti rescues, while leaving the personal-foundation quadrant (2H, 6H, 10H) to operate via lord-placement alone."

Over-aspected: 1H (Saturn 3rd + Rahu 7th), 4H (Mars 8th + Jupiter 9th, take-turns), 8H (Saturn 10th directly on stellium), 11H (Rahu 5th on Saturn), 12H (Jupiter 5th own-sign + Mars 4th). स्थिती. सात दृष्टी-धारा एकत्रित (मंगळ ४/७/८, गुरू ५/७/९, शनी ३/७/१०, राहू ५/७/९, केतू ५/७/९, अधिक सूर्य-चंद्र-बुध-शुक्र सार्वत्रिक ७व्या) घर-उपस्थितीवर आच्छादून कुंडलीचा संपूर्ण दृष्टी-नकाशा. दृष्टी-नकाशा घर-उपस्थिती-नकाशावर थर लावल्यास कुंडली कोणती घरे अति-दृष्ट (अनेक दृष्टींनी संकोचित), कोणती दृष्टी-न्यून (मोठ्या दृष्टीशिवाय, स्वामीद्वारे स्वतंत्र चालतात), व कोणती विरोधी दृष्टी-धारा (क्रूर व शुभ दोन्ही — काळजीपूर्वक क्रमवार वाचन आवश्यक) बाळगतात ते उघड. जड-दृष्ट घरे त्यांचा स्वामी वा दशा प्रज्वलित होताना अधिक तीव्रतेने प्रज्वलित; दृष्टी-न्यून घरे दशा-प्रज्वलनापासून अधिक स्वतंत्र; विरोधी-धारा घरे दोन्ही जीवने क्रमाने जगतात (सरासरीने नव्हे). शास्त्रीय शक्यता. दृष्टी-नकाशा वाचनातून कुंडलीची कोणती घरे सर्वात-व्यस्त रंगमंच, कोणती सर्वात-शांत, व कुठे कुंडलीचे तणाव एकवटले याबद्दल संरचनात्मक निष्कर्ष. अति-दृष्ट घरे (३+ मोठ्या दृष्टी) कुंडलीची सर्वात-घटनात्मक घरे — त्यांच्या दशा-खिडक्या सर्वात-महत्त्वाच्या जीवन-घटना; नेटिव्ह सक्रिय-प्रवर्तनशिवायही ही घरे "मोठ्या आवाजात" अनुभवतो. दृष्टी-न्यून घरे (मोठ्या ग्रहांकडून दृष्टी नाही) स्वामीनुसार चालतात — शांत, अधिक अंदाजनीय, कमी अकस्मात-प्रज्वलन. विरोधी-धारा घरे (मोठ्या ग्रहांकडून क्रूर व शुभ दोन्ही दृष्टी) कुंडलीची सर्वात-सूक्ष्म वाचन-आव्हाने: घर दोन्ही जीवने जगते, ती जीवने क्रमाने उलगडतात (क्रूर-दशा-खिडक्यांत क्रूर, शुभ-दशा-खिडक्यांत शुभ). कनिष्ठ-सल्लागार धारा सरासरी करतात ("त्या रद्द होतात"); ज्येष्ठ-सल्लागार त्यांना क्रमवार करतात ("आळी-पाळी"). ज्योतिषी-ग्राहक-संवाद. "धर्मेश, तुझ्या कुंडलीचा संपूर्ण दृष्टी-नकाशा एका झलकेत. अति-दृष्ट घरे: ४ कर्क मंगळाची ८वी (संकेत) + गुरूची ९वी (संरक्षण); ८ वृश्चिक शनीची १०वी थेट चार-ग्रह स्तेलियमवर (संपूर्ण संशोधन-इंजिन संरचित); १२ मीन गुरूची ५वी (स्व-राशी संरक्षण) + मंगळाची ४थी (परदेशात धैर्य); १ मेष केतू शनीची ३री (ओळख-संरचना) + राहूची ७वी (नोडल परस्पर); ११ कुंभ शनी राहूची ५वी (लाभ-नेटवर्किंग). दृष्टी-न्यून घरे: २ वृषभ फक्त गुरूची ७वी (सौम्य व शुभ); ६ कन्या कोणतीही मोठी दृष्टी नाही (अस्तंगत ६L बुध व विप्रीत-हर्ष-योगाद्वारे पूर्णतः चालतो); १० मकर कोणतीही मोठी दृष्टी नाही (१०L शनी ११व्यात चालतो). विरोधी-धारा घरे: ४ (मंगळ ८वी + गुरू ९वी — संकेत वि संरक्षण) व ५ सिंह (शनी ७वी + केतू ५वी — दोन्ही निर्बंधक, दोन्ही सर्जनशीलता-संतान विलंबित). ४ कुंडलीचे प्राथमिक 'आळी-पाळी' घर — मंगळाची संकेत-रेषा मंगळ-महा-खिडक्यांत प्रज्वलित (मंगळ-शनी अंतर्दशा २०२७-२०२८, मंगळ-चंद्र काल २०३१-२०३२), गुरूचे संरक्षण गुरू-महा-खिडक्यांत वर्चस्व (गुरू महादशा २०५०+). ५ कुंडलीचे प्राथमिक 'समांतर-निर्बंध' घर — शनी व केतू दोन्ही विलंब-व-विरक्ती लादतात, ते एकमेकांना बळकटी देतात आळी-पाळी न-घेता. म्हणून तुझ्या ५व्यासाठी 'विलंबित, संरचित, विरक्त सर्जनशीलता-व-संतान' वाचन. कुंडलीचा दृष्टी-नकाशा ८-९-१२ अक्षाला दिग्बल-दृष्टी-संरक्षणासह अनुकूल, तर व्यक्तिगत-पाया-चतुर्थांश (२-६-१०) फक्त स्वामी-स्थानाद्वारे चालण्यासाठी सोडलेला." निष्कर्ष. अति-दृष्ट: १ (शनी ३री + राहू ७वी), ४ (मंगळ ८वी + गुरू ९वी, आळी-पाळी), ८ (शनी १०वी थेट स्तेलियमवर), ११ (राहू ५वी शनीवर), १२ (गुरू ५वी स्व-राशी + मंगळ ४थी). दृष्टी-न्यून: २, ६, १०. विरोधी-धारा/आळी-पाळी: ४ (मंगळ वि गुरू). विरोधी-धारा/समांतर-निर्बंध: ५ (शनी ७वी + केतू ५वी, दोन्ही विलंबक). दृष्टी-नकाशा ८-९-१२ व्यक्ति-पार अक्षाला दिग्बल-संरक्षणासह अनुकूल, व्यक्तिगत-पाया-चतुर्थांश फक्त स्वामी-स्थानाद्वारे चालतो.

Learning Note — Reading conflicting aspect streams / विरोधी दृष्टी कशी वाचावी

Rule. When a house receives both a malefic and a benefic aspect from major planets, do not average them — read them as two parallel timelines that activate in sequence. The malefic aspect manifests during the malefic's dasha-or-transit-trigger; the benefic aspect manifests during the benefic's dasha-or-transit-trigger. The house lives both lives, but it lives them in sequence — not in average. A junior consultant says "the two aspects cancel each other, so the house is neutral." A senior consultant says "the two aspects take turns, so during Mars-windows the watch-line fires and during Jupiter-windows the rescue dominates." The senior reading produces accurate dasha-timing; the junior reading produces vague averaging.

Applied. 4H Cancer in this chart receives Mars's 8th-drishti (watch-line: mother-health, home-disruption, vehicle-event) AND Jupiter's 9th-drishti (rescue-line: wisdom-protection, dharma-anchored emotional life). During Mars-major windows — Mars MD now (Jan 2025 – Jan 2032), particularly Mars-Saturn AD (Jun 2027 – Jul 2028) and Mars-Moon PD (Jun 2031 – Jan 2032) — the Mars 8th-drishti fires as the active watch-line. During Jupiter-major windows — Jupiter MD opening 2050 — the Jupiter 9th-drishti dominates as the active rescue. The reading "they cancel" is wrong; the reading "they take turns" is correct. This is the take-turns method.
