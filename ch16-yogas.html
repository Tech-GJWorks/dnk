Yogas in this Chart या कुंडलीतील योग

A yoga (योग — literally "union") is a specific planetary configuration with a named, classical, predictable effect. Hundreds of yogas exist in Vedic literature — Parashara's Brihat-Parashara-Hora-Shastra lists hundreds; Phaladeepika adds variations; Brihat-Jataka catalogues regional schools; modern commentators bring nakshatra-specific and chara-karaka-specific yogas. Only a small fraction of all named yogas is structurally active in any single chart. The astrologer's task in any consultation is to check each major yoga-category against the placements, name what fires (with degree of strength), name what fails (and why), and identify yogas that fire only partially (where one of the qualification-criteria is missing). The honest tally — including the negatives — is the whole point: a yoga-section that lists only the positives is a sales-pitch, not a reading. This chapter walks every major yoga-category against Dharmesh's chart in the full five-block teaching scaffold: condition, all classical possibilities, astrologer-to-client narrative, hard verdict, and the Marathi mirror. The categories covered: Pancha-Mahapurusha (the five great-man yogas), Raja-yogas (authority/recognition), Dhana-yogas (wealth), Neecha-Bhanga (debilitation-cancellation), Vipareeta-Raja-yogas (Harsha/Sarala/Vimala — the reversal-yogas), Chandra-yogas (Moon-centred — Chandra-Mangala, Sunapha, Anapha, Durdhura, Kemadruma), Gajakesari, Sannyasa-yoga (renunciation), and the closing tally with firing-windows table.

योग (शब्दशः "एकत्र-येणे") = विशिष्ट ग्रह-रचना ज्याला नामांकित, शास्त्रीय, अंदाजनीय परिणाम. वैदिक साहित्यात शेकडो योग — पराशराचा बृहत्-पराशर-होरा-शास्त्र शेकडो सूचीबद्ध; फलदीपिका भिन्न-रूपे; बृहत्-जातक प्रादेशिक शाखा; आधुनिक भाष्यकार नक्षत्र-विशिष्ट व चर-कारक-विशिष्ट योग आणतात. कोणत्याही कुंडलीत सर्व नामांकित योगांचा थोडकाच भाग संरचनेने सक्रिय. ज्योतिषाचे काम कोणत्याही सल्लागारणीत: प्रत्येक मोठा योग-वर्ग स्थानांविरुद्ध तपासणे, काय प्रज्वलित (बल-पातळीसह) नाव देणे, काय अयशस्वी (व का) नाव देणे, व फक्त आंशिक प्रज्वलित होणारे योग ओळखणे (जिथे योग्यता-निकषांपैकी एक चुकलेला). प्रामाणिक मोजणी — नकारात्मकांसह — हीच मुख्य गोष्ट: फक्त सकारात्मक यादी करणारे योग-विभाग विक्री-पिच, वाचन नव्हे. हा अध्याय प्रत्येक मोठा योग-वर्ग धर्मेशच्या कुंडलीविरुद्ध पूर्ण ५-खंडी-स्वरूपात. वर्ग: पंच-महापुरुष, राज-योग, धन-योग, नीच-भंग, विप्रीत-राज (हर्ष/सरल/विमल), चंद्र-योग (चंद्र-मंगळ/सुनफा/अनफा/दुर्धरा/केमद्रुम), गज-केसरी, संन्यास-योग, व अंतिम मोजणी प्रज्वलन-खिडकी-सारणीसह.

16.1 Pancha-Mahapurusha check (the five great-man yogas) पंच-महापुरुष योग-तपासणी

Condition / state. The five Mahapurusha (महापुरुष — "great-man") yogas fire when Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, or Saturn sits in its own-sign or exaltation-sign AND in a kendra (1H, 4H, 7H, 10H) from the lagna or from the Moon. The five named yogas: Ruchaka (Mars), Bhadra (Mercury), Hamsa (Jupiter), Malavya (Venus), Sasa (Saturn). Each delivers a distinct "great-man" signature: Ruchaka = warrior-leader; Bhadra = scholar-business; Hamsa = sage-teacher; Malavya = beauty-art-luxury; Sasa = disciplined-administrator-with-late-life-mastery. Both conditions (dignity AND kendra-placement) must be met for the full yoga; many charts have one without the other, producing partial signatures.

All classical possibilities (Layer 1). Each Mahapurusha-yoga is a structural lifetime-stamp. When fully formed, the native carries the "great-man" signature of that planet visibly through life: Ruchaka-natives have warrior-leader presence, often in military, surgery, or executive-action roles; Bhadra-natives have scholar-business presence, often in writing, teaching, intellectual-services; Hamsa-natives have sage-teacher presence, often in philosophy, religion, dharmic-counseling; Malavya-natives have beauty-art-luxury presence, often in arts, design, hospitality, relationship-counseling; Sasa-natives have disciplined-administrator presence, often in administration, structural-roles, late-life-recognized service. The yoga's strength depends on both criteria: planet-dignity (own-sign, moolatrikona, exaltation) AND kendra-position. Both conditions create full yoga; only-one-condition produces partial. Some modern schools count any-own-sign-or-exaltation as Mahapurusha-trigger regardless of house-type; the conservative Parashari reading is strict — both conditions required. The standard reading splits the difference: if only-own-sign-or-exaltation, partial yoga; if both, full yoga; if neither, absent. Mahapurusha-yogas fire most strongly during the dasha-windows of the participating planet — Saturn-Mahapurusha matures in Saturn MD; Jupiter-Hamsa in Jupiter MD; and so on.

Astrologer-to-client narrative (Layer 2). "Dharmesh, the Pancha-Mahapurusha yogas are five named configurations that produce 'great-man' signatures when fully formed. Each requires two things: the planet must sit in its own sign or exaltation, AND in a kendra house (1, 4, 7, or 10 from lagna or Moon). Let me walk all five for your chart. Ruchaka (Mars): Mars must be in Aries/Scorpio (own) or Capricorn (exaltation), and in a kendra. Your Mars is in Sagittarius (friendly, not own/exalt) in 9H (trine, not kendra). Fails on both criteria. Ruchaka does not form. Bhadra (Mercury): Mercury must be in Gemini/Virgo (own) or Virgo (exaltation), and in a kendra. Your Mercury is in Scorpio (neutral) in 8H (dusthana) and combust. Fails completely. Bhadra does not form. Hamsa (Jupiter): Jupiter must be in Sagittarius/Pisces (own) or Cancer (exaltation), and in a kendra. Your Jupiter is in Scorpio (friendly, not own/exalt) in 8H (dusthana). Fails. Hamsa does not form. Malavya (Venus): Venus must be in Taurus/Libra (own) or Pisces (exaltation), and in a kendra. Your Venus is in Sagittarius (neutral) in 9H (trine, not kendra). Fails. Malavya does not form. Sasa (Saturn): Saturn must be in Capricorn/Aquarius (own) or Libra (exaltation), and in a kendra. Your Saturn is in Aquarius (own sign — first condition ✓) in 11H (upachaya, not strict kendra — second condition ✗). Own-sign dignity is met; kendra is not. This is the partial-Sasa case. The own-sign Saturn delivers most of the Sasa benefits — late-life administrative mastery, disciplined wealth-accumulation, service-respect, cumulative power-position — but without the full kendra-anchored Mahapurusha title. Some modern schools count it as full Sasa; conservative Parashari calls it partial. The benefits are real either way; only the label is partial."

Hard verdict / forecast. Of the five Pancha-Mahapurusha yogas, only Sasa-partial forms in this chart. Ruchaka, Bhadra, Hamsa, Malavya all fail (planets in friendly/neutral signs and/or non-kendra houses). Sasa-partial: Saturn own-sign Aquarius 11H meets the dignity criterion but not the strict-kendra criterion (11H = upachaya). Own-sign Saturn alone delivers most of the Mahapurusha benefits — late-life administrative mastery, disciplined wealth, service-respect, cumulative power-position. Matures fully in Saturn MD (Jan 2066 – Jan 2085).

"धर्मेश, पाचही चालू. रुचक: मंगळ धनूत (मित्र, स्व/उच्च नव्हे) ९व्या त्रिकोणात (केंद्र नव्हे). दोन्ही अटी अयशस्वी. तयार नाही. भद्र: बुध वृश्चिकात तटस्थ ८व्या दुस्थानात अस्तंगत. पूर्णतः अयशस्वी. नाही. हंस: गुरू वृश्चिकात मित्र-स्व/उच्च नव्हे, ८व्या दुस्थानात. नाही. मालव्य: शुक्र धनूत तटस्थ ९व्या त्रिकोणात. नाही. शस: शनी कुंभेत स्व-राशी (पहिली अट ✓) ११व्या उपचयात (कडक केंद्र नव्हे — दुसरी अट ✗). स्व-राशी-दिग्बल पूर्ण; केंद्र नाही. आंशिक-शस. स्व-राशी एकट्याने बहुतेक शस-फायदे — वय-सह-प्रशासकीय-प्रभुत्व, शिस्तबद्ध-धन, सेवा-आदर, संचित-शक्ती — पूर्ण केंद्र-नांगर महापुरुष शीर्षकाशिवाय. आधुनिक काही पूर्ण शस गणतात; संरक्षक-पराशरी आंशिक. फायदे दोन्ही प्रकारे खरे; फक्त शीर्षक आंशिक." निष्कर्ष. पाच महापुरुषांपैकी फक्त शस-आंशिक. इतर सर्व (रुचक-भद्र-हंस-मालव्य) अयशस्वी. आंशिक-शस: शनी स्व-राशी कुंभ ११ दिग्बल पूर्ण-कडक-केंद्र निकष नाही (११ उपचय). स्व-राशी एकट्याने महापुरुष-फायदे. शनी महादशा (जानेवारी २०६६ – जानेवारी २०८५) मध्ये पूर्ण परिपक्व.

16.2 Raja-yogas (authority and recognition) राज-योग — अधिकार व मान्यता

Condition / state. Raja-yoga (राज-योग — "king-union") is formed when a kendra-lord (1L/4L/7L/10L) and a trikona-lord (1L/5L/9L) associate by one of three mechanisms: yuti (same-sign), mutual aspect, or parivartana (sign-exchange). The strongest single Raja-yoga axis is the 1L-9L association (kendra-trikona-overlap with the strongest trine). 5L-9L association is the next-strongest. Multiple Raja-yogas in one chart produce multiple authority-pillars; a single strong Raja-yoga produces one reliable rise. For Aries lagna in this chart: kendra-lords = Mars (1L), Moon (4L), Venus (7L), Saturn (10L); trikona-lords = Mars (1L), Sun (5L), Jupiter (9L). The associations between these in the chart:

All classical possibilities (Layer 1). Raja-yoga delivers authority, recognition, leadership-position, command-presence, and the king-yoga-flavour to the dasha-windows when the participating lords fire. The classical reading: when a Raja-yoga is active, the native achieves visible recognition during the participating planets' dasha-windows; without the yoga, recognition is harder to come by even with native talent. Strength factors: (a) strict-kendra-with-strict-trikona is stronger than overlapping lordships; (b) own-sign or exaltation dignity of either lord strengthens; (c) parivartana is the strongest mechanism (stronger than yuti, equal to mutual-7th-aspect, more permanent because positional); (d) multiple Raja-yogas create cumulative recognition arc; (e) Raja-yogas placed in dusthanas (6/8/12) operate with delay-or-with-crisis-tinge but do fire eventually; (f) dasha-firing-windows for Raja-yogas must include both planets — Raja-yoga between Mars and Jupiter fires most strongly in Mars-Jupiter AD, Jupiter-Mars AD, or both planets' transits crossing the yoga-positions.

Astrologer-to-client narrative (Layer 2). "Dharmesh, let me walk the Raja-yoga associations in your chart. Mars-Jupiter parivartana — Mars in Sagittarius (Jupiter's sign), Jupiter in Scorpio (Mars's sign). Mars is your 1L (lagna-lord, kendra-by-lagna) AND 8L (dusthana). Jupiter is your 9L (trikona, strongest dharma-trine) AND 12L (dusthana). The 1L-9L parivartana is a textbook Raja-yoga — kendra-trikona at the lord-level, locked permanently by sign-exchange. This is your chart's primary Raja-yoga, and it carries the strongest single classical signature: 1L-9L parivartana is named in Brihat-Parashara as one of the most-powerful Raja-yoga configurations. Sun-Jupiter same-sign in 8H — Sun is your 5L (trikona) and Jupiter is your 9L (trikona). 5L-9L association is classically the strongest single Raja-yoga combination after the 1L-9L axis. The two trine-lords sharing a sign produces a Raja-yoga even though both sit in dusthana 8H — the dusthana-tinge means the yoga ripens through transformation-experience, but the yoga itself fires fully. Fires strongest in Mars-Jupiter AD Jul 2026 – Jun 2027 (when both Mars-Jupiter parivartana and Sun-Jupiter Raja-yoga activate together) and again in Rahu-Jupiter AD inside Rahu MD. Kendra-kendra associations in 9H — Moon (4L, kendra) yuti with Mars (1L, kendra) and Venus (7L, kendra) — three kendra-lords share one sign. Strict classical Raja-yoga requires kendra-trikona overlap (not pure kendra-kendra), but the kendra-kendra fusion is recognized as a yoga-adhipati signature, contributing to the chart's authority-presence even without strict Raja-yoga title. Summary: Two strong classical Raja-yogas active: (a) Mars-Jupiter 1L-9L parivartana, (b) Sun-Jupiter 5L-9L same-sign in 8H. Plus multiple kendra-kendra associations in 9H reinforcing. Activation peaks: Mars-Jupiter AD Jul 2026 – Jun 2027 — the chart's primary recognition window, when both Raja-yogas fire simultaneously."

Hard verdict / forecast. Two classical Raja-yogas active: (a) Mars-Jupiter parivartana = 1L+8L ↔ 9L+12L sign-exchange = chart's primary Raja-yoga (1L-9L axis, strongest classical configuration); (b) Sun-Jupiter same-sign in 8H = 5L-9L Raja-yoga (second-strongest classical configuration, qualified by 8H dusthana tinge). Plus kendra-kendra associations in 9H (Mars-Moon-Venus together = three kendra-lords) supporting authority-presence. Primary firing-window: Mars-Jupiter AD (Jul 2026 – Jun 2027) — both Raja-yogas activate simultaneously, the chart's primary recognition-window. Learning Note — Yoga-firing depends on dasha / योग-फळ दशेवर अवलंबून

This is the single most-important practical principle for consultation: do not promise yoga-effects in any year without confirming the dasha-window. A yoga sitting dormant for forty years before its planet's dasha fires looks like the yoga is "not working" — when in fact it is simply waiting. Honest consultation timing-maps each yoga to its firing-window.
