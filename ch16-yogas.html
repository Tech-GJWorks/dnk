Chapter 16

Yogas in this Chart या कुंडलीतील योग

A yoga (योग) is a specific planetary configuration with a named, classical, predictable effect. Hundreds exist; only a small fraction is structurally active in any single chart. This chapter walks every major yoga-category against Dharmesh's placements, naming what fires, what fails, and what fires only partially. The honest tally — including the negatives — is the whole point.

16.1 Pancha-Mahapurusha check पंच-महापुरुष योग-तपासणी

Condition / state. The five Mahapurusha (महापुरुष) yogas fire when Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, or Saturn sits in its own-sign or exaltation-sign and in a kendra (1H, 4H, 7H, 10H) from the lagna or from the Moon. The five named yogas: Ruchaka (Mars), Bhadra (Mercury), Hamsa (Jupiter), Malavya (Venus), Sasa (Saturn).

Possibilities (Layer 1). Each Mahapurusha-yoga delivers a distinct "great-man" signature when fully formed: Ruchaka — warrior-leader; Bhadra — scholar-business; Hamsa — sage-teacher; Malavya — beauty-art-luxury; Sasa — disciplined-administrator-with-late-life-mastery. The yoga requires both dignity (own/exalt) AND kendra-placement. Many charts have one condition without the other — producing partial signatures.

This-chart specific (Layer 2). Walking the five: Ruchaka (Mars): Mars in Sagittarius (friendly, not own/exalt) in 9H (trine, not kendra) — fails on both criteria; does NOT form. Bhadra (Mercury): Mercury in Scorpio (neutral) in 8H (dusthana) combust — fails completely; does NOT form. Hamsa (Jupiter): Jupiter in Scorpio (friendly, not own/exalt) in 8H (dusthana) — fails; does NOT form. Malavya (Venus): Venus in Sagittarius (neutral) in 9H (trine, not kendra) — fails; does NOT form. Sasa (Saturn): Saturn in Aquarius (own sign) in 11H — own-sign ✓ but 11H is upachaya not strict kendra ✗ — partial Sasa via own-sign dignity alone.

Conclusion. Of the five Mahapurusha-yogas, only Sasa-partial forms. Saturn's own-sign-but-upachaya placement delivers most of the Sasa benefits (late-life administrative mastery, disciplined wealth, service-respect) without the full kendra-anchored Mahapurusha-yoga title.

16.2 Raja-yogas राज-योग

Condition / state. Raja-yoga (राज-योग) is formed when a kendra-lord (1L/4L/7L/10L) and a trikona-lord (1L/5L/9L) associate — by yuti, mutual aspect, or sign-exchange.

Possibilities (Layer 1). Raja-yoga delivers authority, recognition, leadership-position, and the king-yoga-flavour to the dasha-windows when the participating lords fire. A chart with multiple Raja-yogas produces multiple authority-pillars; a chart with one strong Raja-yoga produces a single but reliable rise.

This-chart specific (Layer 2). For Aries lagna: kendra-lords = Mars (1L, 4L? no — 4L is Moon), Moon (4L), Venus (7L), Saturn (10L). Trikona-lords = Mars (1L), Sun (5L), Jupiter (9L). The key associations: Mars-Jupiter parivartana (Mars in Sagittarius = Jupiter's sign; Jupiter in Scorpio = Mars's sign) — this binds 1L+4L? no, Mars is 1L+8L not 4L. But Mars=1L (kendra-lord by lagna-itself) and 8L (dusthana); Jupiter=9L (trikona-lord) and 12L (dusthana). The 1L-9L parivartana is a textbook Raja-yoga. Moon-Mars yuti in 9H: Moon=4L (kendra), Mars=1L (kendra) — kendra-kendra fusion (not classical Raja-yoga; kendra-trikona is the strict definition; but yoga-quality of kendra-kendra is recognized as a yoga-adhipati signature). Moon-Venus yuti: Moon=4L (kendra), Venus=7L (kendra) — kendra-kendra. Mars-Venus yuti: Mars=1L (kendra), Venus=7L (kendra) — kendra-kendra. Sun in 8H: Sun=5L (trikona) alone with Mercury (3L+6L) and Jupiter (9L+12L) — Sun-Jupiter same-sign association is 5L-9L Raja-yoga, classically the strongest single Raja-yoga.

Conclusion. Two classical Raja-yogas: (a) Mars-Jupiter 1L-9L parivartana, (b) Sun-Jupiter 5L-9L same-sign in 8H. Plus multiple kendra-kendra associations in 9H. Activation: Mars-Jupiter AD (Jul 2026 – Jun 2027) fires both Raja-yogas simultaneously — the chart's primary recognition window.

16.3 Dhana-yogas धन-योग

Condition / state. Dhana-yoga (धन-योग) fires when wealth-significator-houses (2H, 5H, 9H, 11H) and their lords associate.

Possibilities (Layer 1). Multiple recognized variants exist: 2L+11L association (direct wealth), 5L+9L association (poorva-punya wealth), 2L in 11H (income converting to wealth), 11L in 2H (gains becoming family-wealth), and so on. Each variant has its own dasha-firing window.

This-chart specific (Layer 2). Walking the wealth-lord positions: 2L Venus in 9H — 2L-in-9H is itself a dhana-yoga (wealth via dharma); 5L Sun in 8H with 9L Jupiter — Sun-Jupiter 5L-9L association in 8H is the strongest dhana-cum-raja yoga; 11L Saturn in 11H — 11L in own house anchors gain. Mars-Jupiter parivartana further binds the dharma-wealth line into the identity-line. Jupiter's 7th-drishti from 8H lands on 2H — wealth-house blessed by the AK. Five overlapping dhana-yogas total. Firing-windows: any dasha activating Sun, Jupiter, Venus, or Saturn brings money. Strongest single window: Mars-Jupiter AD 2026-2027; second: Rahu-Saturn AD 2037-2040; final climax: Saturn MD 2066+.

Conclusion. Five overlapping Dhana-yogas active. Wealth-formation is structurally robust over the lifetime, peaking in the Saturn periods of Rahu MD and full Saturn MD.

16.4 Neecha-Bhanga check नीच-भंग तपासणी

Condition / state. Neecha-Bhanga (नीच-भंग = debilitation-cancellation) fires when a debilitated planet has one of four rescues: (a) the debilitation-lord is in a kendra from lagna or Moon, (b) the exaltation-lord of the debilitated planet is in a kendra, (c) the debilitated planet is aspected by its exaltation-lord, (d) the debilitated planet is yuti with its debilitation-lord.

Possibilities (Layer 1). Neecha-Bhanga converts a low placement into a high effect — but only when one of the rescues fully fires.

This-chart specific (Layer 2). No graha is at its strict debilitation point in this chart. Sun's debilitation is Libra (Sun is in Scorpio, not debilitated). Mars's debilitation is Cancer (Mars is in Sagittarius, friendly). Mercury's debilitation is Pisces (Mercury is in Scorpio, neutral). Jupiter's debilitation is Capricorn (Jupiter is in Scorpio, friendly). Venus's debilitation is Virgo (Venus is in Sagittarius, neutral). Saturn's debilitation is Aries (Saturn is in Aquarius, own sign). Moon's debilitation is Scorpio (Moon is in Sagittarius, neutral). None debilitated.

Conclusion. Neecha-Bhanga does NOT form because no graha is debilitated. This is a structurally clean chart in the debilitation dimension.

16.5 Vipareeta-Raja-yogas (Harsha / Sarala / Vimala) विपरीत-राज: हर्ष/सरल/विमल

Condition / state. Vipareeta-Raja-yoga fires when a dusthana-lord (6L/8L/12L) sits in another dusthana. Three named variants: Harsha = 6L in 8H or 12H; Sarala = 8L in 6H or 12H; Vimala = 12L in 6H or 8H.

Possibilities (Layer 1). Each variant produces a different reversal-blessing: Harsha — windfall through opponent's defeat, recovery from chronic disease, debt-release; Sarala — longevity-extension, transformation-mastery; Vimala — discipline-from-restriction, hidden-resource-channels.

This-chart specific (Layer 2). Harsha: 6L Mercury sits in 8H — yes, Harsha fires. Combustion qualifies but does not cancel. Sarala: 8L Mars sits in 9H (not 6H or 12H) — Sarala does NOT form. Vimala: 12L Jupiter sits in 8H — yes, Vimala fires. So two Vipareeta-Raja-yogas active: Harsha + Vimala. Both fire most strongly during Mars-Mercury AD 2028-2029 (Harsha activates) and Mars-Jupiter AD 2026-2027 (Vimala activates). Combined effect: chronic-problem reversal in late 2020s, dharma-via-crisis maturation throughout Mars MD.

Conclusion. Two of three Vipareeta-Raja-yogas active: Harsha (qualified by Mercury combustion) and Vimala (full strength via AK Jupiter). Sarala absent.

16.6 Chandra-yogas चंद्र-योग

Condition / state. Chandra-yogas (चंद्र-योग) are Moon-centred combinations. Most important here: Chandra-Mangala (Moon-Mars yuti or mutual aspect), Sunapha (planet in 2nd from Moon), Anapha (planet in 12th from Moon), Durdhura (planets in both 2nd and 12th from Moon), Kemadruma (no planet in 2nd or 12th from Moon, no planet with Moon — produces poverty-misery unless broken).

Possibilities (Layer 1). Chandra-Mangala fuses emotion-finance-courage. Sunapha gives wealth-through-effort. Anapha gives comfort. Durdhura gives both — strong wealth-yoga. Kemadruma is the dreaded poverty-yoga but breaks easily when ANY planet sits with the Moon or in the immediately-adjacent houses.

This-chart specific (Layer 2). Chandra-Mangala: Moon and Mars in same sign Sagittarius 9H — full Chandra-Mangala-yoga active. Strong. Sunapha: 2nd from Moon = Capricorn (10H) — Uranus there (outer planet, doesn't count classically; if counted, weak). Anapha: 12th from Moon = Scorpio (8H) — Sun, Mercury, Jupiter, Pluto stellium — full Anapha-yoga active (multiple planets in 12th from Moon = strong Anapha). Durdhura: requires planets in BOTH 2nd and 12th from Moon — 2nd has only Uranus (generational); strict Durdhura partial / fails on classical-only reading. Kemadruma: Moon is yuti with Mars, Venus, Neptune in same sign 9H — Kemadruma is structurally broken (rescued) by the yuti itself. Gajakesari (Jupiter in kendra from Moon): Moon in 9H, Jupiter in 8H — 12th from Moon is 8H, which is a dusthana from Moon, not a kendra. Gajakesari does NOT form.

Conclusion. Chandra-Mangala (full), Anapha (full), Kemadruma-bhanga (Kemadruma broken). Durdhura partial. Gajakesari fails (Jupiter not in kendra from Moon).

16.7 Gajakesari गज-केसरी

Condition / state. Gajakesari (गज-केसरी) requires Jupiter in a kendra (1, 4, 7, 10) from the Moon — not from lagna. Moon is in 9H Sagittarius; kendras from Moon are 9H itself (1st-from-Moon), 12H (4th-from-Moon), 3H (7th-from-Moon), 6H (10th-from-Moon). Jupiter is in 8H Scorpio.

Possibilities (Layer 1). Gajakesari delivers wisdom, fame, lasting recognition, eloquence, and "elephant-and-lion" combined strength (gentle wisdom + courageous authority) when active.

This-chart specific (Layer 2). Jupiter at 8H Scorpio is the 12th-from-Moon (9H Sagittarius) — 12th is a dusthana from Moon, not a kendra. Gajakesari does NOT form. This is a structural absence. The chart compensates via the Mars-Jupiter parivartana and via Jupiter's Atma-Karaka status — but the specific Gajakesari-flavour (gentle eloquence, instant-recognition, fame-by-presence) is missing. Dharmesh's wisdom-display is research-led and slow-revealed rather than instantly-magnetic. The absence is honest — many otherwise-strong charts lack Gajakesari.

Conclusion. Gajakesari fails. Compensating yogas (Mars-Jupiter parivartana, Atma-Karaka Jupiter Karakamsa-Pisces) provide alternative wisdom-channels.

16.8 Sannyasa-yoga and the renunciate signatures संन्यास-योग

Condition / state. Sannyasa-yoga (संन्यास-योग) fires under several classical rules; the most-cited: (a) four or more planets in one house (especially in a sign of Saturn or Jupiter) often produces a renunciate temperament; (b) Saturn aspecting or yuti with a heavily-loaded house pushes the native toward formal renunciation in some readings; (c) Ketu on lagna with strong 9H or 12H activity indicates spiritual investigative pull.

Possibilities (Layer 1). Sannyasa-yoga rarely produces literal monastic renunciation in modern charts; it more commonly indicates a renunciate temperament — the native who carries deep philosophical detachment, low attachment to material display, and a strong inward-pull-toward-meaning even while functioning in worldly roles.

This-chart specific (Layer 2). The chart has multiple Sannyasa-indicators: (a) 4-graha stellium in 8H Scorpio (Saturn aspecting via 10th-drishti from own-sign), (b) 4-graha stellium in 9H Sagittarius (Jupiter's sign, including Jupiter as 9L is in 8H but the Mars-Jupiter parivartana ties them), (c) Ketu on lagna with 9th-drishti landing on 9H stellium, (d) Atma-Karaka Jupiter in 8H with Karakamsa-Pisces rescue. Combined, the renunciate-temperament signature is unusually strong — but the literal-monastic-life signature requires more (e.g., 4L-12H or 1L in 12H). Dharmesh carries the temperament without the literal calling. The renunciate pull surfaces strongest during Jupiter MD (2050 onwards) when AK-Karakamsa-Pisces matures.

Conclusion. Sannyasa-yoga active at the temperament level, not at the formal-life-renunciation level. Investigation-led philosophical detachment, not monastic withdrawal.

16.10 Conclusion — yoga-tally योग-संख्या

Condition / state. Compressed tally of all the above.

Possibilities (Layer 1). A chart's yoga-architecture predicts its lifetime trajectory. Many active yogas concentrated in specific dasha-windows produce sequential life-peaks; sparse yogas produce a flatter life-arc. Yogas can compensate for weak placements and vice versa.

This-chart specific (Layer 2). The active yoga-list reads: Sasa-partial + Mars-Jupiter parivartana + Sun-Jupiter Raja-yoga + 5 Dhana-yogas + Harsha + Vimala + Chandra-Mangala + Anapha + Kemadruma-bhanga + Sannyasa-temperament. Absent: Ruchaka, Bhadra, Hamsa, Malavya, Sarala, Gajakesari, Neecha-Bhanga (no need). The single most-important active yoga is the Mars-Jupiter parivartana — it is the chart's hidden binding agent fusing 1H-8H-9H-12H lordships and converting the 8H stellium from "congestion" to "integration." The single most-important absence is Gajakesari — which honestly removes the instant-fame signature this chart would otherwise carry.

Conclusion. The chart's yoga-architecture is dharma-and-discipline weighted: research-mastery, slow-wealth-accumulation, late-life recognition. Not a flash-fame chart; a deep-mastery chart.

Learning Note — Yoga-firing depends on dasha / योग-फळ दशेवर अवलंबून

Rule: A yoga exists structurally from birth, but it delivers its effect only when the participating planets are activated by dasha or major transit. A chart with ten yogas all weighted to one planet delivers all ten in that planet's dasha; a chart with yogas distributed across many planets delivers in sequence.

Applied: Mars-Jupiter parivartana sits dormant until Mars-Jupiter AD (Jul 2026 – Jun 2027) when both lords are activated simultaneously. This 11-month window is therefore the chart's single biggest yoga-firing — Raja-yoga + parivartana + Vimala all activate together. The window deserves remedy-preparation, intention-setting, and major life-decision alignment.
