Doshas in this Chart या कुंडलीतील दोष

A dosha (दोष — literally "fault" or "blemish") is an afflictive configuration — a structural fault-line in the chart that pulls one life-domain off its smooth trajectory. The Vedic-Jyotish doshas form a distinct category from yogas: where a yoga is a positive-union producing predictable benefit, a dosha is a negative-configuration producing predictable strain unless a classical-cancellation (bhanga — भंग) rescues it. Many doshas are loudly-marketed by predatory astrologers — "your chart has Kaal-Sarp, you need a ₹50,000 puja immediately" — and many are over-stated, under-checked, or sold without disclosing the standard cancellation-rules. A competent consultant always checks each named dosha against (a) the firing-criterion, (b) the standard bhanga-rules, (c) the chart-specific evidence — and delivers an auditable verdict per dosha. This chapter walks each major dosha against Dharmesh's chart in the full five-block teaching scaffold: condition, all classical possibilities, astrologer-to-client narrative, hard verdict, and the Marathi mirror. Categories covered: Manglik (Mangal-dosha), Kaal-Sarp (and its 12 named house-variants), current Sade-Sati phase check, Pitru-dosha, Shrapit-dosha, Pisaca-dosha, Vish/Grahan/Kemadruma bundle, Ganda-Mool/Daridra/Guru-Chandala bundle, and the honest tally with audit-trail. The ethical standard for this chapter is the audit-trail: every verdict must be supported by a placement-fact, every bhanga-check must be shown, and no elaborate remedy is recommended unless the dosha is structurally active without bhanga.

दोष (शब्दशः "त्रुटी" वा "कलंक") = कुंडलीतली दोष-रेषा जी एक जीवन-क्षेत्राला त्याच्या सहज-मार्गावरून ढकलते. वैदिक-ज्योतिषाचे दोष योगांहून वेगळा वर्ग: योग सकारात्मक-संयोग अंदाजनीय लाभ देणारा, दोष नकारात्मक-रचना अंदाजनीय ताण देणारा जोपर्यंत शास्त्रीय रद्द (भंग) त्याला वाचवत नाही. अनेक दोष शोषक-ज्योतिषांकडून मोठ्याने विकले — "तुझ्या कुंडलीत काल-सर्प, ₹५०,००० पूजा तातडीने हवी" — आणि अनेक अति-उच्चारित, कमी-तपासलेले, मानक रद्द-नियम न-उघड करता विकले. सक्षम सल्लागार नेहमी प्रत्येक नामांकित दोष तपासतो: (अ) प्रज्वलन-निकष, (ब) मानक भंग-नियम, (क) कुंडली-विशिष्ट पुरावा — आणि प्रत्येक दोषासाठी तपासणीयोग्य निष्कर्ष देतो. हा अध्याय धर्मेशच्या कुंडलीविरुद्ध प्रत्येक मोठा दोष पूर्ण ५-खंडी-स्वरूपात. वर्ग: मांगलिक, काल-सर्प (व १२ नामांकित घर-प्रकार), सध्याची साढेसाती तपासणी, पितृ-दोष, शापित-दोष, पिशाच-दोष, विष/ग्रहण/केमद्रुम, गंड-मूल/दरिद्र/गुरू-चांडाल, व प्रामाणिक मोजणी तपासणी-मार्गासह. नैतिक मानक: तपासणी-मार्ग — प्रत्येक निष्कर्षाला स्थान-तथ्याचा आधार, प्रत्येक भंग-तपासणी दृश्य, आणि भंगाशिवाय संरचनेने सक्रिय दोष असल्याशिवाय विस्तृत उपाय नको.

17.1 What a dosha is — and what it is not दोष म्हणजे काय आणि काय नाही

Condition / state. A dosha is NOT a curse and NOT a permanent verdict. It is a structural predisposition with named cancellation-rules (bhanga). Reading a dosha without checking its bhanga is malpractice. Every dosha must be (a) named, (b) confirmed by placement-criteria, (c) checked against each classical bhanga-rule, (d) delivered as either active (firing without bhanga), partial (firing with one-or-more bhangas reducing severity), or cancelled (firing-criteria met but bhanga overrides completely). The dosha section of any chart-reading is a defensive check — designed to identify what protects against affliction-marketing and what genuinely needs simple-classical attention.

All classical possibilities (Layer 1). The major doshas examined here: Manglik (Mangal-dosha), Kaal-Sarp, Sade-Sati, Pitru, Shrapit, Pisaca, Vish, Grahan, Kemadruma, Ganda-Mool, Daridra, Guru-Chandala. Each has specific firing-criteria and cancellation-rules. A dosha that fires without bhanga produces a specific life-domain weight; a dosha that has bhanga is structurally neutralized. Doshas operate on a spectrum: some are real and require simple-classical remedies (pitru-tarpan, occasional rituals at dasha-openings); some are real but heavily over-marketed (Kaal-Sarp with predatory pricing); some are folk-tradition additions not in primary Parashari literature (Shrapit, Pisaca — medieval-school additions); some are simply non-applicable (Sade-Sati when Saturn is not transiting the relevant signs). The honest tally distinguishes all four categories. Predatory consultants conflate them — selling Sade-Sati pujas to clients whose Sade-Sati is years away, selling Kaal-Sarp remedies without naming the three structural-bhangas. The ethical bar is the audit-trail: every claim shown, every check visible, every recommendation justified.

Astrologer-to-client narrative (Layer 2). "Dharmesh, before walking each dosha individually, I need you to understand what a dosha really is. A dosha is not a curse and not a permanent verdict on your life. It is a structural fault-line that produces predictable strain in a specific life-domain IF and only IF there is no cancellation rescuing it. Every dosha has cancellation-rules in the classical texts. Reading a dosha without checking its bhanga is malpractice. Some astrologers will tell you 'your chart has Kaal-Sarp dosha, you need an immediate puja worth fifty thousand rupees' — and they will NOT tell you about the three structural-bhangas in your chart that already contain that dosha. That is a sales-pitch, not a reading. The ethical standard is auditable: every dosha I name, I will (a) cite the classical firing-criterion, (b) check it against your specific placements, (c) name and check each bhanga-rule, (d) land the verdict as active, partial, or cancelled. You should be able to follow my logic at every step. If I cannot show you the audit-trail, you should not pay for my opinion. Walking through your chart, eight of the eleven dosha-categories I check come out clean — either absent or broken by bhanga. Three categories show structural-presence, but all three carry classical bhanga-rescues that contain them. The recommended response is awareness plus simple classical rituals at specific dasha-openings, not elaborate annual pujas, not expensive yantra-gemstone stacks. Let me walk each one."

Hard verdict / forecast. A dosha is a structural predisposition with named cancellation-rules, not a curse. Reading without bhanga-check is malpractice. This chart's dosha-section uses the audit-trail standard: every dosha named, every bhanga-rule checked, every verdict supported by placement-fact. No elaborate remedy recommended unless dosha is structurally active without bhanga.

स्थिती. दोष = शाप नाही, कायम-निष्कर्ष नाही. नामांकित रद्द-नियमांसह (भंग) रचनात्मक पूर्व-प्रवृत्ती. भंग न-तपासता दोष-वाचन = गैर-व्यवहार. प्रत्येक दोष: (अ) नाव द्या, (ब) स्थान-निकषाने पुष्टी, (क) प्रत्येक शास्त्रीय भंग-नियम तपासा, (ड) सक्रिय (भंगाशिवाय), आंशिक (एक-वा-अधिक भंग तीव्रता कमी), वा रद्द (निकष पूर्ण पण भंग पूर्णतः रद्द) म्हणून निष्कर्ष. कोणत्याही कुंडली-वाचनातला दोष-विभाग संरक्षण-तपासणी — दोष-विपणनाविरुद्ध काय संरक्षण करते व काय खरोखर साधे-शास्त्रीय लक्ष आवश्यक. शास्त्रीय शक्यता. मोठे दोष: मांगलिक, काल-सर्प, साढेसाती, पितृ, शापित, पिशाच, विष, ग्रहण, केमद्रुम, गंड-मूल, दरिद्र, गुरू-चांडाल. प्रत्येकाचे विशिष्ट निकष व रद्द-नियम. भंगाशिवाय प्रज्वलित = जीवन-क्षेत्र-वजन; भंगासह = संरचनेने तटस्थ. दोष श्रेणी: काही खरे व साधे-शास्त्रीय उपाय मागतात; काही खरे पण जड-विपणन; काही लोक-परंपरा-भर (शापित, पिशाच — मध्ययुगीन); काही फक्त लागू नाहीत (शनी संबंधित राशींत न-गोचर असता साढेसाती). प्रामाणिक मोजणी चारही श्रेणी वेगळ्या ठेवते. शोषक-सल्लागार त्यांना एकत्र मिसळतात — कुंडल्यांना वर्षे-दूर साढेसाती-पूजा विकतात, कुंडलीत तीन रचनात्मक-भंग असूनही काल-सर्प-उपाय विकतात. नैतिक मानक: तपासणी-मार्ग. ज्योतिषी-ग्राहक-संवाद. "धर्मेश, दोष म्हणजे काय हे समजून घे. दोष = शाप नव्हे, जीवनावर कायम-निष्कर्ष नव्हे. रचनात्मक दोष-रेषा जी रद्द-शिवाय विशिष्ट क्षेत्रात अंदाजनीय ताण देते. प्रत्येक दोषाचे रद्द-नियम शास्त्रीय ग्रंथांत. भंग न-तपासता दोष-वाचन गैर-व्यवहार. काही ज्योतिषी सांगतील 'तुझ्या कुंडलीत काल-सर्प, तातडीने ५०,००० पूजा' — आणि तुझ्या कुंडलीत तो दोष आधीच रोखणारे तीन रचनात्मक-भंग सांगणार नाहीत. ती विक्री-पिच, वाचन नव्हे. नैतिक मानक तपासणीयोग्य: प्रत्येक दोष मी नाव देतो, (अ) शास्त्रीय निकष उद्धृत, (ब) तुझ्या स्थानांविरुद्ध तपासणी, (क) प्रत्येक भंग-नियम नाव व तपासणी, (ड) सक्रिय/आंशिक/रद्द निष्कर्ष. तू प्रत्येक पायरीवर माझे तर्क पाळू शकशील. तपासणी-मार्ग दाखवू शकत नसेन तर माझ्या मतासाठी पैसा देऊ नकोस. तुझ्या कुंडलीतून जाताना, ११ दोष-वर्गांपैकी आठ स्वच्छ — एकतर अनुपस्थित वा भंगाने रद्द. तीन वर्ग रचनात्मक-उपस्थिती दाखवतात, पण तिनींना शास्त्रीय भंग-रक्षा रोखत. शिफारस: जागरूकता + विशिष्ट दशा-उघडीला साधे-शास्त्रीय विधी; विस्तृत वार्षिक-पूजा नाही, महाग रत्न-यंत्र-ढीग नाही. एक-एक चालू." निष्कर्ष. दोष = नामांकित रद्द-नियमांसह रचनात्मक पूर्व-प्रवृत्ती, शाप नव्हे. भंग न-तपासता वाचन = गैर-व्यवहार. ह्या कुंडलीचा दोष-विभाग तपासणी-मार्ग-मानक: प्रत्येक दोष नाव, प्रत्येक भंग तपासणी, प्रत्येक निष्कर्ष स्थान-तथ्याने आधारित. भंगाशिवाय संरचनेने सक्रिय दोष असल्याशिवाय विस्तृत उपाय नको.

17.2 Manglik (Mangal-dosha) मांगलिक

Condition / state. Mangal-dosha (मंगळ-दोष / Manglik) fires when Mars sits in 1H, 2H, 4H, 7H, 8H, or 12H from the lagna (or, in many schools, also from the Moon and from Venus). The classical six-house list: 1, 2, 4, 7, 8, 12. Some strict-Tamil schools add 9H as a semi-Manglik position (minority view; not standard). Mars in this chart is at 9H Sagittarius Mula Pada 1.

All classical possibilities (Layer 1). Manglik produces marriage-line strain — delayed marriage, partner-conflict, or in severe-Manglik cases longevity-watch on the spouse. The dosha has six classical bhanga (cancellation) rules: (a) Mars in own-sign (Aries/Scorpio) or exaltation-sign (Capricorn) cancels; (b) age-30+ Manglik naturally weakens (Manglik weakest after 28-30 years per most schools); (c) partner is also Manglik (Manglik-Manglik match cancels mutually); (d) Jupiter aspects 7H or aspects Mars; (e) Mars in friend's-sign with benefic aspect; (f) Mars yuti with Jupiter. Any one full bhanga reduces Manglik to non-effective; multiple bhangas overdetermine the cancellation. Modern reading: Manglik is real but heavily over-applied; perhaps 50%+ of charts technically qualify as Manglik under the 8-house rule, but most have one or more bhangas operating, so the dosha is non-issue in the majority of marriages.

Astrologer-to-client narrative (Layer 2). "Dharmesh, Mangal-dosha or Manglik is the most-marketed dosha in modern North-Indian astrology. Let me check it for you. The classical firing-criterion: Mars in 1H, 2H, 4H, 7H, 8H, or 12H from your lagna. Your Mars is in 9H. Mars is NOT in any of the six Manglik-firing houses. The standard 8-house Manglik rule (counting from lagna, from Moon, from Venus) checked against your chart: Mars in 9H from lagna — not Manglik. Mars in 1H from Moon (which is in 9H) — that would be Manglik counting from Moon. So some schools would flag a Moon-Manglik reading. But the standard reading uses lagna as the primary reference; from lagna your Mars is clean. Even if we accept a partial Moon-Manglik reading, the standard bhangas operate: (a) Mars is in friendly sign Sagittarius (Mars-Jupiter friendship) — partial bhanga; (b) you'll be past 30 when marriage stabilizes — natural bhanga; (c) Mars-Jupiter parivartana means Jupiter's sign houses Mars + Jupiter's influence operates structurally on Mars through the sign-exchange — equivalent to Jupiter-aspect-on-Mars bhanga. So even under the conservative reading that counts the Moon-Manglik partial signature, multiple bhangas cancel it. Manglik does NOT form for your chart. No Manglik-remedy needed. Any marriage-line strain in your chart comes from Rahu-in-7H (karmic-marriage signature) and Venus-as-double-marak (2L+7L), not from Mangal-dosha. A consultant selling Manglik-remedies for your chart is selling a non-existent dosha."

Hard verdict / forecast. Manglik does NOT form for this chart. Mars in 9H (not in the six Manglik-firing houses from lagna). Even if Moon-Manglik partial signature is counted by strict schools, multiple bhangas operate: Mars-Jupiter friendship (friendly sign), age-30+ window, Mars-Jupiter parivartana (Jupiter-equivalent-aspect via sign-exchange). No Manglik-remedy needed. Marriage-line concerns in this chart trace to Rahu-7H karmic signature and Venus-double-marak, NOT to Mangal-dosha.

स्थिती. मंगळ-दोष (मांगलिक) = मंगळ १/२/४/७/८/१२ मध्ये लग्नापासून (अनेक शाखांत चंद्र व शुक्रापासूनही). शास्त्रीय सहा घर-यादी. काही कडक-तमिळ शाखा ९ अर्ध-मांगलिक मानतात (अल्पमत; मानक नाही). ह्या कुंडलीत मंगळ ९ धनू मूळ पाद १. शास्त्रीय शक्यता. मांगलिक विवाह-रेषेला ताण — विलंबित विवाह, जोडीदार-संघर्ष, गंभीर-मांगलिक मध्ये जोडीदार-आयुष्य-संकेत. सहा शास्त्रीय भंग: (अ) स्व-राशी (मेष/वृश्चिक) वा उच्च (मकर) रद्द; (ब) ३०+ वय नैसर्गिक दुर्बल; (क) जोडीदारही मांगलिक (परस्पर रद्द); (ड) गुरू ७व्यावर वा मंगळावर दृष्टी; (इ) मित्र-राशी मंगळ शुभ-दृष्टीसह; (फ) मंगळ-गुरू युती. एक पूर्ण भंग दोष कमी; एकाधिक रद्द जादा-निश्चित. आधुनिक: मांगलिक खरे पण जड-अति-लागू; ५०%+ कुंडल्या तांत्रिकदृष्ट्या ८-घर नियमाने पात्र, बहुतेकांत भंग चालत, बहुतेक विवाहांत प्रश्न नाही. ज्योतिषी-ग्राहक-संवाद. "धर्मेश, मांगलिक आधुनिक उत्तर-भारतीय ज्योतिषातला सर्वाधिक-विकला-जाणारा दोष. तपासू. निकष: मंगळ १/२/४/७/८/१२ लग्नापासून. तुझा मंगळ ९. मांगलिक प्रज्वलित-घरांत नाही. मानक ८-घर नियम (लग्न-चंद्र-शुक्र पासून): लग्नापासून ९ = मांगलिक नाही. चंद्रापासून (९ चंद्र) १ मंगळ — काही शाखा चंद्र-मांगलिक-आंशिक. पण मानक वाचन लग्न-प्राथमिक; लग्नापासून स्वच्छ. आंशिक चंद्र-मांगलिक स्वीकारले तरी भंग चालतात: (अ) मंगळ मित्र-राशी धनू (मंगळ-गुरू मित्रता) — आंशिक भंग; (ब) ३०+ नंतर विवाह स्थिर — नैसर्गिक भंग; (क) मंगळ-गुरू परिवर्तन = गुरू-राशी मंगळ + गुरू-प्रभाव रचनेने मंगळावर राशी-अदलाबदलीने = गुरू-दृष्टी-समतुल्य भंग. एकाधिक भंग रद्द. मांगलिक तयार नाही. उपाय नको. विवाह-रेषा-ताण राहू-७ (कर्मिक) व शुक्र-दुहेरी-मारक (२L+७L) मधून, मंगळ-दोषातून नव्हे. तुझ्या कुंडलीसाठी मांगलिक-उपाय विकणारा अस्तित्वहीन दोष विकतो." निष्कर्ष. मांगलिक तयार नाही. मंगळ ९ (सहा प्रज्वलन-घरांत नाही). आंशिक चंद्र-मांगलिक स्वीकारले तरी भंग: मंगळ-गुरू मित्रता, ३०+ वय, मंगळ-गुरू परिवर्तन. उपाय नको. विवाह-काळजी राहू-७ व शुक्र-मारक मधून, मंगळ-दोष नव्हे.

17.3 Kaal-Sarp and Kaal-Amrit (the nodal-axis dosha) काल-सर्प व काल-अमृत

Condition / state. Kaal-Sarp-dosha (काल-सर्प-दोष) fires when all seven classical planets (Sun through Saturn) sit on one side of the Rahu-Ketu axis. Kaal-Amrit is the same configuration but with all seven on the Ketu-to-Rahu side (variant naming; some schools distinguish the two by which node is in front of the planetary span). There are 12 named house-variants based on which houses the Rahu-Ketu axis occupies: Anant (Ra 1H/Ke 7H), Kulik (Ra 2H/Ke 8H), Vasuki (Ra 3H/Ke 9H), Shankhapala (Ra 4H/Ke 10H), Padma (Ra 5H/Ke 11H), Mahapadma (Ra 6H/Ke 12H), Takshaka (Ra 7H/Ke 1H), Karkotaka (Ra 8H/Ke 2H), Shankhanad (Ra 9H/Ke 3H), Ghatak (Ra 10H/Ke 4H), Vishdhar (Ra 11H/Ke 5H), Sheshnag (Ra 12H/Ke 6H). The 12 named-variant categorization is the classical taxonomy. Rahu in this chart at 7H Libra, Ketu at 1H Aries — this is the Takshaka Kaal-Sarp variant per strict classification (Ra 7H/Ke 1H). Note: some commentators name the same configuration "Padma" when counted from Rahu's side; the Brihat-Parashara mainstream uses Takshaka for 7H Rahu / 1H Ketu.

All classical possibilities (Layer 1). Kaal-Sarp produces life-stage karmic compression — the native carries past-life unfinished business as a major life-theme; sense of "swimming against the current" for long periods; sudden-event flavour to major life-transitions. The 12 named variants each carry distinct flavour: Takshaka (Ra 7H/Ke 1H) = partnership-axis karmic compression, marriage and significant alliances reactivate past-life arcs, identity-self-question reactivates spiritual past-life work. Important reality-check: Kaal-Sarp-dosha is not in Parashara's primary list. Brihat-Parashara-Hora-Shastra does not name Kaal-Sarp explicitly; the dosha is developed in later medieval and modern commentary literature. Some strict-Parashari consultants do not weight it at all; they read Rahu-Ketu placements through standard nodal-aspect rules without naming Kaal-Sarp as a separate category. Other consultants (the majority of modern Indian astrology) accept Kaal-Sarp as a real category. The honest reading: name it, check the configuration, name the 12-variant, identify the bhanga-rescues. Standard Kaal-Sarp bhangas: (a) any classical planet on the wrong side of the axis (which would break the "all-seven-on-one-side" criterion); (b) yoga-rescues like parivartanas or strong Mahapurusha-yogas anchoring the chart structurally; (c) Atma-Karaka or Jupiter in the planetary span providing dharma-protection; (d) strong dispositor of the nodal axis (which gives the nodes themselves dignity).
