Chapter 17

Doshas in this Chart या कुंडलीतील दोष

A dosha (दोष) is an afflictive configuration — a "fault-line" in the chart that pulls one life-domain off its smooth trajectory. Many doshas are loudly-marketed by predatory astrologers; many are over-stated; many have classical cancellations that competent consultants must check before delivering any verdict. This chapter checks each major dosha against Dharmesh's chart, walks the rescue-rules, and lands an honest verdict per dosha.

17.1 What a dosha is — and what it is not दोष म्हणजे काय

Condition / state. A dosha is not a curse and not a permanent verdict. It is a structural predisposition with named cancellation-rules (bhanga / भंग). Reading a dosha without checking its bhanga is malpractice. Every dosha must be (a) named, (b) confirmed by placement-criteria, (c) checked against bhanga, (d) delivered as either active, partial, or cancelled.

Possibilities (Layer 1). The major doshas examined here: Manglik (Mangal-dosha), Kaal-Sarp, Sade-Sati, Pitru, Shrapit, Pisaca, Vish, Grahan, Kemadruma, Ganda-Mool, Daridra, Guru-Chandala. Each has specific firing-criteria and cancellation-rules. A dosha that fires without bhanga produces a specific life-domain weight; a dosha that has bhanga is structurally neutralized and should not be marketed as remedy-requiring.

This-chart specific (Layer 2). The honest tally for this chart involves several partial-or-cancelled doshas and one or two active ones. The chapter walks each, names the classical rule, applies it, checks the bhanga, lands the verdict.

Conclusion. The dosha-section is read as a defensive check, not as a sales pitch. No remedy is recommended unless the dosha is structurally active without bhanga.

17.2 Manglik (Mangal-dosha) मांगलिक

Condition / state. Mangal-dosha fires when Mars sits in 1H, 2H, 4H, 7H, 8H, or 12H (the classical 8-house rule includes 1, 2, 4, 7, 8, 12 — six houses). Mars in this chart is at 9H Sagittarius Mula Pada 1.

Possibilities (Layer 1). Manglik produces marriage-line strain — delayed marriage, partner-conflict, or in severe-Manglik cases longevity-watch on the spouse. The dosha has six classical bhangas: (a) Mars in own-sign or exaltation cancels, (b) age-30+ Manglik naturally weakens, (c) partner is also Manglik (Manglik-Manglik match cancels), (d) Jupiter aspects 7H, (e) Mars in friend's-sign with benefic aspect, (f) Mars yuti with Jupiter.

This-chart specific (Layer 2). Mars sits in 9H — not in any of the six Manglik-firing houses. Manglik does NOT form. However, classical schools differ: a small minority of conservative Tamil schools count 9H Mars as semi-Manglik because of Mars's aggressive nature near marriage-significators. The vast majority of mainstream Parashari + modern North-Indian schools do not count 9H Mars as Manglik. Furthermore, even if a semi-Manglik reading were applied, the Mars-Jupiter parivartana acts as bhanga-equivalent (Mars sitting in Jupiter's sign with mutual lord-exchange = Jupiter's influence on Mars = aspect-equivalent rescue).

Conclusion. Manglik does NOT form. No Manglik-remedy needed. Marriage-line strain in this chart comes from Rahu-in-7H and Venus-as-double-marak, not from Mangal-dosha.

17.3 Kaal-Sarp and Kaal-Amrit काल-सर्प / काल-अमृत

Condition / state. Kaal-Sarp-dosha (काल-सर्प-दोष) fires when all seven classical planets (Sun through Saturn) sit on one side of the Rahu-Ketu axis. Kaal-Amrit is the same configuration but with all seven on the Ketu-to-Rahu side (variant naming).

Possibilities (Layer 1). Kaal-Sarp produces life-stage karmic compression — the native carries past-life unfinished business as a major life-theme; sense of "swimming against the current" for long periods; sudden-event flavour to major life-transitions. There are 12 named variants by the houses Rahu-Ketu occupy. The dosha is heavily over-marketed; classical Parashari does not name it as a primary dosha and many schools regard it as a folk-tradition addition.

This-chart specific (Layer 2). Rahu is at 00°34′ Libra (7H); Ketu is at 00°34′ Aries (1H). Check planet positions: Sun 8H Scorpio, Moon 9H Sagittarius, Mars 9H Sagittarius, Mercury 8H Scorpio, Jupiter 8H Scorpio, Venus 9H Sagittarius, Saturn 11H Aquarius. From Ketu (1H Aries) moving forward through the zodiac: Taurus, Gemini, Cancer, Leo, Virgo, Libra (Rahu) — that's the "first half" 1H→7H. From Rahu (7H Libra) moving forward: Scorpio (Sun, Me, Ju in 8H), Sagittarius (Mo, Ma, Ve in 9H), Capricorn (10H), Aquarius (Saturn in 11H), Pisces (12H) — back to Aries (Ketu). All seven classical planets sit in the 8H–11H range — that is the "second half" between Rahu (7H) and Ketu (1H) going forward through Scorpio-to-Pisces. Yes — Kaal-Sarp-dosha forms, with all seven planets between Rahu (7H) and Ketu (1H) on the going-forward-direction side. The specific variant is Padma Kaal-Sarp (Rahu in 7H, Ketu in 1H — the partnership-axis variant).

Conclusion. Padma Kaal-Sarp-dosha is structurally active. However, three bhanga-rescues operate: (a) Saturn in own-sign at the far end of the planetary span anchors the dosha rather than letting it spin loose, (b) Mars-Jupiter parivartana provides yoga-equivalent rescue, (c) Jupiter as Atma-Karaka inside the span carries dharma-protection. Effect: karmic-spine-of-life along partnership-axis (matches the Rahu-7H reading), but the dosha is structurally contained, not loose.

17.4 Sade-Sati current phase साढेसाती सध्याचा टप्पा

Condition / state. Sade-Sati (साढेसाती) = Saturn's 7.5-year transit over the 12th, 1st, and 2nd from the natal Moon. Natal Moon: 17°53′ Sagittarius (9H). Saturn transit phases: Phase 1 (Saturn in 12th-from-Moon = Scorpio), Phase 2 (Saturn in Moon's own sign Sagittarius), Phase 3 (Saturn in 2nd-from-Moon = Capricorn). Current date: 15 May 2026. Saturn is currently transiting Pisces (since March 2025; will move to Aries in March 2028). 12th from Moon-sign Sagittarius = Scorpio. 1st-from-Moon = Sagittarius. 2nd-from-Moon = Capricorn. Saturn in Pisces is 4th-from-natal-Moon — NOT in any of the three Sade-Sati phases.

Possibilities (Layer 1). Sade-Sati delivers a 7.5-year structural-test cycle. Phase 1 strains the family / pre-self; Phase 2 strains the self / identity / health; Phase 3 strains the wealth / family-of-birth. Most natives experience real-but-survivable difficulty; some experience life-restructuring; very few experience the dreaded version. Bhanga-rules: Saturn in own-sign in transit, Saturn yuti with Jupiter in transit, lagna-lord in kendra, natal Saturn strong.

This-chart specific (Layer 2). Past Sade-Sati: Saturn in Scorpio (Phase 1) Nov 2014 → Jan 2017 + Nov 2017 → Jan 2020 (with retrograde Sagittarius gaps); Saturn in Sagittarius (Phase 2) Jan 2020 → Apr 2022; Saturn in Capricorn (Phase 3) Apr 2022 → Jan 2023 + Jul 2022 → Jan 2023. So Dharmesh's previous Sade-Sati cycle ran approximately Nov 2014 → Jan 2023 (with retrograde-extension windows). It is complete. The current Saturn transit (Pisces since March 2025) is 4th-from-Moon — Ardha-Ashtama-Shani (अर्ध-अष्टम-शनी) for some schools, but NOT Sade-Sati. The next Sade-Sati cycle: Saturn enters Scorpio again March 2041 — that's the next Phase 1. Dharmesh's current life is post-Sade-Sati for the next 15 years.

Conclusion. Sade-Sati is NOT active in May 2026. The previous cycle (2014-2023) was completed. The next cycle begins March 2041. Current Saturn transit in Pisces is unrelated to Sade-Sati.

17.5 Pitru-dosha पितृ-दोष

Condition / state. Pitru-dosha (पितृ-दोष) fires under multiple classical rules: (a) Sun in 9H (father-house) afflicted; (b) Sun yuti with Rahu/Ketu/Saturn anywhere; (c) 9L weak and afflicted; (d) Rahu or Ketu in 9H. Many schools also include Sun in 8H with malefic aspect as a Pitru-signature.

Possibilities (Layer 1). Pitru-dosha indicates ancestral-line karmic-weight surfacing in the native's life — typically father-line health/distance issues, paternal-grandfather lineage carrying a karmic-debt, or rituals owed to ancestors. The dosha is real in classical Jyotish but its remedies (pitru-tarpan, Kashi pilgrimage, etc.) are simple and inexpensive — beware predatory consultants charging large sums.

This-chart specific (Layer 2). Sun is in 8H Scorpio — a dusthana — with Mercury combust nearby and Saturn casting 10th-drishti. Sun is not in 9H, but Sun is afflicted by being in dusthana under Saturn's full drishti. This produces a partial Pitru-dosha signature via the Sun-in-8H + Saturn-10th-drishti combination, indicating father-line carries serious / health-marked / structural-distance dimension. 9L Jupiter sits in 8H — also a dusthana — adding a second Pitru-line factor. No Rahu/Ketu in 9H itself. Net verdict: partial Pitru-dosha present (two of four classical criteria met), structurally moderate not severe.

Conclusion. Partial Pitru-dosha active. Simple classical remedies (pitru-paksha tarpan, occasional Gaya / Kashi visit) sufficient; no elaborate ritual sales pitch warranted.

17.6 Shrapit-dosha (Saturn + Rahu/Ketu yuti) शापित-दोष

Condition / state. Shrapit-dosha (शापित-दोष = "cursed-dosha") fires when Saturn is yuti or in tight mutual-aspect with Rahu or Ketu — particularly in dusthanas (6/8/12) or in the kendras.

Possibilities (Layer 1). Shrapit produces a karmic-curse signature — chronic-frustration in a specific life-domain, unexplained obstacles, sense of being "blocked" despite effort. The dosha was developed in later medieval Indian-classical texts and is not in Parashara's primary list; many strict-Parashari consultants do not weight it.

This-chart specific (Layer 2). Saturn is in 11H Aquarius. Rahu is in 7H Libra. Ketu is in 1H Aries. Saturn aspects 1H Ketu by 3rd-drishti (full strength in Parashari). This is a Saturn-Ketu drishti-relationship, NOT a yuti. Some Shrapit-readings count tight mutual aspect — Ketu's 7th-drishti from 1H lands on 7H Rahu (not Saturn), and Saturn's 3rd-drishti from 11H lands on 1H Ketu — so there is a one-way drishti from Saturn to Ketu but not mutual. Rahu's 5th-drishti from 7H lands on 11H Saturn — that gives the mutual Saturn-Rahu drishti relationship. Partial Shrapit signature via Saturn-Rahu mutual drishti through 5th-and-7th positions across the chart. Not full Shrapit (no yuti), but partial.

Conclusion. Partial Shrapit via Saturn-Rahu mutual drishti. Strict-Parashari schools do not weight this; medieval-schools do. Effect (if accepted): occasional sense-of-blocked-effort in network/gains-line during Rahu-Saturn AD 2037-2040.

17.7 Pisaca-dosha (Mercury + Rahu yuti) पिशाच-दोष

Condition / state. Pisaca-dosha (पिशाच-दोष = "ghost-disturbance dosha") fires when Mercury is yuti with Rahu in any house — particularly in dusthanas or yuti-yoga producing mental-disturbance signatures.

Possibilities (Layer 1). Pisaca produces anxiety, mental-restlessness, intrusive-thought patterns, sleep disturbance, in extreme readings the "spirit-influence" interpretation of older folk-tradition. Modern consultants read it as a psychological-symptomatology signature.

This-chart specific (Layer 2). Mercury sits in 8H Scorpio. Rahu sits in 7H Libra. They are in adjacent signs — NOT in the same sign — so they are NOT in yuti. Mercury and Rahu have no direct aspect on each other either (Mercury aspects only 7th from itself = 2H Taurus; Rahu's 5/9 aspects don't reach Mercury). Pisaca-dosha does NOT form.

Conclusion. Pisaca-dosha absent. No mental-disturbance signature from Me-Ra combination. (Mental-line caveats in this chart come from combust-Mercury and Capricorn-amsha Moon, but those are different signatures, not Pisaca.)

17.8 Vish / Grahan / Kemadruma विष / ग्रहण / केमद्रुम

Condition / state. Three dosha-checks bundled. Vish-yoga (विष-योग): Moon yuti with Saturn — produces emotional-heaviness, chronic-melancholy. Grahan-dosha (ग्रहण-दोष): Sun or Moon yuti with Rahu or Ketu — produces eclipse-flavour to that luminary's significations. Kemadruma (केमद्रुम): no planet on either side of Moon, no planet with Moon — produces poverty-misery if not broken.

Possibilities (Layer 1). Each has standard bhangas: Vish breaks if Jupiter aspects; Grahan breaks if Jupiter or Venus aspects; Kemadruma breaks if any planet sits with Moon or adjacent to Moon.

This-chart specific (Layer 2). Vish: Moon in 9H Sagittarius; Saturn in 11H Aquarius — different signs, no yuti. Vish does NOT form. Grahan: Sun in 8H Scorpio with Mercury, Jupiter, Pluto — no Rahu or Ketu in 8H. Moon in 9H with Mars, Venus, Neptune — no Rahu or Ketu in 9H. Rahu is in 7H, Ketu in 1H — neither yuti with Sun or Moon. Grahan-dosha does NOT form. Kemadruma: Moon in 9H; check 8H (Sun, Mercury, Jupiter, Pluto — multiple planets) and 10H (Uranus only — classical-strict counts no since Uranus is outer-graha). Even strict-classical Kemadruma requires no planet in 2nd AND 12th from Moon AND no planet with Moon. Moon is yuti with Mars-Venus-Neptune in same sign — so Kemadruma is structurally broken by the yuti itself, regardless of 2nd/12th-from-Moon. Kemadruma-bhanga active.

Conclusion. All three of Vish, Grahan, Kemadruma are absent or broken. The Moon-Sun line is structurally clean in this chart from these three doshas.

17.9 Ganda-Mool / Daridra / Guru-Chandala गंड-मूल / दरिद्र / गुरु-चांडाल

Condition / state. Three more dosha-checks. Ganda-Mool (गंड-मूल): Moon in one of the "gandanta" nakshatras (Ashvini, Ashlesha, Magha, Jyeshtha, Mula, Revati — boundary or Ketu/Mercury-nakshatras) at birth. Daridra-yoga (दरिद्र-योग): 11L in 6/8/12 with affliction. Guru-Chandala (गुरु-चांडाल): Jupiter yuti with Rahu.

Possibilities (Layer 1). Ganda-Mool indicates early-childhood vulnerability and lifelong nakshatra-specific karmic-imprint. Daridra indicates wealth-leak. Guru-Chandala indicates wisdom-mixed-with-illusion (sometimes scholar with controversial views; sometimes corrupted dharma).

This-chart specific (Layer 2). Ganda-Mool: Moon in Purvashadha Pada 2 — Purvashadha is NOT one of the six gandanta-nakshatras (the six are Ashvini, Ashlesha, Magha, Jyeshtha, Mula, Revati). Ganda-Mool does NOT apply to Moon. However, Mars is in Mula nakshatra (which IS a gandanta-nakshatra) and the lagna is in Ashvini (also gandanta). So while the strict "Ganda-Mool-via-Moon" definition does not apply, the chart carries gandanta-flavour via Mars-in-Mula and Ashvini-lagna. Some schools call this Mool-prakrutik rather than Mool-dosha; effect is generally investigative-spiritual-temperament rather than affliction. Daridra: 11L Saturn is in 11H own-sign — NOT in 6/8/12. Daridra-yoga does NOT form. Guru-Chandala: Jupiter in 8H Scorpio; Rahu in 7H Libra — different signs, NOT yuti. Guru-Chandala does NOT form.

Conclusion. Ganda-Mool absent (Moon not in gandanta); Daridra absent; Guru-Chandala absent. Mars-in-Mula and Ashvini-lagna add gandanta-flavour at the temperament-level only.

17.11 Conclusion समारोप

Condition / state. Final synthesis.

Possibilities (Layer 1). Three dosha-categories define a chart's afflictive load: (a) marriage / partnership (Manglik, Kaal-Sarp-partnership-variant), (b) emotional-mental (Vish, Grahan, Pisaca, Kemadruma), (c) ancestral / karmic (Pitru, Shrapit, Guru-Chandala, Ganda-Mool). A chart heavy in one category needs targeted remedies; a chart spread thin across many is structurally healthier.

This-chart specific (Layer 2). Dharmesh's chart shows: marriage-line (Padma Kaal-Sarp active but contained, Manglik absent) — primary concern is Rahu-7H + Venus-marak, not the doshas; emotional-mental (all four doshas absent or broken) — clean; ancestral-karmic (partial Pitru, partial Shrapit) — moderate, simple remedies sufficient. The strongest single dosha-feature is the Padma Kaal-Sarp, but it operates with three structural bhanga-rescues (Saturn own-sign, Mars-Jupiter parivartana, Jupiter-AK), meaning the karmic-spine of life follows the Ra-Ke axis without spinning loose. The dharma-line in 9H literally provides the chart's stabilizing axis against the Kaal-Sarp's pull.

Conclusion. The dosha-architecture is structurally moderate. Three active (one contained, two partial); eight absent or broken. Recommended remedies in ch32 will be classical-simple, not elaborate or expensive: pitru-tarpan during pitru-paksha, occasional Rahu/Ketu peace-rituals during Rahu MD opening (2032), and standard Hanuman Chalisa for Mars MD support. No gemstone-heavy or yantra-heavy remedy stack is structurally warranted by the chart.

Learning Note — How to read a dosha section ethically / दोष-वाचनाची नैतिकता

Rule: For every dosha, the consultant must (a) name the classical firing-criterion, (b) check it against the chart, (c) name the bhanga-rules, (d) check each bhanga, (e) deliver the verdict with the supporting placement-fact visible. The reader of this book should never deliver a dosha-verdict to a real client without showing the audit-trail.

Applied: In Dharmesh's chart, eight of eleven doshas check out absent or broken — but a predatory consultant could easily market a "Kaal-Sarp puja worth ₹50,000" without mentioning the three structural bhangas that contain the dosha. The honest reading is "Yes Kaal-Sarp is present, AND yes it is structurally contained, AND therefore the recommended response is awareness + simple Rahu-peace ritual at Rahu MD opening, not elaborate annual pujas." The ethical bar is the audit-trail.
