Vimshottari — Reading the Clock विमशोत्तरी दशा-पद्धती

A natal chart is the static sky at a single moment. Vimshottari (विमशोत्तरी) is the moving clock that walks the chart through 120 years of life, handing the steering wheel from one planet to the next in a fixed sequence. Without Vimshottari, the chart is a map without a journey; with it, every placement gains a calendar of activation. This chapter sets out what Vimshottari is, how it is calculated from the Moon's nakshatra at birth, what timeline it produces for Dharmesh, and how to read it under the six-axis frame this book uses for every dasha period.

18.1 What Vimshottari is विमशोत्तरी म्हणजे काय

Vimshottari literally means "of one-hundred-and-twenty." It is the principal Parashari dasha system — the one Maharishi Parashara names as the most suitable for Kaliyuga in the Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra. The full cycle is exactly 120 solar years, divided into nine unequal mahadasha (महादशा, MD) periods, one for each of the seven classical grahas and the two lunar nodes. Each MD is further subdivided into nine antardashas (अंतर्दशा, AD), each AD into nine pratyantardashas (प्रत्यंतर्दशा, PD), and so on through pratyantar (फलदशा) and sookshma (सूक्ष्म) levels — five layers in all. The five-layer subdivision is what gives Vimshottari its predictive precision: an MD names the decade's theme, an AD names the year's chapter, a PD names the month-to-six-week event-window.

18.2 The 120-year cycle १२० वर्षांचे चक्र

The nine MD-durations are fixed and never change: Ketu 7, Venus 20, Sun 6, Moon 10, Mars 7, Rahu 18, Jupiter 16, Saturn 19, Mercury 17. Their order is also fixed — Ketu always follows Mercury, Venus always follows Ketu, and so on around the wheel. The single variable is the starting planet and the residual duration of its first MD, which together depend on the Moon's exact longitude at birth. Every native therefore lives through the same nine planet-themes in the same order, but each native begins at a different point in the cycle and carries a different residual at birth. This is why two natives born minutes apart in different nakshatras can have completely different dasha-arcs even though their lagna and most placements are identical.

18.3 Birth-MD calculation for this chart जन्म-दशा गणित

The Moon at birth was at 17°53′56″ Sagittarius — inside Purvashadha (पूर्वाषाढा) nakshatra, ruled by Venus (शुक्र). The Moon's position within the nakshatra determines the residual. Purvashadha spans Sagittarius 13°20′ to 26°40′; the Moon at 17°53′ has traversed 4°33′ of the nakshatra's 13°20′ span, i.e. roughly 34% — leaving 66% of the Venus MD still to run. Venus's total MD is 20 years; 66% of that is 13 years, 1 month, 25 days. This residual 13Y 1M 25D of Venus MD was the dasha Dharmesh was born into. The next MD-handover was therefore Sun at 19-Jan-2009 (age 13), Moon at 20-Jan-2015 (age 19), Mars at 19-Jan-2025 (age 29) — the dasha currently running.

18.5 The five-level subdivision पाच-स्तरीय उप-विभाजन

Each MD subdivides into nine ADs, each AD into nine PDs, each PD into nine sookshma, and so on. The math is geometric — at four levels deep, a single MD has 9 × 9 × 9 × 9 = 6,561 micro-windows. In practice, the working astrologer reads three levels (MD → AD → PD) for life-event timing and only descends to sookshma when narrowing within a 30-day window. For Dharmesh, the entire 81 PDs of the current Mars MD are computed and listed in ch02a §PD inside Ma MD. As of 15 May 2026, the active triplet is Mars MD → Mars–Rahu AD → Mars–Rahu–Moon PD (12-May-2026 → 13-Jun-2026).

18.6 The 9-AD sequence inside any MD कोणत्याही MD अंतर्गत ९ अंतर्दशा

Inside every MD, the AD-sequence always starts with the MD-lord itself, then follows the standard Vimshottari order from there. So Mars MD opens with Mars–Mars, then Mars–Rahu, Mars–Jupiter, Mars–Saturn, Mars–Mercury, Mars–Ketu, Mars–Venus, Mars–Sun, Mars–Moon. The AD-duration inside an MD is proportional — Mars-Mars AD is 7 × 7 / 120 = 4.9 months, while Mars-Rahu AD is 7 × 18 / 120 = 12.6 months. The combined lord-pair (MD-lord + AD-lord) is what the astrologer reads: "Mars activates the chart's lagna-and-8H theme; Rahu sub-activates the 7H foreign-partnership theme; the combined window is identity-thrust meeting unconventional partnership signals."

18.7 The six-axis read frame सहा-अक्षीय वाचन-चौकट

This book reads every AD through six axes — the same six every time, so the reader builds muscle memory. For each MD-AD pair, ask:

  1. House of MD-lord + house of AD-lord — what life-areas does this pair activate?
  2. Lordship of MD-lord + AD-lord — which other houses are pulled in via lordship?
  3. Yutis & aspects of both — who else gets pulled into the conversation?
  4. Nakshatra-amshas of both — what flavour does the sub-lord layer add?
  5. Karaka significations — what does each planet karaka-rule (career, marriage, health, etc.)?
  6. Friend/enemy/neutral relationship between MD-lord and AD-lord — does the pair cooperate or conflict?

The combination of these six axes produces a window-specific verdict that no single rule can give alone. Junior astrologers read only axis 1 ("Mars-MD activates lagna") and stop — producing thin readings.

18.8 The transit-overlay rule गोचर-अधिच्छादन-नियम

Dasha alone is half the story. A dasha is a permission slip — it tells you which planet is allowed to fire. The actual firing happens when a transit (gochar, गोचर) hits the same theme. The classical rule: Dasha is the inner trigger, transit is the outer trigger. An event manifests when both align. For Dharmesh, Mars MD plus Saturn transiting Aries (Sade Sati Phase 2, 2025–2027, sitting on his lagna and on Ketu) is the most-loaded transit-overlay of the entire Mars MD — the chart's lagna-lord is the dasha-lord while Saturn is transiting the lagna itself. Every AD within this window inherits the Sade Sati pressure. The next overlay, Jupiter transiting Aries (2023) and Taurus (2024) and Gemini (2025), pushes upward; the third overlay, Rahu in Pisces and Ketu in Virgo (2023–2025) then Rahu in Aquarius and Ketu in Leo (2025–2027), activates the 12H–6H and 11H–5H axes successively.

18.9 Triangulation — three confirmations before any verdict त्रिकोणीकरण — कोणत्याही निष्कर्षापूर्वी तीन पुष्टी

A trained astrologer never delivers a single-source verdict. Every event-prediction is triangulated across three independent signals: (a) Vimshottari dasha-triplet (MD-AD-PD) supports the event; (b) gochar (transit) of slow-movers Saturn/Jupiter/Rahu over the relevant natal house or natal lord supports the event; (c) divisional chart (D9 for marriage, D10 for career, D7 for children, D60 for fine-tuning) confirms the structural possibility. If only two of the three align, the event is "probable but soft" — a 50–60% window. If all three align, the event is "high-conviction" — a 75–85% window. The book never claims certainty above this band; Vedic technique is probabilistic, not deterministic.

Alt-readings — other dasha systems

Yogini dasha (8-year cycle): A shorter parallel system used in North India and especially for short-term confirmation. Dharmesh's Yogini period as of 2026 is the Ulka-Yogini (Mars-themed) — coincidentally matching the Mars Vimshottari MD, which strengthens the Mars-thematic forecast.

Char dasha (Jaimini): A rashi-based dasha (each sign rules for a variable count of years). Useful for life-event triangulation. The native's current Char dasha period as of 2026 falls in Sagittarius — activating the 9H stellium (Moon-Mars-Venus) and reinforcing the Vimshottari Mars-MD theme.

Ashtottari dasha (108-year cycle): Used when Rahu-Ketu axis is prominent, as in this chart (1H–7H nodal axis). Ashtottari gives Rahu a longer slot — useful when triangulating any Rahu-related period.

Learning Note — When to descend to PD-level / प्रत्यंतर्-दशा कधी पाहावी

Rule: MD gives the decade-theme; AD gives the year-chapter; PD gives the month-event-window. Do not descend to PD until you have a specific event-question (marriage date, surgery window, job-offer timing). PD-level reading without a target question produces noise.

Applied: For Dharmesh's marriage-question, the relevant PDs are inside Mars-Jupiter AD (Jul 2026 – Jun 2027) — specifically Ma–Ju–Ve PD (20-Dec-2026 → 15-Feb-2027) and Ma–Ju–Mo PD (04-Mar-2027 → 01-Apr-2027). मराठी सारांश

विमशोत्तरी = १२० वर्षांचे प्रमुख दशा-चक्र, ९ ग्रहांच्या निश्चित कालखंडात विभागलेले (केतू ७, शुक्र २०, सूर्य ६, चंद्र १०, मंगळ ७, राहू १८, गुरू १६, शनी १९, बुध १७). सुरुवात चंद्राच्या जन्म-नक्षत्र-स्वामी पासून. धर्मेशचा चंद्र पूर्वाषाढा-पाद २ (शुक्र-नक्षत्र) मध्ये असल्यामुळे जन्मतःच शुक्र-MD-शेष १३ वर्षे १ महिना २५ दिवस होती. क्रम: शुक्र (बालपण) → सूर्य (२००९-१५) → चंद्र (२०१५-२५) → मंगळ (२०२५-३२, सध्या चालू) → राहू (२०३२-५०) → गुरू (२०५०-६६) → शनी (२०६६-८५) → बुध (२०८५-२१०२). प्रत्येक MD-मध्ये ९ AD, प्रत्येक AD-मध्ये ९ PD — ही पाच-स्तरीय रचनाच भविष्यवेधी अचूकता देते. प्रत्येक AD सहा-अक्षांद्वारे वाचावी (भाव, स्वामित्व, युती-दृष्टी, नक्षत्र-अंश, कारक, मैत्री-शत्रुत्व). शेवटी कोणताही निष्कर्ष देण्यापूर्वी तीन पुष्टी आवश्यक: दशा + गोचर + वर्ग-कुंडली. पुढच्या चार MD-स्वामींपैकी तीन (मंगळ, राहू, गुरू) ८व्या-९व्या भावाशी जोडलेले — संपूर्ण जीवन-चाप धर्म-संशोधन-रूपांतर-केंद्रित आहे.
