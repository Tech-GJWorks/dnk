19.2 Mars MD general themes मंगळ-MD ची सामान्य लक्षणे

The seven years feel fast: short attention-spans on individual chapters, multiple identity-iterations, rapid decision-making cycles. Generic themes for any Mars MD: assertion of self, courage in undertakings, possible aggression or impatience, real-estate involvement, vehicle changes, accidents or surgical incidents, sibling-events, fire-or-blood-related episodes, kshatriya-style action (litigation, competitive sport, military or technical career). Mars in friendly sign (here, Sagittarius) keeps the period constructive rather than destructive.

Specific to this chart: Mars-MD activates the entire 9H stellium (because Mars sits there with three other significators), so the seven years are dharma-driven rather than purely combative. The 1L+8L dual-lordship means identity-thrust runs through transformation-channels — Dharmesh does not just change jobs, he changes the shape of his life. The 4th-aspect on 12H supports foreign travel and any spiritual or moksha-line opening; the 7th-aspect on 3H supports writing, courage-projects, sibling activation; the 8th-aspect on 4H is the watch-line — mother's health, home disruption, vehicle accidents become heightened-probability events. The Mars-Venus yuti activates marriage-windows during Mars-Venus and Venus-Mars sub-periods. Read Mars-MD as the chart's identity-and-dharma compression chamber — seven years that compress a decade of inner work.

Learning Note — How to read MD-AD-PD-transit overlay / दशा-गोचर-अधिच्छादन वाचनाची पद्धत

Rule: For any active window, list four things on one line: (a) MD-AD-PD lord positions natally, (b) transit-Saturn position, (c) transit-Jupiter position, (d) transit-Rahu/Ketu axis. If three of these four point to the same house or theme, the window is "high-conviction firing." If only two align, "probable." If only one, "background tone."

Applied to current window (May 2026): (a) Ma-MD = 9H, Ra-AD = 7H, Mo-PD = 9H → identity + partnership + emotion-dharma triplet; (b) Saturn transit Aries on lagna — Phase 2 Sade Sati; (c) Jupiter transit Gemini-Cancer — 3H/4H; (d) Rahu Aquarius (11H natal Saturn) / Ketu Leo (5H empty). Three of four point to the 1H-7H-9H axis. High-conviction window for partnership decisions, identity-articulation events, and Sade-Sati-shaped maturation.

मंगळ-महादशा १९-जाने-२०२५ ते २०-जाने-२०३२, वयाची २९ ते ३६ — सात वर्षांचा ओळख-व-रूपांतर एकत्रित कालखंड. मंगळ लग्नेश + ८-स्वामी, ९व्या भावी मूळ-पाद-१ मध्ये धनू-दुहेरी अंशात; चंद्र-मंगळ युती (भावना-धैर्य-धन एकत्रित). नऊ अंतर्दशांचा वळसा: मंगळ-मंगळ (२०२५ ओळख-पुनर्रचना) → मंगळ-राहू (२०२५-२६ अपारंपरिक-भागीदारी, सध्या चालू) → मंगळ-गुरू (जुलै-२०२६ ते जून-२०२७ — सर्वाधिक विवाह-शक्यता) → मंगळ-शनी (२०२७-२८ करिअर-दिशा-परीक्षण) → मंगळ-बुध (२०२८-२९ भाषण-सेवा, अस्त-दोषाचा संयम) → मंगळ-केतू (२०२९ अल्प-वैराग्य-वैद्यकीय-निरीक्षण) → मंगळ-शुक्र (डिसें-२०२९ ते फेब्रु-२०३१ — पर्यायी विवाह + द्वि-मारक आरोग्य-निरीक्षण) → मंगळ-सूर्य (फेब्रु-जून-२०३१ अधिकार/पिता/अपत्य) → मंगळ-चंद्र (जून-२०३१ ते जाने-२०३२ भावनिक-आर्थिक-मातृ-समारोप). सात वर्षांपैकी ५.५ साडे-साती दबावाखाली (टप्पा १-२-३, बाहेर पडणे २०२९). गोचर-अधिच्छादन: शनी मेष-वृषभ-मिथुन, गुरू मेष ते वृश्चिक एक पूर्ण फेरी, राहू-केतू अक्ष पाच राशी-जोड्या ओलांडतो. प्रमुख त्रिकोणीकरण-खिडक्या: मा-गु + गुरू-कर्क (विवाह), मा-श + राहू-मकर (करिअर), मा-शु + गुरू-वृश्चिक (रूपांतर-विवाह), मा-सू + गुरू-धनू-वर-गुरू-परत (आत्म-कारक-ओळख).
