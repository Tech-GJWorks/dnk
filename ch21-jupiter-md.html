Jupiter Mahadasha — Wisdom and Dharma Peak गुरू-महादशा

Jupiter MD opens 19 January 2050 and runs for 16 years — Dharmesh's age 54 to 70. Because Jupiter is the chart's Atma-Karaka (AK), this is the soul-purpose ripening period — what the entire life-arc has been preparing for. Jupiter sits in 8H Scorpio Jyeshtha Pada 4 with Pisces-amsha (own-amsha rescue, Karakamsa lagna). This chapter walks the 16 years' nine ADs in compact form.

21.1 Jupiter natal profile गुरू — जन्म-विश्लेषण

Position: Jupiter 27°26′ Scorpio Jyeshtha Pada 4, in 8H. Sign-dignity: Friendly (Jupiter handles Scorpio well despite Mars-rulership). Lordship: Jupiter rules Sagittarius (9H) and Pisces (12H) — so Jupiter is 9L+12L. Nakshatra-amsha: Jyeshtha Pada 4 = Pisces-amsha (Jupiter own — Karakamsa lagna Pisces). Atma-Karaka: Yes — highest-degree classical planet at 27°26′. Yutis: Sun, Mercury(combust), Pluto in 8H Scorpio. Aspects from Jupiter: 5th on 12H Pisces (own-sign), 7th on 2H Taurus, 9th on 4H Cancer. Karaka: wisdom, husband (female chart), children (male chart), dharma, teaching, banking, expansion. Verdict: Vipareeta-axis active because 9L+12L in 8H is a complex dusthana-blend; Pisces-amsha rescues; AK signature dominates.

21.2 Jupiter MD general themes गुरू-MD सामान्य लक्षणे

Jupiter MD (गुरू-महादशा) of 16 years is the second-longest after Rahu. Generic Jupiter MD brings wisdom-ripening, teaching or guru-role, children-related events, marriage-renewal or strengthening, financial-cushion-building through ethical channels, banking or finance career advances, pilgrimage and dharma-undertakings, religious or scholarly works, second-marriage probability for those whose first marriage didn't crystallise. Health generally stable; Jupiter is the chart's natural protector. The MD is classically called the "blessed period" — when grace flows.

For this chart, Jupiter as AK in 8H Pisces-amsha makes the MD a soul-recognition window. Dharmesh's deeper life-purpose — research, hidden-knowledge, transformative teaching, healing-line — crystallises in this MD. The 9L+12L lordship pulls dharma and moksha together: late-life pilgrimage, charity, possibly inheritance event (12L Jupiter), publishing or writing. Children (Jupiter karaka) become a central life-axis. The 8H placement keeps the dharma-line research-oriented rather than ritual-religious.

Jupiter MD's 16 years from 54 to 70 form the chart's dharma-and-wisdom ripening arc. Arc: soul-recognition (Ju-Ju) → structural wisdom (Ju-Sa) → research publication (Ju-Me) → moksha pilgrimage (Ju-Ke) → late-life dual-benefic family (Ju-Ve) → recognition/retirement (Ju-Su) → soul-emotion-mother integration (Ju-Mo) → property/medical (Ju-Ma) → MD-closure with Guru-Chandala caution (Ju-Ra).
