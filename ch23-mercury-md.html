Mercury Mahadasha — If Life Reaches Ninety बुध-महादशा

Mercury MD opens 19 January 2085 — Dharmesh's age 89. Whether the native reaches this dasha depends on longevity factors examined in chapter 30. Mercury MD runs 17 years (to 2102, age 106), the second-longest among classical-graha MDs. Mercury in this chart is combust by Sun, located in 8H Scorpio Anuradha Pada 3, ruling 3H and 6H. The combustion and dusthana placement give this MD a complex character even at advanced age. This chapter previews the 9 ADs in compact form.

23.1 Mercury natal profile बुध — जन्म-विश्लेषण

Position: Mercury 10°08′ Scorpio Anuradha Pada 3 in 8H. Combust: within 1°15′ of Sun — Mercury's intellectual voice fully suppressed. Lordship: Mercury rules Gemini (3H) and Virgo (6H) — so Mercury is 3L+6L. Nakshatra-amsha: Anuradha Pada 3 = Aquarius-amsha (Saturn). Yutis: Sun (combust), Jupiter (wide), Pluto (tight). Aspects on Mercury: Saturn's 10th-aspect from 11H. Karaka: intellect, speech, education, business, hands, nerves, younger-siblings. Verdict: Vipareeta-Harsha-yoga partial (6L in 8H but combust qualifies it).

23.2 Mercury MD general themes बुध-MD सामान्य लक्षणे

Mercury MD (बुध-महादशा) of 17 years — for a native in their nineties — is unusual because Mercury at this age does not bring the youthful intellect-business themes; rather, it activates whatever Mercury-line residue remains: writing/dictation work, late-life teaching to grandchildren, business-legacy finalisation, sibling-events (siblings deceased or aged), medical-detail-management. The combust state means this MD is structurally muted — Mercury cannot fully express. The Vipareeta-Harsha-partial yoga gives some windfall-through-disputes residual. For practical purposes, this MD is consultation-relevant only if the native survives well past 89; chapter 30 examines that question.

23.12 Synthesis संश्लेषण

If reached, Mercury MD's 17 years from 89 to 106 form the longevity-extension arc of this chart. Combust Mercury caveats every AD. Major marak-watch concentrations: Me-Ve (2088–2091), Me-Su (2091), Me-Ma (2093–2094). The MD's structural function — if reached — is legacy-completion, memory-integration, and final dharma-articulation. Whether the native lives through to Ketu MD (2102) depends on longevity-yoga factors examined separately.

Mercury MD — operational summary

Window: 19-Jan-2085 → 20-Jan-2102 · age 89–106 · 17 years (longevity-conditional)
MD-lord profile: Mercury 8H Scorpio Anuradha P3 Aquarius-amsha · 3L+6L · combust · Vipareeta-Harsha-partial
Macro-theme: Legacy-completion, memory-integration, longevity-management; combust caveat throughout
Concentrated marak-watch: Me-Ve, Me-Su, Me-Ma ADs
Cycle-4 Sade Sati overlay: begins 2086, runs into early Me-Ke
Saturn fourth return: approx 2096–2097 inside Me-Ra AD

Alt-readings — combust Mercury MD

Parashari strict: Combust Mercury MD = weakened Mercury-effect throughout — speech-and-intellect themes underactive. The dasha runs as if Sun were sub-firing alongside Mercury.

Modern: Combustion only affects external expression; inner intellect remains functional. Late-life dictation, voice-recording, or assisted-publication possible.

Nadi: Combust Mercury MD is often a "silent legacy" period — outer fame muted, inner depth recorded for posterity.
