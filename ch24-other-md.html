This chapter completes the Vimshottari calendar by walking the three historical MDs Dharmesh has already lived through: Venus residual (birth Nov 1995 → Jan 2009) covering infancy through early teens; Sun MD (Jan 2009 → Jan 2015) covering ages 13-19, the secondary-school-and-college-entrance years; Moon MD (Jan 2015 → Jan 2025) covering ages 19-29, the early-career and identity-formation years. 24.1 Venus Residual MD २४.१ शुक्र-शेष महादशा · 25-Nov-1995 → 19-Jan-2009 · 13Y 1M 25D

Window & condition. Venus residual MD — the partial MD Dharmesh was born into. From birth 25-Nov-1995 (or thereabouts, per ch02a) to 19-Jan-2009. Age 0 → 13. The longest residual a chart can carry (Venus MD is 20 years).

Classical possibilities. Venus residual MDs at birth typically deliver: comfortable infancy if Venus is dignified (lavish-care imprint), early aesthetic sensitivity (music, art, food appreciation noticed from very early), strong mother-and-female-figure imprint (Venus karaka of feminine), good early-health (Venus is a benefic protector), early-childhood-sweetness, and a foundational "ease" or "abundance" template that the rest of life either confirms or compensates for. When Venus is afflicted (combust, debilitated, in dushthana), the residual delivers early-childhood-deprivation imprint instead. When Venus is dignified, the residual delivers indulged-and-supported infancy. The 13-year length means the imprint is deep and shapes the personality's "comfort-default" for the lifetime.

This-chart specific. Dharmesh's Venus is 2L+7L in 9H Sagittarius Mula Pada 2 (Venus-ruled nakshatra Purvashadha rules the Moon — and Mula is Ketu-ruled, but Venus at this degree sits inside Mula Pada 2 which is Capricorn-amsha = Saturn-amsha). Venus is yuti with Mars in 9H. The 13-year residual imprinted: (a) Saturn-amsha-flavoured aesthetic sensibility — early-childhood preferences were measured-and-considered rather than indulgent (the Saturn-amsha gave Venus its containing form); (b) dharma-line family imbedding — Venus in 9H means the family's dharma-orientation (religious-practices, ethical-values, ancestral-traditions) was the dominant childhood environment; (c) mother (4L Moon also in 9H with Venus) and Venus together at this period = strong mother-as-aesthetic-and-dharma-guide imprint; (d) Mars-Venus yuti firing through the early years gave the childhood courage-with-grace temperament. Specific biographical anchors the family would recognise: kindergarten/primary-school period (1999-2005) under the Venus-residual ADs of Su, Mo, Ma, Ra inside Venus MD — typical school-events fired predictably; the Venus-Venus AD opening at birth and continuing through age 3.4 was the most-Venus-imprinted phase (extremely comfort-and-grace-laden infancy).

Transit-overlay (historical). Saturn transited Aries (1996-1999, on lagna), Taurus (1999-2002, 2H), Gemini (2002-2005, 3H), Cancer (2005-2008, 4H), Leo (2008-2011, 5H). The Saturn-on-lagna 1996-1999 (just after birth) is structurally significant: Saturn-on-lagna at age 0-3 is an "early discipline imprint" that classical texts associate with stable, organized early-childhood. Jupiter completed one full transit-cycle plus during this 13-year window. Rahu/Ketu shifted multiple sign-pairs.

Hindsight verdict. The Venus-residual MD delivered a stable, dharma-and-aesthetic-rich early childhood with strong mother-figure presence and measured-and-grace-laden temperament-imprint. The lifetime "comfort-default" is set toward dharma-family-aesthetics rather than ego-display, which subsequent MDs have built on.

24.2 Sun MD २४.२ सूर्य महादशा · 19-Jan-2009 → 20-Jan-2015 · 6Y

When Sun is dignified (own-sign Leo, exalted Aries, in kendra-trikona), the MD delivers strong authority-imprint and academic-success. When Sun is afflicted (debilitated Libra, combust, in dushthana), the MD delivers authority-conflict, academic-stall, father-stress.
