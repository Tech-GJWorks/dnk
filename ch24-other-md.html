Other Mahadashas — Historical Reference इतर महादशा — ऐतिहासिक संदर्भ

Before Mars MD opened in January 2025, Dharmesh lived through three MDs since birth: the residual of Venus MD (1995–2009), Sun MD (2009–2015), and Moon MD (2015–2025). This chapter compresses each into a historical reference — what the chart predicted, what each window's signature was, and how its events have shaped the present Mars MD. Far-future MDs (Ketu, second-Venus cycle) are noted briefly but treated as out-of-scope for practical consultation.

24.1 Venus MD (residual at birth) शुक्र-MD (जन्म-शेष) · 25-Nov-1995 → 19-Jan-2009 · 13Y 1M 25D

Condition: Born into Venus MD with 66% of its 20-year cycle remaining. Moon at Purvashadha Pada 2 (Venus's nakshatra) gives Venus a deep-natal-anchor as Janma-Nakshatra-lord. Venus natally is 2L+7L double-marak in 9H Mula Pada 2 Capricorn-amsha.

What the chart predicted for this window (age 0–13): A childhood structured by Venus's signatures — partnership-and-art-and-comfort themes flavouring early formative years. Venus's Capricorn-amsha would have given the childhood a disciplined, slow-build flavour rather than dramatic surges. Venus being double-marak means health-watch should have been active during Venus's own sub-firings (Ve-Ma at birth, Ve-Sa 2001–2005, Ve-Ke 2007–2009).

Themes that should have appeared: Schooling onset in Ve-Ra AD (1996–1999), family routine consolidation in Ve-Ju AD (1999–2001), early academic discipline in Ve-Sa AD (2001–2005), curiosity/exploration in Ve-Me AD (2005–2007), and detachment-or-completion events at end of Ve-Ke (2007–2009) leading into Sun MD adolescence. Mother-line (Venus's natural aspect on 9H-Moon) active throughout.

Transit highlights: Saturn transited through Pisces, Aries (Sade Sati cycle 0 in 1998–2000 — toddler-stage cosmic-context), Taurus, Gemini, Cancer, Leo across this window. Jupiter completed roughly one full zodiacal pass. Rahu/Ketu axis cycled through 4–5 sign pairs.

24.2 Sun MD सूर्य-MD · 19-Jan-2009 → 20-Jan-2015 · 6Y · Age 13–19

Condition: Sun natally is 5L in 8H Scorpio Anuradha Pada 2, combust-agent for Mercury, receives Saturn's 10th-aspect. Sun-MD activates the 5H-children-devotion-intellect house from an 8H-dusthana position.

What the chart predicted for this 6-year window: Adolescence-and-early-college years played through 8H-themes — research-curiosity, hidden-knowledge fascination, transformative learning rather than mainstream-bright achievement. Sun in 8H plus Saturn's aspect would have given the public-recognition channel a muted character — quiet competence rather than public visibility. 5L Sun firing supports passion-discovery, devotional-line opening, or intellectual-vocation-articulation.

Themes that should have appeared: Su-Su AD (Jan-May 2009) board-exam-level event; Su-Mo (May-Nov 2009) emotional adolescent identity; Su-Ra (Mar 2010-Feb 2011) unconventional choice; Su-Ju (Feb-Nov 2011) academic milestone; Su-Sa (Nov 2011-Nov 2012) discipline event; Su-Me-Ke-Ve (2012-2015) school-college transition completing into Moon MD. Father-figure prominence shifts; possibly health-event for father given Saturn-10th-aspect on Sun.

Transit highlights: Saturn through Virgo-Libra (Sade Sati Phase 1-2 of cycle 1, 2009–2012). Jupiter through Aquarius-Pisces-Aries-Taurus. Rahu/Ketu through Capricorn/Cancer to Sagittarius/Gemini.

24.3 Moon MD चंद्र-MD · 20-Jan-2015 → 19-Jan-2025 · 10Y · Age 19–29

Condition: Moon natally is 4L in 9H Sagittarius Purvashadha Pada 2 — janma-graha. Yuti with Mars, Venus, Neptune. Chandra-Mangala yoga, broken-Kemadruma. Moon MD activates the janma-nakshatra's deepest signature for a full decade.

What the chart predicted for this 10-year window: A decade of emotional-mind-mother-themed expansion — higher-education completion, early-career launch, partnership-discovery (Venus-yuti firing), mother-care episodes, emotional self-discovery through dharma-channels (9H placement). Chandra-Mangala yoga's finance-courage-emotion fusion would have produced major financial-decisions taken under emotional clarity. The Janma-graha MD is structurally the most-emotionally-defining decade of any chart.

Reported by the native (confirmed): The native reports this decade as containing higher-education completion, early-career launch, mother-care episodes, and emotional self-discovery — fully consistent with the chart's prediction. Mo-Mo (2015) emotional reset; Mo-Ma (2015–2016) first career-thrust; Mo-Ra (2016–2017) unconventional choice; Mo-Ju (2017–2019) educational expansion; Mo-Sa (2019–2020) Covid-era discipline-event (Saturn in Capricorn/Aquarius globally); Mo-Me (2020–2022) commerce-and-communication phase; Mo-Ke (2022) brief detachment; Mo-Ve (2022–2024) relationship-discovery and art-line activation; Mo-Su (2024–2025) authority-and-decision-making close before Mars MD opened. The recorded birth-time passes the Moon-MD rectification check.

Transit highlights: Saturn through Scorpio-Sagittarius-Capricorn-Aquarius-Pisces during this MD. Sade Sati cycle 1 active from late-2014 to early-2017 (Phase 2-3) — overlapping early Moon MD with lagna-pressure. Jupiter completed roughly one full zodiacal pass. Rahu/Ketu through Leo/Aquarius, Cancer/Capricorn, Gemini/Sagittarius, Taurus/Scorpio, Aries/Libra, Pisces/Virgo. The decade's heaviest transit-overlay was the Sade Sati cycle 1 close in 2017 and Saturn's transit through 10H Capricorn (2020–2023) firing 10L Saturn natal.

24.4 Late-life MDs — Ketu MD and Venus MD-2 (out-of-scope) केतू-MD व शुक्र-MD-२

24.5 Synthesis — what the past MDs built पूर्व-MD-नी काय बांधले

The three past MDs from birth to age 29 form a coherent foundation for the present Mars MD: Venus MD (0–13) built the Capricorn-amsha disciplined-Venus base — slow childhood, comfort-discipline, mother-anchor; Sun MD (13–19) built the muted-authority adolescence — research-curiosity instead of bright achievement; Moon MD (19–29) built the dharma-emotional engine — education completion, mother-care, partnership-discovery, Chandra-Mangala finance-courage fusion. By the time Mars MD opened in January 2025, the native arrived with: complete formal-education, an articulated emotional-architecture, identified partnership-aspirations, financial-decision capability, and a defined dharma-line.

The present Mars MD therefore does not have to do foundational identity-work from scratch — it does actualisation work. Marriage, career-pivot, identity-articulation are all completions of what the Moon MD discovered rather than originations from scratch. This is the standard Vimshottari logic: each MD builds on the previous, and the seven-year Mars MD's relatively short length is enough only because the 10-year Moon MD did the deeper preparation.

Learning Note — Reading past MDs as rectification proof / पूर्व-MD-नी जन्म-वेळ-शुद्धीकरण

Rule: Before delivering forward-looking predictions, an astrologer must verify that the chart's past MDs delivered events consistent with the recorded birth-time. If past MDs fit, future predictions can be made with confidence. If past MDs misfire significantly, the birth-time may need rectification.

Applied to this chart: Moon MD (2015–2025) predicted dharma-emotional decade with education+career+partnership themes. Native confirms: higher-education completion, early-career launch, mother-care episodes, partnership-discovery — all consistent. Mars MD opening (Jan 2025) predicted identity-reset/career-acceleration — native confirms job-change and accelerated career drive. Birth-time passes first-pass rectification. Forward predictions in chapters 19–23 can be made with confidence.
