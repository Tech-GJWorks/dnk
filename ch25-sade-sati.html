Sade Sati साडेसाती — शनीचा साडेसात-वर्षीय भ्रमण

Sade Sati (साडेसाती) — literally "seven-and-a-half" — is the 7.5-year window when transit Saturn (gochar Shani) crosses the sign before the natal Moon, the Moon-sign itself, and the sign after it. Of every transit in the Vedic predictive toolkit, this one is the most discussed in Indian households — and the most mythologised. The chapter unpacks the rule, walks the three 2.5-year phases, and then settles the question this native's family is most likely asking: "is Sade-Sati running for Dharmesh right now?" The answer is no — Sade-Sati for this Sagittarius Moon already completed in April 2022. The next Sade-Sati does not begin until roughly 2041. The chapter therefore documents the past Sade-Sati (2014-2022) for forensic reading, and clears the current Saturn transit (Pisces-Aries-Taurus-Gemini 2023-2032) as a different Saturn-from-Moon configuration that chapter 26 examines in full.

25.1 What Sade-Sati is साडेसाती म्हणजे काय

Condition. Sade-Sati is defined as transit Saturn occupying one of three signs counted from the natal Moon: the 12th from Moon, the Moon-sign itself, and the 2nd from Moon. Saturn spends roughly 2.5 years per sign (rashi-bhraman 2.5 वर्षे प्रति राशी), so the total span is approximately 7.5 years.

Possibilities. Classical texts (Phaladeepika फलदीपिका, Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra बृहत् पाराशर होरा शास्त्र) treat Sade-Sati as Saturn's structural audit of the emotional ground (Moon). The 12th-from-Moon phase strains the unconscious — sleep, expenses, hidden losses, foreign-pulls. The Moon-sign phase strains the conscious mind directly — health, mother, emotional stability, career-pivot. The 2nd-from-Moon phase strains family, wealth, speech, and food-habit. Sade-Sati is not punishment; it is recalibration. Souls who use the 7.5 years for discipline, simplification, and karmic-payment emerge stronger; those who resist it through denial or excess accumulate further karmic load for the next visitation (Saturn returns to Moon-sign every ~29.5 years).

This chart specific. Dharmesh's natal Moon sits at 17°53′ Sagittarius Purvashadha Pada 2 in 9H. Therefore the Sade-Sati window-signs are Scorpio (12th from Moon), Sagittarius (Moon-sign), Capricorn (2nd from Moon). Saturn's transit through these three signs ran 02-Nov-2014 → 29-Apr-2022 — the native's age-19 to age-26 window. That arc is already past. Current transit Saturn (Pisces → Aries → Taurus → Gemini, 2023-2032) is 4th-5th-6th-7th from Moon — a different transit signature handled in chapter 26.

Conclusion. Sade-Sati is a Saturn-over-Moon audit, not a generic difficulty. For this native it ran 2014-2022 and is over. The current Saturn-watch belongs to a separate transit-pattern.

25.2 The three 2.5-year phases तीन अडीच-वर्षीय टप्पे

Condition. Each Sade-Sati partitions into Phase 1 (12th from Moon), Phase 2 (over Moon), Phase 3 (2nd from Moon). Each phase has its own signature.

Possibilities. Phase 1 — descending phase (पूर्वार्ध): Saturn in 12th from Moon erodes the unconscious anchor. Sleep disrupts; expenses rise; foreign-or-distance pulls activate; hidden enemies surface; the emotional reservoir empties. The native often feels "tired without reason." Phase 2 — peak phase (शिखर-काळ): Saturn over Moon delivers direct contact with the emotional engine. Mother-health, marriage-stress, career-pivot, relocation, loss of an anchor-figure, identity-recompute. This is the densest 2.5 years. Phase 3 — ascending phase (उत्तरार्ध): Saturn in 2nd from Moon stresses family, speech, food, and accumulated wealth. The lessons of Phase 2 are now publicly tested — does the native speak truth, feed the family, hold the inheritance with dignity? Phase 3 is the harvest of Phases 1-2: clean karma yields stabilisation; unfinished karma yields one more cycle of difficulty.

This chart specific. Phase 1 ran Nov-2014 to Jan-2017 (Saturn in Scorpio = 12th from Sagittarius Moon, which is also 8H for Aries lagna — double-loading the dusthana). Phase 2 ran Jan-2017 to Jan-2020 (Saturn in Sagittarius directly over 9H Moon-Mars-Venus stellium — the densest segment). Phase 3 ran Jan-2020 to Apr-2022 (Saturn in Capricorn = 2nd from Moon, also 10H for Aries lagna = career-pivot under Saturn-pressure). The 7.5-year window neatly spanned ages 19-26, overlapping Moon MD (Jan 2015 – Jan 2025) almost perfectly. The native's emotional-mind dasha and Saturn's emotional-mind audit ran simultaneously — a karmically tight squeeze.

Conclusion. Three distinct phases, each 2.5 years. For this chart they ran 2014-2017, 2017-2020, 2020-2022 — overlapping Moon MD almost in full.

25.3 This-chart's Moon-sign and Sade-Sati status या कुंडलीची चंद्र-राशी व साडेसाती-स्थिती

Condition. Moon at 17°53′ Sagittarius (Purvashadha Pada 2) in 9H. Janma-rashi = Sagittarius (धनु राशी). Saturn natal = Aquarius 11H, own-sign. Saturn currently transiting Pisces (Mar-2023 to Mar-2025) then Aries (Mar-2025 to Mar-2027) then Taurus (Mar-2027 to Mar-2030) then Gemini (Mar-2030 to Mar-2032+).

Possibilities. A reader unfamiliar with Saturn-from-Moon arithmetic will often hear the phrase "Sade-Sati" and assume any current Saturn-difficulty is Sade-Sati. The rule is strict: Sade-Sati = Saturn in 12th, 1st, or 2nd from Moon-sign — not from lagna, not from any planet. For a Sagittarius Moon, the only Sade-Sati signs are Scorpio, Sagittarius, Capricorn. Saturn currently in Pisces / Aries / Taurus / Gemini falls outside the window. The 7.5-year clock for this native is silent.

This chart specific. As of 15 May 2026, Saturn transits early Aries (5th from Sagittarius Moon, 1st from Aries lagna — Saturn over the lagna degree is a separate gochar marker handled in chapter 26). The native is in post-Sade-Sati recovery. Classically, the four-year window immediately after Sade-Sati ends is when the discipline-deposits made during the 7.5 years pay out — career-stabilisation, marriage-window-opening, financial-consolidation. This is structurally consistent with Mars MD beginning Jan-2025, three years after Phase 3 ended. The next Sade-Sati cycle for Sagittarius Moon begins approximately October 2041 (Saturn enters Scorpio), runs through October 2049 — overlapping Rahu MD's tail and Jupiter MD's opening months.

Conclusion. Current Saturn transit is NOT Sade-Sati for this chart. Sade-Sati was 2014-2022, complete. Next cycle 2041-2049.

The lessons of Phases 1-2 are publicly tested: does the native speak truth, manage money cleanly, support the family with discipline? When Phase 3's Saturn is in own-sign (as here), the strain converts more cleanly into structural gain — Saturn rewards the disciplined and audits the wasteful. The 10th-aspect of Saturn-Capricorn on 7H Rahu activates partnership-questions; 7th-aspect on 4H stresses home; 3rd-aspect on 12H stresses foreign-line.

Conclusion. Past Sade-Sati passed cleanly because natal Saturn was own-sign. Current Saturn transit (Pisces-Aries-Taurus-Gemini) is a different gochar configuration, not Sade-Sati. Next Sade-Sati begins October 2041 — plan remedies from 2039 onward.

Learning Note — Sade-Sati arithmetic and the natal-Saturn rescue / साडेसाती गणित व नैसर्गिक-शनी-रक्षण

Rule: Sade-Sati strength = (transit Saturn's house from Moon) modulated by (natal Saturn's dignity). A natal Saturn in own-sign, exalted, or in Mooltrikona converts Sade-Sati into a structural-promotion window despite surface strain. A natal Saturn debilitated, combust, or in dusthana converts Sade-Sati into a destructive window. Same transit, opposite outcomes.

Applied to this chart: Natal Saturn 24°11′ Aquarius own-sign 11H Purvabhadra Pada 2 Pisces-amsha — own-sign with Jupiter-amsha softening = one of the strongest possible natal Saturns. Sade-Sati 2014-2022 therefore ran as a structural audit-and-promotion rather than as a destructive episode. The 7.5 years overlapped Moon MD almost perfectly, which would have been catastrophic for a weak-Saturn native — but here it produced education-completion, early-career-launch, and pre-Mars-MD restructuring.

Hidden risk: A junior reader who reads only "Saturn in Sagittarius 2017-2020 = Sade-Sati Phase 2 = bad" without checking natal-Saturn dignity will catastrophise. The reverse mistake — assuming all Sade-Sati is survivable — is equally wrong for natives with weak natal Saturn. The natal-Saturn dignity is the single most important Sade-Sati modulator.

मराठी सारांश

साडेसाती म्हणजे चंद्र-राशीच्या आधीच्या, चंद्र-राशीच्या स्वतःच्या आणि नंतरच्या राशीतून शनीचा भ्रमण — एकूण साडेसात वर्षे. या कुंडलीची चंद्र-राशी धनू (पूर्वाषाढा पाद २, ९व्या भावात). त्यामुळे साडेसातीच्या राशी वृश्चिक, धनू, मकर. शनी वृश्चिकात नोव्हें-२०१४ ते जाने-२०१७ (पहिला टप्पा — ८व्या भावी ग्रहांचा अंतर्गत लेखापरीक्षण), शनी धनुत जाने-२०१७ ते जाने-२०२० (दुसरा टप्पा — ९व्या भावी चंद्र-मंगळ-शुक्र-नेपच्यून-वर थेट शनी, सर्वात दाट खिडकी, विशेषतः चंद्र-शनी AD एप्रिल-२०१९ ते नोव्हें-२०२० मध्ये शिखर), शनी मकरात जाने-२०२० ते एप्रिल-२०२२ (तिसरा टप्पा — स्व-राशी शनी १०व्या भावी = कारकीर्द-पुनर्रचना व कुटुंब-संपत्ती लेखापरीक्षण). साडेसाती एप्रिल-२०२२ ला संपली. सध्या साडेसाती चालू नाही. सध्याचा शनी (मीन-मेष-वृषभ-मिथुन २०२३-२०३२) चंद्रापासून ४-५-६-७ या स्थानांत — हे वेगळे गोचर, अध्याय २६ मध्ये चर्चा. पुढची साडेसाती सायं-२०४१ ते सायं-२०४९. नैसर्गिक-शनी रक्षण: कुंडलीतील शनी कुंभ-स्व-राशीत ११व्या भावी अत्यंत बलवान — त्यामुळे २०१४-२०२२ ची साडेसाती विध्वंसक न होता संरचनात्मक लेखापरीक्षण व पदोन्नती-खिडकी म्हणून चालली. पुढच्या साडेसातीसाठी (२०४१ पासून) हनुमान-भक्ती, शनिवारी तिळ-तेलाचा दीप, शनी-स्तोत्र, वडीलधाऱ्यांची सेवा हे उपाय २०३९ पासूनच सुरू करावेत.
