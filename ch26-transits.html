Gochar — Transits 2025-2030 गोचर — सध्याचे ग्रह-भ्रमण

Gochar (गोचर) is the predictive overlay: the moving sky placed atop the natal chart to time events. The natal chart is the seed; gochar is the season. Neither delivers an event alone — only their intersection does. A dasha-AD-PD identifies which natal planet is firing; a transit identifies when within that period the event peaks. This chapter walks the gochar framework, then traces the four slow-movers — Saturn (Shani), Jupiter (Guru), and the Rahu-Ketu axis — across 2025-2030, mapping each onto this chart's natal houses. The currently-running Mars MD's underlying Mars-gochar peaks are added because the lagnesh's transit-position modulates every PD of the running dasha. Two firing windows emerge as the densest predictive points: Mars-Jupiter AD (Jul-2026 to Jun-2027) and Mars-Venus AD (Dec-2029 to Feb-2031).

26.1 The gochar rule — transit overlay on natal dasha गोचर-नियम: नैसर्गिक-दशेवर भ्रमण-थर

Condition. An event fires when three layers align: (a) the running Mahadasha / Antardasha / Pratyantar-dasha lord signifies the event by natal-house-lordship, (b) the transit of that same lord (or its dispositor) activates the relevant natal house, (c) the daily transit Moon (Chandra-gochar) on the event-day touches a related house. Two layers can produce a low-grade event; three layers are required for a major firing.

Possibilities. Classical Vedic predictive practice uses gochar primarily for the slow-movers (Saturn 2.5 years/sign, Jupiter 1 year/sign, Rahu-Ketu 1.5 years/sign) because their transits provide a stable seasonal backdrop. Faster planets (Mars 45-60 days/sign, Sun 30 days/sign, Mercury-Venus variable, Moon 2.25 days/sign) are used for fine-grain event-day timing. The slow-movers identify the 6-12 month windows; the faster planets identify the day. Combined with dasha-AD-PD logic, the gochar overlay tells the astrologer "marriage probability is high in late-2029 to early-2031, with the densest single 2-month sub-window being February-April 2030" — a precision impossible from either dasha or transit alone.

This chart specific. Dharmesh is in Mars MD (lagnesh + 8L) — meaning every gochar reading must check Mars-transit position alongside the running AD/PD-lord transit. Mars's own gochar peaks (when Mars transits 1H, 4H, 7H, 8H, 10H — the kantaka and self-houses) become miniature event-windows inside the 7-year Mars MD. The slow-mover audit below interlocks with the Mars-MD spine.

Conclusion. Gochar is the seasonal weather; dasha is the climate. Both must align for a real event. The lagnesh-MD adds Mars-transit as a third critical layer.

Learning Note — Three-layer alignment for event-prediction / घटना-भविष्यवाणीचे तीन-थर संरेखन

Rule: No major event fires from gochar alone, dasha alone, or panchanga alone. Three layers must agree: (1) running MD-AD-PD lord signifies the event by natal-house-lordship, (2) slow-mover transit (Saturn / Jupiter / Rahu-Ketu) activates the relevant natal house, (3) daily transit Moon on the event-day touches the same theme. A four-layer alignment adds the running PD's transit-house — used for fine-grain day-prediction.

Applied to this chart (marriage prediction worked example): Mars-Venus AD (Dec-2029 to Feb-2031) = layer 1 (Venus as 7L). Jupiter transit over 7H Rahu in Libra (Oct-2029 to Oct-2030) = layer 2. Mars transit over 7H Libra Sep-Nov 2029 and again over 9H Sagittarius Feb-Mar 2030 = layer 3 (lagnesh activation). Daily transit Moon on Rohini or Mrigashira or Hasta or Anuradha or Revati nakshatras (Venus / Moon / Mercury / Saturn / Mercury sub-lords aligned with natal panchanga) = layer 4 for day-pick. The four-layer overlap defines the muhurta window. Most likely sub-window: February to April 2030 when all four layers converge.

Hidden risk: Reading dasha alone produces 14-month windows (too wide). Reading transit alone produces 30-60-day windows (too narrow, miss the dasha-confirmation). Only the layered read is reliable.

Alt-readings — gochar schools

Parashari Vedha system: Counts transits from the natal Moon-sign and applies vedha (obstruction) — when a planet transits a favourable position from Moon but a malefic simultaneously transits the obstructing position, the favourable effect is cancelled. For this chart, when Jupiter is exalted Cancer 4H from Moon (4th from Sagittarius Moon = 8th — actually a difficult position by Moon-vedha rules), the lagna-reading and Moon-reading diverge. Parashari Vedha would soften the positive verdict on Jupiter-Cancer 2026-2027 when read from Moon; the lagna-reading remains positive. The book uses lagna-reading as primary, noting the Moon-reading caveat.

K. N. Rao tradition (compound transit): Reads transits only of the running MD lord and AD lord. For this chart, focus on Mars-transit (MD lord) and the AD-lord's transit (varies). Other planets are background. This narrows the gochar reading sharply and is the cleanest predictive method for short-window event-firing.

Modern composite (Vedic-Western synthesis): Adds outer-planet transits to the slow-mover trio. Pluto's transit through Aquarius 2024-2043 transits over natal Saturn 11H — a generation-scale audit of the chart's structural anchor. This adds depth but is unnecessary for routine event-prediction.
