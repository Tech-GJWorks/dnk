Divisional Charts वर्ग-कुंडल्या — D9, D10, D7, D12

A divisional chart (varga-kundli, वर्ग-कुंडली) magnifies one slice of life under a different lens. The D1 (Rashi-kundli) is the master photograph; each varga is a microscope trained on a single life-domain. Parashara prescribed sixteen vargas (shodashavarga, षोडशवर्ग); modern practice condenses to four critical lenses: D9 Navamsa (नवांश — marriage, dharma, fortune), D10 Dasamsa (दशांश — career, public role), D7 Saptamsa (सप्तांश — children, creative legacy), D12 Dvadasamsa (द्वादशांश — parents, lineage). A reading earns its weight only when D1 and the relevant varga converge; divergence demands a softer prediction. This chapter walks each varga's role, reads this chart's positions, and locates the points of D1-varga agreement.

27.1 Why divisional charts वर्ग-कुंडल्या का

Condition. The D1 chart shows all life-themes simultaneously through twelve houses. A divisional chart isolates one theme by sub-dividing each sign into smaller arcs (D9 = 9 sub-arcs of 3°20′ each, D10 = 10 of 3° each, D7 = 7 of 4°17′ each, D12 = 12 of 2°30′ each) and re-mapping every planet's longitude into the corresponding sub-arc's sign.

Possibilities. Classical Jyotish (Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra Adhyaya 6-7) describes each varga as governing a specific bhava-class: D9 governs spouse-and-dharma (the second half of life, the inner fruition of D1), D10 governs karma-and-vocation, D7 governs progeny, D12 governs parents. The principle: if a theme is shown strong in D1 but weak in its varga, the surface promises but the depth withholds — events open in the outer life but do not satisfy inwardly. If a theme is weak in D1 but strong in its varga, the inner ripeness builds quietly until a transit unlocks it. The cleanest reading occurs when D1 and varga both show strength — surface and depth agree.

This chart specific. Dharmesh's D1 has been read through chapters 7-15. The four divisional readings below test each domain (marriage, career, children, parents) against the D1's standing verdict. Convergence is checked at the close of each section; the chapter ends with a summary-grid.

Conclusion. Vargas are not predictions in themselves — they are agreement-tests. D1 + varga = reliable verdict; D1 - varga = softer prediction; D1 vs varga (opposite signals) = wait-and-watch.

स्थिती (वर्ग का). D1 कुंडली बारा भावांतून सर्व जीवन-थीम एकाच वेळी दाखवते. वर्ग-कुंडली एक थीम वेगळी करते — प्रत्येक राशीला लहान कमानींत विभागून (D9 = ९ उप-कमानी प्रत्येकी ३°२०', D10 = १० प्रत्येकी ३°, D7 = ७ प्रत्येकी ४°१७', D12 = १२ प्रत्येकी २°३०') आणि प्रत्येक ग्रहाचे अंश त्या उप-कमानीच्या राशीत पुन्हा-मॅप करते. शास्त्रोक्त शक्यता. बृहत्-पाराशर-होरा-शास्त्र अध्याय ६-७ प्रत्येक वर्गाचे विशिष्ट भाव-वर्गावर अधिकार वर्णन करते: D9 जोडीदार-व-धर्म (D1 च्या आयुष्याचा दुसरा अर्धा, आंतरिक फलप्राप्ती), D10 कर्म-वा-व्यवसाय, D7 संतती, D12 आई-वडील. तत्त्व: एखादी थीम D1 मध्ये बलवान पण त्याच्या वर्गात दुर्बल असेल तर पृष्ठ वचन देतो पण खोली देत नाही — बाह्य आयुष्यात घटना उघडतात पण आंतरिक समाधान नाही. थीम D1 मध्ये दुर्बल पण वर्गात बलवान असेल तर आंतरिक परिपक्वता शांतपणे वाढते जोपर्यंत गोचर ती उघडत नाही. सर्वात-स्वच्छ वाचन तेव्हाच होते जेव्हा D1 आणि वर्ग दोन्ही बल दाखवतात. या कुंडलीसाठी. धर्मेशचा D1 अध्याय ७-१५ मधून वाचला आहे. खालील चार वर्ग-वाचन प्रत्येक क्षेत्र (विवाह, कारकीर्द, संतती, पालक) D1 च्या उभ्या निकालाशी तपासतात. प्रत्येक विभागाच्या शेवटी संगती तपासली जाते; अध्यायाचा शेवट सारांश-तक्ता. निष्कर्ष. वर्ग स्वतःत भाकित नाहीत — ते संगती-कसोट्या आहेत. D1 + वर्ग = विश्वसनीय निकाल; D1 - वर्ग = सौम्य भाकित; D1 विरुद्ध वर्ग (विरुद्ध संकेत) = थांबा-आणि-पहा.

27.2 D9 Navamsa — marriage and dharma D9 नवांश — विवाह व धर्म

Condition. Navamsa-lagna: Gemini (मिथुन) — ruled by Mercury. 7H of D9 = Sagittarius (Jupiter-ruled). 7L of D9 = Jupiter, which sits in D9-10H Pisces (own-sign). Rahu in D9: Libra 5H (with Mercury).

Possibilities. D9 governs the inner ripening — both marriage and dharma. A strong D9-lagna lord, strong 7L of D9, and a strong Venus-in-D9 (for male charts) signal a structurally-supported marriage. A Karakamsa lagna (the D9 sign of the Atma-Karaka) reveals the soul's primary spiritual current. A 7L of D9 in own-sign or exalted is one of the cleanest marriage-strength markers; a 7L in dusthana of D9 (6/8/12 of D9) signals delay or difficulty. Venus in own-sign, exalted, or in kendra-trikona of D9 protects marital comfort; Venus debilitated or combust in D9 strains the partnership.

This chart specific. Three structural strengths stand out in this D9: (a) 7L Jupiter in own-sign Pisces in D9-10H — own-sign rulership of the partnership-house of D9, sitting in the career-public-respect house of D9 = an exceptionally clean marriage-strength marker, and additionally indicates the spouse will hold dharma-or-knowledge stature; (b) Venus own-sign Taurus in D9-12H with Saturn — Venus own-sign protects marital comfort, the Venus-Saturn yuti in D9 echoes the Mula-Capricorn-amsha Venus of D1 (chapter 11), reinforcing structured partner-temperament; (c) Mars own-sign Aries with Ketu in D9-11H — Mars in own-sign in upachaya of D9 supports gains-through-partnership-action, but Ketu-Mars yuti adds detachment to gains (Mars-Ketu yuti in D9 is the Mahanidhi-yogi cluster repeating across vargas, see chapter 14). The Atma-Karaka Jupiter's D9 position (Pisces — own-sign) becomes the Karakamsa lagna — the soul's primary current is devotional-philosophical Pisces, exactly as the D1 reading predicted. Two cautions: Moon-Sun-Pluto cluster in D9-4H Virgo indicates emotional restructuring around home/mother themes inside marriage; Rahu-Mercury yuti in D9-5H Libra adds unconventional or technologically-mediated currents to romantic origin-story (long-distance, online, or cross-cultural meeting probable).

Conclusion. D9 strongly supports marriage. 7L own-sign + Venus own-sign + Karakamsa Pisces = surface (D1's Mars-Venus 9H + Jupiter 9L AK) and depth agree. Partner-temperament: dharma-stature, structured, possibly cross-cultural origin.

D9 मध्ये शुक्र: वृषभ १२व्या भावी (स्व-राशी, शनीसोबत). D9 मध्ये मंगळ: मेष ११व्या भावी (स्व-राशी, केतूसोबत). D9 मध्ये चंद्र: कन्या ४थ्या भावी (सूर्य व प्लूटोसोबत). D9 मध्ये शनी: वृषभ १२व्या भावी (शुक्रासोबत). D9 मध्ये राहू: तूळ ५व्या भावी (बुधासोबत). शास्त्रोक्त शक्यता. D9 आंतरिक परिपक्वता-वा-धर्म नियंत्रित करते. बलवान D9-लग्न-स्वामी, बलवान D9 चा ७-स्वामी, आणि बलवान D9 मधील शुक्र (पुरुष कुंडलीसाठी) रचनात्मकदृष्ट्या समर्थित विवाह सूचित करतात. कारकांश लग्न (आत्म-कारकाची D9 राशी) आत्म्याची मुख्य अध्यात्मिक धारा प्रकट करते. Learning Note — Reading a varga in five moves / वर्ग वाचनाचे पाच पाऊल

Rule: Every varga is read in five steps: (1) varga-lagna and its lord, (2) the varga-house corresponding to the domain (7H for D9, 10H for D10, 5H for D7, 4H/9H for D12), (3) that house's lord's varga-placement, (4) the domain-karaka's varga-placement (Venus for D9-male, Sun-Saturn for D10, Jupiter for D7-male, Sun-Moon for D12), (5) the D1 sign of the same planet — to check for varga-D1 sign-resonance (a depth-marker). The fifth step is the most overlooked.

Applied to D9 (worked example): (1) D9-lagna Gemini ruled by Mercury — Mercury in D9-5H Libra with Rahu (Mercury exalted Virgo not reached; Mercury in Libra-with-Rahu adds unconventional flavour). (2) D9-7H Sagittarius (the marriage-house). (3) 7L Jupiter in D9-10H Pisces own-sign — strongest marker. (4) Venus (D9-male karaka) in D9-12H Taurus own-sign with Saturn — structured-comfort. (5) Jupiter's D1 sign = Scorpio; Jupiter's D9 sign = Pisces. Different signs but both Jupiter-friendly (Pisces is Jupiter's own; Scorpio is friendly). The depth-resonance is medium-strong — Jupiter carries the Atma-Karaka stamp into D9 own-sign, reinforcing the soul-and-marriage thread.

Hidden risk: Reading only steps 1-2 of varga without checking the karaka (step 4) and the D1-resonance (step 5) produces a flat varga reading. For this chart specifically, missing step 4 would miss Venus's own-sign Taurus rescue; missing step 5 would miss Jupiter's Pisces-D9-from-Scorpio-D1 thread (which links the AK to the marriage-house).
