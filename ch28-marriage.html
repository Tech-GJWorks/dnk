Marriage विवाह — सप्तम भाव, शुक्र व दारकारक

Marriage in Jyotish is read not from a single significator but from a triangulation: the 7th house (विवाह-स्थान — the bhava of partnership), the 7th lord (saptamesha — where its energy is deployed), Venus (kalatra-karaka — कलत्र-कारक, the natural significator of wife in a male chart), the D9 Navamsa (the dharmic-marriage chart), and the Darakaraka (Jaimini's spouse-significator — the planet at lowest degree). For Dharmesh, these five evidences converge on a single picture: marriage that arrives during the Mars Mahadasha, partner-energy textured by Venus's Sagittarius-Mula placement, and the chart's critical 14-month window of Mars-Venus Antardasha (14-Dec-2029 → 13-Feb-2031).

28.1 The 7th house — its significations सप्तम भाव

Condition. 7H here is Libra (Venus-ruled, airy, cardinal), occupied by Rahu at 00°34′ Chitra Pada 3. The cusp itself sits at the same degree-band as the lagna (chart is set with equal-house simplification for the diamond — actual 7H cusp is 08°28′ Libra). Rahu sits very near the cusp.

Possibilities. The 7H (सप्तम भाव) governs spouse, marriage, business-partnership, public dealings, open enemies, foreign travel for business, the second half of life, and the "other" in every transaction. Classical Parashari (पाराशरी) literature lists three readings for any 7H: (a) sign-significations of the rashi occupying it, (b) lordship-deployment of the 7L, (c) condition of the planets actually sitting in 7H. A Libra 7H is the most marriage-prone of the twelve rashis — Venus's home sign of partnership-as-aesthetic-balance. Rahu in 7H is one of the most-discussed placements in classical texts: it can produce unconventional unions, foreign-tinged or different-community partners, late or twice-marriage, a magnetic-attraction-then-confusion pattern, and sometimes legal-or-status ambiguity in the early partnership phase. The Chitra-pada-3 Aquarius-amsha overlays Saturn's discipline, producing a craftsmanship-and-structure flavour rather than chaotic Rahu impulse.

This chart specific. Rahu on the 7H cusp at 00°34′ Libra Chitra-3 (कुंभ-अंश) gives the partner-line three signatures: unconventional or cross-community attraction, a partner with technical/foreign/structured-craft pull, and a relationship that matures only after structural maturity is established (Saturn-amsha demands time). The mutual 1H-7H nodal axis means every partnership decision is karmically weighted — Dharmesh cannot enter marriage casually; the soul itself contracts to discharge past-life debt through this union. Rahu MD opens 19-Jan-2032; if marriage is delayed beyond Mars MD, Rahu MD's first 2–3 years (Rahu-Rahu AD 2032-2034) become the secondary window with strong foreign-or-unconventional flavour.

Conclusion. 7H Libra-Rahu = magnetic, karmic, structured-when-mature partnership; first marriage window is Mars-MD, secondary is early Rahu-MD.

28.2 The 7th lord — Venus in 9H Sagittarius सप्तमेश शुक्र

Condition. Venus (शुक्र) is 7L here. Venus sits at 03°34′ Sagittarius Mula Pada 2, in 9H, in yuti with Moon, Mars, and Neptune. Venus is simultaneously 2L (dhana) — making it a double-marak (मारक) karaka.

Possibilities. A 7L placed in 9H is one of the most dharma-favourable partnership signatures. The marriage-line is routed through the fortune/father/guru/long-journey house — meaning marriage typically arrives via a dharmic introduction (family alliance, guru-circle, pilgrimage, foreign-education meeting), the partner often carries a teaching/spiritual/foreign-cultural dimension, and the union itself reinforces the native's dharma-line rather than scattering it. 7L in 9H is also a classical marker of marriage being "fated" (bhagya-driven) rather than chosen casually. The shadow: when 7L is also 2L (double-marak), Venus's periods carry simultaneous marriage-activation and health-watch — the same dasha that brings the wedding can also bring health-pressure on the native or on family-elders.

This chart specific. Venus in own-nakshatra Mula Pada 2 (Capricorn-amsha) is unusually significant — Venus sits in one of its own three nakshatras (Bharani, Purvaphalguni, Purvashadha — though Mula is Ketu's nakshatra, not Venus's own; the Mula placement is significant because the Moon, Venus's sub-period-trigger, sits in Venus's own Purvashadha just degrees away). The Capricorn-amsha imposes Saturn-structure: the spouse is likely from a structured-family background, slightly older or more mature in temperament, and the alliance is age-asymmetric or maturity-asymmetric in some measurable way. Venus's yuti with Mars (Chandra-Mangala-Shukra triple in 9H) couples passion-courage-aesthetic into one fused tone — the relationship will not be tepid. The double-marak status is the chart's single most-watched 14-month window: Mars-Venus AD 14-Dec-2029 → 13-Feb-2031.

Conclusion. Venus 7L in 9H Mula = dharma-fated, mature, fire-toned alliance; Mars-Venus AD is the most-probable wedding window with parallel health-watch on family elders.

28.3 Venus as kalatra-karaka in this chart कलत्र-कारक शुक्र

Condition. Beyond lordship, Venus is the natural significator of wife in a male chart — kalatra-karaka (कलत्र-कारक). Its sign, nakshatra, dignity, yutis, and aspects describe the spouse's archetype.

Possibilities. Classical texts decode the kalatra-karaka by reading Venus's placement as a portrait of the wife herself. Venus's sign gives her physical-and-temperamental colour. Venus's nakshatra gives her emotional sub-current. Venus's yutis describe the qualities she will bring into the marriage. Venus's aspects describe what she will receive from the world. Venus's house-placement describes the life-domain where the marriage will unfold its primary lessons.

This chart specific. Venus in Sagittarius = the wife is dharmic, optimistic, philosophical-leaning, well-travelled or travel-curious, often from a teacher/professor/spiritual-or-cultural family. Venus in Mula Pada 2 Capricorn-amsha = she carries a quiet structural discipline, perhaps a research or scholarly streak, ages well, and brings a slow-build maturity rather than youthful glamour. Venus yuti Mars = she is courageous, action-capable, possibly with technical or sports-leaning skill; this yuti also flags the Manglik-axis discussion (chapter 17). Venus yuti Moon = she brings emotional anchoring, possibly from a strong mother-line, and her emotional life is interlaced with her financial/aesthetic life. Venus in 9H = the marriage's main territory is dharma, long-arc meaning-making, possibly foreign-settlement together. The wife enters the chart as a fellow-pilgrim, not a domestic-cohabitant in the narrow sense.

Conclusion. Kalatra-karaka portrait: a structured, dharma-oriented, courageous, mature partner who joins the dharma-line rather than diverting from it.

28.4 D9 Navamsa — marriage indicators नवांश-कुंडली

Condition. D9 lagna is Gemini (Mercury-ruled). In D9, Venus sits in 11H Aries with Saturn; Jupiter sits in 10H Pisces (own sign — exalted in some traditions); Mars and Ketu are in 11H. The D9-7H is Sagittarius, ruled by Jupiter who sits in D9-10H.

Possibilities. The D9 chart is read as a parallel-marriage-chart: the D9-lagna reveals the dharmic-self that enters the marriage, the D9-7H reveals the spouse's underlying dharmic-nature, the D9-7L reveals what the spouse does, and the position of Venus in D9 reveals the relational climate after marriage. A strong Jupiter in D9 (especially in own sign or kendra) is one of the classical "good marriage" signatures, indicating wise spouse and dharmic union.

This chart specific. Gemini D9 lagna gives Mercury the dharmic-rashi-lord role — but Mercury is combust in D1 8H, slightly muting the D9 self-projection. However, the rescue is significant: D9 Jupiter in 10H Pisces (own sign) is the chart's single strongest D9 signature — Jupiter is exalted-or-own in the D9 dharma-action house, blessing the marriage with wisdom-orientation and ethical anchoring. Venus in D9 11H Aries with Saturn places the wife-significator in the gains-house with structural discipline — the marriage materially benefits both parties, but only through Saturnian patience. Mars-and-Ketu in D9 11H reinforce the gains-but-through-effort theme. The D9 reads coherently with the D1: structured, dharmic, materially supportive marriage that ripens slowly.

Conclusion. D9 confirms D1's verdict — own-sign Jupiter in D9-10H is the rescue-anchor that makes this a structurally-blessed dharmic alliance.

28.5 Darakaraka — Jaimini's spouse-significator दारा-कारक

Condition. The Darakaraka (दार-कारक) under Jaimini is the planet at the lowest degree among the seven classical planets (Sun, Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn). Reviewing degrees in this chart: Sun 08°53′, Moon 17°53′, Mars 02°20′, Mercury 10°08′, Jupiter 27°26′, Venus 03°34′, Saturn 24°11′. The lowest is Mars at 02°20′.

Possibilities. The Darakaraka is read as the karmic-blueprint of the spouse-encounter — the planet that introduces the spouse into the native's life and the planet whose qualities the spouse will most-strongly mirror. In Jaimini's system, the Darakaraka's sign, nakshatra, and house-placement give a second-layer portrait of the spouse that supplements (sometimes contradicts) the kalatra-karaka portrait. When Mars is Darakaraka, the spouse-meeting is action-triggered, courage-themed, often through a sibling or sports-or-technical-context, and the spouse herself carries Mars-qualities: forthrightness, action-capability, perhaps a medical/engineering/uniformed-service streak.

This chart specific. Mars-as-Darakaraka in 9H Sagittarius Mula Pada 1 places the spouse-blueprint in the dharma-house in own-nakshatra Mula. The spouse will be encountered in a dharmic-or-research-or-foreign context (matching the 7L-in-9H reading), will carry Mars's action-capability layered over Mula's depth-research instinct, and the meeting itself will feel consequential rather than incidental — Mula's deity Nirriti governs "what must be uprooted" — the spouse arrives precisely when an old identity-pattern is being dismantled and a dharma-rebuild is underway. The Darakaraka in own-nakshatra is also a signature of soul-level recognition at first or second meeting. The fact that Darakaraka is the running MD-lord (Mars MD 2025-2032) compresses the spouse-encounter timeline: this very seven-year window is when Mars-as-Darakaraka activates the spouse-introduction.

Conclusion. Darakaraka Mars in 9H Mula = dharma-context spouse-meeting, action-toned recognition, fated-feeling first encounter — and Mars MD is precisely the window.

28.6 Marriage timing windows in Mars MD विवाहाच्या काळ-खिडक्या

Condition. Mars MD runs 19-Jan-2025 → 19-Jan-2032. Within it, nine Antardashas. We rank each AD by marriage-firing probability based on the planet's relation to 7H, 7L, Venus, and Darakaraka.

Possibilities — general timing rules. The strongest marriage-firing AD inside any MD is the one ruled by a planet that (a) is Venus or in yuti with Venus, (b) lords 7H or sits in 7H, (c) connects to the Darakaraka, (d) activates the Navamsa-Lagna or its lord. Secondary support comes from Jupiter transits in the 7H or 11H, Venus transits in the 7H or in own-sign, and the Sade-Sati phase status. The AD that scores on three or more of these criteria is the prime window.

28.7 Conclusion — the marriage signature विवाह-निष्कर्ष

Condition. Five evidences converge — 7H Libra-Rahu, 7L Venus in 9H Mula, kalatra-karaka Venus's portrait, D9 own-sign Jupiter in 10H, Darakaraka Mars in 9H Mula.

This chart specific. The marriage signature reads: dharma-fated alliance, structurally mature spouse with Mars-courage and Venus-aesthetic balance, first meeting in a dharmic or research-context, ceremony most-probable in Mars-Venus AD (Dec 2029 – Feb 2031), and parallel health-watch on family elders during the same 14 months. The marriage strengthens dharma rather than diverting from it, brings material gains via the spouse's own structural capability (D9 Venus in 11H Aries with Saturn), and is karmically loaded on the 1H-7H nodal axis. Post-marriage, Rahu MD opens 2032 — the foreign-settlement-or-status-leap decade unfolds with the spouse as fellow-traveller.

Conclusion. A dharma-rooted, fire-toned, mature, slow-ripened marriage — most-probable window 14-Dec-2029 → 13-Feb-2031.

Learning Note — Reading marriage from five evidences / पाच पुराव्यांवरून विवाह वाचणे

Rule: Marriage cannot be predicted from any single significator. Five evidences must be triangulated: (1) 7H sign + occupants, (2) 7L's placement + condition, (3) Venus as kalatra-karaka in its own right, (4) D9's 7H + 7L + Venus, (5) Darakaraka under Jaimini. When four-of-five agree, the verdict is confident; when three-of-five agree, expect a softer or delayed expression; when two-or-fewer agree, the chart names ambivalence and the astrologer should not commit to timing.

Applied here: All five evidences point to dharma-context, structurally-mature, fire-toned alliance during Mars MD. Five-of-five agreement is rare and gives the astrologer permission to name the window (Mars-Venus AD) with confidence. The double-marak caveat on Venus is the only shadow; it does not negate the marriage-firing, it adds a parallel health-watch on family elders.

Hidden risk: A junior reader who looks only at the 7H Libra-Rahu placement may overweight the "Rahu confusion" reading and miss that D9's own-sign Jupiter and Darakaraka's own-nakshatra both rescue and elevate the alliance. The Rahu-in-7H signature describes the texture of attraction, not the quality of the marriage.

Alt-readings — marriage timing schools

Classical Parashari (strict): Reads marriage-firing AD as the AD ruled by 7L (Venus) or the AD where 7L is in dasha-aspect to running MD-lord. Both conditions met in Mars-Venus AD — full agreement with the verdict above.

K. N. Rao / "Jaimini Chara-dasha synthesis": Adds Chara-dasha layer reading. Chara-dasha for this native runs Libra-rashi-dasha at approximately ages 30-37 (mid-2025 onwards) — Libra is the 7H itself, doubling the marriage-firing during Mars MD. Independent confirmation of the Vimshottari window.

Tajik / annual-chart school: Would compute Varshaphal (annual return) for ages 34 (Nov 2029 → Nov 2030) and 35 (Nov 2030 → Nov 2031) — both years overlap Mars-Venus AD. A strong Varshaphal Venus-in-7H or Venus-with-7L confirms the wedding-firing in those specific years.

Tamil Nadi tradition: Reads Saptamamsa (D7 — though D7 is technically the children-chart, some Tamil traditions use it for spouse-character) and Trimsamsa (D30) supplements. Both layers in this chart reinforce structured-mature-spouse reading.
