Marriage विवाह — सप्तम भाव, शुक्र व दारकारक

Marriage (विवाह — vivaha) is not read in Jyotish from a single significator. Classical Parashari (Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra), Phaladeepika (Mantreshwara), Saravali (Kalyana Varma), Jataka Parijata (Vaidyanatha), and Jaimini Sutras all converge on a five-evidence triangulation: the 7th house (विवाह-स्थान — the bhava of partnership), the 7th lord (saptamesha — where its energy is deployed), Venus (kalatra-karaka — कलत्र-कारक, the natural significator of wife in a male chart), the D9 Navamsa (the dharmic-marriage chart and the chart's second-half-of-life blueprint), and the Darakaraka (Jaimini's spouse-significator — the planet at lowest degree). To these five, the modern reader adds three secondary checks: Upapada lagna (UL) from Jaimini, 2H for family-after-marriage and second-marriage, and aspects on 7H from Jupiter/Saturn/Mars/Rahu/Ketu — each of which colours the union's texture. For Dharmesh, these eight evidences converge on a single coherent picture: marriage that arrives during the Mars Mahadasha, partner-energy textured by Venus's Sagittarius-Mula placement, and the chart's critical 14-month window of Mars-Venus Antardasha (14-Dec-2029 → 13-Feb-2031). The chapter walks every classical rule, every Manglik check, every cancellation, every marriage-yoga, then converts the classical machinery into an astrologer-to-client narrative usable in actual consultation.

28.1 The 7th house — its significations सप्तम भाव

Condition. 7H here is Libra (Venus-ruled, airy, cardinal), occupied by Rahu at 00°34′ Chitra Pada 3. The cusp itself sits at the same degree-band as the lagna (chart is set with equal-house simplification for the diamond — actual 7H cusp is 08°28′ Libra). Rahu sits very near the cusp.

All classical possibilities (Layer 1). The 7H (सप्तम भाव) is the yuvati-bhava in BPHS — the house of the female-partner, the marriage-ceremony, business-partnerships, public-dealings, open enemies (rivals seen face-to-face), foreign travel for business, the second half of life, and the "other" in every transaction. Parashara's first rule: read 7H from three angles simultaneously — (a) the sign-significations of the rashi occupying it, (b) the lordship-deployment of the 7L, (c) the condition (dignity + aspects + yutis) of any planet actually sitting in 7H. Classical readings of each sign as 7H: Aries-7H = impulsive partner, possible early-strife or hasty-marriage; Taurus-7H = sensual, stable, slow-moving; Gemini-7H = communicative, possibly two marriages (Mercury duality); Cancer-7H = emotional, home-centred, mother-flavour; Leo-7H = authoritative, possibly drama-tinted, status-conscious; Virgo-7H = analytical, service-oriented, possible delay; Libra-7H = the most marriage-prone sign — Venus's home of partnership-as-aesthetic-balance, generally indicating harmonious, beauty-aware, refined partner; Scorpio-7H = intense, possibly secretive, transformation-themed; Sagittarius-7H = dharma-aware, philosophical, possibly foreign or different-culture; Capricorn-7H = mature, structured, possibly elder partner; Aquarius-7H = unconventional, intellectually-led, friend-becomes-spouse; Pisces-7H = devotional, sensitive, spiritual-flavour. Rahu in 7H is one of the most-discussed placements in classical texts (Phaladeepika 6.11; Saravali 33.45; Brihat Jataka 12) — it can produce: unconventional unions (inter-caste, inter-community, inter-faith, large age-gap, long-distance), foreign-tinged or different-region partners, late marriage or twice-marriage, magnetic-attraction-then-confusion pattern, sometimes legal-or-status ambiguity in the early partnership phase, and a karmic-debt-discharge through the union. The pada (sub-quarter) of Chitra-3 places Rahu in Aquarius-amsha — Saturn's discipline overlay — producing a craftsmanship-and-structure flavour rather than chaotic Rahu impulse. Mars (Chitra's nakshatra-lord) sitting in 9H Mula activates the nakshatra-dispositor chain: 7H Rahu → Chitra → Mars → 9H Mula → Ketu (Mula's lord) → 1H Aries → Mars again, a closed loop that returns the entire partnership-line into the Mars-Ketu nodal axis.

Astrologer-to-client narrative (Layer 2). Dharmesh, when an astrologer looks at your 7H, three things become visible at the same time. First, your partnership-house is Libra — the most naturally marriage-prone sign in the zodiac. This alone tells me the marriage will happen; your chart does not name a celibate-or-solitary signature. Second, Rahu sits right at the 7H cusp. This is a karmic-flag: the partnership you are heading toward is not a casual social-arrangement; the soul itself has signed a contract to discharge a long-standing debt through this union. In practical terms, this typically expresses as a partner from outside your immediate community, a meeting that does not follow the usual arranged-family-matching pathway (it will likely involve a dharmic or research or travel or technical context), and a recognition that feels fated even on the first meeting. Third, Rahu in Chitra-Aquarius-amsha tames Rahu's natural disorientation into a Saturn-structured craft-form — your partner will not be erratic or unstable; she will carry her own structural discipline, her own craft or research line, and the relationship will mature only after both of you have established structural maturity individually. Practically, the chart says: do not rush, do not panic, do not force the meeting through artificial channels — the partner is being escorted to you by the chart's own karmic machinery; your job is to remain dharmically rigorous and recognize her when she arrives.

Hard verdict / forecast. 7H Libra-Rahu Chitra-3 Aquarius-amsha = magnetic, karmic, structured-when-mature partnership; first marriage window is Mars-MD, secondary is early Rahu-MD; partner from outside immediate community or arriving via unconventional pathway; structurally disciplined, craft-or-research oriented, recognized at first meeting as karmic-significant.

संपूर्ण मराठी विवेचन — सप्तम भाव. सप्तम भाव (विवाह-स्थान / युवती-भाव) हा कुंडलीच्या पाच सर्वाधिक चर्चित भावांपैकी एक — पती-पत्नी संबंध, विवाह-समारंभ, व्यवसाय-भागीदारी, सार्वजनिक व्यवहार, उघड शत्रू, परदेश-व्यापार-यात्रा, आयुष्याचा उत्तरार्ध, आणि प्रत्येक देवाणघेवाणीतला "दुसरा" यांचे प्रतिनिधित्व. बृहत् पाराशर होरा शास्त्र, फलदीपिका, सारावली, जातक पारिजात या शास्त्रीय ग्रंथांत सप्तम भाव वाचण्याचे तीन कोन सांगितले आहेत — (अ) त्यात बसलेल्या राशीचे फल, (ब) सप्तमेशाचे स्थान-व-अवस्था, (क) सप्तमात बसलेल्या ग्रहाची दशा-दृष्टि-युति. दर्मेशच्या कुंडलीत सप्तम भाव तूळ राशी (शुक्रस्वामी, वायु-तत्त्व, चर-राशी) आहे आणि त्यात राहू ०°३४′ चित्रा पाद ३ बसला आहे — हे फार महत्त्वाचे संयोजन. तूळ ही बारा राशींपैकी सर्वात विवाह-योग्य राशी मानली जाते कारण शुक्र म्हणजे सौंदर्य-संतुलन-भागीदारी, आणि चर-राशी म्हणजे "घडवून आणणारी" — विवाह नक्की घडेल, बसून राहणार नाही. राहू ७व्या भावी असण्याचे शास्त्रीय फल फलदीपिका ६.११, सारावली ३३.४५, बृहत् जातक १२ या ग्रंथांत विस्ताराने सांगितले आहे: (१) अपारंपरिक संबंध (आंतरजातीय, आंतरधर्मीय, मोठा वयभेद, दूरचा प्रदेश), (२) परदेशी किंवा वेगळ्या समाजातून जोडीदार, (३) विलंबित-विवाह किंवा दुसरा विवाह, (४) चुंबकीय आकर्षण-व-संभ्रम यांचा एकत्रित नमुना, (५) सुरुवातीच्या काळात कायदेशीर-किंवा-दर्जा-संदर्भात अस्पष्टता, (६) पूर्वजन्म-कर्म-निरसनासाठी विवाह. चित्रा-पाद ३ म्हणजे कुंभ-अंश — शनीच्या शिस्तीची छटा — त्यामुळे राहूचा गोंधळ शिस्तबद्ध शिल्प-कौशल्यात बदलतो; जोडीदार अस्थिर-धरसोडी नसेल, तर तिच्या स्वतःच्या संरचनात्मक शिस्तीने आणि स्वतःच्या कौशल्य-रेखेने पुढे आलेली असेल. चित्राचा नक्षत्र-स्वामी मंगळ आहे, आणि मंगळ ९व्या भावी मूळ नक्षत्रात बसला आहे — म्हणजे ७व्या भावी राहूचा कारक-धागा थेट ९व्या भावी मंगळाशी जोडलेला आहे, आणि मंगळ हा लग्नेश + ८ल + दारकारक + सध्याची महादशा-स्वामी (४-५ भूमिकांत) आहे. हे फार-फार महत्त्वाचे आहे: दर्मेश, तुमच्या विवाहाचा संपूर्ण नक्षत्र-दिशा-धागा मंगळावरून जातो — आणि सध्या मंगळाची महादशा चालू आहे (२०२५-२०३२). पुढच्या सात वर्षांत, विशेषतः मंगळ-शुक्र अंतर्दशेत (डिसें-२०२९ ते फेब्रु-२०३१), तुमचा जोडीदार तुमच्या आयुष्यात येतो हे जवळजवळ निश्चित. ती कुणी सामान्य ओळखीची मुलगी नसेल — ती तुमच्या समान-समाजाच्या बाहेरून, धर्म/संशोधन/प्रवास/तंत्रज्ञान या संदर्भात भेटणारी, स्वतःची स्वतंत्र शिल्प-कौशल्य-रेखा असलेली, परिपक्व मनाची, आणि पहिल्या भेटीतच "ही कोण आहे, मला आधीच ओळखीची वाटते" अशी अंतर्जाणीव देणारी असेल. राहू-१H-केतू-१H अक्ष म्हणजे प्रत्येक भागीदारी-निर्णय कर्म-गुरुत्व बाळगतो; विवाह कधीच "उगाच एखादी मुलगी पाहून करायची" अशी गोष्ट तुम्हाला होणार नाही — तुमचा आत्मा प्रत्येक भेट पारखून घेईल, आणि जेव्हा "ती" येते तेव्हा सर्व अंतर्बाह्य ओळख-घटक एकत्र पटतात. ७व्या भावी राहू असलेल्या व्यक्तींनी पारंपरिक कुटुंब-मध्यस्थ विवाह-प्रक्रियेवर पूर्ण अवलंबून न राहता मॅट्रिमनी-व्यासपीठे, व्यावसायिक नेटवर्क, धर्म-गोष्टी-गटा, परदेशी-शिक्षण-समूह — असे एकाहून अधिक मार्ग खुले ठेवावेत; जोडीदार कोणत्या मार्गातून येईल हे आधीच ठरवणे चुकीचे ठरते. दर्मेश, तुमच्या या ७व्या भावी राहूला घाबरण्याचे कारण नाही — हा "अशुभ" राहू नाही, हा "कर्मविशेष" राहू आहे, आणि कुंभ-अंशामुळे तो आत्म-संरचना मागतो, अराजक देत नाही. विवाह उत्तम होईल, फक्त परंपरेच्या साच्यात बसवायचा प्रयत्न करू नका.

28.2 The 7th lord — Venus in 9H Sagittarius सप्तमेश शुक्र

Condition. Venus (शुक्र) is 7L here. Venus sits at 03°34′ Sagittarius Mula Pada 2, in 9H, in yuti with Moon (Chandra), Mars (Mangala), and Neptune. Venus is simultaneously 2L (dhana) — making it a double-marak (मारक) karaka.

All classical possibilities (Layer 1). A 7L's house-placement is one of the four primary marriage-determinants. Parashara's twelve-house rule for 7L: 7L in 1H = native dominant in marriage, possibly age-mismatch; 7L in 2H = spouse brings family wealth, food-connection, voice/speech-flavoured partner; 7L in 3H = spouse younger or sibling-like, possible co-worker partner; 7L in 4H = home-based marriage, spouse from same locality, emotional anchoring; 7L in 5H = love-marriage, child-blessing through spouse, dharma-trine support; 7L in 6H = service-or-medical partner but also relationship-strain or delay; 7L in 7H = own-house return, natural-marriage signature with sign-specific timing; 7L in 8H = transformation-marriage, spouse with hidden-asset or research-line, longevity-watch on spouse; 7L in 9H = dharma-fated marriage, partner from dharmic-introduction (family-alliance, guru-circle, pilgrimage, foreign-education-meeting), spouse with teaching/spiritual/foreign-cultural dimension, the union reinforces native's dharma-line — one of the most-favourable 7L placements; 7L in 10H = career-stabilizing marriage, spouse contributes publicly to native's status; 7L in 11H = friend-becomes-spouse, gain-supporting marriage; 7L in 12H = foreign-residence marriage, spouse from far-away land or with moksha-orientation, possible bed-pleasure dimension. The 7L in 9H signature in particular is described in Mantreshwara's Phaladeepika 9.27 as "bhagya-yukta-kalatra" — fortune-conjoined-wife — meaning the marriage itself becomes the vehicle through which the native's bhagya (luck/dharma) operates in the world. The shadow-rule, equally classical: when 7L is also 2L (double-marak), the planet's periods carry simultaneous marriage-activation and health-watch — the same dasha that brings the wedding can also bring health-pressure on the native or on family-elders. Saravali 35.18 names this the "vivaha-aroga-yugma" — the marriage-illness-pair — and prescribes specific upayas during such windows: Devi-puja, Maha-Mrityunjaya, donating yellow cloth to a married Brahmin couple, and avoiding major surgery or aggressive medical-decisions for elders during the same window.

Astrologer-to-client narrative (Layer 2). Dharmesh, your 7L is Venus and she sits in 9H Sagittarius Mula — and this is, in my reading-experience, one of the four or five strongest marriage-signatures a chart can carry. Let me explain what this gives you. First, 9H is the dharma-house — the house of fortune, father, guru, long-journeys, faith, higher-education. Your 7L sitting here means your partnership-line is routed through your dharma-line. Translation: you will not meet your wife at a bar, you will not meet her through a casual social-introduction designed for romance. You will meet her in a context where higher-meaning is in play — a teaching circle, a research collaboration, a pilgrimage, a guru-event, a foreign-education program, a dharmic-mentor's family-network. The meeting will feel like fortune-arriving — you will not have to chase her, the chart will deliver her at exactly the time you are dharmically ready. Second, Venus in Mula nakshatra is significant. Mula is the "root-uprooter" nakshatra — Ketu-ruled, deity Nirriti. Venus in Mula means your wife arrives precisely at a moment when an old identity-pattern is being uprooted in your life; her arrival accompanies and accelerates that uprooting. This is not loss; this is karmic-clearing that opens the next chapter. Third, the double-marak status. This is the only shadow on an otherwise beautiful signature. Venus being both 7L (marriage) and 2L (family-wealth + family-elders) means during her major windows — particularly Mars-Venus AD (Dec 2029 to Feb 2031) — the same energy that delivers the wedding may also pressure your family-elders' health, especially on the paternal side (because Sun-5L sits in 8H aspected by Saturn, doubling the Pitru-line attention). What this means practically: schedule the wedding in this window, yes, but in the same window, schedule annual health-checks for your father and any elder paternal relatives, perform the Pitru-Paksha tarpan annually, and recite Maha-Mrityunjaya 11 times daily through the entire 14 months. The marriage will happen and the elders will be largely protected; the chart simply asks you to attend to both axes consciously.

Hard verdict / forecast. Venus 7L in 9H Mula = dharma-fated, mature, fire-toned alliance; Mars-Venus AD (14-Dec-2029 → 13-Feb-2031) is the most-probable wedding window; parallel health-watch on family elders during the same 14 months; meeting context will be dharmic/research/teaching/travel rather than casual-social.

संपूर्ण मराठी विवेचन — सप्तमेश शुक्र. सप्तमेश (७वा स्वामी) कोणत्या भावात बसला आहे यावरून विवाहाचे चार-पैकी एक मुख्य निर्धारक मिळते. पाराशरांची बारा-भाव-नियमावली शुक्रासाठी अशी: शुक्र-१H = स्व-केंद्रित विवाह; २H = कुटुंब-धन-वाणी-जोडीदार; ३H = सहोदर-स्वभाव-जोडीदार; ४H = घर-केंद्रित-स्थानिक विवाह; ५H = प्रेम-विवाह, अपत्य-आशीर्वाद; ६H = सेवा-संबंध पण विलंब; ७H = स्व-गृह-नैसर्गिक; ८H = परिवर्तन-विवाह, गूढ-संशोधन-जोडीदार; ७L ९H = धर्म-निर्दिष्ट विवाह — पाराशर व मंत्रेश्वर यांच्या ग्रंथांत हे "भाग्य-युक्त-कलत्र" (फलदीपिका ९.२७) म्हणून गौरवले आहे — विवाहच भाग्याचे वाहन बनतो; १०H = कारकीर्द-स्थैर्य-विवाह; ११H = मित्र-जोडीदार-लाभ; १२H = परदेश-निवास, मोक्ष-छटा. दर्मेशचा शुक्र ९व्या भावी धनू राशीत मूळ नक्षत्र पाद २ कुंभ-अंशात बसला आहे, चंद्र-मंगळ-नेपच्यून यांच्यासोबत — हा अत्यंत प्रबळ धर्म-केंद्रित विवाह-योग आहे. व्यावहारिक अर्थ: दर्मेश, तुमची पत्नी कुठेतरी सहज-सामाजिक-मेळाव्यात नव्हे, तर एखाद्या उच्च-अर्थपूर्ण संदर्भात भेटेल — शिक्षण-वर्ग, संशोधन-सहकार्य, तीर्थयात्रा, गुरु-समूह, परदेशी-शिक्षण-कार्यक्रम, धार्मिक-गुरूच्या कुटुंब-नेटवर्क. भेट "भाग्य आले" असे जाणवेल — तुम्हाला तिचा पाठलाग करावा लागणार नाही, चार्ट तिला नेमक्या वेळी पोहोचवेल. मूळ-नक्षत्र म्हणजे "मूळ उपटणारे" नक्षत्र — केतु-शासित, निऋती-देवता; शुक्र मूळात म्हणजे एका जुन्या आत्म-ओळख-पद्धतीचे उपटून पडण्याच्या क्षणी पत्नी प्रकट होते — तिचे आगमन त्या उपटण्याला सोबत व गती देते. हे नुकसान नव्हे, हे कर्म-शुद्धीकरण आहे जे पुढच्या अध्यायाला उघडते. द्वि-मारक छाया: शुक्र २ल (धन-कुटुंब-वडीलधारे) व ७ल (विवाह) दोन्ही असल्याने मंगळ-शुक्र AD काळात (डिसें-२०२९ ते फेब्रु-२०३१) विवाह आणि कुटुंब-वडीलधार्‍यांच्या आरोग्याकडे एकाच वेळी लक्ष देणे आवश्यक — विशेषतः वडिलांच्या व पितृ-रेषेतील वडीलधार्‍यांच्या (कारण सूर्य-५ल ८व्या भावी शनी-दृष्ट आहे, पितृ-दोष असून). सारावली ३५.१८ मध्ये याला "विवाह-आरोग्य-युग्म" म्हटले आहे आणि उपाय सांगितले आहेत: देवी-पूजा, महामृत्युंजय जप, विवाहित ब्राह्मण-जोडप्याला पिवळ्या वस्त्राचे दान, आणि वडीलधार्‍यांच्या गंभीर शस्त्रक्रिया-व-आक्रमक वैद्यकीय-निर्णय शक्यतो या काळाबाहेर करावे. व्यवस्थापन-नियोजन: विवाह या काळात ठरवा, होय, पण त्याच काळात वडिलांच्या व कोणत्याही पितृ-रेषेतील वडीलधार्‍यांच्या वार्षिक आरोग्य-तपासणी, वार्षिक पितृ-पक्ष तर्पण (भाद्रपद कृष्ण पक्ष, सप्टें-ऑक्टो), आणि १४ महिने दैनिक महामृत्युंजय मंत्र ११ वेळा — हे तीन उपाय सोबत ठेवल्यास विवाह सहज होईल आणि वडीलधारे मुख्यतः सुरक्षित राहतील. चार्ट दोन्ही अक्षांकडे चेतनेने लक्ष देण्यास सांगतो — एकाला निवडून दुसर्‍याला सोडण्यास नव्हे.

28.3 Venus as kalatra-karaka कलत्र-कारक शुक्र

Condition. Beyond lordship, Venus is the natural significator of wife in a male chart — kalatra-karaka (कलत्र-कारक). Its sign, nakshatra, dignity, yutis, aspects, and house-placement describe the wife herself as a portrait — independent of the 7H/7L axis.

All classical possibilities (Layer 1). Saravali (Kalyana Varma) chapter 50, Phaladeepika chapter 12, and Jataka Parijata chapter 11 all give a kalatra-karaka portraiture method. The five reading-keys are: Venus's sign = wife's physical-and-temperamental colour (Aries = athletic-impulsive, Taurus = sensual-stable, Gemini = communicative-young, Cancer = emotional-nurturing, Leo = regal-proud, Virgo = analytical-serving, Libra = aesthetic-diplomatic, Scorpio = intense-secretive, Sagittarius = philosophical-optimistic, Capricorn = disciplined-mature, Aquarius = unconventional-friend, Pisces = devotional-sensitive); Venus's nakshatra = wife's emotional sub-current (each of the 27 nakshatras has a deity and a shakti that the wife carries); Venus's yutis = the qualities she brings into the marriage (Mars-yuti = courage and action-capability, Saturn-yuti = discipline and longevity, Jupiter-yuti = wisdom and dharma, Mercury-yuti = communication and youthfulness, Rahu-yuti = unconventional flavour, Ketu-yuti = detachment or spiritual streak, Sun-yuti = pride and authority, Moon-yuti = emotional anchoring and mother-line strength); Venus's aspects = what she receives from the world (benefic aspects = social ease, malefic aspects = struggle-tested but resilient); Venus's house-placement = the life-domain where the marriage will unfold its primary lessons. The Mula-nakshatra-Venus signature specifically (used by Tamil and Bengali traditions) gives the wife a "root-research" instinct — scholarly, depth-investigation, sometimes alternative-medicine or psychological-depth orientation, often with a slow-bloomer career-arc and a strong maturity-aging that produces compounded grace over time.

Astrologer-to-client narrative (Layer 2). Dharmesh, when I read Venus as the kalatra-karaka in your chart — independent of all the lordship-logic — a very specific portrait emerges. Venus in Sagittarius means your wife is philosophical, optimistic, naturally inclined toward big-picture thinking; she will be the kind of person who reads, who asks "why," who is interested in cultures and ideas and faiths beyond her immediate environment; she is well-travelled or travel-curious and often comes from a teacher-or-professor-or-spiritually-aware family lineage. Venus yuti Mars means she is courageous and action-capable; she will not be a passive-decorative partner; she has her own physical-and-mental forthrightness, possibly a technical or sports or martial-discipline background, and the relationship will have heat, action, and direct-speech rather than reticence. संपूर्ण मराठी विवेचन — कलत्र-कारक शुक्र. स्वामित्व सोडून, शुक्र हा पुरुष-कुंडलीत पत्नीचा नैसर्गिक कारक — कलत्र-कारक. सारावली ५०, फलदीपिका १२, जातक पारिजात ११ या तिन्ही ग्रंथांत पाच वाचन-कीज दिल्या आहेत: शुक्राची राशी = पत्नीचा शारीरिक-मानसिक रंग; शुक्राचे नक्षत्र = तिचा भावनिक उप-प्रवाह (२७ नक्षत्रांच्या देवता व शक्ती); शुक्राची युती = ती विवाहात कोणते गुण आणते (मंगळ-युती = धैर्य-कृति, शनी-युती = शिस्त-दीर्घायुष्य, गुरु-युती = ज्ञान-धर्म, बुध-युती = संवाद-तरुणाई, राहू-युती = अपारंपरिक-छटा, केतु-युती = वैराग्य-अध्यात्म, सूर्य-युती = अभिमान-अधिकार, चंद्र-युती = भावना-मातृ-रेखा); शुक्रावरील दृष्टी = ती जगाकडून काय मिळवते; शुक्राचे भाव-स्थान = विवाह कोणत्या जीवन-क्षेत्रात आपले धडे शिकवेल. दर्मेश, तुमचा शुक्र धनू राशीत मूळ-नक्षत्र पाद २ मकर-अंशात — पत्नी म्हणजे: तत्त्वज्ञान-प्रवण, आशावादी, मोठ्या-चित्राकडे नैसर्गिक झुकाव असलेली; ती सुंदरपणे वयस्क होते (मकर-अंशामुळे दीर्घ-काल-लवचिकता), आणि नात्याचा सौंदर्य-वक्र उलटा — प्रारंभी शांत-बद्ध, मध्यात खोलावत, उत्तरार्धात तेजस्वी. शुक्र-मंगळ युती म्हणजे ती धैर्यवान-कृति-क्षम; निष्क्रिय-शोभेची भागीदार नाही; तिचा स्वतःचा शारीरिक-मानसिक स्पष्टपणा, तांत्रिक-क्रीडा-शिस्त पार्श्वभूमी शक्य; नात्यात उष्णता-कृति-थेट-बोलणे राहील. शुक्र-चंद्र युती म्हणजे ती भावनिक-स्थैर्य आणते; भावनिक-कलात्मक-आर्थिक जीवन एकमेकांत गुंतलेले; संशोधन-शास्त्रीय धागा, तत्त्वज्ञ-आशावादी पृष्ठ, भावनिक-स्थैर्य गाभा; ती तुमच्या धर्म-रेषेला सामील होते, तिच्यापासून ती तुम्हाला वळवत नाही; सुंदर वयस्क होते; विवाह सौंदर्य-संचित करतो, प्रारंभी जळून संपवत नाही.

28.4 D9 Navamsa — marriage indicators नवांश-कुंडली

Condition. D9 lagna is Gemini (Mercury-ruled). In D9, Venus sits in 11H Aries with Saturn; Jupiter sits in 10H Pisces (own sign — exalted in some traditions); Mars and Ketu are in 11H. The D9-7H is Sagittarius, ruled by Jupiter who sits in D9-10H.

All classical possibilities (Layer 1). The D9 (Navamsa, नवांश) is regarded by Parashara as the chart's strongest secondary — when D1 and D9 disagree, D9 generally wins for marriage, dharma, and the second-half of life. The Navamsa is computed by dividing each rashi into nine sub-parts of 3°20′ each and re-projecting the planet into the resulting nine-fold zodiac. Six classical reading-rules for D9 in marriage analysis: (1) D9 lagna and its lord reveal the dharmic-self that enters the marriage — the inner-self that walks into the wedded life; (2) D9-7H sign and occupants reveal the spouse's underlying dharmic-nature, deeper than the social-persona shown in D1-7H; (3) D9-7L and its placement reveal what the spouse does as a soul-function in the marriage; (4) Venus in D9 reveals the relational climate after marriage — the texture of intimacy, the daily-emotional rhythm; (5) Atma-Karaka in D9 (the Karakamsa Lagna) names the spiritual-vocation of the marriage itself; (6) Jupiter in D9 is the marriage-blessing indicator — strong Jupiter in D9 (own-sign, exalted, in kendra-trine) is one of the classical "good-marriage" signatures, indicating wise spouse, dharmic union, and protection from major marital crises. The Vargottama principle (a planet in the same sign in D1 and D9) doubles a planet's strength; the Pushkar-amsha and Pushkar-navamsa rules further refine. A D9 with strong own-sign or exalted Jupiter generally indicates a marriage that survives major life-tests; a D9 with debilitated 7L or 7L in dusthana without rescue indicates strain or separation potential. Parashara's "marriage-rescue" rule: even when D1 indicates difficulty, a strong D9-7H or D9-Venus or D9-Jupiter can fully rescue and convert the marriage into a stabilized union.

Astrologer-to-client narrative (Layer 2). Dharmesh, in my own consultation-practice, the D9 chart is where I look last for marriage — because it is the deciding evidence. Everything I have shown you so far in D1 — Libra-Rahu 7H, Venus 7L 9H, kalatra-karaka portrait — could in theory be undermined by a weak D9. So let me show you what the D9 actually says. This is the single strongest D9-signature your chart could carry. It is the classical "blessed marriage" signal — wisdom-orientation, ethical anchoring, protection from major marital crises, dharma-grounding of the wedded life. Your D9 Venus sits in 11H Aries with Saturn. Venus in 11H of D9 places your wife-significator in the gains-house — meaning the marriage materially benefits both of you, generates income, expands your friend-circle, increases social standing — but only through Saturnian patience (because Saturn is right there with Venus). Translation: the marriage will bring you both wealth and recognition, but it will not be a quick-rich, glamour-marriage; the gains compound over years through disciplined joint-effort. Mars-Mars is the cleanest, most-self-aligned period of the entire Mahadasha — the self (Mars) running on its own time (Mars), so the native feels coherent, decisive, capable of forming attachments without internal contradiction. Rahu's nature is to magnify everything it touches in the early phase and then withdraw, leaving the native staring at what looks like a hollow space where intensity had been. This is the universal Rahu-AD signature. But in this specific chart, Rahu's effect on relationship is sharper than usual for one critical reason: Rahu sits natally in the 7th house. The 7th house is the partner-house, so when Mars (the MD-lord and the self) activates Rahu (as AD-lord and as 7H occupant), the partner-image itself becomes Rahu-coloured. This is why the natural arc of Mars-Rahu AD on a chart with Rahu in 7H is: intense connection → period of confusion or distance → sudden detachment or shattering. The break is not because the partner is wrong; the break is because the partner-house is currently being held in Rahu-energy, which is fundamentally an energy of de-coupling-after-coupling. Beyond the dasha-mechanism, the transit overlay from late 2025 onwards has been actively pressing the same axis. By April-May 2026, with Mars-Rahu AD still running (it concludes 05-Jul-2026), and transit Saturn still pressing 9H from Pisces, and transit Rahu now in Aquarius pressing natal Saturn in 11H — the structural forces converge on a break. The astrologer must name this clearly: this break was not a personal failure, not a wrong choice, not a karmic punishment. It was a Rahu-shadow event riding on Saturn-pressure, occurring in the one AD inside Mars MD that was structurally hostile to relationship-continuity. The pain is real; the structural cause is identifiable; the same pain in the same AD would have visited any chart with this same Rahu-in-7H signature.
