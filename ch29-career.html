Career कर्म-भाव — व्यवसाय, आजीविका व आत्मा-कारक

Career (कर्म — karma, "action") in Jyotish is the most-asked question and the most-singular wrong-answer-prone one. No single house decides it. Classical Parashari (Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra ch 26-27), Phaladeepika (Mantreshwara ch 14), Saravali (Kalyana Varma ch 35-37), Jataka Parijata (Vaidyanatha ch 9), and Jaimini Sutras all converge on a five-evidence triangulation: the 10th house (कर्म-स्थान — the bhava of action and public-recognition), the 10th lord (karmesha — where the career-engine is deployed), the Atma-Karaka (आत्मा-कारक — the soul-significator under Jaimini, the planet at highest longitudinal degree), the D10 Dasamsa (दशांश — the career-only divisional chart), and the running Mahadasha-lord's relation to the career-houses. Beyond these five, the secondary evidences are: 2H (income/family-wealth), 6H (service/employment), 11H (gains/fulfillment-of-desires), Sun and Saturn (natural karakas of authority and work-discipline), Mercury and Jupiter (natural karakas of business and wisdom-vocation), and the D24 Chaturvimsamsa (education/skill). For Dharmesh, the chart's career-engine is Mars: lagnesh (1L) + 8L + Darakaraka + current MD-lord — four roles converge in one planet. The career trajectory unfolds in two arcs: Mars MD (19-Jan-2025 → 19-Jan-2032, identity-action consolidation) → Rahu MD (19-Jan-2032 → 19-Jan-2050, status-leap and foreign-or-unconventional expansion) → Jupiter MD (2050 onwards, soul-vocation ripening). This chapter walks every classical career-yoga, every Raja-yoga and Dhana-yoga that fires, every AD-by-AD timing window in Mars MD, then converts the classical machinery into astrologer-to-client narrative for consultation.

29.1 The 10th house — its significations दशम भाव

Condition. 10H here is Capricorn (Saturn-ruled, earthy, cardinal), occupied by Uranus at 03°51′ Uttarashadha Pada 3. No classical Vedic planet sits in 10H; the cusp itself is structurally activated only by the modern outer-planet Uranus and by the 10L Saturn's distant rulership from 11H.

All classical possibilities (Layer 1). The 10H (दशम भाव — karma-bhava) governs profession, public-recognition, authority, social status, career-action, the father (alternate karaka after 9H), the kind of work the world rewards the native for, and the visible-fruit of all earlier-life effort. Parashara's three-angle rule for 10H reads: (a) the sign-significations of the rashi occupying it, (b) the lordship-deployment of the 10L, (c) the condition (dignity, aspects, yutis) of any planet actually sitting in 10H. Classical readings of each sign as 10H rashi: Aries-10H = pioneering-leadership, engineering, military, sports, surgery; Taurus-10H = stable-establishment-careers, banking, real-estate, agriculture, food; Gemini-10H = communication, writing, journalism, IT-software, sales; Cancer-10H = nurturing-professions, healthcare, hospitality, mother-care, water-related; Leo-10H = authority-positions, government, management, performance-arts, gold; Virgo-10H = analytics, accounting, healthcare, service, detail-work; Libra-10H = law, diplomacy, design, partnership-businesses, luxury; Scorpio-10H = research, investigation, surgery, occult, transformation-industries; Sagittarius-10H = teaching, dharma-work, foreign-collaboration, law, banking; Capricorn-10H = the most Saturnine career-signature — structured, hierarchical, slow-build, accountability-heavy, often in established institutions, government, engineering, real-estate, mining, oil-and-gas, traditional manufacturing, civil service, or law-and-administration; Aquarius-10H = unconventional careers, technology, innovation, social-impact, group-leadership; Pisces-10H = devotional-creative-healing professions, foreign-residence careers, photography, music, hospitality. Uranus in 10H (modern Vedic-Western synthesis) adds a distinct signature: technology, innovation, system-design, IT-or-software-architecture, disruption-industries, sudden role-changes, freelance/consulting structures, and an instinct for "the next thing" rather than the established thing. Combined Capricorn-rashi + Uranus-occupant = structured-technology — engineering-IT, system-architecture, technology-consulting, fintech, deep-tech, or research-engineering. The Uttarashadha-Pada-3 nakshatra-pada places Uranus in Capricorn-amsha (Saturn doubled) — reinforcing the structural discipline. The deity of Uttarashadha is Vishvadevas (universal gods) and its shakti is "the power of unchallenged victory" — careers driven from this nakshatra deliver lasting recognition rather than quick fame.

Astrologer-to-client narrative (Layer 2). Dharmesh, when an astrologer opens your chart and looks at the 10H, three signals show simultaneously. First, the rashi is Capricorn — Saturn's own sign — which immediately tells me the career-line is going to be Saturn-textured: slow-build, accountability-heavy, structured, recognition-arriving-late-but-permanent. This is not the chart of a "viral overnight success"; this is the chart of "the engineer who is quietly known across the industry by year-12." Second, no classical Vedic planet sits in 10H — which means the 10H is read primarily through its lord Saturn and through Uranus as the modern occupant. The empty-Capricorn 10H without natal occupants typically requires the native to consciously construct the career; it does not auto-launch. The combination of "Capricorn 10H + Uranus + 10L Saturn elsewhere" reads as: the public-action engine is a built-thing, not an inherited-thing. Third, Uranus in 10H is the chart's strongest modern-vocation signal. The native will be drawn to technology, system-design, IT-software, engineering-automation, deep-tech, or unconventional structures (freelance, consulting, founder-track). Importantly, the Saturn-base under the Uranus-overlay means the technology-work must be coupled with patience — quick-disruption startups will exhaust you faster than slow-disciplined system-building careers. The Uttarashadha-Pada-3 placement adds Capricorn-amsha discipline; the deity Vishvadevas signals work that has a "service-to-many" dimension. Practically: build a technical-or-research career inside a structured organization for the first decade, then optionally leap into independent practice or founding role in the Rahu MD years.

Hard verdict / forecast. 10H Capricorn-Uranus + 10L Saturn own-sign 11H = structured technology-or-research career, slow-build, gain-converting, recognition arrives in 30s and consolidates in 40s; Uranus signals tech/innovation/system-design; Saturn-base demands patience and accountability; first decade in structured-institution, optional founder-shift in Rahu MD (2032 onwards).

संपूर्ण मराठी विवेचन — दशम भाव. दशम भाव (कर्म-स्थान) कुंडलीच्या तीन सर्वाधिक महत्त्वाच्या भावांपैकी एक — व्यवसाय, सार्वजनिक ओळख, अधिकार, सामाजिक स्थान, कर्म-कृति, वडील (९व्या नंतरचा दुय्यम कारक), आणि जगात "तू काय आहेस" याचे दृश्य-प्रकटीकरण. बृहत् पाराशर होरा शास्त्र अध्याय २६-२७, फलदीपिका अध्याय १४, सारावली अध्याय ३५-३७, जातक पारिजात अध्याय ९ या ग्रंथांत १०व्या भावाचे वाचन तीन कोनांतून सांगितले आहे: (अ) त्यात बसलेल्या राशीचे फल, (ब) दशमेशाचे स्थान-व-अवस्था, (क) १०व्यात बसलेल्या ग्रहाची दशा-दृष्टि-युति. प्रत्येक राशीचे १०H म्हणून शास्त्रीय फल: मेष = अग्रणी-नेतृत्व, अभियांत्रिकी, सेना, शस्त्रक्रिया; वृषभ = बँकिंग, स्थावर मालमत्ता, कृषी; मिथुन = संवाद, पत्रकारिता, IT, विक्री; कर्क = आरोग्यसेवा, आदरातिथ्य; सिंह = अधिकार-पद, सरकार, कला; कन्या = विश्लेषण, लेखा, सेवा; तूळ = कायदा, राजनय, सौंदर्य; वृश्चिक = संशोधन, गूढ, परिवर्तन; धनू = शिक्षण, धर्म-कार्य, कायदा; मकर = सर्वात शनी-स्वरूपी कर्म-संकेत — संरचित, श्रेणीबद्ध, हळू-विकास, जबाबदारी-केंद्रित, सरकार-संस्था-अभियांत्रिकी; कुंभ = अपारंपरिक-तंत्रज्ञान-सामाजिक; मीन = भक्ती-कला-उपचार-परदेश. दर्मेशच्या कुंडलीत १०H मकर राशी आहे, आणि त्यात युरेनस ०३°५१′ उत्तराषाढा पाद ३ बसला आहे — कोणताही शास्त्रीय वैदिक ग्रह १०व्या भावी नाही. आधुनिक वैदिक-पाश्चात्त्य संमिश्र वाचनात युरेनस १०व्या भावी = तंत्रज्ञान, नवकल्पना, यंत्रणा-संरचना, IT-सॉफ्टवेअर-स्थापत्य, विघटन-उद्योग, अचानक भूमिका-बदल, फ्रीलान्स/सल्लागार रचना, "पुढची गोष्ट" शोधण्याची प्रवृत्ती. एकत्रित मकर + युरेनस = संरचित-तंत्रज्ञान. उत्तराषाढा पाद ३ मकर-अंशात (शनी-दुप्पट) म्हणजे संरचनात्मक शिस्तीचा द्विगुणित ठसा. नक्षत्र-देवता विश्वदेव (सर्व देव एकत्र) आणि शक्ती "अप्रतिहत-विजय" — येथून येणारी कारकीर्द क्षणिक-प्रसिद्धी न देता दीर्घ-काल-टिकणारी ओळख देते. दर्मेश, तुमची कारकीर्द "रात्रीत प्रसिद्ध होणारी" प्रकारची नाही — ती "बारा-वर्षांत संपूर्ण उद्योग-क्षेत्रात शांतपणे ओळखले जाणारे अभियंता" प्रकारची आहे. १०व्या भावी कोणताही वैदिक ग्रह नसल्याने कारकीर्द आपोआप-सुरू होत नाही — ती तुम्हाला जाणीवपूर्वक बांधावी लागते. युरेनस-छटा तुम्हाला तंत्रज्ञान, यंत्रणा-डिझाइन, IT-सॉफ्टवेअर, अभियांत्रिकी-स्वयंचलन, खोल-तंत्रज्ञान, किंवा अपारंपरिक-रचना (फ्रीलान्स, सल्लागार, संस्थापक-मार्ग) यांकडे ओढेल; पण शनी-पाया धैर्य मागतो — झटपट-विघटन स्टार्टअप्स तुम्हाला थकवतील, हळू-शिस्तबद्ध यंत्रणा-निर्माण कारकीर्द तुम्हाला पोषतील. व्यावहारिक मार्ग: पहिले दशक संरचित-संस्थेत तांत्रिक-संशोधन कारकीर्द उभारा, मग राहू-MD वर्षांत (२०३२ नंतर) स्वतंत्र-व्यवसाय किंवा संस्थापक-भूमिकेकडे झेप घ्या. ३०व्या वर्षी ओळख दिसू लागेल, ४०व्या वर्षी ती दृढ होईल, ५०व्या वर्षी ती अप्रतिहत-विजय रूपात स्थापित होईल — विश्वदेव-नक्षत्राच्या शक्तीशी पूर्ण सुसंगत.

29.2 The 10th lord — Saturn in 11H own-sign दशमेश शनी

Condition. The 10L is Saturn (शनि). Saturn sits at 24°11′ Aquarius Purvabhadra Pada 2, in 11H — own sign. Saturn's 10th-aspect from 11H falls on 8H Scorpio (where the Sun-Mercury-Jupiter-Pluto stellium sits). Saturn is simultaneously 10L (career) + 11L (gains), making it a double-yoga-anchor for Aries lagna.

All classical possibilities (Layer 1). The 10L's house-placement is one of the four primary career-determinants. Parashara's twelve-house rule for 10L: 10L in 1H = native is the career (self-employed, founder, identity = work); 10L in 2H = career generates direct family-wealth, food-or-voice profession; 10L in 3H = career in writing, communication, technical-skill, courage-based work; 10L in 4H = career rooted in home, real-estate, education, mother-related fields; 10L in 5H = career involves children-creativity-speculation, education, entertainment, investment; 10L in 6H = service-employment-medical-legal careers, opposition-overcome work; 10L in 7H = partnership-led career, public-dealings, foreign business; 10L in 8H = research-investigation-transformation careers, insurance, occult, surgery, longevity-medicine; 10L in 9H = teaching-dharma-foreign career, law, philosophy, publishing; 10L in 10H = self-owning kendra, very strong public-action; 10L in 11H = one of the most classically auspicious combinations — career-action directly converts into gains, the work-output feeds the income-channel without intermediate loss, friend-and-network gain through profession, group-leadership; 10L in 12H = foreign-career, behind-the-scenes work, expenditure-related careers (hotels, hospitals, ashrams). The 10L in 11H own-sign compounds the signature: not only does career-action convert to gains, but the gains-channel itself is Saturn-disciplined (long-arc compounding rather than quick-spikes). Classical texts list this as a component of Dhana-yoga (wealth-yoga formed when 10L and 11L combine; here they are the same planet — auto-fulfilling the yoga). Additionally, Saturn in own-sign in any kendra/trikona/upachaya is the foundation of Sasa Mahapurusha Yoga (शश-महापुरुष — one of the Pancha Mahapurusha yogas). The strict definition requires Saturn in kendra (1/4/7/10), but liberal interpretations include strong upachaya (11H qualifies as growth-house). Sasa Mahapurusha gives: gravitas, longevity, structural-discipline-mastery, leadership in established hierarchies, accumulated wealth, and a strong reputation in mature years. Saturn's 10th-aspect from 11H onto 8H is the chart's primary research-career-channel — the career-engine routes deeply into the 8H stellium (Sun, Mercury, Jupiter, Pluto), confirming research-or-deep-tech-or-investigation as a dominant career-flavour.
