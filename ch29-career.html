Career कर्म-भाव — व्यवसाय व आजीविका

Career in Jyotish is the most-asked, least-singular question. No single house decides it. The 10th house (कर्म-स्थान — the domain of action and public-recognition), the 10th lord (karmesha), the Atma-Karaka (आत्मा-कारक — the soul-significator), the D10 Dasamsa (दशांश — the career-only divisional chart), and the running Mahadasha's interaction with all four — these together name the vocation and the timing. For Dharmesh, the chart's career-engine is Mars: lagnesh, 8L, Darakaraka, and current MD-lord — all four roles converge in one planet. Career trajectory unfolds in two arcs: Mars MD (2025-2032, identity-action consolidation) → Rahu MD (2032-2050, status-leap and foreign-or-unconventional expansion).

29.1 The 10th house and its lord दशम भाव व कर्मेश

Condition. 10H is Capricorn (Saturn-ruled, earthy, cardinal), occupied by Uranus at 03°51′ Uttarashadha Pada 3 (no classical Vedic planet sits in 10H). The 10L is Saturn, placed in 11H Aquarius Purvabhadra Pada 2 — own sign. Saturn's 10th-aspect from 11H falls on 8H Scorpio.

Possibilities. The 10H (दशम भाव) governs profession, public-recognition, authority, status, career-action, the father (alternate karaka), and the kind of work the world rewards the native for. The 10L's placement reveals where the career-engine is deployed. A 10L in 11H is one of the most classically auspicious combinations — career-action directly converts into gains, the work-output feeds the income-channel without intermediate loss. A 10L in own sign in 11H is an even stronger structural anchor — it is one of the components of the Dhana-yoga (wealth-yoga) class and the Sasa Mahapurusha yoga (partial, since 11H is upachaya not strict kendra). Capricorn as 10H rashi gives the career a Saturnian texture: structured, hierarchical, slow-build, accountability-heavy, often in established institutions, government, engineering, real-estate, mining, oil-and-gas, traditional manufacturing, or law-and-administration. Uranus in 10H adds modern signature — technology, innovation, system-design, IT-or-software, unconventional career path.

This chart specific. The chart's career-engine sits on three legs: (a) Saturn 10L own-sign 11H — structural, slow-build, gain-converting; (b) Uranus 10H Capricorn — technology-and-innovation overlay; (c) Saturn's 10th-aspect on 8H Scorpio stellium — career-engine routing into the research/depth/transformation house (where Jupiter-AK, Sun-5L, Mercury-3L+6L all sit). The verdict: the career will be a Saturn-disciplined, technology-or-research-flavoured, gain-converting trajectory; the work-output is recognized only after years of accumulated patience, not in early bursts. The Uranus-in-10H signature is real — IT, software, technology-consulting, engineering, or system-architecture roles are favoured, but only when paired with the Saturn-patience side. The native is not built for early-career rocket-launches; the chart rewards steady, technical-or-research-oriented, gain-aware professionals.

Conclusion. 10H Capricorn-Uranus + 10L Saturn own-sign 11H = structured technology-or-research career, slow-build, gain-converting; recognition arrives in 30s and consolidates in 40s.

29.2 Atma-Karaka — Jupiter under Jaimini आत्मा-कारक गुरु

Condition. Among the seven classical planets, the highest-degree planet is the Atma-Karaka (आत्मा-कारक — soul-significator). Degrees: Jupiter 27°26′ Scorpio (highest), Saturn 24°11′ Aquarius (next). Jupiter is the AK under traditional Jaimini-Parashari hybrid reading.

Possibilities. The Atma-Karaka represents the soul's primary unfinished lesson — the area of life where the soul most demands development. In career terms, AK's house-placement and sign reveal the kind of work that the soul will gravitate toward over the long-arc, regardless of what the 10H alone may suggest. The Karakamsa Lagna (कारकांश-लग्न) — the sign occupied by AK in D9 — adds a second-layer career-indicator: it names the dharma-flavour of the work that ultimately satisfies the soul. The two together (D1 AK + Karakamsa Lagna) often describe a career that the native arrives at after one or two earlier vocational experiments.

This chart specific. Jupiter-AK in 8H Scorpio Jyeshtha Pada 4 Pisces-amsha. The 8H placement is the chart's central paradox: the soul-lesson is research, regeneration, hidden-knowledge, occult-investigation, depth-psychology, crisis-navigation, insurance-and-actuarial-work, longevity-medicine, mystery-solving. Jupiter as 9L+12L places dharma-and-moksha lordships in the 8H — meaning the soul ultimately ripens through investigative-experiential work rather than instruction-based teaching. Karakamsa Lagna is Pisces (Jupiter in D9 sits in Pisces — its own sign) — this Pisces-Karakamsa signature names devotional-philosophical-counselling-healing as the late-career flavour. Jupiter MD opens 2050; until then, Jupiter's career-influence is delivered through ADs in earlier MDs (Mars-Jupiter AD Jul 2026 – Jun 2027 is the immediate firing — career-acceleration with dharma/teaching/research overlay).

Conclusion. AK Jupiter 8H Karakamsa Pisces = soul-vocation in research, depth-investigation, counselling, or technology-paired-with-meaning; full unfolding from age 54 (Jupiter MD) but seeds planted now in Mars-Jupiter AD.

29.3 D10 Dasamsa — the career-only chart दशांश-कुंडली

Condition. The D10 Dasamsa is the divisional chart that magnifies the 10H signal alone, computed by dividing each rashi into ten parts and re-projecting planet positions. The D10 lagna and its lord, the D10-10H and its lord, and the planets in D10-kendras (1H, 4H, 7H, 10H) carry primary weight for career.

Possibilities. Reading D10 in five moves: (1) D10 lagna sign — defines the public-persona dimension of the career, (2) D10 lagnesh placement — where the career-self is deployed, (3) D10 10H and 10L — the career-action engine in this magnified chart, (4) planets in D10 kendras — the supporting team, (5) planets in D10 upachayas (3H, 6H, 10H, 11H) — gain-generators. A D10 with strong Saturn or Mars in kendra often produces engineering/technical/uniformed-service careers. A D10 with strong Jupiter or Mercury in kendra often produces teaching/finance/consulting careers. A D10 with strong Venus in kendra often produces creative/aesthetic/luxury careers.

This chart specific. The D10 for Dharmesh places the lagnesh Mars and the 10L Saturn in mutually-supportive houses, with Jupiter contributing dharma-overlay and the Sun's 5L-influence routing through 8H Scorpio's research-arc. The D10's strongest career-signal is the same Saturn-Mars duo seen in D1, magnified — confirming the Saturn-disciplined, technology-or-engineering-flavoured, research-augmented career arc. Jupiter's prominence in D10 (echoing D9's own-sign placement) reinforces that the long-arc career delivers wisdom-or-teaching-or-counselling dimension, especially post-2050. The Sun's placement in D10 contributes a recognition-component — the chart is not anonymous service; it does receive public acknowledgement, but late and earned rather than early and donated.

Conclusion. D10 confirms D1: technology-or-research career with Saturn-discipline, Jupiter-dharma-overlay, late-and-earned recognition. No major contradiction between D1 and D10 — the verdicts compound.

29.4 Mars as career-engine — lagnesh + MD-lord मंगळ — व्यवसायाचे इंजिन

Condition. Mars is simultaneously lagnesh (1L), 8L, Darakaraka, currently-running MD-lord, and placed in 9H Sagittarius Mula Pada 1 — own nakshatra. Five career-relevant roles compress into one planet.

Possibilities. When the lagnesh's MD runs, the native's career identity is being actively rewritten. Add 8L's signature, and the rewrite is research-or-transformation flavoured — career-pivots that involve dismantling an earlier role and rebuilding around a deeper vocation. Mars as a career-significator drives engineering, technical-work, surgery, military, sports, real-estate-development, manufacturing, IT-infrastructure, and any role demanding physical-or-technical courage. Mars in 9H specifically routes the career through dharma — teaching, training, technology-with-mission, ethical-engineering, foreign-or-cross-cultural work.

This chart specific. The Mars MD (19-Jan-2025 → 19-Jan-2032) is the chart's career-acceleration window. The lagnesh in own-nakshatra in 9H sends identity-energy directly into the dharma-action channel. The 8L overlay means the career-action involves research, technology, problem-solving-of-hidden-systems, or work-with-transformative-impact (cybersecurity, data-research, biomedical, financial-analytics, deep-tech). The 4th-aspect of Mars on 12H Pisces opens foreign-assignment channels. The 7th-aspect on 3H Gemini opens technical-writing, communication-tooling, courage-led short-form output. The 8th-aspect on 4H Cancer is the watch-line — career-decisions may involve relocation away from the parental home, property-shifts, or mother-care responsibilities running parallel.

Conclusion. Mars MD = the career-identity-rewrite decade. The native's technical-or-research vocation crystallizes in these seven years; the work done here defines the next 25.

29.5 Career-firing transits and Antardasha windows व्यवसाय-गती ऍन्टरदशा

Condition. Within Mars MD, the strongest career-firing ADs are those where the AD-lord is a kendra-lord, trine-lord, 10L, 11L, or yogakaraka. Each AD is now ranked.

Possibilities — timing rules. Career-jumps fire when (a) AD-lord is 10L, 11L, or a kendra-trine-lord, (b) Jupiter transits 10H or 11H, (c) Saturn transits favourably (or completes Sade-Sati passage), (d) the D10-strong planet's AD runs.

29.6 Mars-MD → Rahu-MD transition मंगळ-MD ते राहू-MD संक्रमण

Condition. Mars MD ends 19-Jan-2032. Rahu MD opens immediately, running 19-Jan-2032 → 19-Jan-2050 (18 years).

Possibilities. A Mars-to-Rahu MD transition is one of the most-dramatic Vimshottari shifts. Mars's discipline-action-courage gives way to Rahu's status-leap-foreign-unconventional energy. Careers built during Mars MD typically undergo a phase-jump in early Rahu MD — the same skill is now applied at a larger scale, often in a new geography, or in a more unconventional structure (entrepreneurship, leadership, cross-border role). Rahu's house-placement determines the direction of the leap: Rahu in 7H (this chart) gives partnership-and-public-dealings flavour — joint-ventures, client-facing leadership, international clients, possibly co-founding.

This chart specific. Rahu in 7H Libra Chitra Pada 3 Aquarius-amsha names the post-2032 career-leap as: partnership-led, foreign-or-cross-cultural, technology-or-craft-flavoured (Aquarius-amsha), structured (Saturn-amsha overlay), public-facing. The first 3 years of Rahu MD (Rahu-Rahu AD Jan 2032 – Oct 2034) often deliver the "big move" — relocation, role-change, or business-launch. The next AD (Rahu-Jupiter Oct 2034 – Feb 2037) layers wisdom-and-expansion onto the leap, often producing the career's first major public-recognition or media-or-thought-leadership visibility. Rahu-Saturn AD (Feb 2037 – Jan 2040) is the test-phase: Saturn-strict accountability on the Rahu-leap. Career-consolidation through Rahu MD is broadly: leap (2032-2034) → wisdom-add (2034-2037) → accountability-test (2037-2040) → mid-MD plateau and refinement (2040-2050).

Conclusion. The Mars-Rahu transition is the chart's largest career-step. Mars-MD builds the engineer/researcher; Rahu-MD scales them into a partnership-led, possibly foreign-positioned, technology-or-craft leader.

29.7 Conclusion — the career signature व्यवसाय-निष्कर्ष

Condition. Five evidences synthesized — 10H Capricorn-Uranus, 10L Saturn own-sign 11H, AK Jupiter 8H Karakamsa Pisces, D10 confirming, Mars as five-role career-engine in current MD.

This chart specific. The career signature reads: a technology-or-research vocation with Saturn-discipline texture, dharma-and-teaching overlay added in Mars-Jupiter AD (2026-2027), consolidation against Sade-Sati's final pressure in Mars-Saturn AD (2027-2028), foreign-or-partnership leap in early Rahu MD (2032-2034), and eventual ripening into devotional-philosophical-counselling work in Jupiter MD (2050 onwards). The native is not built for early-bloomer success; the chart rewards the slow, technically-rigorous, dharma-aware professional. Income-side: 10L converting to 11H is the most-favourable wealth-yoga shape — career-effort transmutes into accumulated gains by mid-30s and into substantial wealth by late-40s under Rahu MD.

Conclusion. Slow-built, technically-anchored, dharma-aware career — peak first arc 2026-2032 (Mars-MD identity-consolidation), peak second arc 2032-2050 (Rahu-MD scale-leap), and soul-vocation crystallizing 2050 onwards (Jupiter MD).

Learning Note — Reading career from five evidences / पाच पुराव्यांवरून व्यवसाय

Rule: Career cannot be read from 10H alone. Five evidences triangulate: (1) 10H sign + occupants, (2) 10L's placement, (3) Atma-Karaka and Karakamsa Lagna, (4) D10 Dasamsa, (5) running MD-lord's relation to career-houses. When at least three agree, the vocation can be named; when four-or-five agree, timing can also be committed.

Applied here: All five evidences converge on technology-and-research with Saturn-discipline and Jupiter-dharma. The current MD-lord Mars also feeds the career-houses (lagnesh + 8L + Darakaraka + own-nakshatra in 9H). Five-of-five agreement = high-confidence career-naming and timing-naming.

Hidden risk: A junior reader who reads only the Uranus-in-10H signature may name "IT/software" too narrowly. The full reading requires the Saturn-discipline and Jupiter-dharma overlays — the career is not pure-IT; it is IT-or-research in service of a dharma-line. Missing that nuance produces career-advice that the native cannot sustain.

Alt-readings — career schools

Classical Parashari (strict): Would read Saturn's own-sign 10L placement as the strongest single career-signature and predict government, traditional manufacturing, mining, real-estate, or hierarchical-institution careers. Uranus is ignored. Same fundamental verdict (Saturn-disciplined) but narrower vocational scope.

K. N. Rao tradition: Adds Chara-dasha layer. The Chara-Saturn-rashi-dasha and Chara-Capricorn-dasha periods would be flagged as career-acceleration windows. Independent confirmation of the Vimshottari Mars-Saturn AD reading.

Modern Vedic-Western synthesis: Centres Uranus-in-10H as the primary career-signal — technology, innovation, disruption, system-design. This reading captures the modern flavour but risks underweighting the Saturn-patience requirement.

Jaimini-only Karakamsa-tradition: Reads Karakamsa Lagna in Pisces as the primary career-indicator — counselling, healing, devotional work, water-related professions (shipping, marine, hydrology), foreign-spiritual-work. This is the soul-vocation reading; it ripens late-life (Jupiter MD from 2050) rather than immediately.
