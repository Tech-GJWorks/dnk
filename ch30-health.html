Health आरोग्य — षष्ठ, अष्टम, द्वादश व लग्नेश

Health in Jyotish is read across four axes: the trika houses (6H — disease/त्रिक, 8H — chronic conditions/longevity, 12H — hospital/loss/dissolution), the lagna and lagna-lord (the body itself and its constitutional vitality), the dosha-load (Vata-Pitta-Kapha imbalances signalled by planet groupings), and the dasha-transit overlay that names the windows when latent vulnerability surfaces. For Dharmesh, the chart shows a strong constitutional foundation (Mars lagnesh in own-nakshatra) shadowed by a heavy 8H stellium (Sun, Mercury-combust, Jupiter, Pluto), Mars-Venus yuti's Manglik-axis, and Sade-Sati Phase 3 currently running over the Moon. Health windows can be named with precision.

30.1 The trika houses — 6H, 8H, 12H त्रिक भाव

Condition. 6H is Virgo (Mercury-ruled), empty of classical planets, lord Mercury in 8H combust. 8H is Scorpio (Mars-ruled), occupied by Sun, Mercury-combust, Jupiter, Pluto — a four-planet stellium. 12H is Pisces (Jupiter-ruled), empty of classical planets, lord Jupiter in 8H Scorpio.

Possibilities. The 6H (षष्ठ भाव) governs acute illness, daily-routine-health, immunity, enemies, debts, and service-work. The 8H (अष्टम भाव) governs chronic conditions, longevity, sudden events (accidents, surgeries), insurance, inheritance, and hidden transformation. The 12H (द्वादश भाव) governs hospitalization, sleep, foreign-residence, expenditure, and dissolution-events. An empty 6H is broadly favourable (no enemies sitting in the enemy-house), but the 6L's placement determines if the daily-health-and-service channel functions cleanly. An 8H stellium concentrates the chart's transformation-and-research load — the body becomes the laboratory where deep change happens, often via crisis-events that mark the life. A 12L in 8H ties hospitalization-and-expenditure into the chronic-transformation house — surgery or extended treatment is a possibility when 8H or 12H is fired.

This chart specific. The 4-planet 8H stellium is the chart's most-watched health signature. Sun-8H is associated with vitality-mute, heart-and-eye-watch from mid-life. Mercury-combust 8H is associated with nervous-system-overload, anxiety, gastric-ulcer-prone digestion, and speech-suppression under stress. Jupiter-8H is the rescue — natural benefic in dusthana provides karma-cushion (Viparita-Raja-yoga partial), liver-and-pancreas significations preserved, recovery-capacity preserved. Pluto-8H adds depth-psychology and regenerative-medicine resonance — recovery from major events is possible because Pluto's regeneration-archetype is structurally available. The 6L Mercury combust in 8H gives Vipareeta-Harsha-yoga (windfall-through-opponents-losing) but combustion softens the rescue — daily-health-routine needs explicit attention rather than auto-running. The 12L Jupiter in 8H favours research-or-foreign-hospital-care if needed but is generally protective. Net verdict: strong constitutional foundation with a flagged 8H stellium requiring lifestyle-discipline (diet, sleep, gastric care, eye-care) and a Mercury-anxiety watch in stress-windows.

Conclusion. Trika summary: 6H clean but 6L combust 8H requires routine-attention; 8H stellium is the central health-feature, balanced by Jupiter-rescue; 12L 8H is structurally protective. No catastrophic longevity-flag; lifestyle-discipline the central instruction.

30.2 The lagna-lord — Mars's constitutional vitality लग्नेशाची प्राण-शक्ती

Condition. Lagna Aries 08°28′ Ashvini Pada 3. Lagnesh Mars at 02°20′ Sagittarius Mula Pada 1 — own nakshatra, friendly sign, 9H placement.

Possibilities. The lagna-lord is the chart's constitutional-vitality engine. A strong lagnesh (own sign, own nakshatra, exalted, or in kendra-trine) gives the body a strong base-immunity and recovery-capacity that absorbs ordinary stressors without acute illness. A weak lagnesh (debilitated, combust, in dusthana without rescue) opens the body to easy fatigue and slow recovery. Lagnesh in 9H specifically gives the body a robust constitutional foundation tied to dharma-line — the native's body is supported by faith/purpose rather than mere fitness-training.

This chart specific. Mars in own-nakshatra Mula Pada 1 (double-Sagittarius — Jupiter-amsha-with-Mars-rashi) is among the strongest possible lagnesh placements for Aries lagna. Ashvini lagna-nakshatra (deity: Ashvini-Kumaras = celestial physicians) gives the body natural healing-and-recovery instinct. The combination produces a wiry, warm-constitutioned, action-capable body with strong recovery-from-injury, good athletic capacity, and an immune-system that responds well to dharma-aligned lifestyle (yoga, structured exercise, sattvic diet). The Mars-MD currently activating this lagnesh means the next seven years are the body's peak-vitality window — if the native trains, builds strength, or addresses any latent issues during 2025-2032, the gains compound. The shadow: Mars-driven body is also accident-prone (especially head and upper-body — Aries rules the head), and Mars's 8H lordship adds surgery-or-acute-event watch in Mars sub-periods.

Conclusion. Lagnesh Mars strong = strong base-vitality, good recovery, peak-fitness window during current Mars MD. Accident-watch (head, upper-body) during Mars-Ketu and Mars-Mars sub-PDs.

30.3 Dosha load — summary दोष-भार सारांश

Condition. Doshas (दोष — chart-afflictions, not Ayurvedic doshas) are explicit chart-burdens with stated remedies. Chapter 17 details; this section names the health-relevant ones.

Possibilities. The four health-relevant doshas in any chart are: Kuja-dosha / Manglik (Mars in 1/4/7/8/12 — affects marriage and longevity sub-windows), Shrapit-dosha (Saturn-Rahu yuti or aspect — chronic-pattern), Pitru-dosha (Sun afflicted by Saturn/Rahu — paternal-line and self-authority), Stree-rina / Sarpa-dosha (specific yutis indicating past-life debts that surface as health-pressure in certain windows).

This chart specific. Kuja-dosha (Manglik): Mars in 9H — 9H is not a strict Manglik-house in the orthodox listing (1/4/7/8/12), so this chart is not classically Manglik. However, the Mars-Venus yuti in 9H is a parallel "Mangal-Shukra" axis that some traditions flag as a softer Manglik-equivalent. Pitru-dosha: Sun in 8H aspected by Saturn's 10th-aspect from 11H is a classical Pitru-dosha signature — paternal-line karmic-load present; remediation through Pitru-tarpan and the Sun-mantra recommended (chapter 32). Shrapit-dosha: No Saturn-Rahu yuti or close aspect in this chart — Shrapit absent. Sade-Sati Phase 3: Saturn transiting Pisces (12H from natal Moon in Sagittarius) — currently active, peaks around 2026-2027 transit-arc. Phase 3 affects body-immunity, sleep, foreign/hospital-related events. Combust Mercury: Adds nervous-system-anxiety as a dosha-equivalent health-load. Net dosha-burden: moderate, manageable with explicit lifestyle and remedial discipline (chapter 32).

Conclusion. Dosha-load summary: not Manglik (orthodox), Pitru-dosha present, Shrapit absent, Sade-Sati Phase 3 active, Mercury-combust nervous-anxiety load present. Three remediable items, one transit-window to wait out.

Learning Note — Reading health from four axes / चार अक्षांवरून आरोग्य

Rule: Health-reading combines four axes — trika houses (6/8/12), lagna-lord strength, dosha-load, and dasha-transit overlay. The astrologer must not name specific diseases (that is medical territory); the astrologer names vulnerability windows and recommended disciplines. The medical professional names the diagnosis; the astrologer names the timing-and-direction of vigilance.

Applied here: Lagnesh strong → robust base. 8H stellium with Jupiter rescue → research-vocation-but-also-watch areas. Pitru-dosha + Sade-Sati Phase 3 → specific remediation (chapter 32). Ma-Sa AD 2027-2028 → annual check-up year. Combined verdict: protected body with one flagged transit-window. The reader notices: a 90-minute health-read produced specific timing (Jun 2027 – Jul 2028 annual check-up) and specific dietary direction (cooling Pitta-pacifying with Vata-secondary care).

Hidden risk: A reader who only sees the 8H stellium may over-predict catastrophic illness; that reading misses Jupiter's rescue and the strong lagnesh foundation. Equally, a reader who only sees the strong lagnesh may dismiss legitimate 8H watch-areas. Both axes must be balanced; both protections and risks must be named.

Alt-readings — health schools

Classical Parashari (strict): Reads 8H stellium and Pitru-dosha as primary; recommends Aditya-Hridaya stotra, Pitru-tarpan, and gemstone Ruby (Sun-strengthening) — but Ruby is debatable for Aries lagna where Sun is 5L (functional benefic, gemstone could be recommended), so chapter 32 weighs carefully.

K. N. Rao tradition: Reads Saturn's strength and dasha-AD overlap with transit as the primary timing-engine; would emphasize Ma-Sa AD 2027-2028 as the year-of-pause for major decisions. Independent confirmation.

Ayurveda-Jyotish synthesis: Reads each MD as a dosha-cycle (Mars = Pitta, Saturn = Vata, etc.) and prescribes seasonal-and-MD-aligned dincharya (दिनचर्या — daily routine). The Pitta-pacifying Mars-MD prescription is standard.

Lal Kitab tradition: Would prescribe specific household-remedies — feeding rotis to dogs (Saturn-remedy for Sade-Sati), donating jaggery on Tuesdays (Mars-remedy), avoiding leather purchases (8H-related). Treated as supplementary in the main reading; chapter 32 includes lifestyle-relevant Lal-Kitab items.
