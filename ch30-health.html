Health (आरोग्य — arogya) in Jyotish is read across seven evidence-axes: (1) the trika houses (6H — acute disease/त्रिक; 8H — chronic conditions/longevity; 12H — hospital/loss/dissolution); (2) the lagna and lagna-lord (the body itself and its constitutional vitality); (3) the dosha-load (Vata-Pitta-Kapha imbalances signalled by planet groupings — distinct from Ayurvedic dosha); (4) the karakas (Sun = vitality/heart/eye; Moon = fluids/mind/lungs; Mars = blood/muscle/inflammation; Mercury = nerves/skin/speech; Jupiter = liver/pancreas/fat; Venus = reproductive/throat/kidneys; Saturn = bones/joints/teeth/longevity); (5) the dasha-transit overlay that names the windows when latent vulnerability surfaces; (6) the D6 Shashtamsa and D30 Trimsamsa (disease-specific divisional charts); (7) Ayushya calculation (longevity-estimation by Pinda-Ayu, Amsa-Ayu, or Nisarga-Ayu methods). Classical sources: BPHS chapters 41-43 (rishtas, balarishtas, longevity), Phaladeepika chapter 17, Saravali chapter 39, Jataka Parijata chapter 14. For Dharmesh, the chart shows a strong constitutional foundation (Mars lagnesh in own-nakshatra) shadowed by a heavy 8H stellium (Sun, Mercury-combust, Jupiter, Pluto), Mars-Venus yuti's 9H axis (not orthodox-Manglik but parallel-pattern), and a current Saturn-Pisces/Aries Kantaka-Shani transit (4th-from-Moon = half-malefic; NOT Sade-Sati — that completed Apr 2022; next Sade-Sati cycle ~Oct 2041). The chart names robust longevity (Madhya-Ayu range 65-85 years probable), with specific watch-windows. This chapter walks every classical ill-health rule, every cancellation, every dosha and its remediation, then names AD-by-AD vulnerability windows under Mars MD.

30.1 The 6th house — its significations षष्ठ भाव

Condition. 6H is Virgo (Mercury-ruled, earthy, dual), empty of classical planets. 6L Mercury sits in 8H Scorpio combust by Sun, in yuti with Jupiter and Pluto.

All classical possibilities (Layer 1). The 6H (षष्ठ भाव — ari-bhava / shatru-bhava / roga-bhava) governs acute illness, daily-routine-health, immunity, infections, enemies (open and hidden), debts, litigation, service-work, employees-and-subordinates, and the body's defensive-system. Parashara's three-angle rule applies: read the rashi, the 6L's placement, and any occupant. The 6H of disease covers: acute infections (fever, flu, food-poisoning), digestive disorders (especially when Mercury-related), parasitic infestations, accidental injuries, work-related stress-illnesses, and the body's autoimmune-or-allergic responses. An empty 6H is generally favourable (no enemies sitting in the enemy-house, no fixed-disease-occupant), but the 6L's placement determines if the daily-health-and-service channel functions cleanly. Classical readings of 6L by house: 6L in 1H = self-injuring, body-as-battleground, autoimmune tendency; 6L in 2H = food-related illness, dental-throat, family-conflict; 6L in 3H = sibling-conflict, communication-stress, hand-arm injuries; 6L in 4H = home-stress, mother-conflict, chest-or-lung issues; 6L in 5H = child-or-romance conflict, intellectual-stress, possible stomach issues; 6L in 6H = own-house (Harsha Yoga partial) — disease-overcome-by-disease, strong immunity; 6L in 7H = spouse-conflict, possible litigation, lower-back/kidney; 6L in 8H = Harsha Yoga full — windfall-through-opponents-losing, but also gastric-research-deep-organ flavour; 6L in 9H = guru-conflict, father-friction, hip-thigh; 6L in 10H = workplace-conflict, career-stress-illness; 6L in 11H = friend-network conflicts, calf-and-shin; 6L in 12H = hospital-foreign-or-spiritual-retreat-related dissolution. 6L in 8H is Vipareeta-Raja-yoga / Harsha-yoga — one of the most-favourable disease-chart placements, producing windfall-through-opposition.
