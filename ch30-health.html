Health आरोग्य — षष्ठ, अष्टम, द्वादश, लग्नेश व आयुर्मान

Health (आरोग्य — arogya) in Jyotish is read across seven evidence-axes: (1) the trika houses (6H — acute disease/त्रिक; 8H — chronic conditions/longevity; 12H — hospital/loss/dissolution); (2) the lagna and lagna-lord (the body itself and its constitutional vitality); (3) the dosha-load (Vata-Pitta-Kapha imbalances signalled by planet groupings — distinct from Ayurvedic dosha); (4) the karakas (Sun = vitality/heart/eye; Moon = fluids/mind/lungs; Mars = blood/muscle/inflammation; Mercury = nerves/skin/speech; Jupiter = liver/pancreas/fat; Venus = reproductive/throat/kidneys; Saturn = bones/joints/teeth/longevity); (5) the dasha-transit overlay that names the windows when latent vulnerability surfaces; (6) the D6 Shashtamsa and D30 Trimsamsa (disease-specific divisional charts); (7) Ayushya calculation (longevity-estimation by Pinda-Ayu, Amsa-Ayu, or Nisarga-Ayu methods). Classical sources: BPHS chapters 41-43 (rishtas, balarishtas, longevity), Phaladeepika chapter 17, Saravali chapter 39, Jataka Parijata chapter 14. For Dharmesh, the chart shows a strong constitutional foundation (Mars lagnesh in own-nakshatra) shadowed by a heavy 8H stellium (Sun, Mercury-combust, Jupiter, Pluto), Mars-Venus yuti's 9H axis (not orthodox-Manglik but parallel-pattern), and Sade-Sati Phase 3 currently active. The chart names robust longevity (Madhya-Ayu range 65-85 years probable), with specific watch-windows. This chapter walks every classical ill-health rule, every cancellation, every dosha and its remediation, then names AD-by-AD vulnerability windows under Mars MD.

30.1 The 6th house — its significations षष्ठ भाव

Condition. 6H is Virgo (Mercury-ruled, earthy, dual), empty of classical planets. 6L Mercury sits in 8H Scorpio combust by Sun, in yuti with Jupiter and Pluto.

All classical possibilities (Layer 1). The 6H (षष्ठ भाव — ari-bhava / shatru-bhava / roga-bhava) governs acute illness, daily-routine-health, immunity, infections, enemies (open and hidden), debts, litigation, service-work, employees-and-subordinates, and the body's defensive-system. Parashara's three-angle rule applies: read the rashi, the 6L's placement, and any occupant. The 6H of disease covers: acute infections (fever, flu, food-poisoning), digestive disorders (especially when Mercury-related), parasitic infestations, accidental injuries, work-related stress-illnesses, and the body's autoimmune-or-allergic responses. An empty 6H is generally favourable (no enemies sitting in the enemy-house, no fixed-disease-occupant), but the 6L's placement determines if the daily-health-and-service channel functions cleanly. Classical readings of 6L by house: 6L in 1H = self-injuring, body-as-battleground, autoimmune tendency; 6L in 2H = food-related illness, dental-throat, family-conflict; 6L in 3H = sibling-conflict, communication-stress, hand-arm injuries; 6L in 4H = home-stress, mother-conflict, chest-or-lung issues; 6L in 5H = child-or-romance conflict, intellectual-stress, possible stomach issues; 6L in 6H = own-house (Harsha Yoga partial) — disease-overcome-by-disease, strong immunity; 6L in 7H = spouse-conflict, possible litigation, lower-back/kidney; 6L in 8H = Harsha Yoga full — windfall-through-opponents-losing, but also gastric-research-deep-organ flavour; 6L in 9H = guru-conflict, father-friction, hip-thigh; 6L in 10H = workplace-conflict, career-stress-illness; 6L in 11H = friend-network conflicts, calf-and-shin; 6L in 12H = hospital-foreign-or-spiritual-retreat-related dissolution. 6L in 8H is Vipareeta-Raja-yoga / Harsha-yoga — one of the most-favourable disease-chart placements, producing windfall-through-opposition. However, when 6L is also combust (as Mercury is here), the rescue-strength is reduced by 30-40% and the native must consciously support the channel through routine-discipline rather than rely on auto-protection.

Astrologer-to-client narrative (Layer 2). Dharmesh, your 6H is Virgo and structurally empty of classical planets — which is structurally favourable. An empty disease-house means no fixed-disease-occupant; you are not born with a chronic-condition-marker visible in 6H. The 6L Mercury sits in 8H — and this is interesting. On the surface, "6L in 8H" looks bad. But classical tradition reads this as Harsha-yoga / Vipareeta-Raja-yoga: when the disease-house-lord goes into another dusthana (8H here), the dusthanas mutually cancel and produce a "windfall-through-opponents-losing" pattern. In disease terms: when an opponent illness or enemy or debt threatens, the chart's structure makes the threat fall apart before it materializes. You will repeatedly find that situations that should have produced illness or conflict resolve themselves favourably. Now the catch: Mercury is combust (within ~14° of Sun in the same sign). Combustion mutes a planet's functional voice by about 30-40%. So your Harsha-yoga is real but partial-strength. Practical translation: the auto-protection works most of the time, but when you let routine-discipline lapse (irregular meals, late nights, anxiety-driven decision-making), the cushion thins. Daily routine, hydration, sleep-discipline, anxiety-management — these become the conscious counterweight to the partial-Harsha. Specifically, watch the digestive system (Mercury rules nerves and gut-brain axis; combustion in 8H amplifies anxiety-driven gastric issues), and watch for nervous-system fatigue under stress. The 6H Virgo signature also flags daily-routine-cleanliness as health-relevant — Virgo is the most cleanliness-and-detail-oriented sign; honour it through scheduled meals, organized workspace, and methodical health-tracking.

Hard verdict / forecast. 6H Virgo empty + 6L Mercury combust 8H = partial Harsha-yoga; auto-protection real but reduced by ~30%; daily-routine-discipline becomes the conscious counterweight; digestive and nervous-system are the watch-channels; no fixed chronic-disease occupant.

संपूर्ण मराठी विवेचन — षष्ठ भाव. षष्ठ भाव (अरि-भाव / शत्रु-भाव / रोग-भाव) तीव्र रोग, रोजची-आरोग्य-दिनचर्या, प्रतिकारशक्ति, संसर्ग, उघड-व-गुप्त शत्रू, कर्जे, खटले, सेवा-काम, कर्मचारी-अधीनस्थ, आणि शरीराची संरक्षण-यंत्रणा यांचे प्रतिनिधित्व करतो. पाराशर तीन-कोन-नियम लागू: राशी, ६ल-स्थान, बसलेला ग्रह. ६व्या भावात कोणताही शास्त्रीय ग्रह नसल्यास सामान्यतः अनुकूल (शत्रू शत्रू-घरात नाहीत, स्थिर-रोग-वासी नाही), पण ६लचे स्थान दैनिक-आरोग्य-व-सेवा वाहिनी कशी चालते हे ठरवते. ६ल-८H = हर्ष-योग / विपरीत-राज-योग — सर्वात-अनुकूल रोग-कुंडली स्थानांपैकी एक, "विरोध-कोसळल्याने-लाभ" नमुना. परंतु ६ल अस्तंगत असेल (बुधासारखे), तर रक्षण-शक्ती ३०-४०% कमी होते आणि व्यक्तीला दिनचर्या-शिस्तीतून जाणीवपूर्वक वाहिनी राखावी लागते. दर्मेश, तुमचा ६H कन्या राशीत रिकामा — रचनात्मकरीत्या अनुकूल; जन्मतः स्थिर-कोणत्याही रोगाचा चिन्ह ६व्यात नाही. ६ल बुध ८व्यात — पृष्ठभागावर वाईट वाटले तरी शास्त्रीय हर्ष-योग; दुष्टान-स्वामी दुसर्‍या दुष्टानात गेला तर दुष्टान परस्पर-रद्द होतात आणि "विरोधी-कोसळल्याने-लाभ" नमुना तयार होतो. रोग-दृष्टीने: जिथे आजार/संघर्ष/कर्ज धोका दिसतो, चार्ट-रचना तो धोका साकार होण्यापूर्वी कोसळतो; जे आजार व्हायला हवे होते ते टळतात. पकड: बुध अस्तंगत (सूर्यापासून ~१४° च्या आत त्याच राशीत); अस्तंगताने ग्रहाचा कार्यात्मक-आवाज ३०-४०% कमी होतो; तुमचा हर्ष-योग खरा पण आंशिक-शक्ति. व्यावहारिक: स्वयं-संरक्षण बहुतेक वेळी काम करते, पण दिनचर्या-शिस्त ढीली पडली (अनियमित जेवण, उशीर रात्र, चिंता-निर्णय) तर कुशन पातळ होते. रोज दिनचर्या, जलपान, झोप-शिस्त, चिंता-व्यवस्थापन — हे जाणीव-प्रति-वजन; विशेषतः पचन-तंत्र (बुध मज्जा व पोट-मेंदू-अक्ष नियंत्रित करतो; अस्तंगत-८व्यात चिंता-प्रेरित पचन-त्रास वाढतात) आणि तणाव-काळी मज्जा-तंतू-थकवा यांकडे लक्ष द्यावे. ६H कन्या-छटा दैनिक-स्वच्छता-तपशील आरोग्य-संबंधित ठेवते — कन्या सर्वात स्वच्छता-तपशीलप्रिय राशी; ठरावीक-वेळ-जेवण, व्यवस्थित कार्यस्थळ, पद्धतशीर आरोग्य-नोंदी ठेवून तिचा सन्मान करा.

30.2 The 8th house — its significations and the stellium अष्टम भाव व चार-ग्रह स्तंभ

All classical possibilities (Layer 1). The 8H (अष्टम भाव — mrityu-bhava / randhra-bhava / ayu-bhava) governs chronic conditions, longevity, sudden events (accidents, surgeries, crises), insurance, inheritance, in-law-wealth, hidden transformations, occult and depth-investigation, sexual-energy, and ultimately death-and-rebirth processes. An 8H stellium concentrates the chart's transformation-and-research load — the body becomes the laboratory where deep change happens, often via crisis-events that mark the life-arc. Classical readings: Sun in 8H = vitality-mute, possible heart-or-eye issues from mid-life, paternal-line karma-load, ego-tested-through-crisis; Mercury in 8H = research-intelligence, nervous-system-load, anxiety-prone, possible gastric-or-skin issues, speech-suppressed-under-stress; Jupiter in 8H = the natural-benefic-rescue, liver-and-pancreas significations preserved, recovery-capacity preserved, deep-wisdom-through-crisis pattern, longevity-cushion; Pluto in 8H = regenerative-capacity, depth-psychology resonance, transformative-recovery from major events. In 8H combust Mercury produces specific signatures: nervous-anxiety, gastric-ulcer-prone digestion, speech-suppression under stress, possible skin-rashes from accumulated nervous-load, and tendency to over-think crisis-decisions. The combust-Mercury must be remediated through pranayama and Saraswati-mantra (chapter 32). Jupiter in 8H is the chart's primary 8H-rescue — natural-benefic in dusthana = Vipareeta-Sarala-yoga partial (similar to Harsha but for 8L; here Jupiter is not 8L but its presence converts the 8H load). Saturn's 10th-aspect from 11H onto 8H (10H-aspect rule) adds Saturnine structural-discipline to the 8H stellium — career-research-channel routes through the 8H, and chronic conditions if any will be slow-developing and slow-responding-to-treatment rather than acute.

Astrologer-to-client narrative (Layer 2). Dharmesh, your 8H is the chart's most-discussed health area, and rightly so — four planets sit there. Let me walk through them in order. For your own body, watch the heart-and-eye-line from mid-life (annual cardiology + ophthalmology check-up from age 35). Mercury combust in 8H (3L+6L): Mercury rules nerves, speech, skin, digestion, and intellectual-anxiety. Combust in 8H = nervous-system-overload pattern under stress, gastric-ulcer-prone digestion, possible speech-suppression in high-pressure situations, and skin-flare-ups under accumulated anxiety-load. Jupiter in 8H (9L+12L, AK): Here is your rescue. Jupiter the great-benefic in 8H is one of the strongest health-protection signatures classical texts describe. It produces a partial-Sarala-yoga effect — major crises are softened, recovery is supported, longevity is preserved. Jupiter rules liver-and-pancreas significations; these are protected. Pluto in 8H: Modern-Vedic-Western synthesis reads this as regenerative-capacity — when major events do happen, the body has structural ability to regenerate rather than collapse. Combined verdict: strong 8H load but balanced by Jupiter-rescue + Pluto-regeneration + Mars-lagnesh strong-vitality outside. The chart names specific watch-channels (heart, eye, gastric, nervous-system, skin), specific protections (Jupiter rescue, Pluto regeneration), and specific responsibilities (daily routine-discipline, pranayama, anxiety-management). It does not name a catastrophic-illness or longevity-collapse; it names a body that responds well to dharma-aligned lifestyle and pushes back when discipline lapses.

Hard verdict / forecast. 8H stellium = Sun-Mercury-Jupiter-Pluto in Scorpio; Mercury combust softens 6L-Harsha by 30%; Jupiter is the primary rescue (partial Sarala-yoga); Pluto adds regeneration; watch-channels = heart, eye, gastric, nervous-system, skin; protections structurally present; no catastrophic flag.

30.3 The 12th house — sleep, hospital, dissolution द्वादश भाव

Condition. 12H is Pisces (Jupiter-ruled, watery, dual), empty of classical planets. 12L Jupiter sits in 8H Scorpio with the stellium.
