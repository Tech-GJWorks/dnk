Children संतान — पंचम भाव, गुरु, सप्तमांश व पुत्र-प्राप्ति-योग

Children (संतान — santana / पुत्र — putra) in Jyotish are read from five-evidence triangulation: the 5th house (पंचम भाव — the bhava of progeny, intellect, devotion, past-life merit / poorva-punya), the 5th lord and its placement, Jupiter as putra-karaka (पुत्र-कारक — the natural significator of children in a male chart; Moon in some traditions for female chart), the D7 Saptamsa (सप्तमांश — the children-only divisional chart), and the Beeja-Sphuta and Kshetra-Sphuta (बीज-स्फुट / क्षेत्र-स्फुट — fertility-specific calculations from BPHS chapter 38). Beyond these five, classical evidence includes: 9H from 5H (eldest-child's fortune-house), aspects on 5H from Jupiter/Saturn/Mars/Rahu/Ketu, and the Saptamesha (D7 lagna-lord). Classical sources: BPHS chapter 38 (Putra Adhyaya — "Chapter on Children"), Phaladeepika chapter 15, Saravali chapter 37, Jataka Parijata chapter 12. For Dharmesh, the 5H is Leo (empty, no classical occupants) ruled by Sun-in-8H; Jupiter-putra-karaka sits in 8H Scorpio with Atma-Karaka status. The chart names progeny that arrives later rather than earlier, with a wisdom-or-research-oriented child-signature, and timing windows that cluster in late Mars MD and early Rahu MD. This chapter walks every classical putra-prapti yoga, every childlessness-yoga and its cancellation, every D7 reading-rule, then names AD-by-AD timing windows.

31.1 The 5th house — its significations पंचम भाव

Condition. 5H is Leo (Sun-ruled, fiery, fixed), empty of classical planets. The cusp itself sits at the same degree-band as the lagna; no aspect from Jupiter directly on 5H (Jupiter sits in 8H — 5th-aspect from 8H is on 12H, not 5H; 9th-aspect from 8H is on 4H Cancer, also not 5H). No direct planetary occupant.

All classical possibilities (Layer 1). The 5H (पंचम भाव — putra-bhava / vidya-bhava / poorva-punya-bhava) governs children, intellect, devotional inclination, past-life merit (poorva-punya), creativity, romantic-love-before-marriage, sports/games, speculation, and the mantra-or-yantra-power inherited from past births. Parashara mandates reading 5H from three angles: rashi-significations, occupants' significations, and aspects-received. An empty 5H without major aspects is structurally neutral — it neither blesses nor obstructs progeny on its own; the 5L's placement decides. Classical readings of each sign as 5H: Aries-5H = bold children, possibly fewer in number, sports-or-courage-oriented; Taurus-5H = stable children, often firstborn-female, sensual-artistic; Gemini-5H = twins-possible, communication-intelligent children, multiple-progeny; Cancer-5H = emotionally-bonded children, mother-figure-role; Leo-5H = children with leadership-and-authority potential, often one strong son or a child with distinct identity-mark, possibly delayed; Virgo-5H = analytically-intelligent children, possibly delayed, service-oriented; Libra-5H = aesthetic-balanced children, partnership-oriented; Scorpio-5H = transformational-research-children, intense bonds, fewer in number; Sagittarius-5H = dharma-oriented wise children, possibly foreign-living; Capricorn-5H = disciplined-mature children, delayed-arrival pattern; Aquarius-5H = unconventional-friend-like children, technology-or-research orientation; Pisces-5H = devotional-creative-sensitive children, often spiritually-aware. The 5L Sun specifically (Aries lagna has Leo 5H) gives children with leadership-or-authority potential, often one strong son or a child with a distinct identity-mark; Sun's house-placement determines the texture and timing of progeny.

31.3 Jupiter as putra-karaka पुत्र-कारक गुरु

Condition. Jupiter (गुरु) is the natural putra-karaka in a male chart. Position: 27°26′ Scorpio Jyeshtha Pada 4, in 8H. Friendly sign, Pisces-amsha (Jupiter's own amsha — exceptional dignity), Atma-Karaka, 9L+12L. Yuti with Sun-5L, Mercury-combust, Pluto.

All classical possibilities (Layer 1). Jupiter as putra-karaka is read separately from its lordship-role. Its sign, nakshatra, yutis, house, and dignity all paint the children-picture independent of the 5H/5L axis.
