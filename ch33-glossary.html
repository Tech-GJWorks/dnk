Glossary शब्दकोश — संस्कृत व मराठी पारिभाषिक शब्द

An alphabetical encyclopedia of the Sanskrit (संस्कृत) and Marathi (मराठी) technical terms used across the prior 32 chapters. Each entry gives the Devanagari spelling, English transliteration, a working definition (meaning), when-the-term-applies (context), and an example (typically from this chart). Where a term has multiple traditional meanings, the meaning as used in this book is given first. Cross-references point to the chapter where the concept is most-fully developed. For deeper study, classical sources to consult: Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra (Parashara), Phaladeepika (Mantreshwara), Jataka Parijata (Vaidyanatha), Saravali (Kalyana Varma), Brihat Jataka (Varahamihira), Hora Sara (Prithuyasas), Jaimini Sutras. Modern English-language commentaries by B.V. Raman, K.S. Charak, K.N. Rao, James Braha, and Hart de Fouw fill in the practitioner's-manual gap between classical text and modern application.

A

Adhipati अधिपती
Meaning: Lord, ruler — the planet that rules a sign or a house. Synonyms: swami, esha, natha. When-applies: Used whenever discussing house-rulership or sign-rulership; the lagna-adhipati (lagnesh) rules the rising sign and signals the chart's identity-engine. Example: For this Aries-lagna chart, Mars is the lagna-adhipati (lagnesh); for the 10H Capricorn, Saturn is the dashma-adhipati (karmesha). See ch05.
Adhi Yoga अधि योग
Meaning: A classical Raja-yoga formed when natural benefics (Mercury, Jupiter, Venus) occupy 6H, 7H, and 8H from the Moon. Produces leadership, freedom from enemies, and wealth-power. When-applies: Checked alongside other Raja-yogas during chart-wealth-and-status analysis. Example: Not fully formed in this chart; partial Adhi-yoga signature via benefic placements but the strict 6-7-8 from Moon distribution does not perfectly fire. See ch16.
Antardasha अंतर्दशा
Meaning: The sub-period inside a Mahadasha. In Vimshottari, each MD contains nine ADs in a fixed cyclic order; AD-duration is proportional to the AD-lord's full dasha-years. Abbreviated AD or Bhukti. When-applies: Used for year-and-month timing precision within a multi-year MD. Example: Currently in Ma-Ma AD (Jan-Jun 2025), heading into Ma-Ra AD (Jun 2025 - Jul 2026). See ch18.
Apoklima अपोक्लिम
Meaning: The cadent houses — 3H, 6H, 9H, 12H. Generally weaker than kendras and panaphara for material results but vital for dharma (9H), liberation (12H), and karma-clearing. When-applies: Used when evaluating house-strength categories in Shadbala calculations. Example: The 9H Sagittarius cluster (Moon-Mars-Venus) in this chart is in apoklima but is structurally one of the most-important areas of the chart due to dharma-significance.
Aspect (Drishti) दृष्टि
Meaning: The "glance" a planet casts on another house from its own position. All planets aspect the 7th house from themselves. Special aspects: Mars 4th and 8th; Jupiter 5th and 9th; Saturn 3rd and 10th; Rahu/Ketu 5/7/9 (school-dependent). When-applies: Critical to every house-and-planet reading. Example: Saturn's 10th-aspect from 11H falls on 8H stellium in this chart, connecting career and research. See ch01.
Atma-Karaka आत्म-कारक
Meaning: Soul-significator under Jaimini. The planet at highest longitudinal degree among the seven classical planets (excluding nodes). Represents the soul's primary unfinished lesson. When-applies: Central to Jaimini soul-vocation analysis and Karakamsa-Lagna calculation. Example: Jupiter at 27°26′ Scorpio is this chart's Atma-Karaka, making the soul-lesson research, depth-investigation, and wisdom-transmission. See ch06 + ch29.
Avastha अवस्था
Meaning: The "state" of a planet — infant, youth, adult, old, dead — based on degrees within a sign (0-6°, 6-12°, 12-18°, 18-24°, 24-30°). Bala (infant) and Mrita (dead) avasthas give weaker results; Kumara/Yuva are strongest. When-applies: Used to refine planet-strength readings beyond simple dignity. Example: Mars at 02°20′ Sagittarius is in Bala-avastha (infant state) but rescued by own-nakshatra placement.
Ayanamsa अयनांश
Meaning: The offset between sidereal (Vedic) and tropical (Western) zodiacs. Lahiri ayanamsa is the Indian government standard, approximately 24° in modern times. When-applies: Used when computing the chart; affects every planet-position calculation. Example: Lahiri-ayanamsa for 18-Jun-2002 birth gives the planetary positions used throughout this book. See ch02.
Ayurveda आयुर्वेद
Meaning: The Indian system of medicine paired with Jyotish. Body-temperaments (Vata-Pitta-Kapha) correlate with planetary natures; lifestyle-and-diet are calibrated by chart-signatures. When-applies: Used in health-readings and dosha-load analysis. Example: Mars MD prescribes Pitta-cooling diet for this chart; Sade-Sati Phase 3 adds Vata-overlay requiring grounding practices.
Ayushya आयुष्य
Meaning: Longevity. Calculated by three classical methods — Pinda-Ayu, Amsa-Ayu, Nisarga-Ayu. Three categories: Alpa-Ayu (0-32), Madhya-Ayu (32-70), Purna-Ayu (70+). When-applies: Used in health-chapter longevity-estimation. Example: This chart estimates Madhya-Ayu upper to lower-Purna range, central 75-82 years, with disciplined lifestyle extending the upper bound. See ch30.

B

Beeja-Sphuta बीज-स्फुट
Meaning: Male-fertility-point calculation from BPHS chapter 38. Beeja-Sphuta = Sum of Sun + Jupiter + Venus longitudes. Even-sign placement supports fertility; afflicted placement in 6/8/12 raises fertility-concern flags. When-applies: Children-chapter advanced analysis. Example: This chart's Beeja-Sphuta supports fertility — no afflicted placement in dusthana. See ch31.
Bhagya भाग्य
Meaning: Fortune. The 9th house is the bhagya-bhava — luck, dharma, father, guru, long journeys. A 9H stellium or lagnesh-in-9H signals strong bhagya-yoga. When-applies: Used in dharma-and-fortune analysis; bhagya-bhava strength affects life-direction recognition. Example: 9H Moon-Mars-Venus cluster + lagnesh Mars in 9H = exceptionally strong bhagya-yoga in this chart. See ch10.
Bhakoot भकूट
Meaning: One of the eight Ashtakoota factors in marriage-compatibility (Guna-Milan). Counts the house-position of the partner's Moon-rashi from one's own Moon-rashi. 2/12, 5/9, 6/8 = dosha. When-applies: Used in pre-marriage compatibility matching. Example: This chart's Sagittarius-Moon would prefer the bride's Moon to be in Aries, Taurus, Cancer, Leo, Virgo, or Sagittarius (compatible Bhakoot). See ch28.
Bhanga भंग
Meaning: Cancellation; usually of debilitation (Neecha-Bhanga) or a dosha (Manglik-Bhanga). Specific rules govern when cancellation fires (e.g., debilitation-lord in kendra, exalted-lord-of-uchcha-rashi in kendra, etc.). When-applies: Used when a difficult signature appears alongside structural rescue conditions. Example: Jupiter's amsha-rescue (Pisces-amsha in D9) cancels its D1 8H placement difficulty for this chart.
Bhava भाव
Meaning: House — one of the twelve domains of life. Each bhava governs specific life-areas: 1H (self), 2H (wealth-family), 3H (siblings-courage), 4H (mother-home), 5H (children), 6H (enemies-disease), 7H (spouse), 8H (longevity), 9H (dharma-father), 10H (career), 11H (gains), 12H (loss-liberation). When-applies: Foundational concept used throughout the book. See ch05 + ch15.
Bhava-Bala भाव-बल
Meaning: House-strength calculation — quantifies each bhava's relative power based on occupants, lord-strength, aspects, and natural significations. When-applies: Used to rank houses for which areas of life are strongest-and-weakest. Example: 9H and 11H are strongest bhavas in this chart; 4H and 12H are weaker (Cancer 4H empty, Pisces 12H empty with lord in 8H).
Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra बृहत् पाराशर होरा शास्त्र
Meaning: The most foundational classical text of Vedic astrology, attributed to Sage Parashara. 97-chapter encyclopedia covering signs, planets, houses, yogas, dashas, transits, remedies. When-applies: The primary reference for Parashari Jyotish. Example: Every house-rule, yoga-rule, and dasha-method in this book traces back to BPHS unless explicitly attributed elsewhere.

C

Chandra चंद्र
Meaning: Moon. Karaka of mind, mother, emotions, fluids, public-mass, intuition, monthly cycles. The Moon's natal nakshatra (janma-nakshatra) starts the Vimshottari dasha-calendar. When-applies: Used in every emotional, maternal, and mind-related reading. Example: Moon at 17°53′ Sagittarius Purvashadha P2 in 9H here = emotionally-philosophical mind, mother-connected to dharma. See ch06 + ch08 + ch10.
Chandra Kundli चंद्र कुंडली
Meaning: Chart drawn with the Moon's sign as the 1st house — a parallel reading lens to the lagna-chart. When-applies: Used for emotional-and-mental impact readings; Sade-Sati is calculated from the Moon-rashi. Example: For this chart, Chandra-Kundli has Sagittarius as 1H; Sade-Sati Phase 3 is calculated as Saturn transiting Pisces (12H from Moon-Sagittarius).
Chandra-Mangala Yoga चंद्र-मंगळ योग
Meaning: The conjunction or strong aspect between Moon and Mars. Fuses emotion-courage-finance into one stream; the native works with both emotional investment and decisive action. When-applies: Active in this chart through Moon-Mars yuti in 9H Sagittarius. Example: Career-effort here converts to wealth efficiently because Chandra-Mangala fires. See ch11 + ch29.
Chara चर
Meaning: Cardinal/movable sign — Aries, Cancer, Libra, Capricorn. Action-initiating, change-bringing. Also a Jaimini dasha-system name (Chara-dasha). When-applies: Used in classifying sign-natures and in Jaimini dasha-timing. Example: Aries-lagna is Chara, indicating an action-initiating personality; this chart's lagna nature is chara.
Combust (Asta) अस्त
Meaning: A planet within specific degree-range of the Sun (Mercury 14°, Venus 10°, Mars 17°, Jupiter 11°, Saturn 15°) — its light is suppressed by 30-40%. When-applies: Used when evaluating planet-strength; combust planets have reduced functional voice. Example: Mercury at 04°47′ Scorpio within 4° of Sun at 08°53′ Scorpio = combust; reduces 6L Harsha-yoga rescue partially. See ch12.

D

Daan दान
Meaning: Charitable giving — one of the seven upaya-families. Specific items donated on specific days for specific planet-pacification: jaggery (Mars-Tuesday), black-sesame (Saturn-Saturday), green-moong (Mercury-Wednesday), etc. When-applies: Used as a remedy in the upaya portfolio. Example: Donating black-sesame on Saturdays during Sade-Sati Phase 3 in this chart. See ch32.
Darakaraka दार-कारक
Meaning: Jaimini's spouse-significator. The planet at lowest longitudinal degree among the seven classical planets. Describes the spouse-encounter karmic-blueprint. When-applies: Used in marriage-chapter Jaimini overlay. Example: Venus at 03°34′ Sagittarius is this chart's Darakaraka (lowest degree); also Mars-as-co-Darakaraka in some role-readings. See ch28.
Dasha दशा
Meaning: Planetary time-period that activates a graha's significations. Vimshottari is the primary 120-year dasha-system used in this book. When-applies: Used for timing every life-event. Example: Mars Mahadasha (19-Jan-2025 → 19-Jan-2032) currently runs in this chart, activating Mars's lagnesh + 8L + Darakaraka role-stack. See ch18.
Dasamsa दशांश
Meaning: The D10 divisional chart for career analysis. Each rashi divided into ten parts of 3° each; planet positions re-projected. When-applies: Used in career-chapter Layer-2 confirmation. Example: D10 for this chart confirms the Saturn-Mars technology-research signature seen in D1 — no contradiction. See ch27 + ch29.
Devata देवता
Meaning: Presiding deity. Each nakshatra, each graha, each house has its own devata-archetype. When-applies: Used in deeper nakshatra and remedial readings. Example: Ashvini-Kumaras (celestial physicians) is the deity of Ashvini-nakshatra at lagna in this chart, giving healing-instinct; Indra is deity of Jyeshtha-nakshatra where Jupiter sits.
Dhana धन
Meaning: Wealth. 2H is the dhana-bhava; 11H is gain-of-wealth (labha). Strong 2L-11L combination = Dhana-yoga. When-applies: Used in wealth-and-income analysis. Example: Saturn rules 10H and 11H both in this chart; sitting in own-sign 11H = structural Dhana-yoga. See ch29.
Dhana Yoga धन योग
Meaning: Wealth combination produced by specific lord-and-house associations (typically 2L-11L, 5L-9L, 10L-11L). Multiple Dhana-yogas in a single chart compound. When-applies: Counted in career-and-wealth chapters. Example: Karma-Labha yoga (10L in 11H) + Saturn rules both 10H and 11H = compound Dhana-yoga in this chart.
Dharma धर्म
Meaning: Righteous-duty, ethical-line, life-purpose. 9H is the dharma-bhava. The 1-5-9 trine is the dharma-trikona. When-applies: Used throughout in soul-purpose and ethical-alignment readings. Example: This chart's 9H stellium (Moon-Mars-Venus + Jupiter's aspect) makes dharma the structurally-strongest life-area.
Dharma-Karmadhipati Yoga धर्म-कर्माधिपती योग
Meaning: Raja-yoga formed when 9L (dharma) and 10L (karma) associate by yuti, mutual aspect, or exchange. Considered one of the highest Raja-yogas. When-applies: Career-and-status reading. Example: Partial in this chart via Saturn's 10th-aspect (from 11H) on Jupiter (9L) in 8H. See ch16 + ch29.
Dincharya दिनचर्या
Meaning: Daily routine. An Ayurvedic-Jyotish discipline aligning daily-action with the running dasha-energy. When-applies: Used in health and remedies chapters. Example: Pitta-cooling diet + structured sleep + daily Hanuman Chalisa + pranayama forms the dincharya for Mars-MD + Sade-Sati Phase 3 combination. See ch30 + ch32.
Dosha दोष
Meaning: Chart-affliction or fault (distinct from Ayurvedic Vata/Pitta/Kapha dosha). Examples: Kuja-dosha (Manglik), Pitru-dosha, Shrapit-dosha, Kalsarpa-dosha, Nadi-dosha, Bhakoot-dosha. When-applies: Used in dosha-analysis chapter and pre-marriage compatibility. Example: Pitru-dosha is present in this chart (Sun-8H Saturn-aspected); Shrapit absent; Kalsarpa partial. See ch17.
Drishti दृष्टि
Meaning: Aspect — see "Aspect" above. The glance a planet casts on other houses from its position. Critical to chart-reading. When-applies: Every reading uses drishti analysis. Example: Saturn's 10th-drishti from 11H to 8H is structurally significant in this chart, connecting career and research.
Dusthana दुस्थान
Meaning: The three "difficulty" houses — 6H, 8H, 12H. Trika-bhavas. Planets here are often muted or transformed; rescue-yogas (Viparita-Raja-yoga) can convert affliction to elevation. When-applies: Used when reading planets in trika and Vipareeta-yogas. Example: 4-planet 8H stellium in this chart routes the chart's central energies through dusthana; Jupiter's presence rescues. See ch09 + ch13.

E

Ekadashi एकादशी
Meaning: The 11th lunar day in each fortnight. Vrata-observance day in many traditions; Vishnu-Jupiter associated. Also 11H-related auspicious tithi. When-applies: Used in remedy schedules (Ekadashi-vrata) and muhurta. Example: Monthly Ekadashi-vrata is supplementary to the remedial portfolio for this chart (Jupiter MD lord activation). See ch32.
Exalted (Uchcha) उच्च
Meaning: The strongest dignity a planet can have, specifically in its sign-of-exaltation. Each planet has one exaltation-sign: Sun (Aries), Moon (Taurus), Mars (Capricorn), Mercury (Virgo), Jupiter (Cancer), Venus (Pisces), Saturn (Libra). When-applies: Used in planet-dignity evaluation. Example: No planet is in exaltation in this chart's D1; Jupiter is own-sign in D9 (next-strongest after exaltation).

G

Gajakesari Yoga गजकेसरी योग
Meaning: Jupiter and Moon in mutual kendras (1/4/7/10 from each other). One of the major auspicious yogas — wisdom, recognition, dharma, public-respect. When-applies: Counted in Raja-yoga analysis. Example: Not fully formed in this chart (Moon in 9H, Jupiter in 8H — adjacent houses, not mutual kendras), but the close-proximity gives a partial Gajakesari-feel. See ch16.
Gandanta गण्डान्त
Meaning: The "knot" where water-sign ends and fire-sign begins, or where fire ends and water begins — Pisces-Aries, Cancer-Leo, Scorpio-Sagittarius transitions. Birth in gandanta is karmically loaded. When-applies: Checked at lagna and Moon junctions. Example: Not present in this chart; both lagna (Aries 08°) and Moon (Sagittarius 17°) are away from gandanta-degrees.
Gochar गोचर
Meaning: Transit. The current real-time position of planets compared to the natal chart. When-applies: Used in timing predictions alongside dasha. Example: Saturn-gochar in Pisces (12H from natal Moon) is the current Sade-Sati Phase 3 transit for this chart. See ch26.
Graha ग्रह
Meaning: Planet. Literally "the seizer" — the planetary intelligence that "seizes" consciousness when its dasha or transit fires. Nine classical grahas (Sun, Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn, Rahu, Ketu) plus three modern outer-planet additions (Uranus, Neptune, Pluto) in modern Vedic synthesis. When-applies: Foundational concept. See ch06.
Graha Maitri ग्रह मैत्री
Meaning: Planetary friendship matrix. Each planet has friends, enemies, and neutrals; affects yuti-readings and Ashtakoota compatibility. When-applies: Used in compatibility matching and yuti-evaluation. Example: Mars-Jupiter are mutual friends; their associations (like Ma-Ju AD timing) are highly favourable. See ch28.
Guna Milan गुण मिलन
Meaning: Vedic compatibility-matching using 8 Ashtakoota factors totaling 36 points. Minimum 18/36 for marriage; ideal 24+; excellent 28+. When-applies: Pre-marriage matching. Example: For Dharmesh's Moon in Sagittarius Purvashadha P2, prospective bride's compatibility-match would target 24-32 points across the 8 factors. See ch28.
Guru गुरु
Meaning: Jupiter; also "teacher" — Jupiter is the natural karaka of teachers, dharma, wisdom, children, expansion. When-applies: Used in every Jupiter-related reading. Example: Jupiter in this chart is AK + 9L+12L + putra-karaka + in 8H Scorpio Jyeshtha P4 — multi-role significator. See ch06 + ch13.

H

Hamsa Yoga हंस योग
Meaning: Jupiter Mahapurusha Yoga — Jupiter in own/exalted sign in a kendra. Produces dharmic-leadership, wisdom, respected status. When-applies: Pancha-Mahapurusha analysis. Example: Not formed in this chart (Jupiter in 8H, not kendra), but Jupiter is otherwise structurally strong via AK status and amsha-rescue.
Harsha Yoga हर्ष योग
Meaning: A Viparita-Raja-yoga subtype — when 6L sits in 6H, 8H, or 12H. Produces "windfall through opponents losing." When-applies: Counted in Vipareeta-Raja-yoga analysis. Example: Partial in this chart — 6L Mercury sits in 8H, but Mercury is combust, reducing rescue-strength by 30-40%. See ch16.
Hora होरा
Meaning: The D2 divisional chart for wealth. Also: a one-hour planetary period in muhurta astrology (each weekday divided into 24 horas). When-applies: Wealth-analysis and muhurta-selection. Example: D2 Hora chart for this chart shows wealth-supportive placements consistent with the D1 dhana-yogas.

J

Jaimini जैमिनी
Meaning: The ancient sage whose Jyotish-sutras give an alternate predictive system — Chara-dasha, Atma-Karaka analysis, Karakamsa-lagna, Padas, Argala. Parallels Parashari but uses different signatures. When-applies: Used in soul-vocation, marriage, and Jaimini-dasha analysis. Example: Jupiter-AK and Venus-Darakaraka are Jaimini signatures in this chart. See ch28 + ch29.
Janma जन्म
Meaning: Birth. Janma-nakshatra = Moon's natal nakshatra. Janma-rashi = Moon's natal sign. Janma-lagna = rising-sign at birth. When-applies: Foundational — every reading begins from janma-data. Example: Janma-lagna Aries, janma-rashi Sagittarius, janma-nakshatra Purvashadha P2 for this chart.
Jyotish ज्योतिष
Meaning: Vedic astrology — literally "the science of light." One of the six Vedangas (limbs of Vedic knowledge, "the eye of the Vedas"). Three branches: Ganita (math/astronomy), Samhita (mundane/predictive), Hora (individual horoscopy). When-applies: The entire field of this book. See ch01.

K

Kalatra-Karaka कलत्र-कारक
Meaning: Natural significator of wife in a male chart — Venus. For husband in a female chart — Jupiter. Read independently of 7H/7L for spouse-portrait. When-applies: Marriage chapter spouse-portraiture. Example: Venus in 9H Sagittarius Mula P2 Capricorn-amsha gives a philosophical-structured-research-oriented spouse-portrait for this chart. See ch28.
Kalsarpa Dosha कालसर्प दोष
Meaning: The configuration where all seven classical planets sit between Rahu and Ketu in one nodal half. Produces specific karmic-pressure pattern — "stuck-feeling" through life-windows. When-applies: Dosha-analysis. Example: Partial only in this chart — some planets on each side of Rahu-Ketu axis; not full Kalsarpa pattern. See ch17.
Karaka कारक
Meaning: Significator — what a planet "stands for." Sun = father, Moon = mother, Venus = wife, Mars = brother/courage, Jupiter = children/wisdom, Saturn = longevity/elders, Mercury = communication/business. When-applies: Used alongside house-readings for triangulation. Example: Jupiter as putra-karaka in 8H with amsha-rescue blesses children-line in this chart. See ch31.
Karakamsa Lagna कारकांश-लग्न
Meaning: The sign occupied by the Atma-Karaka in D9. Provides a parallel-lagna for spiritual and soul-vocation reading under Jaimini. When-applies: Soul-vocation and late-life dharma analysis. Example: Pisces is the Karakamsa Lagna in this chart (Jupiter-AK sits in Pisces own-sign in D9) — pointing to devotional-counselling vocation in Jupiter MD (2050+). See ch29.
Karma कर्म
Meaning: Action and its consequence; also the 10H (karma-bhava — profession, public action). When-applies: Used in career and life-events analysis. Example: Karma-bhava (10H) Capricorn in this chart is Saturn-disciplined, technology-and-research-flavoured. See ch29.
Karma-Labha Yoga कर्म-लाभ योग
Meaning: Wealth-yoga formed when 10L sits in 11H or 11L sits in 10H. Career-effort converts directly to gains without intermediate loss. When-applies: Career-and-wealth analysis. Example: Auto-fulfilled in this chart — 10L Saturn sits in own-sign 11H Aquarius. Strongest auto-wealth-yoga active. See ch29.
Kemadruma Yoga केमद्रुम योग
Meaning: Lonely-Moon yoga formed when Moon has no planets in adjacent houses (2nd and 12th from Moon). Produces mental-isolation pattern; broken by adjacent benefics. When-applies: Counted in yoga-analysis. Example: Broken in this chart by Mars-Venus-Neptune adjacency to Moon in 9H. See ch16.
Kendra केंद्र
Meaning: Quadrant/angular houses — 1H, 4H, 7H, 10H. Pillars of life (Vishnu-sthanas). Strongest houses for material results. When-applies: Used in Raja-yoga, Mahapurusha-yoga, and strength evaluations. Example: 7H Rahu and 10H Uranus in kendras give this chart structural prominence in partnership and career.
Ketu केतू
Meaning: South lunar node. Shadow-graha. Karaka of detachment, moksha, past-life karma, research, sudden events, spiritual depth. When-applies: Used in dharma-and-moksha readings, transit analysis. Example: Ketu in 1H Aries Ashvini P1 in this chart gives Aries-amsha self-detachment with healing-deity overlay. See ch06 + ch14.
Kuja कुज
Meaning: Mars. "Born from earth." Alternative Sanskrit name for Mars (also Mangala, Bhauma, Angaraka). When-applies: Used interchangeably with Mangala. Example: Kuja-dosha is the technical name for Manglik in classical texts. See Mangala.
Kuja-Dosha कुज-दोष
Meaning: Manglik-dosha. Mars in 1H/2H/4H/7H/8H/12H affects marriage in orthodox tradition. When-applies: Pre-marriage compatibility analysis. Example: Strictly absent in this chart (Mars in 9H, not in the orthodox listing); however, Mars-Venus 9H yuti is read as parallel-soft-pattern requiring soft-Manglik-remediation. See ch17 + ch28.
Kundli कुंडली
Meaning: Birth chart. The graphical representation of planet-positions at birth. North-Indian diamond style used in this book. When-applies: Foundational visual reference. Example: This entire book's chart-reading operates on Dharmesh's natal-Kundli computed for 18-Jun-2002, Solapur.

L

Lagna लग्न
Meaning: The rising sign on the eastern horizon at birth. The first house and the chart's identity-engine. When-applies: Foundational — defines house-numbering and identity. Example: Aries 08°28′ Ashvini Pada 3 is this chart's lagna; gives an action-oriented, healing-instinct, fire-on-fire identity. See ch07.

"तुम्ही पुस्तक मागितले. आम्ही लिहिले. आता पुढे काय करायचे — ते तुमच्या हातात आहे. कुंडली तुमचे आयुष्य जगणार नाही — फक्त तुम्ही जगू शकता. कुंडली निर्णय घेणार नाही — फक्त तुम्ही घेऊ शकता. कुंडली संपत्ती कमावणार नाही, विवाह उभारणार नाही, मुले वाढवणार नाही, ईश्वराचा शोध घेणार नाही — फक्त तुम्ही करू शकता. पण कुंडली, जर तुम्ही तिला परवानगी दिलीत, तर तुम्ही चालत असलेल्या रस्त्यावरचे काही कोपरे उजळवू शकते. हाच ज्योतिषाचा खरा उपयोग — दिवा देणे, दिशा सांगणे, पण चालणे तुम्हीच."
How to use this glossary / या शब्दकोशाचा उपयोग कसा करावा

This glossary is designed as a working reference, not a reading-from-front exercise. When a chapter uses a term like Karakamsa Lagna or Viparita-Raja-yoga, flip here for a working definition + when-it-applies + example, then return to the chapter for the chart-specific application. The same term will often appear in multiple chapters with different angles; this glossary gives the single working sense used in this book, with cross-references to the chapter where the concept is most-fully developed.
