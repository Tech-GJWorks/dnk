Glossary शब्दकोश — संस्कृत व मराठी पारिभाषिक शब्द

An alphabetical reference for the Sanskrit (संस्कृत) and Marathi (मराठी) technical terms used across the prior 32 chapters. Each entry gives the Devanagari spelling, English transliteration, and a 1-3 sentence working definition. Where a term has multiple traditional meanings, the meaning as used in this book is given first. Cross-references point to the chapter where the concept is most-fully developed.

A

TermदेवनागरीDefinition
AdhipatiअधिपतीLord, ruler. In Jyotish: the planet that rules a sign or a house. Synonyms: swami, esha. Lagnesh = lagna-adhipati = lord of the rising sign. See ch05.
Antardashaअंतर्दशाThe sub-period inside a Mahadasha. In Vimshottari, each MD contains nine ADs in a fixed cyclic order. Abbreviated AD or sometimes Bhukti. See ch18.
Apoklimaअपोक्लिमThe cadent houses — 3H, 6H, 9H, 12H. Generally weaker than kendras and panaphara for material results but vital for dharma (9H), liberation (12H), and karma-clearing.
Aspect (Drishti)दृष्टिThe "glance" a planet casts on another house from its own position. All planets aspect the 7th house from themselves. Mars additionally aspects 4th and 8th; Jupiter 5th and 9th; Saturn 3rd and 10th; Rahu and Ketu 5/7/9 (school-dependent). See ch01.
Atma-Karakaआत्म-कारकSoul-significator under Jaimini. The planet at highest longitudinal degree among the seven classical planets. Represents the soul's primary unfinished lesson. See ch06 + ch29.
AyanamsaअयनांशThe offset between sidereal (Vedic) and tropical (Western) zodiacs. Lahiri ayanamsa is the Indian government standard, approximately 24° in modern times. See ch02.
Ayurvedaआयुर्वेदThe Indian system of medicine that pairs with Jyotish. Body-temperaments (Vata-Pitta-Kapha) correlate with planetary natures.

B

TermदेवनागरीDefinition
Bhagyaभाग्यFortune. The 9th house is the bhagya-bhava — luck, dharma, father, guru, long journeys. A 9H stellium or lagnesh-in-9H signals strong bhagya-yoga. See ch10.
BhavaभावHouse. The 12 sectors of the chart, each governing a life-domain. Bhava-Bala is the strength-calculation of each house. See ch05 + ch15.
Bhuktiभुक्तीSame as Antardasha (AD). The sub-period within a Mahadasha. Marathi/Sanskrit-equivalent term. See ch18.

C

TermदेवनागरीDefinition
Chandraचंद्रMoon. The graha governing mind, mother, emotions, fluids, public mass, intuition, monthly cycles. The Moon's natal nakshatra (janma-nakshatra) starts the Vimshottari dasha-calendar. See ch06 + ch08 + ch10.
Chandra-Mangala yogaचंद्र-मंगळ योगThe conjunction or strong aspect between Moon and Mars. Fuses emotion-courage-finance into one stream. Active in this chart (Moon-Mars yuti in 9H). See ch11.
CharaचरCardinal sign — Aries, Cancer, Libra, Capricorn. Action-initiating, change-bringing. Also a Jaimini dasha-system name (Chara-dasha).
Combustअस्तA planet within 5-15° of the Sun (varies by planet and tradition) — its light is suppressed. Mercury within 14°, Venus within 10°, Mars within 17°, Jupiter within 11°, Saturn within 15°. Combustion mutes the planet's functional voice. See ch12.

D

TermदेवनागरीDefinition
DaanदानCharitable giving, one of the seven upaya-families. Specific items donated on specific days for specific planet-pacification. See ch32.
Darakarakaदार-कारकJaimini's spouse-significator. The planet at lowest longitudinal degree among the seven classical planets. Describes the spouse-encounter karmic-blueprint. See ch28.
DashaदशाPlanetary time-period that activates a graha's significations. Vimshottari is the primary 120-year dasha-system used in this book. See ch18.
DasamsaदशांशThe D10 divisional chart for career analysis. Each rashi divided into ten parts; planet positions re-projected. See ch27 + ch29.
DevataदेवताPresiding deity. Each nakshatra, each graha, each house has its own devata-archetype.
DhanaधनWealth. 2H is the dhana-bhava; 11H is gain-of-wealth (labha). Strong 2L-11L combination = Dhana-yoga.
Dharmaधर्मRighteous-duty, ethical-line, life-purpose. 9H is the dharma-bhava. The 1-5-9 trine is the dharma-trikona.
Dincharyaदिनचर्याDaily routine. An Ayurvedic-Jyotish discipline aligning daily-action with the running dasha-energy. See ch30 + ch32.
DoshaदोषChart-affliction or fault. Distinct from Ayurvedic dosha (Vata/Pitta/Kapha). Examples: Kuja-dosha (Manglik), Pitru-dosha, Shrapit-dosha, Kalsarpa-dosha. See ch17.
Drishtiदृष्टिAspect (see above). The glance a planet casts on other houses.
Dusthanaदुस्थानThe difficult houses — 6H, 8H, 12H. Trika-bhavas. Planets here are often muted or transformed; rescue-yogas (Viparita-Raja-yoga) can convert affliction to elevation. See ch09 + ch13.

E

TermदेवनागरीDefinition
EkadashiएकादशीThe 11th lunar day in each fortnight. Vrata-observance day in many traditions. Also a 11H-related auspicious tithi.
Exalted (Uchcha)उच्चThe strongest dignity a planet can have, specifically in its sign-of-exaltation (e.g., Sun in Aries, Moon in Taurus, Mars in Capricorn). See Uchcha.

G

TermदेवनागरीDefinition
Gajakesari yogaगजकेसरी योगJupiter and Moon in mutual kendras (1/4/7/10 from each other). One of the major auspicious yogas — wisdom, recognition, dharma. Not fully formed in this chart (Moon 9H, Jupiter 8H — not mutual kendra). See ch16.
Gandantaगण्डान्तThe "knot" where water-sign ends and fire-sign begins, or where fire ends and water begins — Pisces-Aries, Cancer-Leo, Scorpio-Sagittarius transitions. Birth in gandanta is karmically loaded.
GocharगोचरTransit. The current real-time position of planets compared to the natal chart. See ch26.
Grahaग्रहPlanet. Literally "the seizer" — the planetary intelligence that "seizes" consciousness when its dasha or transit fires. Nine classical grahas plus three modern outer-planet additions in this book. See ch06.
GulikaगुलिकA shadowy upagraha (sub-planet) associated with Saturn. Used in muhurta and electional astrology; mostly outside scope here.
GuruगुरुJupiter. Also "teacher". Karaka of wisdom, expansion, children (in male chart), dharma, banking. See ch06 + ch13.

H

TermदेवनागरीDefinition
Harsha yogaहर्ष योगA Viparita-Raja-yoga subtype — when 6L sits in 8H, 6H, or 12H. Produces "windfall through opponents losing." Partial in this chart (6L Mercury in 8H but combust). See ch16.
HoraहोराThe D2 divisional chart for wealth. Also: a one-hour planetary period in muhurta astrology.

J

TermदेवनागरीDefinition
JaiminiजैमिनीThe ancient sage whose Jyotish-sutras give an alternate predictive system — Chara-dasha, Atma-Karaka analysis, Karakamsa-lagna, Padas. Parallels Parashari but uses different signatures. See ch28 + ch29.
Janmaजन्मBirth. Janma-nakshatra = Moon's natal nakshatra. Janma-rashi = Moon's natal sign. Janma-lagna = rising-sign at birth.
Jyotishज्योतिषVedic astrology — literally "the science of light." One of the six Vedangas (limbs of Vedic knowledge). See ch01.

K

TermदेवनागरीDefinition
Kalatra-karakaकलत्र-कारकNatural significator of wife in a male chart — Venus. (For husband in a female chart — Jupiter.) See ch28.
Kalsarpa-doshaकालसर्प-दोषThe configuration where all seven classical planets sit between Rahu and Ketu in one nodal half. Produces specific karmic-pressure pattern. Partial in this chart. See ch17.
KarakaकारकSignificator. Each life-domain has a karaka (Sun = father, Moon = mother, Venus = wife, Mars = brother and courage, Jupiter = children, Saturn = longevity-and-elders). Karaka-readings supplement house-readings.
Karakamsa Lagnaकारकांश-लग्नThe sign occupied by the Atma-Karaka in D9. Provides a parallel-lagna for spiritual and soul-vocation reading. Pisces in this chart (Jupiter-AK sits in Pisces in D9). See ch29.
KaranaकरणHalf-tithi — one of the five panchanga limbs. Taitila karana at birth here. See ch02.
Karkaकर्कCancer rashi. Moon-ruled. 4H here.
Karmaकर्मAction and its consequence; also the 10H (karma-bhava — profession, public action).
Kemadruma yogaकेमद्रुम योगA poverty-and-isolation yoga formed when Moon has no planets in adjacent houses (2nd and 12th from Moon). Broken in this chart by Mars-Venus-Neptune adjacency to Moon. See ch16.
Kendraकेंद्रThe angular houses — 1H, 4H, 7H, 10H. Strongest houses for material results. Kendra-lordship and kendra-occupation are major strength-factors.
KetuकेतूThe South Node of the Moon. Shadow-graha. Karaka of detachment, moksha, past-life completion, research, sudden events. 1H here, Aries Ashvini P1. See ch06 + ch14.
KujaकुजMars. "Born from earth." Alternative Sanskrit name for Mars. See Mangala.
Kuja-doshaकुज-दोषManglik-dosha. Mars in 1H/4H/7H/8H/12H. Affects marriage in orthodox tradition. Not strictly present in this chart (Mars in 9H). See ch17.
KundliकुंडलीBirth chart. The graphical representation of planet-positions at birth. North-Indian diamond style used in this book.

L

TermदेवनागरीDefinition
Lagnaलग्नThe rising sign on the eastern horizon at birth. The first house and the chart's identity-engine. See ch07.
Lagneshलग्नेशLord of the lagna. For Aries-lagna native, Mars is lagnesh. See ch07.
Lordस्वामी / अधिपतीThe planet ruling a sign or a house. Synonyms: adhipati, swami, esha.

M

TermदेवनागरीDefinition
MahadashaमहादशाThe main 6-to-20-year planetary period in Vimshottari. Abbreviated MD. See ch18.
Mahapurusha yogaमहापुरुष योग"Great-person yoga" — formed when one of the five major planets (Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn) is in own-sign or exaltation in a kendra. Sasa Mahapurusha (Saturn) partial in this chart. See ch16.
MangalaमंगलMars. Karaka of courage, younger-brother, property, surgery, blood, muscles. See ch06 + ch11.
Mangalik / ManglikमांगलिकA native with Kuja-dosha — Mars in 1/4/7/8/12. Subject to compatibility-restrictions in orthodox arranged marriages.
Mantraमंत्रSacred sound-formula. The first family of upayas. Recited a fixed number of times (typically 108, 23, or 11 depending on planet). See ch32.
Marak / Marakaमारक"Killer" — death-inflicting houses or lords. 2H and 7H are marak-bhavas; their lords are marak-karakas. Venus (2L+7L) is double-marak in this chart. See ch28.
Mokshaमोक्षLiberation — final release from rebirth. 12H is the moksha-bhava. 4-8-12 is the moksha-trikona.
Muhurtaमुहूर्तElectional astrology — selecting auspicious time-windows for specific actions (marriage, journey, business-start).
MulaमूळThe 19th nakshatra, Sagittarius 0° to 13°20′. Ketu-ruled. Deity Nirriti. "To dig out the root." Mars in own-nakshatra Mula here. See ch11.

N

TermदेवनागरीDefinition
Nakshatraनक्षत्रLunar mansion. 27 divisions of 13°20′ each across the zodiac. Each has a presiding deity, a planet-lord, a shakti, and four padas. The Moon's natal nakshatra (janma-nakshatra) is the chart's most-important nakshatra. See ch04.
Nava-grahaनवग्रहThe nine classical grahas — Sun, Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn, Rahu, Ketu.
NavamsaनवांशThe D9 divisional chart. Each rashi divided into nine parts of 3°20′ each. Used for marriage, dharma, and second-half-of-life reading. See ch27 + ch28.
NeechaनीचDebilitated. The opposite of Uchcha. Each planet has a sign-of-debilitation (e.g., Sun in Libra, Moon in Scorpio, Mars in Cancer). Neecha-Bhanga = cancellation-of-debilitation.
NirritiनिऋतीDeity of Mula nakshatra. "She who destroys what must end" — the goddess of clearing/dissolution.
Nodesराहू-केतूRahu (North Node) and Ketu (South Node) — the two intersection points of Moon's orbit with the ecliptic. Always 180° apart. Shadow-grahas.

P

TermदेवनागरीDefinition
Padaपाद"Foot" — quarter-division of a nakshatra. Each nakshatra has 4 padas of 3°20′ each. The pada determines the navamsa-sign. See ch04.
Panchangaपंचांग"Five-limbed" — the Vedic calendar. Five attributes: tithi (lunar-day), vara (weekday), nakshatra, yoga (Sun-Moon angle), karana (half-tithi). See ch02.
ParasharaपाराशरThe ancient sage of Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra — the foundational text of classical Vedic astrology. The Parashari tradition uses Vimshottari dasha and house-based aspects. See ch01.
PitruपितृPaternal ancestors. Pitru-dosha = karmic-debt on the paternal line, often signalled by Sun's afflictions. Pitru-tarpan = water-offering ritual. See ch17 + ch32.
Pratyantar-dashaप्रत्यंतर्दशाThe sub-sub-period within an Antardasha. Days-to-weeks granularity. Abbreviated PD. See ch18.
Pranayamaप्राणायामBreath-discipline. Anulom-vilom, bhramari, kapalbhati are common forms. Recommended for combust-Mercury and Sade-Sati phases. See ch32.
Punyaपुण्यPast-life merit. Stored in 5H (poorva-punya bhava).
Purvashadhaपूर्वाषाढाThe 20th nakshatra, Sagittarius 13°20′ to 26°40′. Venus-ruled, deity Apas (waters). Janma-nakshatra here (Moon at 17°53′ Sagittarius). See ch02 + ch10.
Putra-karakaपुत्र-कारकNatural significator of children — Jupiter. See ch31.

R

TermदेवनागरीDefinition
RahuराहूThe North Node of the Moon. Shadow-graha. Karaka of foreign, unconventional, illusion, technology, sudden status-leaps, paternal-grandfather. 7H here in Libra Chitra P3. See ch06 + ch12.
Raja-yogaराज-योग"Royal yoga" — combinations producing power, status, recognition. Classically formed by kendra-lord and trine-lord associations.
RashiराशीZodiacal sign. Twelve in number — Mesha (Aries) to Meena (Pisces). Each 30° wide. See ch03.
Retrogradeवक्रीApparent backward motion of a planet from Earth's frame. Symbolically: internalized, reflective, slower-than-direct planet-action.

S

TermदेवनागरीDefinition
Sade-Satiसाडेसाती"Seven-and-a-half" — Saturn's 7.5-year transit over the 12th, 1st, and 2nd houses from natal Moon. Three phases of 2.5 years each. Currently in Phase 3 for this native. See ch25.
Saptamsaसप्तमांशThe D7 divisional chart for children analysis. Each rashi divided into seven parts. See ch27 + ch31.
Saraswati yogaसरस्वती योगMercury-Venus-Jupiter combination producing speech, learning, art. Specific configurations.
Sasa Mahapurushaशश-महापुरुषSaturn in own-sign or exaltation in a kendra. Produces gravitas, longevity, structural-discipline-mastery. Partial here (Saturn own-sign 11H — upachaya, not strict kendra). See ch16.
SaturnशनिKaraka of longevity, service, work-discipline, slow-progress, elders, sorrow-then-mastery. Own sign Aquarius 11H here. See ch06 + ch14.
SevaसेवाSelfless service — one of the upaya-families. Especially Saturn-pleasing.
Shanaischaraशनैश्चर"Slow-mover" — Saturn's full Sanskrit name. The slowest-moving classical planet.
Shastraशास्त्रTreatise, scriptural-science. Hora-Shastra = treatise on horoscopy.
Shrapit-doshaशापित-दोष"Curse-affliction" — Saturn-Rahu yuti or close mutual aspect. Absent in this chart. See ch17.
ShubhaशुभAuspicious. Natural benefics (Jupiter, Venus, Mercury, Moon waxing) are shubha-grahas.
Shukraशुक्रVenus. Karaka of wife (male chart), comfort, art, beauty, vehicles, jewellery, sweet-taste, romance. Also "Friday" — Shukra-vara. See ch06 + ch11.
Sookshmaसूक्ष्मThe sub-sub-sub-period inside a Pratyantar-dasha. Hours-to-days granularity. Used in fine-grain event-prediction.
Sthiraस्थिरFixed sign — Taurus, Leo, Scorpio, Aquarius. Stability, endurance, slow-to-change.
Stotraस्तोत्रDevotional hymn. Aditya-Hridaya, Hanuman Chalisa, Vishnu-Sahasranama are common stotras. See ch32.
Suryaसूर्यSun. Karaka of self-authority, father, vitality, eye, heart, government, ego. 5L in 8H here. See ch06 + ch09.

T

TermदेवनागरीDefinition
Tarpanतर्पणWater-offering ritual, especially for paternal ancestors during Pitru-Paksha. Pitru-dosha remedy. See ch32.
TithiतिथीLunar day. One of the five panchanga limbs. 30 tithis per lunar month (15 each in Shukla and Krishna pakshas). See ch02.
Trikaत्रिकThe three difficult houses — 6H, 8H, 12H. Same as dusthana.
Trine / Trikonaत्रिकोणThe trine houses — 1H, 5H, 9H. The dharma-trikona. Among the most auspicious house-groupings.

U

TermदेवनागरीDefinition
Uchchaउच्चExalted. The strongest dignity. Specific degree in specific sign (e.g., Sun exalted at 10° Aries, Moon at 3° Taurus). No major exaltation in this chart.
UpachayaउपचयThe "growing" houses — 3H, 6H, 10H, 11H. Difficulties and gains both grow over time; planets in upachayas improve with age. Saturn in own-sign 11H here — strong upachaya-occupation. See ch15.

TermदेवनागरीDefinition
Vakriवक्रीRetrograde (see above).
VaraवारWeekday. One of the five panchanga limbs. Saturday (Shanivara) at birth here. See ch02.
Vargaवर्गDivisional chart. The 16 standard vargas (D1, D2, D3, D4, D7, D9, D10, D12, D16, D20, D24, D27, D30, D40, D45, D60). See ch27.

Note — How to use this glossary / या शब्दकोशाचा उपयोग कसा करावा

This glossary is designed as a working reference, not a reading-from-front exercise. When a chapter uses a term like Karakamsa Lagna or Viparita-Raja-yoga, the reader can flip here for a 1-3 sentence working definition, then return to the chapter for the chart-specific application. The same term will often appear in multiple chapters with different angles; this glossary gives the single working sense used in this book, with cross-references to the chapter where the concept is most-fully developed.

For deeper study, classical sources to consult: Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra (Parashara), Phaladeepika (Mantreshwara), Jataka Parijata (Vaidyanatha), Saravali (Kalyana Varma), Brihat Jataka (Varahamihira), Hora Sara (Prithuyasas), Jaimini Sutras. Modern English-language commentaries by B.V. Raman, K.S. Charak, K.N. Rao, James Braha, and Hart de Fouw fill in the practitioner's-manual gap between classical text and modern application.

मराठी सूचना

हा शब्दकोश संदर्भ-साधन म्हणून रचला आहे — पहिल्या अध्यायापासून शेवटपर्यंत वाचण्यासाठी नव्हे. कोणत्याही अध्यायात संस्कृत/मराठी पारिभाषिक शब्द आल्यास येथे एक-दोन वाक्यांत व्याख्या मिळेल आणि क्रॉस-संदर्भ देऊन तो अध्याय कुठे आहे हे दर्शविले आहे. प्रत्येक शब्दाची देवनागरी लिपी + इंग्रजी अनुवाद + व्याख्या या तीन घटकांत मांडली आहे. अधिक खोल अभ्यासासाठी पाराशर बृहत् होरा शास्त्र, फलदीपिका (मंत्रेश्वर), जातक पारिजात, सारावली, बृहत् जातक (वराहमिहिर), होरा सार, जैमिनी सूत्रे हे शास्त्रीय ग्रंथ; आधुनिक भाष्ये बी. व्ही. रामन्, के. एस. चरक, के. एन. राव यांची.
