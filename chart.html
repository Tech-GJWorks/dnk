Key Yogas at a glance

  • शश महापुरुष योग (Sasa Mahapurusha) — Saturn in own sign Aquarius in kendra (here 11H, technically a upachaya not kendra; partial Mahapurusha effect through own-sign dignity)
  • राहू-केतू अक्ष (Rahu-Ketu axis 1H–7H) — past-life karmic axis across self ↔ partner
  • विपरीत राज योग (Vipareeta Raja) — Mercury (6L) is in 8H (a dusthana) → classical Harsha-adjacent vipareeta hint, with strong combustion caveat
  • केमद्रुम योग check (Kemadruma) — Moon has Mars+Venus+Neptune flanking in same sign 9H, so Kemadruma is broken (planets adjacent to Moon)
  • नीच-भंग check (Neechabhanga) — no debilitated graha sits in this chart at standard debilitation degrees (Sun in Scorpio is friendly, not debilitated; Mars/Ju are dignified)
  • Gajakesari check — Moon and Jupiter are in 9H and 8H respectively; not in mutual kendra → Gajakesari does NOT form
