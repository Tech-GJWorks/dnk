About this book / या ग्रंथाबद्दल

This is the second volume in the Gitesh Jawale Astrology Series. The first volume — Gitesh's Kundli — carries the full encyclopedic teaching of every rashi, nakshatra, house, graha, yoga, dosha, dasha, and transit, worked out on a Cancer-lagna sample chart. This DNK volume sits on top of that foundation — it does not repeat the generic material. Where a section needs a rule, it cites the rule and links the parent book. The bulk of every page is application to this specific Aries-lagna chart, currently in Mars Mahadasha (Jan 2025 – Jan 2032).

हा गितेश जवळेच्या ज्योतिष-ग्रंथमालेतील दुसरा खंड. पहिला खंड — गितेशची कुंडली — कर्क-लग्न नमुन्यावर प्रत्येक राशी, नक्षत्र, भाव, ग्रह, योग, दोष, दशा, गोचर यांचे संपूर्ण विश्वकोशीय शिक्षण देतो. हा DNK खंड त्या पायावर उभा आहे. प्रत्येक पानाचा बहुतांश भाग — मेष-लग्न, सध्या चालू मंगळ-महादशा (जाने २०२५ – जाने २०३२) — या विशिष्ट कुंडलीवर लागू करण्यात गुंतलेला आहे.

The Nativeजातक

Dharmesh Naresh Kandare is an Aries-lagna native born on 25 November 1995 at 16:02 IST in Ichalkaranji, Maharashtra. His chart features an 8H Scorpio stellium (Sun, Mercury combust, Jupiter), a 9H Sagittarius stellium (Moon, Mars, Venus) with the lagnesh Mars in own-nakshatra Mula, Saturn own-sign in 11H Aquarius, and the Rahu-Ketu axis across 7H–1H. The chart is currently in Mars Mahadasha (Jan 2025 → Jan 2032), with Mars–Rahu Antardasha running through mid-2026. This book reads that chart end-to-end.

How to read this book / हा ग्रंथ कसा वाचावा

Sequential reading is recommended. Part I builds the foundations (lagna, panchanga, rashis, nakshatras, houses, grahas). Part II isolates the chart's structural signature one element at a time. Part III synthesises house-by-house and stacks yogas + doshas. Part IV walks the entire Vimshottari sequence MD-by-MD. Parts V–VII move into transits, life-domain readings, and the final life-events walk. A reader who jumps straight into Part IV without Parts I–III will get the timing windows but miss why each window matters for this specific chart.
